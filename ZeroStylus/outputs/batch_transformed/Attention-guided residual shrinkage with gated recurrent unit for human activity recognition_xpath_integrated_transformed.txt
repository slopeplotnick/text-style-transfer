Human Action Recognition (HAR) is important for applications such as video surveillance. However, despite the proliferation of HAR methods, their ability to effectively capture spatial features (SF) and temporal features (TF) remains inadequate. To address these limitations, the proposed HAR model comprises two modules: Multiple Residual Shrinkage Building Units (MRSBU) for spatial feature extraction and a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) for temporal modeling. Moreover, the model employs Inception v3 to extract rich spatial features from video frames, and the MRSBU learns complex temporal features through an adaptive soft-thresholding step that removes noisy and redundant features. These representations are subsequently fed into a GRU with temporal attention (TA), which assigns different importance to each frame and performs activity classification. This small and effective model generalizes well across several challenging HAR datasets. Specifically, the MRSBU with GRU achieves accuracies of 99.75%, 99.55%, and 98.95% on UCF50, UCF101, and HMDB51, respectively, and outperforms typical CNN-LSTM, Vision Transformer (ViT), and 3D-CNN models. These results indicate that the model demonstrates effectiveness and robustness across diverse video scenarios, with evidence from real-world surveillance and anomaly detection.
