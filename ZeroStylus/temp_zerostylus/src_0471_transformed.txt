In this section, we delineate the overarching methodology applied across all four studies, except where deviations are noted within their respective sections. Moreover, the lower section illustrates the setup procedures for experiments conducted in all four studies. The simulated dataset was generated using GPT4o-mini, an iteration of GPT-4 updated to October 2023. The model was configured with default parameters, specifically Temperature set at 1.0 and Top P at 1.0, without the application of frequency or presence penalties. Additionally, specific socio-demographic attributes, including age, gender, and ethnicity, were provided in the prompt to characterize an individual; subsequently, the model was tasked with completing a questionnaire designed for evaluation purposes. The prompt was structured to encompass the questions, answer selections, and directives to present the answers as a unified sequence of values demarcated by commas, thereby facilitating more efficient processing. To ensure that responses were not influenced by prior inputs, each prompt was processed independently, with no retention of preceding interactions. Below is an instance of a prompt utilized to produce the simulated data: "Impersonate an individual of [Ethnicity] [Gender] who is [Age] years of age residing in the United Kingdom." To facilitate data collection, participants are invited to respond to a structured [Number of items]-item questionnaire, systematically designed to capture relevant metrics. For each question, a Likert scale is employed, encompassing a range of specified values. We chose to utilize this key to provide responses: [Response Key]. Consequently, the questionnaire items have been structured to address the inquiries presented. Finally, participants are requested to provide their responses as a concise sequence of values, each delineated by a comma.
