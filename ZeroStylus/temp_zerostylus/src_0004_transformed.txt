ReVSeg exhibits remarkable proficiency in the domain of video object segmentation (VOS), consistently exceeding the performance of existing methodologies on standard benchmarks. The results illustrate that this approach not only outperforms methods that utilize latent features but also surpasses those that require no training; moreover, it elucidates the rationale behind its decision-making process. Figure 1 provides evidence that the exclusion of the reasoning steps we incorporated results in both the basic model and its variant enhanced via reinforcement learning (RL) underperforming on complex tasks. Initially, our method demonstrates satisfactory performance prior to the commencement of RL training; however, the integration of RL subsequently enhances its efficacy. To achieve this, we decompose the reasoning process for VOS into a series of well-defined steps, leveraging fundamental model components to construct an interpretable chain of reasoning. In our second approach, we introduce a novel reinforcement learning configuration that optimizes this reasoning chain, enabling the model to make more informed decisions without the necessity for extensive additional training data. Consequently, we establish a new state of the art on VOS tests while ensuring the reasoning process remains interpretable. Recent advancements in fine-tuned models and specialized configurations have notably improved text-guided VOS across benchmarks [3, 5, 29, 45]. Nevertheless, when confronting more complex challenges, such as processing videos with numerous similar objects, rapid movements, or objects that are difficult to discern, the task remains arduous. In this context, methods such as CoT-RVS [13] address these challenges by employing two distinct systems to process individual video frames, which are subsequently linked via textual representations. Although functional, this approach is constrained by the lack of integration between systems and the absence of training mechanisms. In response, we approached reasoning VOS as a structured, step-by-step process within a unified system and leveraged reinforcement learning (RL) to iteratively enhance its performance over time.
