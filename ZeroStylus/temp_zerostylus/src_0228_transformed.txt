The initial investigation was undertaken to acquire grammatical features, followed by a subsequent experiment involving 10,000 noun phrases and sentences generated by Delilah. If the language acquisition processes can reliably distinguish and classify words, the results are represented using graphs with feature-value pairs labeled in a manner consistent with linguistic terminology. As depicted in Figure 3a, the labels utilized by the MODOMA agent are shown, while Figure 3b illustrates more conventional labels. Consequently, a follow-up experiment was conducted using larger data sets containing 10,000 noun phrases and sentences generated by Delilah. The lexical statistics for these data sets are summarized in Table 5. Furthermore, the 35 most frequently occurring words in both data sets are presented in Tables 6 and 7, and these words are predominantly function words. Beyond rank 54/55, where the first significant content word 'forgotten' appears alongside the function word 'at' or 'near', the prevalence of content words begins to increase. Similarly, in the examination of 10,000 sentences generated by Delilah, the most frequent content words identified were 'forgotten' at rank 11, 'works' at position 27, and 'in the morning' at rank 66. Beyond rank 66, the quantity of content words continues to rise. Notably, Delilah uses 'masses' as a numerical value, and this usage is accurately assimilated by the learning process. In the corpus of 10,000 examples, distinguishing between function and content words becomes more straightforward, as words appearing over 100 times are typically function words, exceeding a frequency of 1.7 per thousand within Delilah's sentences. Therefore, based on these tests, the MODOMA parameters are set such that words with a frequency exceeding 2 per thousand are categorized as function words, while others are identified as content words. This learning process is conducted once after accumulating at least 10,000 examples. The parameters are conservatively set to minimize the false classification of function words, with content words being the default category unless compelling evidence suggests otherwise. The initial configuration parameters from this preliminary assessment now serve as the default settings for MODOMA. However, these parameters remain adjustable based on subsequent evaluations and linguistic theories.
