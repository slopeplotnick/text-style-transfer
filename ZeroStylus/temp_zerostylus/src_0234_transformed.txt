A notable implementation of the retrieval-and-synthesis capability is the AI Overviews feature within Google Search. This system, powered by the Gemini model family, functions as a retrieval-enhanced generation pipeline, systematically synthesizing concise summaries from extensive documents to enhance user comprehension and engagement [Reid, 2024]. Consequently, it streamlines the process for users by eliminating the need to manually aggregate information from disparate sources, thereby saving time and effort. However, this raises critical concerns regarding the reliability of the information provided. Specifically, when an AI synthesizes multiple documents into a singular summary, it becomes essential to assess whether the system maintains uniform treatment across all sources or exhibits any preferential biases. Recent studies have focused on exploring biases within AI models. For instance, Santurkar et al. [2023] found that systems such as GPT-4 and Claude frequently express tendencies aligned with liberal social perspectives. In the domain of news summarization, Vijay et al. [2024] proposed methods to quantify biases, specifically by analyzing variations in linguistic patterns and assessing whether the AI disproportionately adheres to a singular perspective. While empirical investigations have predominantly focused on identifying biases in AI-generated summaries, there is a noticeable paucity of research examining whether AI models exhibit biases in the processing of multiple documents. This underscores an operational ambiguity: particularly with longer documents, AI systems may assign varying degrees of importance based on their positional context. Although phenomena such as primacy bias and the tendency to become disoriented in the middle have been observed in various tasks, their influence on the process of summarizing multiple documents remains insufficiently explored. Therefore, this paper investigates whether the sequence of articles, exemplified by abortion news articles, influences their representation in AI-generated summaries. The investigation undertaken in this study is structured to address the research questions outlined in the subsequent sections.
