In configuring both solvers and verifiers, a generation setup was utilized, characterized by a temperature setting of 0.7, a top-p value of 0.9, and a maximum output length of 8192 tokens. To ensure consistency within our process, we elected to discard any output lacking a boxed answer. The experimental evaluations were conducted using vLLM on H200 GPUs. For a comprehensive understanding of the prompts and the methodology used to filter outputs, please refer to Appendix F. An examination of solver performance was conducted across all 37 models evaluated on 9 datasets, with the average performance depicted in Figure 1 and detailed results for each task available in Appendix G. Generally, models with larger capacities demonstrated superior performance. Notably, models from the Qwen3 and DeepSeek groups emerged as leading performers, whereas Llama3-Base exhibited suboptimal performance due to its lack of familiarity with the question-answering format. Moreover, Qwen2.5-Base and DeepSeek showed notable improvements with increased scaling, while other groups exhibited similar trends of enhanced performance as scaling progressed. In this analysis, we examine the correspondence between solver performance and verifier performance, as illustrated in Figure 2. For the 21 post-trained models evaluated across each dataset, we assess the effectiveness of verifiers applied to their corresponding solver models by examining metrics such as verifier accuracy, F1-score, precision, false positive rate (FPR), false negative rate (FNR), and the gain achieved for each solver-verifier pairing. The study further examines the division of metrics for each verifier into three distinct verification setups, calculating the average outcomes across various solvers and datasets for each configuration. Our analysis reveals that verifier accuracy tends to improve concomitantly with enhancements in the solver's accuracy, although other metrics exhibit more intricate patterns. Specifically, we observe an increase in false positive rates during self-verification and when models from the same group are verified, whereas a slight decrease is noted when verifying models from different groups. This indicates that verifiers with advanced solver capabilities are more predisposed to inaccurately assess their outputs or those from their group as correct. Further elaboration on F1-score and precision is provided in Appendix H.
