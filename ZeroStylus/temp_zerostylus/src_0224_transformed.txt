Within the realm of mineral exploration, it is primarily smaller mining enterprises that conduct the majority of exploratory activities. These companies typically operate a limited number of sites and employ a modest team of geoscientists. Their funding is often contingent upon demonstrating specific indicators of success, such as the identification of mineral deposits through initial drilling efforts. As a result, they prioritize securing additional financial resources for further drilling over adopting a scientifically rigorous decision-making framework. Although challenging a geological hypothesis requires substantial time and effort to gather sufficient data, these smaller companies remain predominantly driven by the pursuit of short-term outcomes. Consequently, the need for innovative funding mechanisms becomes apparent. Investing based on individual drill holes is akin to allocating financial resources into a single stock option.
