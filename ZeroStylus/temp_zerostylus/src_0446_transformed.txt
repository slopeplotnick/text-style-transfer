However, it was found that certain volunteers encountered challenges with the repetitive task of updating card details (P1, P4). In the process of story creation, the initial consideration involves conceptualizing the overarching narrative framework, thus commencing with the establishment of event and entity cards to systematically construct the storyline. Thereafter, detail augmentation is applied to the event and entity cards. In alignment with the statement by P4, we document the iterative modifications necessitated throughout the creative process. Streamlining the modification of granular details enhances usability; therefore, improved accessibility to such adjustments is likely to increase the frequency of method adoption. In future analyses, we will investigate methods to convey narrative elements through interactive modalities, as suggested by certain participants (P2, P3), to enhance the storytelling experience. Specifically, Participant 2 expressed a preference for arranging entity cards associated with a specific event atop the corresponding event cards, as entity identification constitutes a straightforward method to determine associations. Furthermore, future iterations may explore the integration of relationship cards as a mechanism to establish connections between distinct entity cards, thereby enhancing the representation of inter-entity relationships and contributing to more cohesive narrative structures. In subsequent analyses, we will explore methodologies to effectively represent the sequence and progression of events by implementing connective lines between event cards, thereby enhancing the clarity and coherence of event flow. Informed by these concepts, we observe significant interaction patterns (Figure 2) and propose three primary design objectives: firstly, the system should facilitate user input of detailed information for each principal component, thereby aiding in the construction of an initial narrative framework. Secondly, the system should aim to enhance user comprehension of the entire narrative configuration, thereby simplifying the recognition of interconnections among entities and events. Lastly, the system should provide constructive feedback and tailored recommendations to refine the narrative framework, thus fostering the development of more structured and cohesive story designs.
