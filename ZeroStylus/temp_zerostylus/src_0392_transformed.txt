On-device AI represents a promising approach for mental health management, as it offers personalized feedback in real-time while maintaining user privacy. By integrating AI functionalities directly into mobile devices, this approach ensures enhanced data security by obviating the need for cloud-based data transmission and minimizing internet dependency. Consequently, the accessibility of mental health support is significantly improved, particularly for individuals residing far from healthcare facilities or facing lengthy wait times for appointments. Furthermore, the AI's capability to operate directly on personal devices provides rapid feedback and facilitates the detection of subtle changes in speech or behavior, which may indicate stress or mood disruptions. The increasing traction of using mobile devices and personal gadgets for mental health monitoring can be attributed to their efficiency, reduced energy consumption, and superior data privacy, providing a compelling alternative to cloud-dependent systems. Recent research has demonstrated that small language models (SLMs) can effectively function on devices such as the Samsung Galaxy S24, supporting tasks like document assistance and health prediction while optimizing memory usage and processing speed. These models are particularly beneficial for practical applications due to their straightforward implementation and minimal resource requirements. Therefore, on-device AI eliminates the necessity for continuous cloud connectivity, thereby safeguarding user privacy. However, within the domain of mental health, SLMs have proven effective for specific tasks, yet there are substantial areas that remain unexplored, limiting their performance on complex data requiring intricate task management. Notably, limited attention has been given to evaluating these models' performance on resource-constrained devices, underscoring the need for further research to ensure their effectiveness in such environments. To address these challenges, Menta introduces an innovative framework that utilizes the LoRA methodology to efficiently manage multiple tasks and mitigate issues related to imbalanced data. The system is designed to maintain high performance across various mental health applications while ensuring efficiency and privacy protection on standard consumer devices.
