In online shopping scenarios, individuals typically request items characterized by multiple attributes simultaneously, such as brand, price range, functionality, or intended audience. Therefore, the system must concurrently account for and integrate these details in their entirety, rather than addressing them in isolation, ensuring their proper organization. The system's decision-making process regarding the presentation of content to users involves attribute extraction and the matching of user requests with appropriate items. This underscores the necessity of evaluating the reliance on diverse data modalities, such as text or images, independently of the user’s query to ensure balanced and effective integration. Moreover, the system should conceptualize the task of relevance as a process of extracting and matching attributes found within the query details and item specifics, subsequently considering the application of this information in determining the content presented to the user. Consequently, the system establishes a pathway from the user's inquiry to the relevant items it identifies, thereby facilitating the understanding of user queries and item attributes. The process begins by deconstructing the question into granular details, utilizing these specifics to systematically filter and select items. Additionally, the process is framed as a two-fold task involving the alignment of user queries with corresponding item attributes, first by ensuring accurate comprehension and mapping of the query to relevant item details, and subsequently by selecting the appropriate items based on these mapped attributes. Navigating these processes often involves complexity and ambiguity, thus ensuring a sufficient level of challenge. For example, when an individual searches for "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set," the system is required to extract and align the desired attributes with the corresponding product features. This begins by mapping the contextual elements of the query to the corresponding attributes of the items. Finally, the system is tasked with ensuring all elements are accurately aligned, which is subsequently verified for consistency.
