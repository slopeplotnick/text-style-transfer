To ensure that no translation was required, the prompts were directly generated in the native language of each team, even though the interface was presented in English. Upon the completion of a specified task, team members created the prompt in their native language. Before initiating the task, all participants were required to complete a brief sample project to ensure a clear understanding of the requirements. Moreover, before generating a minimum of 2,000 prompts, each team thoroughly understood the project objectives. To align with the target areas, materials culturally relevant and specific to domains such as agriculture and health were thoroughly reviewed before the development of the scripted prompts. These materials included governmental publications on public services, entries from Wikipedia in South African languages, a comprehensive database ensuring precise vocabulary, and translated manuals concerning animal health and agricultural practices. The dataset then underwent a meticulous cleaning and preparation process to optimize the text for training speech recognition models effectively. Each stage of data refinement involved the elimination of extraneous elements, the categorization of text into thematic groups, and the identification or annotation of personal information. The collected data was subjected to initial quality checks, including a review for structural issues. Consequently, the dataset was curated to retain exclusively the South African languages that aligned with the intended scope of the study. Instances where the primary language did not align with the anticipated selection were excluded from further consideration; however, the associated confidence scores were retained as supplementary information.
