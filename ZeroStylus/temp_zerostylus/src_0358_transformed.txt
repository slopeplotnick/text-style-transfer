DeepSeek-V3.2 demonstrates performance on par with Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across a range of reasoning benchmarks. Notably, it significantly enhances the agentic capabilities of open-source models, exhibiting exceptional proficiency in long-tail agent tasks as identified in EvalSys (2025), Li et al. (2025), and Luo et al. (2025). Moreover, DeepSeek-V3.2 represents a cost-effective alternative for agentic scenarios, effectively narrowing the performance gap between open-access and leading proprietary models, while maintaining considerably lower costs. In our efforts to further advance the reasoning capabilities of open models, we introduced DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale by relaxing length constraints. Consequently, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale achieves performance parity with the premier closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro (DeepMind, 2025b), and demonstrates gold-medal performance in competitive events such as the IOI 2025, ICPC World Final 2025, IMO 2025, and CMO 2025. Additionally, DeepSeek-V3.2 employs the same architecture as DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp. The primary architectural modification from DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, the last version of DeepSeek-V3.1, is the incorporation of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) through continued training. The DSA framework is primarily composed of two components: a lightning indexer and a fine-grained token selection mechanism. DeepSeek-V3.2 was developed by initializing with a base checkpoint of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which had its context length extended to 128K, followed by continued pre-training and post-training. This continued pre-training comprises two stages, both aligned with the data distribution used for the 128K context extension in DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, we evaluated DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp on a comprehensive suite of benchmarks, demonstrating performance comparable to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. Furthermore, DeepSeek V3.2 Exp significantly improves computational efficiency on long sequences without substantial performance degradation when compared to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, across both short- and long-context tasks.
