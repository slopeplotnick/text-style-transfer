**Procedure:** The experiment was conducted over a duration of approximately 20 to 35 minutes. At the outset, participants were instructed to select an image of their preference, which would serve as the basis for a subsequent writing task. To ensure clarity, participants were asked to confirm their understanding of the content depicted in their chosen image. Following this, they were introduced to the features and interactive functionalities of TaleFrame, a tool designed to aid in narrative construction. Subsequently, participants were tasked with creating a story inspired by their selected image. Throughout the experimental process, the researchers systematically recorded participants' inputs, actions, and verbal responses. Upon completing the narrative task, participants were invited to provide feedback through a structured set of questions aimed at capturing their reflections on the experience. As a token of appreciation, each participant received $5 for their involvement in the study. **User Feedback:** To evaluate user experience, participants rated TaleFrame on a 5-point Likert scale, as illustrated in Figure 8. The evaluation encompassed four primary domains: ① **Framework Construction (Q1-Q3):** TaleFrame was designed to offer users an efficient and intuitive platform for constructing comprehensive and structured story frameworks. This functionality is made possible through its user-friendly interface and input options, which enable users to define key story elements, including entities, events, and their relationships, with ease (DG1). ② **Narrative Visualization (Q4-Q6):** The system facilitates the interactive organization and visualization of narrative components, allowing users to clearly perceive the interconnections among entities and events. This visual clarity enhances the comprehensibility of the story structure. Moreover, TaleFrame provides flexible tools that enable users to iteratively refine or optimize the story framework during the creation process, thus promoting greater engagement and creativity (DG1, DG2). ③ **Narrative Timing and Coherence (Q7-Q9):** TaleFrame supports the maintenance of temporal coherence within the narrative. It assists users in analyzing the sequence of events, ensuring consistency in the progression of actions and behaviors over time. Furthermore, the system identifies potential temporal inconsistencies and provides actionable recommendations to improve emotional depth and thematic alignment. These features facilitate the creation of cohesive plot developments and entity relationships that adhere to a logical timeline (DG2, DG3). ④ **Evaluation and Improvement Suggestions (Q10-Q12):** Finally, TaleFrame employs a systematic evaluation framework to assess the quality of the constructed narrative. Based on this analysis, it offers targeted optimization suggestions, enabling users to refine and enhance the overall quality of their story. This feature ensures that the narrative achieves both structural and emotional depth (DG3).
