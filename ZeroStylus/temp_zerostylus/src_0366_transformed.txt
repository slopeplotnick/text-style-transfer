The evaluation of ADORE demonstrates its effectiveness through significant performance in both offline and online settings. In offline evaluation, since the task is approached as a binary classification problem, the assessment of model performance is conducted through the calculation of precision, recall, and F1 score. Meanwhile, in the online evaluation, we assess its performance in search advertisements by analyzing metrics such as click-through rates, revenue generation, and expert evaluations of ad quality. The analysis reveals that ADORE is compared to other robust models such as BERT, LLAMA-3-8B, and their respective versions. For the BERT models, we examine two variants: the dual-component architecture and the cross-encoder configuration. Furthermore, for the cross-encoder-based BERT, we compare its compact version, the standard model, and the variant augmented with additional information introduced by us. In terms of the LLM model, the utilization of the representation of the last token for decision-making is observed. The study introduces ADORE, akin to BERT-Mini, which leverages online data, synthetically generated data crafted through specific methodologies, and supplementary information. The results, summarized in Table 2, indicate that ADORE achieves superior performance compared to all other models on a large-scale real-world dataset. Upon examination, we observe several key aspects: first, the cross-encoder BERT demonstrates superior performance compared to the two-part BERT configuration, with performance enhancements observed upon increasing its model size. Moreover, incorporating additional information enhances BERT's performance, demonstrating that providing explicit and well-structured information effectively improves model outcomes. However, the efficacy of utilizing LLM for decision-making is inferior to that of BERT. Notably, the LLM module effectively facilitates the generation of challenging examples by leveraging its understanding of the RD section's rules. Additionally, incorporating synthetic data from the DS component enhances the F1 score. Finally, incorporating explicit information into the KD component is of significant importance as it enhances the model's cognitive processes and overall performance.
