This research introduces Lang2Logic, a novel methodology designed to integrate colloquial language with computational logic. Specifically, the Lang2Logic framework converts regular language into Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF), an essential representation for facilitating logical reasoning tasks. By systematically converting human language into a format comprehensible by computational systems, it facilitates the integration of natural language with computational logic. To achieve precise conversion from natural language to CNF, the Lang2Logic system employs advanced computational tools alongside a proprietary linguistic parsing methodology. Consequently, this configuration facilitates the conversion of ordinary language into a SAT formulation—a type of logical problem amenable to computational resolution—thereby enhancing the applicability of SAT solvers in practical scenarios. The Lang2Logic framework aims to bridge the gap between natural language comprehension and computational logic by converting English into CNF, as required by SAT solvers for effective operation. The framework comprises three core components: transforming regular language into a logical representation, converting this representation into CNF, and optimizing the resulting CNF for simplicity. The transformation from natural language to CNF necessitates the integration of essential components to ensure precision and efficacy. Moreover, the framework leverages the ChatGPT o1-mini model API, employing advanced parsing techniques to systematically convert natural language into precise logical expressions. This setup ensures the identification of repeated components, facilitates the expression of logical formulas compatible with other computational tools, and guarantees compliance with the format required by the Sympy converter. However, the utilization of the ChatGPT API circumvents the need to develop intricate grammar rule systems, thereby streamlining the adaptation process, though it may incur higher computational costs and exhibit reduced efficacy when processing larger input datasets. To address these challenges, the preparatory stage involved the utilization of NLTK, which facilitated the refinement of input data. Specifically, the Punkt tokenizer in NLTK segments the text into individual sentences, enabling the o1-mini model to process each sentence independently. This prevents errors such as sentence misalignment or omission. Indeed, the o1-mini model employs a sentence-by-sentence processing methodology, leveraging structured parsing techniques to produce precise logical expressions. The initial illustration demonstrates the conversion of an English sentence into a logical expression, utilizing a structured approach to ensure fidelity to the original meaning.
