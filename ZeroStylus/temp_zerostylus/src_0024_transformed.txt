Subsequently, we adopted a two-stage training framework, encompassing Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL), to integrate diverse Chain-of-Thought (CoT) data types for training the Qwen2.5-VL-7B model. The Qwen2.5-VL-7B model exhibits a parameter scale comparable to the LLM base model utilized in this study, ensuring a consistent basis for performance comparisons. The evaluation results, presented in Tables 13 and 14, reveal that models leveraging LLMs consistently outperform those based on VLMs across both general and challenging datasets, with a particularly pronounced advantage in macro-F1 metrics. This superiority suggests that, when equipped with equivalent CoT capability injection, LLMs demonstrate enhanced reasoning and knowledge acquisition capacities relative to VLMs. However, VLM-based models excel in processing visual salience subsets, as expected, due to their ability to bypass the intermediate step of converting visual data into textual representations, thereby minimizing information loss during image-to-text transformations. Furthermore, as illustrated in Fig. 18, the LORE model has undergone systematic refinements over three distinct phases, marked by advancements across five core dimensions: the articulation of objectives, the configuration of model architecture, the integration of data and prompt engineering strategies, the optimization of evaluation frameworks, and the diversification of application scenarios. Consequently, these phases collectively outline a structured trajectory of innovation, integrating increasingly sophisticated architectural designs, robust data pipelines, and effective deployment methodologies to enhance search relevancy in e-commerce applications. In this context, the initial phase focused on addressing a key challenge—the comprehension of queries within the e-commerce domain, particularly the accurate identification of entity mentions characterized by non-standard nomenclature, such as colloquialisms or unregistered product terms. To tackle this issue, we constructed extensive, high-quality e-commerce datasets and leveraged In-Context Learning (ICL) to systematically inject domain-specific knowledge into the model, thereby strengthening its semantic understanding of e-commerce language and establishing a robust foundation for downstream applications.
