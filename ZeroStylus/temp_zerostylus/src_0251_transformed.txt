When constructing a narrative, one can initiate the process by identifying a central event as the foundation, subsequently shaping the traits and developmental trajectories of the entities associated with that event. Alternatively, users may begin with an entity or object as the focal point and then develop a series of interconnected events surrounding it to enrich the storyline. E2, a doctoral researcher in literature, posits that narratives can be systematically deconstructed into components such as story outlines, sequences, conceptual frameworks, and fundamental linguistic elements. This approach enables a more structured methodology for narrative analysis. By progressively reassembling these components in reverse order, one can ultimately construct a coherent and fully realized narrative. For instance, conceptual elements—including an individual, an action, or an attribute—can collectively form a proposition. Several propositions can then be organized into a sequence, and multiple interconnected sequences collectively contribute to the construction of a complete and unified story. However, the integration of these components alone does not inherently result in a well-constructed narrative. The elements that bind these components together, referred to as relationships, are essential for ensuring the coherence and functionality of the entire narrative system. In discussions with two subject matter experts, it was identified that the structural composition of a narrative can be delineated into four fundamental components: the entities involved, the sequence of events, the interconnected relationships, and the overarching framework that guides the storyline. Furthermore, analysis of expert discussions revealed that the interpretation of these components can vary depending on the context. While events constitute the central framework of a narrative, their nuanced portrayal is not always immediately discernible within the structure through which the story is conveyed. Moreover, the interaction among events within a narrative is critical for creating tension and significance, akin to the emotional progression that underpins the impact of a story on its audience. Similarly, the presence and interaction of characters within a narrative play a pivotal role in shaping the progression of events. In subsequent analyses, we will address the segregation of entity characteristics and relational dynamics to refine the delineation of individual components. This refinement will facilitate a more nuanced understanding of narrative elements, thereby contributing to a more comprehensive framework for narrative construction and analysis.
