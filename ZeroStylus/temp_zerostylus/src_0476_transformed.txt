The exploration of additional tools for prompt enhancement is ongoing, with promising optimizers such as GEPA (Agrawal et al., 2025), TextGrad (Yuksekgonul et al., 2025), MIPRO (Opsahl-Ong et al., 2024), and PromptWizard yet to be integrated into promptsolution. However, the majority of these tools remain confined to research-specific environments that are often characterized by infrequent updates, inadequate documentation, and limited validation. Consequently, these features collectively hinder their usability, particularly in comparative analyses with other methodologies or in practical applications. In contrast, the prompt optimization landscape has seen the emergence of numerous actively maintained libraries and tools, available as both open-source and commercial solutions. Notably, certain freely available optimizers are compatible exclusively with specific methods; for instance, TextGrad, AdalFlow, Microsoft’s PromptWizard, and PromptIM each employ distinct optimization strategies. Meanwhile, prompt-ops supports two optimizers but is predominantly focused on optimizing prompts for Llama models. Conversely, the promptsolution system provides a framework that allows for the use of various optimizers with any LLM implementation. Additionally, the DSPy framework offers a module dedicated to enhancing prompt optimization within any LLM configuration. By incorporating tools such as MIPROv2 and GEPA, DSPy facilitates the integration of LLM training with prompt optimization. While DSPy primarily serves as a versatile tool for constructing AI programs, promptsolution is exclusively dedicated to prompt optimization, making it particularly beneficial for researchers and experts engaged in in-depth investigations. Consequently, promptomatix, a derivative of DSPy, remains more straightforward and introduces a unique approach to optimizing prompts.
