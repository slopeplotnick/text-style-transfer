The methodology for conducting interviews was meticulously planned to ensure coherence and fluidity. The objective was to facilitate a dialogue that resembled a conversation more than a structured Q&A session. Initially, interview sessions commenced with a brief reminder of the principles of SPLE and an explanation of the questionnaire's purpose. This introductory phase was followed by questions aimed at understanding the profile of the interviewee within the organization. Subsequently, we explored general questions concerning the company's developmental processes. This progression led to more specific inquiries about the current methodologies for product development. The intent here was to ascertain stakeholders' perceptions of effective practices and the overall state of progress. Discussing these existing processes seamlessly transitioned into introducing a novel approach that might address identified weaknesses. In the final segment, the discussion focused on the migration to this new approach, prompting participants to express concerns, perceived risks, and potential benefits. Importantly, the last two questions were designed to refine the interview process and effectively conclude the session. All interviews were conducted by the first author, ensuring consistency in the interviewing process. Each session lasted between 30 and 60 minutes. Participants reported appreciating the interviews and feeling included in the migration project. At the conclusion of each interview, we posed the question, "Was there a question you anticipated but was not asked?" This encouraged participants to reflect on all discussed topics and to contribute any additional insights. To ensure uniformity in data analysis, the questions remained unchanged across all interviews. However, the approach allowed participants to consider all aspects without altering the questions, thereby enhancing interview quality. Notes were taken during the interviews to document responses. In adherence to the guidance received, the interviewer was careful to construct questions in a manner that avoided bias or leading the interviewees. The questioning strategy was designed to ensure comprehensive inquiries without influencing responses. The initial interview served as a pilot with our main collaborator in the company to refine the questionnaire and ensure the process's fluidity. Notably, the data from this preliminary interview were excluded from the final analysis.

A preliminary interview was conducted with our primary collaborator at the partner company to refine the interview process and enhance its overall effectiveness. Consequently, the questionnaire was revised to improve its efficacy, thereby ensuring a more streamlined and coherent process for subsequent interviews. However, the data collected during this initial interview were excluded from the formal analysis to maintain the integrity and consistency of the dataset. This approach allowed us to pilot test the interview framework, identify potential improvements, and ensure that the process remained fluid and aligned with the intended objectives.
