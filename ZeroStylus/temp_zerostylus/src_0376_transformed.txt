Unlike traditional cross-entropy loss, it explicitly addresses performance disparities between larger and smaller cohorts, thereby improving the consistency of multi-task fine-tuning procedures. To evaluate the comparative effectiveness of different training methodologies, an empirical analysis was conducted to assess their relative performance and implications. Specifically, our investigation highlights a shift from task-specific to integrated fine-tuning approaches for enhancing mental health prediction models. In this context, we developed an on-device mental health monitoring application for iOS, utilizing llama.cpp<sup>1</sup> as the inference backend. This system leverages GGUF V3 models, which are optimized for efficient performance on mobile devices. The models were converted from PyTorch to GGUF format using the transformers-to-gguf pipeline, enabling streamlined execution on both CPUs and GPUs. The application was implemented in Swift/SwiftUI and integrates llama.cpp through a C++ bridge layer, allowing direct inference on iOS devices without reliance on external servers. At runtime, inference is accelerated by Apple Metal, which optimizes matrix computations and dynamically reallocates operations to the CPU when GPU resources are limited. Additionally, the system employs the capability to utilize up to eight threads, ensuring optimal performance across Apple’s processor cores. To minimize RAM usage, model weights are memory-mapped, enabling components to be loaded dynamically as needed. Furthermore, a 4,096-token context window, combined with efficient cache management and batched processing, ensures stable performance during lengthy input processing. In cases where social media posts exceed an input length of 6,000 characters, they are truncated to meet system constraints. To assess the system’s performance, we evaluated three models—Menta (2.33 GB), Phi-4 Mini (2.40 GB), and Qwen-3 (2.30 GB)—on an iPhone 15 Pro Max equipped with the A17 Pro chip and 8GB RAM. All models were accompanied by open-source code for reproducibility<sup>2</sup>. Notably, the framework supports alternative quantization families, such as Q2_K through Q8_0, allowing flexibility in balancing computational efficiency and predictive accuracy. Our fine-tuned Menta model was developed using four high-quality datasets curated to identify markers of depression, stress, and suicidal ideation from Reddit data.
