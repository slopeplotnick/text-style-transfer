In order to enhance interpretability and enable reasoning-based safety alignment, we incorporate feedback into each sample within our dataset through the utilization of high-capacity models. Specifically, we employ gpt-oss-120b for textual analysis, integrate Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct for processing visual and video data, and utilize Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct for auditory content interpretation. For each instance, the model receives the original content, its safety label, and any identified problem areas, subsequently generating a reasoning critique to elucidate the rationale behind its safety determination. The complete setup used for generating this feedback is detailed in Figure 5. Consequently, unlike simple classification-only models that provide merely binary judgments, OMNIGUARD requires detailed and clear assessments, producing a structured judgment that explicitly determines whether the input is safe or unsafe. Moreover, the Content Safety component seeks to delineate specific categories of violations by identifying particular safety concerns inherent in unsafe content. The reasoning critique framework is designed to elucidate the underlying rationale for model decisions, grounded in established rules and principles, thereby fostering transparency and interpretability in decision-making processes. Therefore, OMNIGUARD extends beyond the mere identification of patterns, facilitating a nuanced analysis of potentially harmful content across diverse formats. In this section, we present comprehensive test results, demonstrating the superiority of OMNIGUARD in our analysis. We evaluate performance across both isolated and combined scenarios on a suite of 15 assessments that encompass text, image, video, and audio modalities. Furthermore, in order to enhance the model's performance in safety evaluations and the management of complex safety scenarios across various formats, we implement tests to ascertain whether integrating reasoning improves these aspects. The objective is to assess whether safety knowledge acquired from established formats can be effectively applied to novel contexts, thereby demonstrating an understanding and management of safety across diverse methodologies.
