Model Paradigm & Backbone: We adopted a Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) paradigm based on the Qwen-1.5 model as our backbone architecture. This approach enabled the effective transfer of general language understanding capabilities to domain-specific e-commerce scenarios. Data & Prompt: Our methodology leveraged knowledge graphs combined with In-Context Learning, where domain knowledge was injected through carefully designed contextual prompts. Consequently, this strategy allowed the model to assimilate e-commerce-specific semantic patterns and entity relationships through advanced learning mechanisms. Evaluation: To comprehensively evaluate model performance, we employed standard benchmark protocols alongside general relevance assessment metrics, ensuring both accuracy and robustness across various query understanding tasks. Application: The trained model was deployed as a high-precision offline judge for label annotation tasks. This application successfully established an efficient annotation assistance system and bad case detection mechanism, significantly enhancing data quality and operational efficiency in downstream workflows. Building upon the foundational query understanding capabilities, this phase tackled more sophisticated challenges in complex query semantic recognition, including the interpretation of intricate queries, implicit semantics, and advanced logical reasoning required for path construction. Simple instruction-following proved insufficient for these multi-step reasoning and judgment tasks that demanded enhanced logical inference capabilities. To address these challenges, we introduced Chain-of-Thought (CoT) technology. Rather than directly requesting judgment outputs, we strengthened the model's ability to identify complex entities and semantics, decompose hierarchical relationships, and ultimately complete path comparisons through comprehensive reasoning chains. This approach significantly enhanced the accuracy of complex query discrimination. Moreover, we established a data synthesis engine based on difficult case mining, enabling iterative feedback optimization. Model Paradigm & Backbone: Subsequently, we synthesized CoT data for attribute extraction and matching tasks and retrained the model using the SFT paradigm with Qwen-2 as the backbone architecture. This methodology facilitated the model's ability to generate intermediate reasoning steps while maintaining task-specific optimization through supervised fine-tuning.
