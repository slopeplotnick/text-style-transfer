Although cov_tgsp and CSP consistently lead in performance (see Table 2 & Figure 3), notable differences emerge across datasets. A dataset-wise analysis reveals that, while CSP and cov_tgsp rank among the top performers in all cases, their effectiveness is noticeably lower on the PhysionetMI dataset compared to Cho2017 and Zhou2016. This discrepancy can likely be attributed to variations in experimental protocols, including sampling rates, the number of electrodes used, and the number of subjects included in each study. These findings underscore the strong dataset dependence of feature extraction methods in motor imagery EEG decoding. For example, on Zhou2016, the combination of CSP with StandardScaler and logistic regression achieved the highest classification accuracy (0.87), closely followed by cov_tgsp with RobustScaler and logistic regression (0.84). Conversely, on Cho2017, cov_tgsp outperformed CSP, achieving a top score of 0.74 compared to CSP’s 0.68. However, methods relying on instantaneous functional connectivity and non-linear features consistently demonstrated lower performance across all datasets. For instance, svd_entropy combined with RobustScaler and logistic regression yielded scores of 0.68 on Zhou2016, 0.59 on Cho2017, and 0.54 on PhysionetMI. Specifically, on PhysionetMI, overall performance was weaker, with the best-performing pipeline (cov_tgsp) achieving only 0.64, while CSP averaged 0.62 across frequency bands. These results suggest that, although non-linear and connectivity-based features effectively capture the complex dynamics of EEG signals, they may lack the discriminative power required for motor imagery classification in this context. Moreover, subject-level analyses further support the general superiority of CSP and cov_tgsp across datasets, as illustrated in Figures 5 and 6 for Cho2017 and Zhou2016. Nonetheless, this trend is less consistent on the PhysionetMI dataset (Figure 7), where these pipelines do not systematically outperform alternative methods. In certain cases, alternative approaches achieve the highest scores; for example, Hjorth parameters surpass CSP and cov_tgsp for participants #57 and #59, while the instantaneous connectivity approach combined with TGSP performs best for participants #17 and #73.
