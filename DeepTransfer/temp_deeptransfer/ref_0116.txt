Data sampling. Utilizing Taobao search logs, we first categorize the data into distinct industries, such as consumer electronics and apparel, and conduct stratified sampling according to the representation of each industry. This step ensures comprehensive coverage of attributes and features within the training data across all industries. Next, we segment the data into high, medium, and low frequency categories based on the frequency of user searches, sampling accordingly to encompass both prevalent high-frequency queries and infrequent, complex queries. Subsequently, the data is allocated to specialized annotators for labeling. Data cleaning. Errors in manual annotation primarily arise from two aspects: first, annotators may overlook certain details, particularly in lengthy queries, leading to omissions; second, the labeling guidelines may be insufficiently comprehensive to address all scenarios, especially given the subjective nature of user preferences. To address these limitations, we employ advanced language models for data filtering: (1) the attribute matching model executes stringent matching strategies, demonstrating superior precision in identifying intricate details that may elude human judgment in complex scenarios; (2) by integrating comprehensive rule-based frameworks into prompts, the model is guided to perform logical reasoning and judgment, effectively identifying and eliminating edge cases that fall outside the scope of predefined rules. The rationale for employing two models is to leverage the powerful long-context comprehension capability of the text model and the multimodal understanding capability. To enable the model to possess comprehensive and in-depth reasoning capabilities, we propose a progressive CoT synthesis pipeline, adopting a stagewise construction strategy that encompasses three stages: Knowledge Injection & Reasoning, Multimodal Comprehension, and Rule Awareness. Initially, Knowledge Injection & Reasoning involves deconstructing user inquiries into comparable attributes systematically aligned with relevant items. Subsequently, Multimodal Comprehension and Rule Awareness evaluate the extent to which items fulfill search requirements by examining item attributes and applying established criteria.
