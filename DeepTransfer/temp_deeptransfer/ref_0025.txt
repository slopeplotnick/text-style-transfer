The project aims to integrate new features and updates into the framework, focusing on both technical IT innovations and advancements in agronomy. Fast development is the primary objective, enabling the creation of applications at a quicker pace than previous iterations. The spirit of Software Engineering combines principles such as code reuse, innovation, and rapid development. Initially conceived as a reusable code base, the project facilitates the migration process. In our investigation, we identified seven commendable practices, with Factorization and Reuse emerging as the most prevalent, cited nine and eight times respectively, as illustrated in Figure 1. Understanding these benefits is crucial as they motivate the team. Customization, a fundamental characteristic of a Software Product Line (SPL), was frequently highlighted by IT specialists, UX designers, and agronomy professionals. An SPL is conceptualized as a platform capable of generating mass-customized software solutions by leveraging a repository of pre-existing software components. Rapidity, mentioned by IT professionals, product owners, and user experience specialists, signifies the accelerated development of new software applications and is the second most frequently cited concept. Moreover, automation is highlighted, referring to the capability to generate software applications autonomously. Information Technology (IT) specialists and Product Owners (POs) perceive a strong association between automation and rapidity. Furthermore, the concept of industrialization, as articulated by IT specialists and User Experience (UX) professionals, refers to the on-demand and cost-effective production of applications. Improving code quality, characterized by consistency, testability, and reliability, was exclusively noted by IT specialists. Additionally, advantages related to sales were articulated by both IT and PO personnel, signifying an increase in software sales or a rapid response to emerging opportunities.
