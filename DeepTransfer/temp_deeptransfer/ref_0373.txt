Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) have facilitated the integration of task-oriented and open-domain conversational systems. This segment of the tutorial will examine this technological evolution and present illustrative examples of potential future directions. Notable examples include the development of novel foundation LLMs pretrained on conversational datasets, end-to-end dialogue training methodologies, and the implementation of self-supervised dialogue simulations. Moreover, another significant prospective direction involves the development of dialogue systems that integrate content from various sources, such as web pages, lists, forms, tables, academic papers with figures and equations, and retrieved documents.
