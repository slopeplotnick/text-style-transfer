Objective Evaluation. ViSAudio demonstrates superior performance across multiple objective metrics on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution datasets, as shown in Table 2. It is benchmarked against several state-of-the-art video-to-spatial-audio generation methods, with detailed descriptions available in Section 5.1. For the BiAudio and MUSIC test sets, both video and caption inputs are utilized, whereas for FAIR-Play, which lacks captions, only video data is employed. Models without text-processing capabilities are evaluated using video inputs exclusively. The results in Table 2 indicate that ViSAudio outperforms other binaural-audio generation methods in overall quality, spatial channel distribution consistency, audio-visual synchronization, and semantic alignment. This underscores the model's ability to generate high-fidelity binaural audio while maintaining coherence between auditory and visual modalities, even under diverse acoustic conditions. Notably, its robust performance on the out-of-distribution FAIR-Play dataset, despite the absence of training on this specific dataset, illustrates its capacity to generalize to novel scenarios. Figure 3 presents a comparative analysis of spatial audio generation across various methods. One illustrative example depicts an individual playing the sitar, with the camera panning from left to right, resulting in the corresponding auditory shift from right to left. We analyze the spectrograms of the binaural audio generated by ViSAudio, the ground truth audio, and other baseline methods. The results demonstrate that ViSAudio achieves the closest alignment with the ground truth, accurately capturing the spatial dynamics of sound. For instance, when the sound source is positioned on the left, the left audio channel exhibits greater intensity, consistent with the auditory perception in real-world scenarios. In contrast, alternative methodologies fail to accurately reproduce rhythmic patterns and do not adequately demonstrate disparities between the left and right audio channels.
