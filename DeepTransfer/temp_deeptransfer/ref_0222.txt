Research in truthful AI aims to develop systems that accurately represent the real world. Previous studies have proposed various methods to enhance truthfulness, such as identifying learned activations aligned with factual accuracy [Burns et al., 2022], modifying model activations during inference [Li et al., 2023], and examining the trade-off between truthfulness and practical utility [Su et al., 2025]. Honest AI focuses on ensuring that AI systems do not intentionally produce statements that contradict their internal representations merely to maximize rewards. This concept is closely tied to the notion of AI deception. In contrast, truth-seeking AI emphasizes the process of evaluating evidence and discovering novel insights. This objective may become increasingly significant as AI systems gain greater autonomy and begin to decide what information to seek based on their contextual understanding [Chan et al., 2023]. While truth-seeking AI aims explicitly to uncover truth, there is no single metric to optimize for this goal. For instance, it may prioritize adherence to Bayesian principles or coherence in reasoning [Wen et al., 2025]. Belief entrenchment refers to the systematic tendency to favor pre-existing beliefs despite contradictory evidence. This phenomenon is closely related to confirmation bias, where individuals selectively seek, interpret, or recall information that aligns with their prior beliefs [Nickerson, 1998]. However, confirmation bias operates at the level of individual information processing and is challenging to measure. In psychology, its measurement is task-specific and often unreliable. Moreover, accurately assessing confirmation bias requires both a comprehensive understanding of an individual's prior beliefs and a reliable method to quantify them, which remains a significant challenge [Berthet, 2021].
