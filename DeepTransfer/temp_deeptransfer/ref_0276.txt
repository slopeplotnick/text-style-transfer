Case 3: Suicidal Risk Level Detection. This case illustrates a misclassification where a Reddit post, intended to provide support and encouragement, was incorrectly categorized as behavior by the Menta model (Figure 12). The misclassification stemmed primarily from the excessive input length, which included a lengthy multi-speaker thread with formatting noise, such as unusual symbols, HTML artifacts, and placeholder tokens like "Hyperactive behavior." These elements acted as misleading cues, causing the model to interpret the post as behavior-oriented rather than supportive. The post itself featured an individual urging another to persevere for the sake of their younger siblings and to continue taking steps to prevent harm caused by their father. Furthermore, the individual advised ignoring a disparaging comment made by a troll, emphasizing that they did not view the person as worthless or deserving of self-harm. The rationale for categorizing the behavior was linked to the individual’s earlier tendency to overcommit, which had led to challenges in academic and social contexts. However, by learning to set boundaries and decline excessive responsibilities, the individual experienced notable improvements in both their well-being and social relationships.
