Guardrail systems serve as external moderation layers to enforce safety constraints during interactions between humans and AI. Initially, these systems relied on basic rule-based filtering mechanisms, which proved effective in limited settings. However, as the landscape evolved and novel challenges emerged, these approaches struggled to adapt to changing safety policies and often suffered from limited coverage and accuracy. Recent advancements have incorporated sophisticated AI models, enhancing their adaptability and capacity for generalization. For instance, Llama Guard, developed by Meta AI, is an LLM-based model fine-tuned on specialized safety datasets to ensure secure user-AI interactions. Moreover, models like Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard are designed to address visual safety concerns, extending the scope of safeguarding measures. Despite these advancements, several challenges persist in the omni-modality era. Prior research has predominantly concentrated on text and image modalities, while video and audio modalities have been treated with overly simplistic methodologies that fail to capture contextual nuances. Furthermore, most existing systems are designed to operate within a single modality or specific scenarios, lacking the capability to integrate and reason across multiple modalities effectively. To address these challenges, OMNIGUARD was developed as the inaugural system capable of managing diverse media types while employing deliberate reasoning to ensure safety. By incorporating omni-modal understanding and explicit reasoning, OMNIGUARD delivers consistent, interpretable, and comprehensive safety assurance across all modalities.
