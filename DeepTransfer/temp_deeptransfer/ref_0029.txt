What considerations do individuals take into account when evaluating AI-generated content? Current study settings, such as those by Hong et al. (2022) and Moura and Maw (2021), often rely on rating scales adapted from related fields. However, these measures may not adequately capture the nuances of how individuals perceive and evaluate AI-generated music. Therefore, this study aims to elucidate genuine perceptions by incorporating open-ended inquiries to identify the subjective criteria and themes underlying participants' judgments. Stimuli were created using the AI tools Stable Audio (v.2.0) and Suno (v.3.5). To ensure ecologically valid stimuli, prompts were informed by the Emotion-to-Music Atlas (EMMA; Strauss et al., 2024), a comprehensive repository of emotional annotations for music excerpts. Specifically, we utilized data from the Music4All-Onion database (Moscati et al., 2022), which categorizes music into five principal genres, each with three subgenres: Electronic (Trance, Downtempo, Dubstep), Jazz (Bossa-Nova, Smooth-Jazz, Swing), Pop (Europop, Indie-Pop, Pop-Punk), Rock (Soft-Rock, RockBallad, Doom-Metal), and Soul/Funk (Funk, Disco, Soul). For each subgenre, prompts were constructed to reflect the predominant emotional characteristics identified through the mean values in the EMMA and their corresponding GEMS-9 descriptors. This process yielded 2–3 prompts per subgenre, resulting in a total of 38 distinct prompts. These prompts were subsequently adapted for use with the AI tools Stable Audio and Suno, generating a total of 76 audio samples. To accommodate the properties of each tool, the following approach was used: For Suno, prompts were generated in instrumental mode, with one of two outputs randomly selected and edited to a duration of 45–60 seconds to ensure consistency with prior research methodologies. For Stable Audio, the term "instrumental" was appended to each prompt, and the outputs were adjusted to approximately 60 seconds in length. Further details on the creation and editing of these audio samples can be found in the supplementary materials.
