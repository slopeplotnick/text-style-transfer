Proper licensing of datasets is fundamental for ensuring both broad usability and ethical access, particularly for low-resource languages. Open licenses, such as Creative Commons (e.g., CC BY 4.0), facilitate large-scale data reuse. Initiatives like Mozilla Common Voice exemplify the utility of open licenses in advancing language technology development. However, access to resources for AI development remains a significant challenge for many researchers in low- and middle-income countries, highlighting ongoing concerns regarding equitable data governance and the empowerment of local communities. In the context of the Swivuriso project, the adoption of the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license was intended to ensure both broad accessibility and ethical accountability. Swivuriso is hosted on platforms such as Zenodo and Hugging Face, accompanied by permanent DOIs and comprehensive documentation. Each release includes audio files, corresponding transcripts, and detailed metadata on demographics, language, and accessibility. Moreover, an ethical use statement is provided, which restricts activities such as voice cloning to safeguard speaker privacy. The CC BY 4.0 license is highly permissive, allowing users to share, adapt, and utilize the dataset for any purpose, including commercial applications, provided that they attribute the creators and indicate any modifications made. Additionally, the license prohibits the imposition of additional legal or technical restrictions, encompasses database rights, and incorporates a "cure period" to rectify any licensing issues, thereby ensuring legal clarity and facilitating global scientific collaboration. Nevertheless, while open licenses are instrumental in enhancing data utility, they frequently fail to address critical considerations such as local agency and the safeguarding of Indigenous knowledge. The indiscriminate application of uniform licenses can inadvertently undermine community ownership, particularly concerning cultural materials or community-derived data. This issue is fostering novel paradigms that emphasize data stewardship, equitable benefit-sharing, and community empowerment, which are increasingly gaining traction in the realm of African data governance.
