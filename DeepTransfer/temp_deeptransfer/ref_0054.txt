Udio can transform natural-language prompts into polished music tracks across various genres, including lyrics in multiple languages. In terms of the perception of AI-generated music, research in computational creativity and machine learning has explored the creative value of these models through subjective listening evaluations. Listeners assess the outputs of these systems in comparison to those of alternative models or compositions created by human musicians. Frequent evaluation metrics include overall quality, creativity, novelty, and music-specific elements such as melody, harmony, and rhythm. A crucial aspect of this assessment is the evaluation of naturalness or humanness, which is conducted either through rating scales or by employing a musical Turing Test, where listeners determine whether a composition was produced by a human or an AI system, under conditions where they may be informed, uninformed, or intentionally misled about the source. In specific tasks, such as generating Bach chorales, even early models like DeepBach produced outputs that were often indistinguishable from human-composed works. To evaluate the emotional dimensions of AI-generated music, listeners are typically tasked with identifying the intended emotions, either by categorizing them into discrete labels such as "happy" or "sad" or by employing dimensional models of affective states. As AI-generated music continues to advance, research has increasingly focused on whether listeners' evaluations are influenced by their perceptions or beliefs regarding its origin. However, the evidence remains inconclusive. Early investigations that manipulated participants' knowledge of the composer’s identity reported no significant bias in evaluations of live recordings of string-quartet performances created by human and computational composers. Similarly, Zlatkov et al. (2023) observed no systematic bias against AI-generated music when using contemporary pop music samples as stimuli. Furthermore, consumer marketing research conducted by Moura and Maw (2021) revealed no consistent preference for classical or pop music based on the perceived authorship of the compositions.
