To ensure the viability of simulated data for computational research, specific subgroups within the data must be directly comparable. Therefore, in each study, we partitioned the simulated datasets into "Male" and "Female" cohorts and conducted invariance tests between them. If successful, this would confirm the feasibility of comparing specific demographics within the data. In this section, we outline the common methodology and workflow employed across all four studies, except where deviations are explicitly noted in their respective sections. Figure 1 illustrates the experimental configurations utilized in each of the four investigations. The simulated datasets were generated using GPT4o-mini, a variant of GPT-4 refined up to October 2023, with default settings including a Temperature of 1.0 and a Top P of 1.0, and no penalties applied for frequency or presence. The model received a procedurally generated prompt detailing an individual with specified demographic attributes such as age, gender, and ethnicity, and was tasked with responding to a psychometric questionnaire intended for evaluation. The prompt included the questionnaire items, response options, and instructions to format the answers as a single string of values separated by commas, thereby facilitating streamlined processing. Each prompt was administered independently, without any influence from preceding prompts, ensuring that responses remained unaffected by prior interactions. For illustrative purposes, a typical prompt was structured as follows: "Impersonate an individual of [Ethnicity], [Gender], aged [Age] years, residing in the United Kingdom. Respond to the [Number of items]-item questionnaire." Each item within the questionnaire is evaluated using a Likert scale ranging from [Likert scale range], and respondents are instructed to utilize the following key for their responses: [Response Key]. The questionnaire items are as follows: [Questionnaire Items]. Please provide your responses in a single, comma-separated string of values.
