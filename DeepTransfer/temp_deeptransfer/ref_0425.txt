In this section, we discuss the limitations of TaleFrame regarding interaction modes and advanced narrative structures, as well as potential directions for future research. - Interaction and visualization. TaleFrame is currently optimized for desktop and laptop environments, utilizing a combination of mouse and keyboard inputs for precise control, which is essential for connecting entities, identifying relationships, and linking events to recognize event sequences. While user studies highlight a preference for drag-and-drop interactions, this mode becomes less effective on mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, due to their smaller screen sizes. Although touch pens and fingers can serve as substitutes for the mouse, the extended interaction processes and the input of relationship information often lead to accidental touches, thereby diminishing the user experience on these devices. To mitigate cognitive load, we have incorporated information visualization techniques, such as color coding, icons, and connecting lines, along with adaptive layout resizing to support various screen dimensions. Moving forward, we aim to explore a wider range of interaction modalities, including touchscreens, keyboard input, and voice commands, to establish a more versatile framework that accommodates diverse user needs across different devices. - Challenges in handling advanced narrative structures in story generation. TaleFrame currently struggles to manage complex event relationships that extend beyond linear temporal sequencing, particularly causal relationships, flashbacks, and other non-linear narrative techniques. Furthermore, the intricate relationships between entities often complicate the connections between events, which can negatively affect the coherence and accuracy of the generated narratives. Despite efforts to enhance the modeling of event dependencies, the system continues to face challenges in handling multi-layered event interweaving, temporal reversals, and interactions involving multiple entities. These difficulties are especially pronounced when narratives include numerous entities, as accurately determining event sequences and causal chains becomes increasingly complex, often resulting in incoherent or logically unclear storylines. As the complexity of the narrative structure grows, the model encounters greater obstacles in understanding and generating these intricate connections. In future research, we will focus on improving the modeling of entity relationships and optimizing strategies for generating non-linear narrative structures. These advancements aim to enhance the system's ability to produce complex, cohesive, and engaging narratives, ultimately providing users with a richer and more logically consistent storytelling experience.
