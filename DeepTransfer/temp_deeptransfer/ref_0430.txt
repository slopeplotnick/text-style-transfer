We utilize three multimodal emotion datasets to train our model and validate its effectiveness. EMER provides high-quality human-verified reasoning-label pairs and serves as the basis for initiating the training process. Meanwhile, DFEW and MAFW are large-scale benchmarks encompassing diverse facial expressions across video, audio, and text modalities. Further details regarding the composition and statistics of these datasets are provided in Appendix 7.1. Consistent with prior studies, we report Unweighted Average Recall (UAR) and Weighted Average Recall (WAR) to evaluate recognition accuracy. Additionally, we employ three complementary metrics—Emotion Explanation Accuracy (EEA), Explanation Prediction Consistency (EPC), and Feature Contribution Relevance (FCR)—to assess the alignment between the generated explanation, the target emotion, and the model's prediction. Precise definitions and annotation protocols for these metrics are outlined in Appendix 7.2. Following the architecture of R1-Omni, our framework integrates SigLIP as the vision encoder, Whisper-large-v3 as the audio encoder, and BERT as the text encoder. Outputs from each modality are projected into the LLM embedding space via modality-specific projectors, implemented as two linear layers to ensure dimensional compatibility with the LLM representation. For instance, a 1-second audio segment generates approximately 50 audio features, while a single video frame produces 729 visual features. The backbone of our model is the Qwen2.5-7B LLM, initialized with pretrained weights from the HumanOmni checkpoint to retain multimodal representations. To evaluate the efficacy of our approach, we conduct comparative analyses of MIGR against existing emotion understanding methods on the MAFW and DFEW datasets. Competing approaches are categorized into two groups: (i) Non-Reasoning Models that directly predict emotion labels, and (ii) Reasoning-Generating Models that produce textual explanations prior to prediction. Initially, we compare MIGR with other reasoning-generating models to validate that the reasoning texts generated by our framework exhibit emotional coherence.
