DeepSeek-V3.2 achieves comparable performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across various reasoning benchmarks. Moreover, it significantly enhances the agentic capabilities of open models, demonstrating exceptional proficiency on long-tail agent tasks introduced in EvalSys (2025) and other studies (Li et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2025). As a highly cost-effective alternative for agent scenarios, DeepSeek-V3.2 substantially narrows the performance gap between open and leading proprietary models while maintaining significantly lower costs. To further advance the reasoning capabilities of open models, we extended the permissible input length to develop DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale. Consequently, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale achieves performance parity with the leading closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro (DeepMind, 2025b), and demonstrates gold-medal performance in prestigious competitions such as the IOI 2025, ICPC World Final 2025, IMO 2025, and CMO 2025. DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale retains the same configuration as DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp. The sole architectural modification from its predecessor, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, is the introduction of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) through continued training. The DSA framework consists of two principal components: a lightning indexer and a fine-grained token selection mechanism. Starting from the base checkpoint of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which features an extended context length of 128K, we conducted continued pre-training followed by post-training to develop DeepSeek-V3.2. This continued pre-training was carried out in two distinct stages, with the training data distribution fully aligned with the 128K long-context extension data used for DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp was evaluated on a comprehensive suite of benchmarks, revealing comparable performance to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. Notably, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp demonstrates enhanced computational efficiency in processing long sequences while maintaining performance parity with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus across both short- and long-context tasks.
