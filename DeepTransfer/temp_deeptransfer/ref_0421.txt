The primary objective is to maximize code reuse and further accelerate the delivery of new applications. To achieve this, the goal is to migrate the existing code base into a higher-level Software Product Line (SPL), specifically at Level 4 or 5 as defined in [vdLSR07]. To ensure a smooth transition, we decided to first conduct interviews with the company's collaborators to evaluate their perceptions of SPL engineering. These interviews were carefully designed to address the research questions outlined in the following section. Moreover, another round of interviews is planned for the end of the project to refine the migration strategy and ensure its success. The subsequent phases of the migration will focus on variability analysis, beginning with the establishment of a process for feature localization to synthesize feature models. Following this, the study of interactions and dependencies among the features will provide a comprehensive understanding of the system. The interviews were conducted to assess perceptions regarding current practices, benefits, risks, and concerns prior to the migration to an SPL. Research Question 1 (RQ1) investigates the perceptions of the company's stakeholders regarding current development best practices. Through this question, we aim to highlight their views on the efficacy of these practices. Research Question 2 (RQ2) explores the stakeholders' perceptions of the anticipated benefits of the future SPL, with the intention of discussing the added value it is expected to bring to application development. Research Question 3 (RQ3) examines the perceived risks associated with adopting the new SPL, focusing on how these risks might affect the stakeholders' daily work. Finally, Research Question 4 (RQ4) addresses the concerns and apprehensions stemming from the previously identified risks. This inquiry aims to assess the fears experienced by the company's stakeholders regarding the migration project, including uncertainties about outcomes, anticipated challenges during the transition, and the necessity for careful planning and effective risk mitigation strategies.
