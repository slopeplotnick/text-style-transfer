The rapid development of artificial intelligence (AI) has driven significant advancements in natural language processing (NLP), with story generation emerging as a prominent research area. Story generation aims to create coherent, engaging, and contextually appropriate narratives based on input [1]. However, existing systems continue to face several challenges, particularly in maintaining narrative consistency, developing entities effectively, and exerting control over story progression [2]. Specifically, translating user intent into desired story content while ensuring control over plot progression, entity actions, and structural integrity remains a critical issue [3]. Therefore, fine-grained control is essential to avoid logical inconsistencies and ensure alignment with user expectations, preserving both plausibility and creativity [4]. Models such as OpenAI's GPT-4 and Meta's Llama-3 [5] have demonstrated powerful capabilities in text generation tasks, producing high-quality textual content. Nevertheless, because LLMs are primarily pre-trained on large-scale, general-purpose datasets, they face limitations in generating sufficiently controlled and structured story content. This issue becomes particularly pronounced in tasks requiring specific narrative structures, where LLMs often struggle to meet users' dual demands for detailed plots and well-defined story frameworks [6]. Consequently, improving the control capabilities of LLMs to ensure that generated stories align with user expectations while maintaining creativity has become an urgent challenge. The combination of fine-tuning techniques and structured input has emerged as a promising solution. By fine-tuning LLMs and incorporating structured input prompts, models can be adjusted to produce more structured and user-aligned content in the story generation domain [7]–[9]. Moreover, this approach not only enhances the quality of generated narratives but also improves the stability and reliability of models in addressing complex story generation tasks. Thus, the integration of fine-tuning methodologies with structured input provides a clear pathway to improving the predictability and consistency of generated content, effectively addressing control challenges inherent in the generation process.
