Our investigation revealed that individuals' perceptions of AI significantly influence their interpretation of music, extending beyond mere auditory aesthetics. Therefore, it is imperative to develop instruments specifically designed for AI-generated music and establish novel multi-factor rating scales. As AI-generated music becomes increasingly prevalent on streaming platforms, socioeconomic concerns related to fairness and transparency must be addressed. Given that attitudes toward AI significantly influence both appreciation and emotional response to AI-generated music, it is crucial to investigate the communication strategies employed regarding its creation and dissemination. For instance, examining transparency concerning the datasets used in training AI models or the distribution of financial benefits derived from AI-generated music could provide valuable insights. Furthermore, exploring the diverse methodologies of AI music creation and its various applications is essential. The collaborative creation of songs by human and AI systems has garnered significant interest, facilitated by advancements in technology that enable co-creative processes. Additionally, expanding upon the research conducted by Moura and Maw (2021), the perception and practical application of AI-generated music warrant further exploration. Our findings indicate that attitudes towards AI are pivotal in shaping the evaluation of AI-generated music, surpassing factors such as composer identity, genre, and personal listening habits. Looking ahead, future research must closely examine the evolution of public attitudes toward artificial intelligence, as these perspectives will significantly influence the acceptance or rejection of AI in music and other cultural domains. The central issue extends beyond whether AI-generated music can be appreciated or perceived as human-like; it concerns broader considerations of what constitutes "authentic" and "valuable" creativity. By critically analyzing audience perceptions, we can determine which forms of creativity—artificial or otherwise—should be acknowledged and under what conditions.
