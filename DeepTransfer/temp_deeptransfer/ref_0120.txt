TokenPowerBench's core contribution is a comprehensive framework for evaluating the power and energy consumption of LLM inference across diverse hardware platforms, model architectures, and task configurations. We briefly summarize the core aspects of our benchmark design here. To enable meaningful comparisons, TokenPowerBench introduces energy-centric metrics, including Joules per token, Joules per response, and instantaneous power draw. These metrics are aligned with the operational phases of transformer-based LLMs, specifically the prefill and decode stages, thereby facilitating precise attribution of energy costs. Unlike traditional performance metrics such as latency or throughput, these energy metrics emphasize sustainability and cost-efficiency, which are critical considerations in modern AI infrastructure. Moreover, the reproducibility of experimental procedures is ensured through a highly granular power measurement methodology, sensitive to hardware configurations and instrumentation fidelity. TokenPowerBench employs a three-tier measurement model, ranging from GPU-specific data acquisition to comprehensive system monitoring using sophisticated instrumentation, allowing its adoption in environments from user-space workstations to institutional testbeds. Furthermore, the tool accommodates a variety of LLM tasks, systematically examining key configuration parameters like batch size, context length, and system architecture to assess their impact on energy consumption. It supports scaling from single-GPU setups to extensive multi-node systems, providing insights into both component-level details and overall energy usage. As the landscape of LLM technology rapidly evolves, TokenPowerBench is designed to adapt accordingly. We will extend coverage beyond our current H100 testbed to include emerging GPU architectures, such as future NVIDIA generations, AMD accelerators, and novel AI chips and DPUs. Additionally, we will enhance functionality to evaluate the trade-offs between energy efficiency and inference accuracy, equipping users with actionable insights to determine the optimal balance between energy consumption and model performance.
