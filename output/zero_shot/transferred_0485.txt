In the context of online shopping, consumers frequently seek products characterized by multiple attributes simultaneously, such as brand, price range, functionality, or target audience. Consequently, it is imperative for the system to integrate these attributes holistically rather than individually, ensuring systematic organization. This integration aids the system in determining the appropriate products to present to users and aligning their inquiries with suitable items. It is essential not to base the reliance on different data modalities (such as text or images) solely on the user's query. Instead, the system should parse the attributes discerned within the query and align them with product attributes, thereby optimizing the information utilized in selecting items to display to the user. Fundamentally, the system operates by constructing a trajectory from the user’s query to the corresponding products. Initially, it deconstructs the query into specific attributes, utilizing these attributes to filter and select products. This process is bifurcated into two phases: first, ensuring the system comprehensively understands the query and correlates it to the relevant product attributes, and second, accurately selecting products based on these attributes. The complexity and ambiguity inherent in these steps present significant challenges. For instance, if a user searches for a "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set," the system must accurately interpret the user's intent and correlate it with the appropriate product features. The preliminary step involves connecting the attributes in the query with those of the products. The challenge lies in ensuring precise attribute matching by the system.
