This paper discusses the Radiologist Copilot, a sophisticated tool designed to assist radiologists in generating automated reports with integrated quality control mechanisms. The system employs an intelligent reasoning framework to orchestrate various tools critical for the reporting process, emulating the decision-making and task execution of a radiologist. The toolset encompasses functionalities such as region identification, analysis planning, template selection, quality assurance, and iterative enhancement based on feedback, all of which contribute to the production of comprehensive radiological reports. Empirical evaluations indicate that the Radiologist Copilot surpasses other leading methodologies in radiology report generation. The significance of automated reporting with quality assurance is underscored by the substantial time requirements associated with processing 3D medical images.   CT2Rep leverages a novel transformer architecture and memory framework to facilitate the generation of reports for 3D chest CT images, while Reg2RG employs a region-focused approach for crafting chest CT reports. However, report generation constitutes only one facet of the overall process and often lacks crucial quality control checks. Medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have been proposed to enhance tasks such as report generation and question answering, with 3D medical VLMs demonstrating efficacy in handling 3D medical images like CT and MRI scans. RadFM introduces a model adept at processing both 2D and 3D medical data. M3D provides an extensive dataset and a model for the analysis of 3D medical images. Merlin offers a 3D model that integrates both structured and unstructured data for abdominal CT interpretation. CT-CHAT is a 3D chest CT model that combines a vision encoder with a pre-trained language model. Med3DVLM enhances multimodal data representation through an efficient encoder and learning strategy. Hulu-Med delivers a model that integrates text, 2D and 3D images, and video into a cohesive system.
