TokenPowerBench is an analytical tool designed to evaluate energy consumption and power usage associated with the execution of large language models (LLMs) across a variety of hardware configurations, models, and task modalities. The primary aim of this tool is to facilitate straightforward, adaptable, and reproducible measurements of energy metrics. It employs energy-based metrics such as joules expended per token, per response, and real-time power usage, systematically correlating these metrics with distinct operational phases of LLMs, such as the prefill and decode stages, to pinpoint specific energy expenditure areas. Unlike traditional performance metrics that emphasize speed or throughput, these energy metrics prioritize sustainability and cost-efficiency, which are pivotal concerns in contemporary AI system development and deployment.  The measurement precision of TokenPowerBench is contingent upon the hardware and instrumentation employed, offering a hierarchical measurement approach. This ranges from basic GPU data analysis to comprehensive system-wide monitoring utilizing sophisticated instrumentation, thereby accommodating both small-scale and large-scale experimental environments. The tool is architected to ensure replicability of experiments, which is vital for robust scientific inquiry.  TokenPowerBench is adept at accommodating diverse LLM task requirements, which may vary in accordance with system scale, model architecture, and application context. It assesses factors such as batch size, context length, and system configuration to understand their impact on energy consumption. This versatility allows it to function effectively within configurations spanning from single GPUs to extensive multi-node systems, capturing both granular and macroscopic energy usage patterns.  Given the rapid evolution of LLM technologies, TokenPowerBench is engineered to be resilient and adaptive, with provisions for compatibility expansions to include emerging GPU architectures, novel processing chips, and other hardware innovations. Furthermore, it intends to incorporate additional features to assess the implications of energy-saving strategies on the quality of the model's output. Such enhancements will enable end-users to make informed decisions regarding the optimization of energy consumption vis-à-vis model performance quality.
