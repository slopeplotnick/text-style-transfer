Consequently, CFAULTS is deemed optimal for C software diagnostics, whereas HSD remains superior for Boolean circuit evaluations. The divergence in performance is attributed to the distinct nature of fault identification in C programs versus Boolean circuits, which necessitates different formula structures. This distinction influences the efficacy of the solver, thereby accounting for the variance in performance across these two domains.
