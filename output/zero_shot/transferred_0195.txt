LLaMA-Factory primarily focuses on generating text rather than converting text generation problems into sorting tasks. Our configuration, termed PEFT-FACTORY, effectively addresses the limited number of PEFT (Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning) methods and facilitates the customization of large language models for sorting tasks. Several alternative configurations exist for training large language models, each with distinctive advantages despite not being as user-friendly as LLaMA-Factory. FastChat is particularly adept at training large models tailored for conversational applications. LitGPT and LMFlow are versatile and user-friendly frameworks compatible with a variety of generative models and training techniques. Axolotl, a terminal-based tool, enables efficient post-training of large models without compromising on functionality or scalability. Open-Instruct focuses on instruction modification for large models, offering diverse models and methodologies for this purpose. H2O LLM Studio provides a business-oriented platform with a graphical interface for the creation and deployment of large models. GPT4All enhances LLaMA with a user-friendly interface to facilitate efficient training. ColossalAI specializes in fine-tuning across multiple nodes, optimizing for distributed computing. LLaMA-Adapter and LLaMA-Accessory are streamlined configurations, with the former incorporating trainable adapters into LLaMA models and the latter providing a comprehensive toolkit for large model development. LLaMA-Adapter is frequently integrated into frameworks such as LitGPT. Upon examining related configurations, we identified three primary features that are either absent or limited in existing systems, yet novel in our approach: the training of large models using methods beyond reprametrization-based PEFT techniques, the modular and dynamic incorporation of new PEFT methods, and the support for training and evaluating large models specifically for sorting tasks.
