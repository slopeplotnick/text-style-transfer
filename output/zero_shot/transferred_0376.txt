This study introduces a novel method aimed at enhancing fairness across various tasks and demographic groups by directly optimizing balanced accuracy. Unlike the conventional cross-entropy (CE) loss function, this approach prioritizes addressing performance disparities between majority and minority groups, thereby facilitating more consistent outcomes in multi-task fine-tuning scenarios. A comparative analysis was conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of distinct training methodologies, specifically examining the impact of single-task versus multi-task fine-tuning on predictive modeling in mental health contexts.  As part of the study, a mental health screening application was developed for the iOS platform, utilizing the llama.cpp backend integrated with GGUF V3 models optimized for mobile devices. Model conversion from PyTorch to the GGUF format was achieved through a specialized conversion pipeline, ensuring efficient execution on both CPUs and GPUs. The application was built using Swift and SwiftUI, with the llama.cpp backend interfaced via a C++ layer, enabling direct operation on iOS devices without reliance on external servers. To enhance computational efficiency, the application leverages Apple Metal for accelerated matrix operations, with dynamic switching to CPU resources when GPU capacities are exceeded. The implementation supports up to eight concurrent threads, exploiting the multi-core architecture of Apple processors. Memory usage is optimized through on-demand model weight loading and minimal RAM consumption. Stability in processing long inputs is ensured by employing a 4,096-token window, sophisticated caching strategies, and batch processing techniques. For lengthy social media content, inputs are truncated to a maximum of 6,000 characters.   Performance evaluations of three models—specifically, the Menta (2.33 GB), Phi-4 Mini (2.40 GB), and Qwen-3 (2.30 GB)—were conducted using an iPhone 15 Pro Max. These models were implemented within an open-source framework, which supports additional quantization strategies to balance computational speed with predictive accuracy. For the Menta model, data collection focused on high-quality datasets curated to detect signs of depression, stress, and suicidal ideation within Reddit discussions. Samples associated with specific disorders were excluded, while domain experts provided annotations for the remaining data. The study leveraged these annotated datasets for model training and evaluation purposes.
