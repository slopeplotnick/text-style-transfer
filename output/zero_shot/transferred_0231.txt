In this study, we employ an evaluative approach that assesses outlines based on foundational principles rather than through comparison with human-generated examples. Given that outlines are inherently concise and primarily demonstrate structural components, juxtaposing them with comprehensive surveys proves ineffectual. Our focus, therefore, is on evaluating organizational coherence, logical progression, and clarity. This evaluative framework aligns more closely with the academic conventions that define a high-quality outline. To facilitate our analysis, we generate a survey derived from the original survey title using each system. All systems possess the capability to access information online, ensuring equitable opportunities for information retrieval.   We examine three categories of systems: those designed for general long-text composition, those specifically tailored for academic paper writing, and those dedicated to survey creation. Uniform input is provided across all systems, and they are assessed utilizing identical criteria. The resulting scores and patterns across these systems are presented in the subsequent table. Systems oriented towards general long-text composition demonstrate proficiency in language utilization and topic relevance; however, their surveys often lack depth, originality, synthesis, and analytical rigor. Systems aimed at paper writing exhibit improved structural capabilities, yet their outputs frequently exhibit rigidity and adherence to templates, thereby limiting depth and diversity of perspectives. Notably, systems specializing in survey creation achieve the highest overall scores, excelling in organization, synthesis of ideas, and differentiation between factual information and subjective viewpoints. Their proficiency is particularly evident in scientific and technological domains, where accuracy and effective organization of information are paramount.  These findings underscore that crafting exemplary surveys necessitates more than mere reliance on general language models; it requires specialized methodologies that emphasize the effective utilization of literature and robust organization of ideas.
