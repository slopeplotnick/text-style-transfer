Node 6 is responsible for managing the retrying process of tasks with feedback, permitting up to three attempts per task. This node utilizes enhanced prompts that integrate the original information with specific feedback to augment reasoning during retries. The system employs sophisticated techniques to modify strategies according to the project phase, transforming weights into reasoning instructions. Prompt variations are dependent on the project's stage, emphasizing different priorities. In the initial stages, the focus is on maximizing success rates with directives such as "select robots with the highest success rates" and "imbalanced workloads are acceptable." In later stages, the emphasis shifts toward balancing workloads, as indicated by instructions like "prioritize workload fairness." This configuration aids the system in naturally adhering to the strategies pertinent to each phase.  The framework operates effectively through three principal methods: (1) modeling the capabilities of robots to forecast their likelihood of task success, (2) generating prompts that incorporate numerical data and workload specifics, and (3) structuring responses to facilitate ease of verification and validation. Robots are categorized into three types—Light, Medium, and Heavy—each possessing distinct strengths and probabilities of task success. These probabilities are determined by the alignment between the robot's capabilities and the task requirements. The success rate for each robot is calculated by evaluating its performance across various task features. A robot's proficiency in executing a task is contingent upon the congruence between the task requirements and the robot's capabilities.  Tasks are sequentially processed from a list comprised of 36 tasks within the TEACH dataset. Each task undergoes a comprehensive process that includes phase determination, prompt generation, reasoning, verification, and assignment before proceeding to the subsequent task. Upon completion of each task, the system updates progress and decreases the number of remaining tasks until all assignments are fulfilled.
