The proposed framework facilitates the efficient reuse of code and the expedited experimentation with novel Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methodologies. Researchers are empowered to independently develop and evaluate new methods, which are seamlessly integrated into the system’s operations through dynamic loading mechanisms. This feature is particularly advantageous for comparative analyses of various PEFT approaches, as it enables the examination of these methodologies under consistent conditions, eliminating the need for redundant coding or the complication of version discrepancies.  The SuperGLUE benchmark comprises seven datasets categorized into four distinct tasks: (1) Natural Language Inference (NLI), which encompasses the CB dataset; (2) Question Answering, which incorporates the MultiRC, ReCoRD, BoolQ, and COPA datasets; (3) Word Sense Disambiguation, featuring the WiC dataset; and (4) Coreference Resolution, which includes the WSC dataset. Furthermore, additional datasets pertinent to text generation, frequently utilized in the evaluation of generative large language models (LLMs), have been incorporated. These encompass six datasets for language reasoning and comprehension, distributed across three tasks: (1) Question Answering, including MMLU, PIQA, SIQA, and OBQA; (2) Natural Language Inference (NLI), comprising the Hellaswag dataset; and (3) Commonsense Reasoning, which includes the WinoGrande dataset.  Moreover, three datasets have been introduced for the resolution of mathematical problems, segmented into three tasks: (1) Question Answering, featuring the MathQA dataset; (2) Mathematics Word Problems, including the GSM8K dataset; and (3) Simple Mathematical Problems, as exemplified by the SVAMP dataset. Lastly, three datasets are designated for code generation, namely Conala, CodeAlpacaPy, and APPS. It is noteworthy that certain datasets may require minor formatting adjustments to comply with the input configuration of the PEFT-FACTORY framework. Further elaboration on this aspect is provided in Appendix B.2.
