The performance of OMNIGUARD is being evaluated through a series of public safety benchmarks that encompass both unimodal (single modality) and multimodal data types. For unimodal evaluation, the system is tested on text, images, videos, and audio data. In the text modality, benchmarks such as BeaverTails, ToxicChat, WildGuardMix, Aegis2.0, and OpenAI Moderation are utilized. For image safety, evaluations are conducted using UnsafeBench, VLGuard, and LlavaGuard, while video benchmarks include SafeSora and SafeWatch-Bench. Audio data are assessed using MuTox (English split) and WildGuardMix-TTS, which converts WildGuardMix test samples into spoken format.   In the case of multimodal testing, OMNIGUARD's performance is examined on image-text, video-text, and audio-text combinations via MM-SafetyBench, VideoSafetyBench, and AIAH datasets. The system's efficacy is primarily measured through Accuracy and F1 score metrics. For benchmarks solely comprising unsafe samples, only the accuracy metric is reported. Additionally, OMNIGUARD's performance is systematically compared to other models of varying scales, including both general-purpose and specialized models, such as those based on large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs).
