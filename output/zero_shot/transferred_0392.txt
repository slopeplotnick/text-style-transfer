The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into mobile devices presents a promising approach for monitoring mental health, offering rapid, personalized feedback while safeguarding user privacy. This technology operates directly on the smartphone, thereby eliminating the necessity of transmitting data to cloud-based systems, enhancing data security and reducing dependence on internet connectivity. The accessibility of AI-driven mental health tools on mobile devices is particularly beneficial for individuals residing in remote areas or those facing prolonged waiting periods for clinical appointments. By functioning directly on the device, AI can promptly analyze variations in speech or behavior indicative of stress or mood disturbances.  The utilization of mobile devices for mental health monitoring is gaining traction due to its efficiency, energy conservation, and superior data privacy compared to cloud-dependent systems. Recent investigations have demonstrated that small language models (SLMs) are capable of operating effectively on devices such as the Samsung Galaxy S24. These models assist in document management and health prediction while consuming minimal memory and operating at increased speeds. Their deployment is favorable for real-world applications, given their ease of implementation and reduced resource requirements, obviating the need for continuous cloud connectivity and thereby enhancing privacy protection.  While some studies indicate that SLMs are adept at specific mental health-related functions, significant research gaps remain, particularly regarding their efficacy with complex datasets. Additionally, limited exploration exists concerning the performance of these models on resource-constrained devices. In response to these challenges, we propose "Menta," an innovative framework employing the LoRA method to address a variety of tasks and adapt to heterogeneous data. Menta is engineered to efficiently manage diverse mental health tasks while ensuring privacy preservation on commonly used devices.
