In the context of file modification, "UnresolvedRetry" refers to repeated, unsuccessful attempts to alter a file. As illustrated in Figure 12-a, an example is provided where a software engineering agent, identified as Dev, employs the function `str_replace` to modify `utils.py` 183 times without success, ultimately reaching a cost limitation. "StrNotFound" occurs when a modification attempt fails due to the absence of the target text in the file, as exemplified in Figure 13-a. This issue often arises from formatting discrepancies or minor textual variations. "NoEffectEdit" is characterized by a scenario in which the original and replacement texts are identical, resulting in no actual change, as depicted in Figure 13-b. In this instance, a string is replaced with itself, leading to resource wastage. "AmbiguousTarget" arises when the target text appears multiple times within the file, creating uncertainty regarding which instance to modify. This is illustrated in Figure 13-c, which shows multiple identical `if` statements, complicating the editor's decision on which instance to modify and highlighting the limitations of string-based editing.
