The attributes, such as the gray color, the target age demographic (18-24), and the design elements (suit set, formal dress), resonate with the preferences typical of this consumer group, which usually inclines towards simplicity and suitability. In the fourth scenario, the congruence between the search query and product is apparent; however, an operational guideline dictates that if a search for new products results in second-hand items, this should be categorized as "weakly relevant." The model has effectively internalized and adheres to such business rules. To address processing latency, a hybrid approach combining offline and online methods was employed.
