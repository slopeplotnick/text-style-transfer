The study of truthful Artificial Intelligence (AI) pertains to the development of AI systems capable of providing accurate representations of reality. Prior research has proposed methodologies to enhance the truthfulness of AI, including identifying behavioral patterns in AI that align with truthfulness and modifying AI decision-making processes. Additionally, there has been discourse on the equilibrium between the truthfulness of AI and its utility. Honest AI endeavors to ensure that AI does not intentionally convey information contrary to its perceived "beliefs" merely to obtain rewards, which is related to the concept of AI deception. Conversely, truth-seeking AI is concerned with evidence assessment and the discovery of novel insights, which could assume greater significance as AI systems become increasingly autonomous, making independent determinations about information acquisition based on situational comprehension.  Truth-seeking AI is fundamentally engaged in discerning truth; however, evaluating its effectiveness in achieving this goal lacks a singular metric. For instance, truth-seeking AI might aspire to adopt Bayesian principles or maintain consistency in reasoning. The phenomenon of belief entrenchment refers to the tendency for individuals to adhere to existing beliefs despite contradictory evidence, akin to confirmation bias, where individuals preferentially seek out, recall, or interpret information that corroborates pre-existing beliefs. Confirmation bias pertains to information processing in specific contexts and presents measurement challenges. In psychological research, quantifying confirmation bias is contingent upon task-specific factors and exhibits variable reliability. Accurate measurement necessitates a comprehensive understanding of an individual's prevailing beliefs and a robust mechanism for their assessment, which poses a significant challenge in the field.
