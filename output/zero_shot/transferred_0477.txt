This study examines the performance of our system across four simulation tests comprising 64 tasks, as well as five real-world tasks, using three distinct types of Variable-Level Autonomy (VLA) policies. The simulation tests employ four scenarios—Simpler, Libero, Robotwin1.0, and Robotwin2.0—which are designed to evaluate different aspects of the system.   The "Simpler" simulation is conducted using the SAPIEN simulator and ManiSkill2 framework. It includes tasks designed for both the WindowX and Google Robot platforms; however, this study focuses predominantly on the tasks associated with the WindowX platform. "Libero" aims to explore lifelong learning in decision-making tasks and consists of four distinct task sets. The analysis in this study emphasizes the most challenging set of tasks, as previous research has demonstrated strong results on the other sets. "Robotwin1.0" and "Robotwin2.0" center on operations involving dual-arm grippers, with an emphasis on task execution and automatic data collection processes.   For the real-world evaluation, we utilize a REALMAN75 dual-arm robotic system within an office-like environment. The testing involves five distinct tasks that incorporate commonly encountered objects, such as books, pens, a cabinet, a charger, a laptop, and a paper ball. The tasks are as follows: (i) *Receive Book*, where the system receives a book from a human and places it on a shelf; (ii) *Storage Charger*, in which the robot picks up a charger, stores it inside a cabinet, and subsequently closes the cabinet door; (iii) *Paper and Pen*, where the robot disposes of a paper ball in a trash bin and places a pen in a designated holder; (iv) *Laptop*, consisting of closing the laptop, unplugging its charger, and placing the charger back inside the cabinet; and (v) *Pick Books*, where the robot utilizes both arms simultaneously to lift and hold a book.   Through this experimental evaluation, the study aims to assess the efficacy and adaptability of the proposed system across simulated and real-world scenarios.
