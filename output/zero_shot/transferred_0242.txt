Policy optimization is a fundamental aspect of reinforcement learning, particularly in the context of language models. Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) is widely adopted to ensure gradual and stable policy changes, especially in scenarios involving human feedback. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) extends upon this by incorporating group advantages to stabilize learning under conditions of limited feedback. OptPO builds on these principles by enhancing efficiency without altering the core methodology.  In summary, existing research has demonstrated that training during testing facilitates better model adaptation, employing various strategies such as uncertainty reduction, collaborative learning, and reinforcement learning. However, these current methodologies are often characterized by substantial computational demands due to repeated iterations. This study contributes to the field by introducing OptPO, which optimizes computational efficiency while maintaining robust performance, thereby advancing and refining existing test-time methodologies.
