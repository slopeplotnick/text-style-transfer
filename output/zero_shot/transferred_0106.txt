In order to enhance comprehension and ensure safety, feedback is incorporated into each example within our dataset through the utilization of advanced models. Specifically, we employ gpt-oss-120b for textual data, Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct for visual and video data, and Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct for auditory data. Each example processed by the model includes the original content, its assigned safety label, and highlighted areas of concern. Subsequently, the model elucidates the rationale behind its safety assessment. The comprehensive framework for generating this feedback is depicted in Figure 5. Contrary to rudimentary models that solely provide binary safety evaluations, OMNIGUARD delivers thorough and transparent safety assessments. The structured response consists of several components: (1) Safety judgment, offering a determination of the input's safety status; (2) Violation categories, identifying specific safety issues when the content is deemed unsafe; (3) Reasoning critique, clarifying the model's decision-making process in relation to established guidelines. This methodology enables OMNIGUARD to transcend mere pattern recognition, facilitating a nuanced analysis of potentially unsafe content across various formats. In this study, we present comprehensive test results of OMNIGUARD, assessing its efficacy in both isolated and integrated contexts across 15 evaluations encompassing text, images, videos, and audio. Furthermore, we conduct analyses to address the following inquiries: (1) Does the incorporation of reasoning enhance the model's proficiency in safety evaluations and its ability to tackle complex safety issues across diverse formats? (2) Can safety insights derived from familiar formats be effectively applied to novel ones, demonstrating the model's capacity to comprehend and manage safety across multiple modalities?
