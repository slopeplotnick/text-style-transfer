In recent developments, artificial intelligence (AI) agents have demonstrated the capability to autonomously engage in cognitive processes, strategic planning, and tool utilization. Notably, AI applications in the medical field have emerged, with agents undertaking complex tasks that traditionally required human expertise. For instance, MMedAgent employs various tools to accomplish medical responsibilities, such as generating reports for chest X-rays. Similarly, MedRAX is an advanced agent specifically designed for interpreting chest X-ray images, utilizing state-of-the-art technological resources. CT-Agent excels in comprehending three-dimensional CT scans, thereby facilitating diagnostic reasoning. However, these agents typically operate using a single tool and lack effective collaboration in the generation of radiology reports.  In response to these limitations, we have developed the Radiologist Copilot, an innovative system that integrates multiple synergistic tools, thereby enabling the execution of more intricate tasks. The preparation of radiology reports is a critical yet time-intensive endeavor that is prone to errors, particularly when dealing with 3D imaging. Ensuring the quality of such reports is crucial, albeit often overlooked in existing methodologies, due to its monotonous nature. To address these challenges, Radiologist Copilot has been conceived as an AI assistant equipped with a comprehensive suite of tools designed to facilitate the creation and quality assurance of radiology reports, thus enhancing the efficiency and support available to radiologists.  As depicted in the accompanying illustration, Radiologist Copilot supports radiologists throughout the entire report-generation process. Initially, it configures the tool library and subsequently employs the action planner and executor to strategize and implement tasks. A memory module is incorporated to monitor actions undertaken and their outcomes, enabling verification of task completion. The action executor is responsible for formulating and executing commands aligned with the planned actions. For each task, it generates the appropriate command for the selected tool, executes the command, and acquires the action's outcome.
