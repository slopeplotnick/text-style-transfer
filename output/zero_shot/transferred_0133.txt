Test-time learning enables models to adapt dynamically during deployment. Initial methodologies centered on straightforward adaptation techniques that avoided altering model parameters, such as minimizing uncertainty and employing batch normalization. More advanced methods later emerged, permitting models to update their parameters during inference, thereby improving their performance on domain-specific tasks. For instance, empirical findings indicate that test-time training enhances model adaptability when confronted with evolving data distributions. Furthermore, certain techniques facilitate the fine-tuning of large language models by incorporating constructive feedback during the inference process. A recent innovation in this domain is test-time policy optimization, which conceptualizes model utilization as a reinforcement learning problem. Methods such as RLVR and TTRL leverage iterative feedback mechanisms, such as majority voting, to guide parameter updates. These approaches aim to ensure consistency and correctness in model reasoning while circumventing the need for labeled data.   However, a significant limitation of these existing methods lies in their reliance on repeated evaluations for each query to achieve reliable outcomes, leading to inefficiencies in terms of time and computational resources. Specifically, simple queries necessitate minimal computational effort, whereas more complex queries require extensive evaluations, thereby rendering the process both slow and computationally expensive. To address this challenge, we propose a novel framework, termed OptPO, designed to optimize efficiency in test-time learning. Rather than predefining the number of iterations required, OptPO employs an iterative evaluation strategy that halts once sufficient evidence is accumulated to produce a confident answer within a predefined error threshold. Additionally, the evaluations conducted during this process are utilized to refine the model’s parameters, ensuring that computational resources are allocated judiciously. Notably, OptPO is compatible with supervised fine-tuning techniques, further enhancing its applicability in test-time training scenarios.
