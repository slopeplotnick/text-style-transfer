The duration of the experiment ranged from approximately 20 to 35 minutes. Initially, participants selected an image that appealed to them for use in a writing exercise. We ensured that the participants comprehended the content and context of the image they selected. Following this, participants were introduced to the functionality of the TaleFrame software. Subsequently, they were tasked with creating a narrative utilizing their chosen image. Throughout the experiment, we meticulously documented their actions and verbal interactions. Upon completion of the narrative task, we conducted a debriefing session to solicit their feedback. Each participant received a monetary compensation of $5 for their involvement in the study. For the purpose of evaluating user feedback, participants were asked to rate TaleFrame on a Likert scale ranging from 1 to 5, reflecting their overall experience.   TaleFrame is designed to facilitate users in constructing a comprehensive story framework with ease, attributed to its user-friendly interface and the availability of options for defining various components of the story. It allows users to interactively organize story elements and illustrates their interconnections, thereby enhancing the narrative coherence. The software provides flexibility for users to modify the story structure dynamically. Additionally, TaleFrame assists in maintaining the temporal consistency of the narrative and offers suggestions for enhancing the emotional and thematic dimensions of the story. It conducts an assessment of the story's quality and provides constructive feedback for improvement.
