The process involves assembling a set of environments, tools, tasks, and verifiers. Subsequently, DeepSeek-V3.2 is applied to perform reinforcement learning on this dataset, retaining only those that successfully pass the evaluation. This results in a total of 1,827 environments and their associated tasks, culminating in a combined total of 4,417. A pertinent example is the challenge of planning a trip that adheres to a complex set of criteria, where formulating a compliant itinerary is challenging, yet verifying its compliance is relatively straightforward.   To illustrate, consider the task of planning a three-day trip commencing in Hangzhou from October 1st to October 3rd, 2025. The itinerary must ensure that different cities are visited, and different accommodations and dining establishments are selected each day. Furthermore, recommendations for hotels, restaurants, and attractions should be relevant to the specific city visited each day. On the second day, budgetary considerations are crucial. If a hotel reservation exceeds 800 CNY per night, dining expenses must remain below 350 CNY for both lunch and dinner, with both dining venues maintaining a minimum rating of 4.0 stars. Additionally, the cost of the afternoon attraction ticket must be less than 120 CNY. Conversely, if the hotel pricing falls between 500-800 CNY, there is more financial leeway, provided at least one restaurant maintains a 4.0-star rating or higher, and the attraction ticket is priced under 180 CNY. For more economical accommodations priced between 200-500 CNY, a single restaurant must have a minimum rating of 3.2.   This scenario necessitates assistance in formulating a suitable travel plan that meets these outlined constraints and preferences.
