Individuals who are less technically inclined or possess a stronger focus on business considerations might exhibit reluctance towards technological change, even if they recognize its advantages, due to concerns about the rapid pace of innovation. Our team, characterized by its small size and adaptability to new technologies, anticipated minimal resistance due to our early consideration of implementing a Software Product Line (SPL). Through comprehensive interviews, we have identified strategies to maintain successful practices, enhance benefits, and address potential concerns. It is crucial to continue effective existing SPL practices to foster comfort among team members. While our operational approach will undergo significant transformation, preserving beneficial practices will facilitate the team's acclimatization to new methodologies.  The SPL framework emphasizes accelerated processes, component reuse, and modularization, principles already integrated into our operations, allowing us to concentrate on leveraging these advantages. Transitioning to an SPL model promotes sustainable reuse and modularization. Additional SPL benefits encompass customization, automation, and a factory-like procedural environment. Furthermore, discussions may encompass aspects such as improved code quality, which contributes to alleviating technical debt. Enhanced code consistency, reduced defects, and the elimination of repetitive code are among the positive outcomes, alongside the resolution of frequent errors that enhance overall code quality. Surprisingly, these attributes are not solely recognized by product owners; IT professionals frequently highlight these advantages with equal emphasis. For example, the adoption of SPL methodologies can enhance the organization's research and development image. Moreover, embracing SPL contributes to accelerated software development processes.
