The Autonomous Domain-Oriented Relevance Engine (ADORE) is composed of three primary components: a domain-specific relevance module, an error-based data generation module, and a knowledge enhancement module leveraging key features. The relevance module employs a reasoning-based framework to autonomously generate challenging samples by analyzing online product representations. The data generation module further strengthens the system by creating diverse and complex sample types tailored to error characteristics. The knowledge enhancement module utilizes critical analytical insights to refine knowledge representation and improve the reasoning and representational capabilities of the online model.  Extensive experimental evaluations were conducted to assess ADORE's performance using large-scale offline datasets and online testing environments. Results indicate that ADORE significantly outperforms existing approaches, delivering improvements in both offline and online metrics. These findings underscore the efficacy of ADORE in addressing complex relevance tasks prevalent in e-commerce applications.  To optimize the online model’s relevance assessment capabilities, a novel approach involving Large Language Models (LLMs) was introduced. Research has demonstrated that employing Chain-of-Thought prompting enhances the reasoning capacities of such models. This methodology was utilized to fine-tune our relevance model, beginning with the use of GPT-4o to generate annotated data based on Chain-of-Thought guidelines. This annotation process mimics human reasoning practices by adhering to predefined guidelines and utilizing illustrative examples, thereby producing a dataset characterized by detailed human-like reasoning.  To ensure accuracy and reliability, the labeled data underwent a verification process against human annotations, with any mismatched samples excluded from the final dataset. The curated dataset was subsequently employed to fine-tune the LLaMA-3 model through optimization of a standard Language Model loss function. This approach demonstrates the capacity of Chain-of-Thought integration to enhance LLM-based reasoning frameworks, contributing to robust improvements in relevance modeling within e-commerce contexts.
