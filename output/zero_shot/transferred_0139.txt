The deployment of large language models (LLMs) or advanced BERT classifiers for real-time online search applications is hindered by concerns related to processing speed and cost. To address these challenges, we employ a knowledge distillation approach, wherein a more simplified model is trained with guidance from a more complex counterpart, as illustrated in Figure 1. Our online application utilizes a streamlined variant of the BERT architecture. To ensure effective learning within the simpler model, we opt for a deep BERT model as the "teacher" model, rather than selecting a model of a different architecture. Furthermore, we incorporate significant insights obtained from another model into the BERT framework, aiming to enhance its ability to discern why particular search queries align more effectively with certain products. To mitigate the presence of extraneous and noisy information resulting from lengthy explanations, we implement a model designed to extract and refine product details that either correlate or do not correlate with the search query. This refinement process enables the simplified BERT model to concentrate on pertinent information. Given that the simplified model cannot process these details in real-time, they are exclusively integrated into the teacher model to maintain consistency. Subsequently, the simplified model undergoes training based on the teacher model's guidance. Due to the lack of publicly available datasets for Chinese e-commerce searches, our evaluation was conducted on a substantial dataset derived from JD app search logs, which comprises search and product pairings that have been verified by experts.
