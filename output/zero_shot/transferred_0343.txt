Guardrails refer to systems designed to ensure safety in interactions between humans and artificial intelligence. Historically, these systems relied on simplistic rules for filtering content, which proved inadequate as technological advancements introduced new challenges. Such rudimentary systems lacked comprehensiveness and precision. Contemporary guardrails have evolved, incorporating advanced AI models that enhance their adaptability and generalization capabilities. For instance, the Llama Guard model facilitates the secure management of user-AI interactions, leveraging specialized safety data from Meta AI. Additionally, models such as Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard address visual safety concerns. Despite these advancements, persistent issues remain. Previous efforts predominantly concentrated on textual and imagery contexts, neglecting the complexities of video and audio, which are frequently addressed using simplistic methods that fail to grasp contextual nuances. Furthermore, many systems are restricted to a single type of media or specific scenarios, thus exhibiting limitations in handling multimodal interactions or cross-media reasoning. In response to these challenges, OMNIGUARD was developed as a pioneering system capable of managing various media types and facilitating comprehensive reasoning across them. OMNIGUARD endeavors to deliver robust and thorough safety assessments across all media formats.
