Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) represents a sophisticated methodology wherein pretrained diffusion models are utilized not only for noise reduction in sample generation but also as optimization objectives across diverse applications. This technique, denoted as SDS, has significantly facilitated the deployment of these models in the generation of both images and videos. The efficacy of SDS in image synthesis has catalyzed its integration into reinforcement learning frameworks, particularly for control tasks. In several implementations, traditional discriminators, as seen in configurations akin to Generative Adversarial Imitation Learning (GAIL), are replaced with diffusion models to effectively discern between authentic and simulated actions. Despite their potential, these approaches necessitate adversarial training and often involve continuous updates of the diffusion model during policy optimization processes. In addition to adversarial configurations, alternative strategies endeavor to directly employ SDS-like methodologies for policy learning. For example, certain approaches facilitate policy training by leveraging pretrained image or video diffusion models that can interpret textual prompts. These methods are capable of generating actions that adhere to textual instructions; however, the resulting behaviors occasionally exhibit unnatural characteristics. A notable antecedent to our work is the SMILING approach, wherein controllers are trained using a process akin to variational score distillation. Unlike SMILING, which requires bespoke diffusion models tailored to each task, our methodology entails the development of a universal motion model derived from stochastic motion data. This generalized model is subsequently utilized to train a variety of control policies by integrating it with specific task objectives within a goal-oriented reinforcement learning paradigm. Furthermore, we delineate several critical design decisions that contribute to the generation of high-quality, naturally plausible movements across a broad spectrum of tasks.
