Our proposed methodology has been designed to optimize compatibility with modern systems capable of processing large volumes of data efficiently, such as vLLM and SGLang. This approach supports both real-time service applications and offline data processing workflows. In Section 4.2, we primarily utilize the two-step vLLM configuration due to its computational efficiency and facilitation of streamlined reasoning processes.   The THINKMERGE framework was evaluated using two experimental paradigms. First, for tasks involving a single correct answer, we analyzed performance on datasets such as AIME 2025 and GPQA Diamond, implementing majority voting to consolidate responses across multiple outputs. Second, for tasks where solutions are open-ended and no definitive answer exists, we conducted evaluations on datasets including LiveCodeBench v5, BrowseComp-en, BrowseComp-zh, GAIA, and xBench-DeepSearch. The emphasis of this latter category is on generating high-quality solutions rather than identifying a singular correct response.   For single-answer tasks, five distinct experiments were conducted, testing models such as Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-14B, and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B. Open-ended coding tasks were evaluated using models such as DeepCoder-14B-Preview, Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-Coder-30A3B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B-Thinking, and Qwen3-Think-30A3B. For research-oriented open-ended tasks, a subset of WebSailor models, including WebSailor-3B, WebSailor-7B, and WebSailor-32B, were utilized. All experiments were conducted with a maximum sequence length set to 32,768 tokens, and additional details regarding sampling configurations are provided in Appendix A.1.  Further evaluation of THINKMERGE was conducted in the context of research tasks requiring tool-based reasoning prior to response generation. Specifically, three Tongyi-WebSailor models—WebSailor-3B, WebSailor-7B, and WebSailor-32B—were assessed on four challenging web-based datasets: BrowseComp-en, BrowseComp-zh, GAIA, and xBenchDeepSearch. The BrowseComp datasets (English and Chinese variants) are characterized by their inclusion of difficult-to-locate factual queries. Given the extensive question set of BrowseComp-en (1,266 queries in total), a random subset of 200 questions was selected for testing, ensuring comparability with BrowseComp-zh.
