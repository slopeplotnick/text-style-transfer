In the left panel of Figure 2, an illustrative example is provided to demonstrate the configuration of the prompt and the extraction of triples for a single training image. This figure illustrates how various details pertaining to the image are systematically organized. The processed output consists of multiple triples aligned with the Fashionpedia ontology, encompassing attributes such as the type of clothing item (depicts), its silhouette (has_silhouette), material composition (has_material_type), pattern (has_pattern), and other relevant relationships defined by Fashionpedia. Each triple adheres to the format (image_id, relation, attribute), facilitating a clear interpretation of the image’s descriptive content. This methodology supports the generation and comparison of conceptual graphs.  To determine the influence of individual samples, the graph corresponding to each generated image was compared against the graphs of all training images by assessing the degree of overlap in their respective nodes. For each prompt, the training images exhibiting the greatest overlap with the graph of the generated image were retained. The specific prompt employed was as follows: "Create triples for the fashion item in the image based on the relationships of Fashionpedia Ontology, provided at: \\({ONTOLOGY_PATH} = \). Focus exclusively on the fashion item of the image with image_id: \\({IMAGE_ID=}) and give me the triples that describe the image (image_id, relationship, attribute_value). Please do not give more comments."   For example, for the image identified as 58810.jpg, the extracted triples included the following: (58810.jpg, depicts, Shirt), (58810.jpg, belongs_to_category, Upperbody), (58810.jpg, has_nickname, None), (58810.jpg, has_silhouette, Slim-fit), (58810.jpg, has_waistline, Straight), (58810.jpg, has_length, Long-sleeved), (58810.jpg, has_collar_type, Classic), (58810.jpg, has_lapel_type, Not Applicable), (58810.jpg, has_neckline, Crew-neck), (58810.jpg, has_sleeve_type, Long Sleeves with Cuffs), (58810.jpg, has_pocket_type, Not Applicable), (58810.jpg, has_opening_type, Button-front Closure), (58810.jpg, has_material_type, Cotton-blend Fabric), (58810.jpg, has_leather_type, None), (58810.jpg, has_fabric_treatment, Not Applicable), (58810.jpg, has_pattern, Plaid), and (58810.jpg, has_animal_pattern, None). The training images whose graphs demonstrated the highest overlap with the graph of the generated image were subsequently utilized in the next phase of the unlearning process.  In addition to identifying similar images, Neo4j was employed to explore the complete graph, enabling the identification of common attributes and the distribution of concepts within the dataset. For the unlearning procedure, the generative model was retrained after the removal of specific training samples. This approach facilitated an evaluation of the influence these samples exerted on the generative output. A total of 10 unlearning experiments were conducted, employing both the baseline method and the proposed approach. In each experiment, images were generated for 15 prompts prior to and following the unlearning process. To ensure consistency with the baseline, a comparable number of training images—those identified as most influential for each prompt—were excluded during preprocessing.
