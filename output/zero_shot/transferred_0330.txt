Details regarding the participants can be found in Table B.3 of the Appendix. There were no significant differences in participant characteristics across the groups. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three experimental groups. In the first group, participants were informed that the music they would hear was created by artificial intelligence (AI). The second group was told that the music was sourced from movie soundtracks, while the third group was informed that the music they would hear was generated by both humans and AI, with the additional task of identifying the origin of each piece. Groups 1 and 3 were provided with explanatory materials about AI and AI music tools prior to beginning the music evaluation task.  Following these instructions, participants were tasked with rating 18 randomly selected music clips, which encompassed various subgenres and utilized different AI tools. Prior to the commencement of the task, participants were instructed to adjust the audio volume to their preferred level and familiarize themselves with the list of emotion-related terms to be used during the evaluation. The order of emotion terms was randomized for each participant but remained consistent across all music clips for that participant. Importantly, participants were instructed to rate the emotional impact of the music based on how it made them feel, rather than inferring the emotions the music was intended to convey.  Each music clip was played twice. During the initial playback, participants were asked to listen attentively without completing any additional tasks. After the first playback, a set of rating questions appeared on the screen, and the clip was played again to facilitate memory recall during the evaluation process. After completing the music ratings, participants were asked to complete a questionnaire collecting information on their personality traits, music-related habits, and demographic background, as well as their perspectives on AI.  At the conclusion of the study, participants were presented with a summary screen outlining the study’s objectives and detailing the intended use of their data. Additionally, an open-ended question was provided, allowing participants to share their thoughts on AI-generated music and their overall experience with the study. Finally, participants received personalized feedback based on their involvement in the study.
