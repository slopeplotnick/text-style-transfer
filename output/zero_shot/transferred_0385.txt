The Menta interface is capable of managing various tasks, including the assessment of stress, depression (evaluated both as a binary outcome and in terms of severity), and suicide risk (also assessed both as a binary outcome and with detailed levels). Upon activation of the "Start Evaluation" function, the interface processes social media posts utilizing the selected model and displays the results, which encompass predicted categories, their accuracy, and additional evaluative details. The model's performance was evaluated across six tasks utilizing datasets that varied from 10 to 3,000 posts, influenced by the complexity of the task and the availability of data. The analysis revealed a balance between processing speed and accuracy when compared to other similar systems. Menta is engineered to be precise, expeditious, and responsive, rendering it a suitable option for practical applications where performance and rapid response are critical. Figure 9 illustrates three metrics: the speed of task processing (ITPS), the time required to produce the first result (TTFT), and accuracy. Each circle depicted in the figure represents a model, with the circle's size indicating memory usage. Falcon prioritizes speed, demonstrating rapid processing and task initiation but at the expense of accuracy. Conversely, StableLM offers higher accuracy but requires longer response times and increased resources, thereby raising costs and slowing user interactions. In contrast, Menta is highly accurate, except for Task 5, where StableLM and Phi-4 Mini slightly outperform it, yet it remains faster and more efficient than StableLM. This indicates that Menta delivers accurate results promptly while maintaining low memory usage, which is crucial for systems with high user engagement. Overall, despite the distinct advantages and disadvantages of each model, Menta is optimally positioned for deployment in real-world environments. It achieves high performance without excessive resource demands, contributing to cost-effectiveness and enhancing user experience.
