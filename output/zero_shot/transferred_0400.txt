Forecasting stock prices presents significant challenges due to the inherent unpredictability of the market, which is highly responsive to contemporary events. Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) have facilitated novel methodologies for stock price prediction by leveraging textual data. Although these models demonstrate proficiency in text comprehension, they often require sophisticated memory systems to utilize historical information consistently, particularly when addressing complex real-world scenarios. Extensive research efforts have been devoted to developing such memory systems; however, these systems frequently encounter difficulties when processing the voluminous and disordered online news content. This content does not directly elucidate price fluctuations and frequently contains superfluous information. The principal challenge lies in discerning and extracting the pertinent details and logical connections from this unstructured text that truly influence stock prices.  To address this issue, the present study proposes an innovative dual-layered memory system. Initially, this system organizes events in two distinct manners: it aggregates daily event specifics to construct a coherent depiction and monitors the temporal evolution of events. This dual approach facilitates the creation of a knowledge base that captures the dynamics of market expectations over time. Subsequently, the system examines the correlation between event patterns and stock price alterations, establishing a secondary knowledge base dedicated to analyzing this connection. Collectively, these processes culminate in a system capable of autonomously acquiring and structuring knowledge.  The utilization of this system involves two primary phases: retrieval and reasoning. Initially, it employs recent events to identify analogous historical situations within its memory, thereby enhancing decision-making in the present context. Subsequently, it integrates recent occurrences, new information, and past examples to forecast stock price changes. Empirical evaluations reveal that this system exhibits superior predictive accuracy compared to legacy systems and enhances interpretability by explicitly illustrating the linkage between events and price fluctuations, alongside providing rational explanations for its conclusions.
