PEFT-FACTORY is an advanced computational tool for assessing classification and efficiency metrics during predictive modeling, alongside evaluating token accuracy and semantic similarity metrics such as ROUGE and BLEU. For classification, it employs standard metrics, specifically Accuracy and F1 scores. To evaluate efficiency in the context of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods, it utilizes the PSCP metric. This versatile metric considers factors such as the number of parameters, memory utilization, and prediction latency. These metrics are implemented within the `train/sft/metric.py` file, tailored for supervised fine-tuning and aligned with the configuration utilized by LLaMA-Factory. This setup includes distinct data classes for each metric, namely `ComputeAccuracy`, `ComputeF1`, and `ComputePSCP`. For classification, a configuration option labeled `compute_classification.metrics` allows for the activation or deactivation of classification metrics, while the efficiency metrics can be controlled via the `compute_pscp` setting. Further elaboration on efficiency metrics is provided in Appendix B.3.   PEFT-FACTORY is engineered for flexibility and user-friendliness, making it a valuable asset for research on PEFT methodologies. To illustrate its role in enhancing reproducibility and benchmarking, two examples are provided. The modular architecture of PEFT-FACTORY facilitates the development and testing of novel PEFT methods by researchers and developers. Currently, the development of new PEFT methods is often hampered by shared code that may be unreliable or difficult to reproduce. Moreover, developers frequently need to independently construct training and evaluation scripts, which can be redundant, error-prone, and lead to inconsistent outcomes.
