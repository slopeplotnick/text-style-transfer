This section delineates the setup of our system, encompassing the construction of the dataset, the configuration of the judge model, and the processes of validation through human evaluation. Our objective is to bridge Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification by conducting a comprehensive analysis of their interrelation. Initially, we outline the design and preparation of our study, followed by an exploration of three principal research questions concerning the efficacy of these methodologies, their potential synergistic effects, and the feasibility of integrating them. To elucidate the relationship between Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification, we conducted an extensive study to comparatively analyze them within a unified framework. The study aims to address the following questions:  1. How do the most effective Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification methodologies perform when applied to identical AI-generated content? Is there a consistently superior approach? 2. Do these methodologies exhibit distinct proficiencies in identifying factual inaccuracies, or are their capabilities largely similar? 3. Can a more robust system be developed by integrating both methodologies, thereby surpassing the performance of each when used individually?  To investigate these questions, our research proceeded in three stages. First, regarding Question 1, we employed a range of prominent and leading methodologies from both domains, conducting direct comparisons. This represents the inaugural direct comparative analysis of these approaches. Second, informed by these findings, we examined the interaction of the two methodologies for Question 2. Our results demonstrated that the integration of Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification yields superior outcomes compared to the deployment of individual methods. A comprehensive case study was also conducted to gain deeper insights into Question 2. Finally, acknowledging their complementary nature, we addressed Question 3 by developing and evaluating two straightforward integrated approaches. We experimented with integration at the final score level and at a systemic level, illustrating that even simplistic integration can significantly enhance performance.
