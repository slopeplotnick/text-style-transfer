Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated significant improvements in their capabilities, and researchers are continuously exploring innovative applications that leverage these models for beneficial purposes. With the increasing integration of LLMs in online communication platforms, there is potential for these models to facilitate enhanced empathetic understanding and improved dialogue among users. This study investigates the prospects of LLMs extending beyond merely identifying harmful content in online interactions to actively contributing to the de-escalation and resolution of conflicts. The research framework is divided into two phases: initially, the model evaluates the fairness of discourse and assesses the emotional dynamics within conversations; subsequently, it generates conciliatory messages aimed at promoting calmness and collaborative conflict resolution.  In order to evaluate the efficacy of these models, researchers constructed an extensive dataset sourced from Reddit and devised a multifaceted assessment approach. This approach includes rule-based scoring, user simulation, and comparative analysis with human judgments. Findings from these evaluations indicate that commercial models exhibit superior performance compared to their open-access counterparts, particularly in logical reasoning and conflict resolution scenarios. The study underscores both the potential and limitations of LLMs in fostering harmonious interactions within digital spaces.  Furthermore, large language models are transforming communication, education, and collaborative practices, with applications extending beyond tasks such as language translation and text summarization to fields like education, healthcare, and mental health. As these models are increasingly embedded in environments characterized by contentious exchanges, there emerges a novel proposition to utilize them not solely for task automation but also to cultivate civility and equity in online conversations. A prevalent issue in digital interactions is the escalation of hostile exchanges, often exacerbated by the absence of face-to-face interactions and non-verbal social cues, which can impede constructive dialogue.
