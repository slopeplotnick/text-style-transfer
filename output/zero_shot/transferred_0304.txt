We aggregated 5,500 Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset, as informed by prior research studies. Our evaluation encompassed several mathematical competition and testing environments, including AIME 2024, AMC, MATH500, GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math. Additionally, we expanded our analysis to include ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond, which cover content beyond conventional settings. The method employed for decoding was greedy decoding, constrained by a 3,000-token limit. We examined the performance of the Cognitive Amplification and Optimization (CAPO) technique in conjunction with four principal reinforcement learning methodologies: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. Each technique was also tested with a CAPO variant to assess the extent of enhancement provided by CAPO. This approach allowed for a comparative analysis of the standalone efficacy of the original methods versus their augmented counterparts. In order to ensure consistency and fairness, the methodological setup was standardized across all methods, with specific training configurations detailed in Appendix B.  The dataset GUI-R1-3K from OS-Atlas was employed, comprising 3,000 examples illustrating vision-language-action across web and mobile platforms. This dataset effectively aligns visual states with natural language instructions and action sequences, rendering it suitable for evaluating reasoning and planning capabilities across varied contexts. For the planning dimension, we examined GUI-Act-Web, OmniAct-Web, and AndroidControl-Low/High benchmarks. For perception evaluation, we utilized Screenshot Pro. A comparative analysis was conducted between GRPO and its CAPO-enhanced version through the primary GUI benchmark framework. This supplementary testing aimed to demonstrate the efficacy of CAPO in diverse reasoning scenarios, extending beyond purely mathematical contexts.
