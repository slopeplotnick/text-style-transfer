The dataset under investigation was generated by GPT-4o. Further information regarding the prompts, datasets, and model configurations is available in the supplementary materials. Prompt engineering is an essential methodology employed to ensure that language models adhere to the intended instructions. In this study, we utilized a technique known as prompt chaining, which facilitates the decomposition of complex tasks into more manageable subtasks, thereby enhancing the models' processing capabilities. Specifically, for the task of story parsing, we divided the process into four distinct components: entity extraction, event identification, story outlining, and relationship detection. The outcomes from each component were sequentially utilized in subsequent steps. To ensure adherence to the prescribed instructions, we employed prompt frameworks. This research utilized the TIDD-EC prompt framework, which comprises five elements: task definition, detailed instructions, specifications of actions to undertake and avoid, examples, and user content. This framework was instrumental in ensuring the accuracy and relevance of model responses. A table is provided that illustrates the prompts applied for the "Entity Extraction" task within the TIDD-EC framework. Additionally, we evaluated four distinct prompting strategies to assess their efficacy in converting narratives into JSON format:  1. Zero-shot prompting: The task was explained in natural language without the provision of examples. 2. TIDD-EC framework: The task was structured utilizing the TIDD-EC framework. 3. TIDD-EC + CoT: Chain-of-thought reasoning was incorporated into the TIDD-EC framework. 4. TIDD-EC + prompt chaining: The task was divided into four segments, each employing the TIDD-EC framework and interconnected through prompt chaining.  This comparative analysis aims to elucidate the most effective methodologies for guiding language models in structured data generation.
