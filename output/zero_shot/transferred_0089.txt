In recent years, there has been significant interest in the development of novel language models with capabilities in voice processing. Proprietary models such as GPT-4o and Gemini-1.5-pro have demonstrated superior performance in tasks including speech recognition, translation, and language comprehension, surpassing the capabilities of previously accessible open models. Despite these advancements, there is a notable scarcity of research focused on the integration of speech with language models to enhance comprehension. Furthermore, the interaction between speech models and language models, particularly within autonomous intelligent systems, is only beginning to be explored. This tutorial aims to provide an overview of the historical application of language models in speech processing, while offering insights into the development of interoperable communicative systems. It will address advanced topics such as multimodal data integration and the foundational principles necessary for aligning non-textual and textual data. Additionally, we will examine illustrative examples that facilitate the understanding of these concepts. The tutorial will also explore recent methodologies for designing models that simultaneously process speech and text, utilizing incremental data generation techniques within combined speech-text frameworks. By reviewing both traditional and contemporary approaches, this tutorial seeks to elucidate the current state of speech-language model integration and identify ongoing challenges within this domain. The session, spanning a duration of three hours, will examine the evolution of speech and language models in the context of voice interfaces and intelligent systems. Each principal topic will be allotted 35 minutes, followed by a 10-minute question period and a 10-minute intermission. Each segment will commence with an overview of the themes, followed by an in-depth analysis of seminal studies that are currently shaping the field. The tutorial will conclude with a discussion on the challenges and emerging research opportunities in the dynamic area of speech and language model integration.
