This paper examines the limitations of TaleFrame in facilitating user interaction and advanced storytelling, as well as potential directions for future research. Currently, TaleFrame is optimized for desktop and laptop usage, employing mouse and keyboard inputs to assist users in connecting and delineating event relationships. While the drag-and-drop feature is generally well-received, its effectiveness on mobile devices such as smartphones and tablets is hindered by the reduced screen size. Although users can employ touch pens or finger gestures in lieu of a mouse, prolonged interaction and the input of relational data may result in errors, thereby compromising the user experience on mobile platforms. To mitigate these challenges, TaleFrame incorporates the use of colors, icons, and lines to visually represent information, alongside responsive layouts that adapt to varying screen dimensions. Future research endeavors aim to explore alternative interaction modalities, including touchscreens, keyboards, and voice commands, to enhance device compatibility and accessibility.  In the context of narrative construction, TaleFrame encounters difficulties in managing intricate event relationships that extend beyond chronological sequencing, encompassing causal linkages, flashbacks, and other sophisticated storytelling techniques. The complexity inherent in these connections poses challenges in maintaining narrative clarity and accuracy. Efforts have been made to refine the representation of event connections; however, intricate narratives and interactions among multiple entities often complicate the sequencing of events and the depiction of causal relationships, resulting in convoluted storylines. As narratives grow increasingly complex, the model's capacity to comprehend and accurately generate these connections diminishes. Future research will focus on improving the modeling of relationships and developing advanced strategies for non-linear storytelling. These enhancements aim to augment the model’s proficiency in crafting complex narratives, thereby providing users with a more engaging and coherent storytelling experience.
