We developed a model, *Story2MIDI*, designed to generate music that aligns with the emotional content of textual narratives. This model was constructed by integrating datasets that analyze emotional attributes in both text and music. The dataset comprises pairs of short text excerpts and musical passages that convey corresponding emotional states. Despite the limited size of the dataset and computational resources, the model demonstrated the ability to identify emotional features in music and utilize them to generate novel compositions reflecting varying emotional tones. Model performance was evaluated using both objective music-related metrics and subjective human assessments, both of which confirmed its capability to align musical output with the intended emotional context.  The increasing popularity of entertainment media—such as music, audiobooks, and podcasts—has been fueled by the rapid expansion of social media and streaming platforms. A significant portion of this media is grounded in storytelling, spanning diverse formats such as films, video games, podcasts, and documentaries. Stories are particularly effective in eliciting emotional engagement from audiences through their characters, narratives, and underlying themes. This emotional resonance contributes to the lasting impact of stories on individuals. Similarly, music possesses a profound ability to evoke and express emotions, making it an integral component of many storytelling experiences. As a result, creators frequently incorporate music into narratives to enhance emotional connection and audience immersion.  The advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) have facilitated the development of innovative models capable of generating diverse types of content. These AI-driven tools hold significant potential for augmenting creative processes, such as the pairing of emotionally congruent music with storytelling media, thereby enriching the overall audience experience.
