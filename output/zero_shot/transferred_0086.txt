In this study, we evaluated seven methodologies for model combination: Linear, Karcher Mean, SLERP, NuSLERP, TIES, DELLA, and Nearswap. These techniques were employed to integrate a bias-inverted model with a pre-trained large language model. The Linear method, as proposed by Wortsman et al. in 2022, is the most fundamental approach. It involves the addition and averaging of the weights of fine-tuned models, offering a cost-effective solution with effective outcomes. The Karcher Mean, introduced by Goddard et al. in 2024, adopts a distinct strategy by considering the curved geometry of the model parameters, which is advantageous in non-Euclidean spaces. Similarly, SLERP, also developed by Goddard et al. in 2024, interpolates model weights along a geodesic path on a spherical manifold, thereby preserving the intrinsic geometry of the parameter space. NuSLERP, another innovation from Goddard et al. in 2024, extends SLERP by allowing differential mixing of model components, emphasizing critical regions to enhance balance and performance. TIES, formulated by Yadav in 2023, amalgamates models by examining parameter differentials crucial for specific tasks, thereby reducing interference and retaining the distinct strengths of each model. DELLA, introduced by Deep in 2024, mitigates interference by pruning less significant updates through an intelligent mechanism that preserves essential components, thereby maintaining the foundational model's performance.
