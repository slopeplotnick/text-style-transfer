We present *Promptolution*, an open-source Python framework designed to facilitate the automated optimization of prompts. This framework has been rigorously tested, demonstrating robust performance and compatibility with a variety of language models, natural language processing (NLP) tasks, and prompt improvement techniques. *Promptolution* offers modularity, allowing users to customize or replace individual components as needed. The tool is particularly beneficial for practitioners seeking to enhance individual prompts, as well as for researchers conducting systematic and reproducible experiments in prompt optimization. In this manuscript, we aim to (1) situate our tool within the context of existing prompt optimization frameworks, (2) detail its design and implementation, (3) evaluate its performance in comparison to alternative methods and unoptimized prompts, and (4) propose future directions for further development. Prompt optimization refers to the use of automated approaches to refine prompts, including task instructions and examples. Methods for prompt optimization can broadly be categorized into two types: continuous and discrete. Continuous methods operate in numerical spaces, whereas discrete methods work directly with textual data. Our framework focuses on discrete optimization, which has the advantage of being more interpretable and universally applicable across language models.  The optimization process in *Promptolution* proceeds iteratively, often employing a language model to generate improved prompt variants. The framework incorporates three distinct optimization techniques: (1) **OPRO**, which utilizes a language model to refine task instructions based on descriptions, examples, and prior results; (2) **EvoPrompt**, which applies genetic algorithms and differential evolution for instructional improvements, with a language model mediating the modifications; and (3) **CAPO**, a novel approach leveraging AutoML to optimize both instructions and examples more efficiently. Among these methods, CAPO proves to be more cost-effective while also outperforming OPRO and EvoPrompt in terms of overall performance.  This study seeks to advance the field of prompt optimization by offering *Promptolution* as a versatile, scalable, and effective tool, contributing both practical utility and methodological innovation to NLP research.
