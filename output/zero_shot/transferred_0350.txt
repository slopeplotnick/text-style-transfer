Despite significant advancements in the generation of sound and music through computational means, there remains an absence of standardized methods for evaluating these systems. In addition to traditional auditory assessments, scholars have devised objective methodologies to evaluate the fidelity of audio reproduction by these systems, as well as their efficacy in categorizing music according to established genres. In this study, we employ both auditory evaluations and objective metrics derived from prior research to assess the effectiveness of our symbolic music generation systems. Our methodology involved generating 100 MIDI samples for each subset of our test set, GoEmotions, and subsequently analyzing the results. The initial phase of our auditory evaluation aimed to determine the degree to which the music produced by our model corresponded with the emotional content conveyed in text stimuli; participants were invited to listen and provide feedback. This task is inherently subjective, requiring participants to discern the emotion embedded in the narrative and evaluate the congruence between the narrative emotion and the corresponding music. Initially, participants were presented with a text excerpt and required to assign it to one of four predefined emotion categories. Subsequently, they listened to two musical selections and selected the one that they perceived to most accurately reflect the narrative's emotional tone. This bifurcated approach enabled us to ascertain both participant consensus regarding the emotional interpretation of the text and the alignment between the music and the emotion conveyed by the text. In instances where musical selections did not precisely correspond to the emotional category identified by participants, we evaluated the congruence of the music with the emotional polarity and energy levels of the text, rather than strict categorical alignment. Each category was delineated by positive/negative valence and high/low energy levels, and for each participant's selection, we assessed the extent to which the music conformed to their perception of the narrative's emotional profile based on these dimensions. This provided a nuanced measure of emotional congruence between music and narrative, allowing for a more flexible evaluation of their emotional alignment.
