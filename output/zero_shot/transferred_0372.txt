In the context of planning a sequence of measurements, such as determining drilling locations, the application of artificial intelligence methodologies, such as the Monte Carlo tree search, can optimize the development of a drilling strategy (Mern and Caers, 2023). The parameters of interest evolve through a systematic approach: initially evaluating conceptual strategies, subsequently assessing resource quantities, and ultimately determining quality metrics. Once multiple strategies are narrowed to a singular viable option, attention should be directed towards quantifying mineral resources followed by an evaluation of resource quality, or by integrating these factors into a quality-amount matrix.  While the adoption of scientific methodologies can potentially enhance the cost-effectiveness and efficiency of exploratory processes, certain non-scientific challenges persist within the industry. These challenges encompass entrenched practices, traditional organizational structures, and the absence of comprehensive integrated software solutions. Typically, companies employ experts specialized in distinct domains, each attending to their respective tasks, often without a comprehensive understanding of their significance to the overarching decision-making process. To harness AI effectively, it is imperative for large corporations to restructure their organizational frameworks, shifting the emphasis from task execution to the resolution of decision-making challenges. This transformation extends beyond mere structural modifications to encompass a learning paradigm shift. While it is not necessary for geologists to acquire data science expertise, enhancing interdisciplinary understanding is crucial, as further discussed in the education section.  Predominantly, specialists continue to operate within a singular modeling framework, utilizing software systems that allow for biased and inflexible interpretations. Over time, these models are often misconstrued as definitive representations of reality. When personnel turnover occurs, the rationale underpinning the interpretations is frequently lost, and the derived form is erroneously regarded as empirical data rather than a hypothetical model. Within corporate environments, a lack of knowledge is frequently equated with failure, fostering the belief that increased time investment and effort would inherently lead to greater knowledge acquisition and uncertainty reduction.
