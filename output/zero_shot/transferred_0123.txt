In this study, we evaluated the performance of models on mathematical problem-solving tasks using five distinct assessments: AMC, Minerva, MATH-500, GSM8K, and Olympiad-Bench. The accuracy of the models' responses was verified through a comparison with correct answers by employing GPT-4o, in alignment with methodologies adopted in previous research. For an assessment of general reasoning capabilities, we examined the models' performance across various disciplines, including physics, biology, business, economics, and law, utilizing benchmarks such as MMLU-Pro, SuperGPQA, GPQA-Diamond, and BBEH. These more challenging tests enable a thorough evaluation of the models' proficiency in complex reasoning. Accuracy was measured using the Exact Match method, which determines the preciseness of answer correspondence, alongside the application of greedy decoding to derive the results.  To investigate the significance of different components within the models, we conducted ablation studies on the Qwen3-8B-Base model, deactivating specific features to observe their impact on performance in both mathematical and general reasoning tasks. The findings are presented in Table 2. Notably, the deactivation of challenger training resulted in the most substantial reduction in performance, with declines of 1.9 and 1.0 points in mathematical and general scores, respectively. Furthermore, the removal of challenger warm-up and curriculum learning also adversely affected performance, underscoring the importance of these components. The mathematical tasks exhibited greater susceptibility to these changes than the general reasoning tasks, a trend consistent with observations reported in Table 1, where mathematical scores demonstrated greater variability contingent on the methodology employed.  Overall, our results indicate that the integration of diverse human data generally enhances model performance, yet targeted data types yield more significant improvements within specific domains. This insight is advantageous for developers, as it allows for strategic guidance of model enhancement by selecting particular data types, thereby maintaining the model's focus on its intended learning objectives and preventing deviation from core learning goals.
