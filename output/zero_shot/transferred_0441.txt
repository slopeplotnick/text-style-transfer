Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods have emerged as a solution to address the challenges posed by the increasing size of large language models. Currently, numerous newly developed PEFT techniques are challenging to replicate, apply, or compare effectively. To address these issues, PEFT-FACTORY has been developed as an integrated platform to streamline the fine-tuning process of large language models through both pre-existing and customizable PEFT methods. This platform is designed to be both adaptable and extensible, incorporating 19 PEFT methods, 27 datasets focused on classification and text generation spanning 12 tasks, and evaluation tools for both general and PEFT-specific metrics. These features render PEFT-FACTORY a straightforward and dependable framework that facilitates reproducibility and method comparison. PEFT-FACTORY is built on the LLaMAFactory platform and is available at [https://github.com/kinit-sk/PEFT-Factory](https://github.com/kinit-sk/PEFT-Factory).  Large language models have demonstrated remarkable performance across a variety of natural language processing tasks, especially following the advent of the transformer architecture. However, the scalability of these models has led to a continual increase in the number of trainable parameters, which in turn raises the cost of fine-tuning, necessitates extensive data, and presents accessibility challenges for many researchers and practitioners. PEFT methods aim to address these challenges by training only a subset of the model’s parameters, thereby achieving performance comparable to full model training. This reduction in trainable parameters can be accomplished by introducing new parameters, selectively training specific parameters, or modifying the model to operate with fewer parameters.
