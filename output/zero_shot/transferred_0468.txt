We have developed a tool named "Promptsolution," which serves as a Python framework designed to facilitate the automatic enhancement of prompts. This open-source tool caters to both individuals who require prompt optimization for specific tasks and researchers engaged in extensive studies involving numerous benchmarks. The framework is constructed to be user-friendly and adaptable, allowing for the interchangeability of components and enabling the exploration of different optimization strategies when a particular one is suboptimal. Although not every optimizer within the library is suitable for every task, there is invariably at least one that performs effectively in each context. The architecture of Promptsolution allows for the straightforward comparison of various optimizers, as it is engineered to conduct such evaluations with minimal additional effort.  We have demonstrated the functionality of the framework, elucidated the integration of its components, and validated its utility and competitiveness through comparative analyses with alternative tools. Looking forward, our objectives include enhancing compatibility with other frameworks, such as DSPy, to enable users to leverage features from both systems. We also aim to introduce functionalities that facilitate the management of extensive experimental setups, including integration with configuration tools like Hydra. To enhance user accessibility, we are developing an intuitive interface that will allow users to monitor experiments and access real-time results.  Given the increasing prevalence of multi-agent systems, we are investigating methods to optimize prompts and communication configurations for scenarios involving multiple agents. Drawing inspiration from AutoML, we are contemplating the incorporation of features that amalgamate multiple optimizers to achieve superior outcomes. In summary, the framework is designed to be scalable and capable of assimilating new methodologies as the field progresses.
