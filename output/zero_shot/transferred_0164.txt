In 2023, Zakharov explored the cognitive mechanisms behind perceptual change detection and probabilistic inference under uncertainty. Petzschner and Glasauer (2011) posited that such phenomena can be attributed to the utilization of Bayesian-like predictive models in conditions of uncertainty. Recent computational frameworks, as articulated by Fountas et al. (2022), suggest that errors in temporal estimation are substantiated by memory-driven anticipatory processes. When individuals evaluate quantities or magnitudes, they tend to integrate ambiguous data with pre-existing cognitive biases, culminating in distinct behavioral patterns. Figure 1 illustrates the tendency of both Llama-4 Maverick and humans to exhibit regression towards the mean, indicative of analogous cognitive processing strategies despite inherent differences. Certain functionalities within language models (LLMs) reflect this heuristic approach; contextual learning, described by Xie et al. (2021), exemplifies an approximate inference strategy, which Qiu et al. (2025) suggest can enhance reasoning capabilities. Furthermore, LLMs deconstruct sequential data by employing surprisal metrics, a method consistent with human event detection, as discussed by Kumar and Fountas (2023, 2025). Nevertheless, substantial research predominantly focuses on explicit cognitive processing or learned behaviors governed by established algorithms, rather than exploratory tasks that reveal emergent cognitive strategies from training data.  Recent advancements in the development of LLMs capable of assimilating multifaceted information modalities have been rapid. Evaluation frameworks such as MMbench and SEED-bench, as mentioned by Liu and Li (2024), are instrumental in analyzing cognitive processing within these models. However, these assessments typically overlook the impact of informational noise on cognitive inference. Our synthetic datasets facilitate a nuanced examination of how LLMs integrate ambiguous information from diverse sources. This burgeoning research domain holds significant promise for the advancement of models that maintain efficacy amid uncertainty.
