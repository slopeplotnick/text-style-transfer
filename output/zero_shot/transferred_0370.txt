Referring Expression Comprehension (REC) is concerned with the identification of an object based on a description articulated in natural language. This research area is pivotal in the interdisciplinary field that integrates visual perception and language understanding. Recent advancements have been achieved by not merely aligning lexical items but by comprehending their semantics and incorporating supplementary contextual knowledge. An extension of this research is the Generalized Referring Expression Comprehension (GREC), which deals with the interpretation of descriptions that may correspond to no objects or multiple objects, exemplified by expressions such as "Pick up those three boxes." This paradigm more closely mirrors natural language usage in everyday discourse, where referents may be ambiguous or nonexistent.   The investigation of these phenomena necessitates extensive corpora of authentic images accompanied by human-generated descriptions, the procurement of which is resource-intensive. Online platforms facilitate cost-effective and efficient testing of these concepts. Nonetheless, challenges arise when models are required to interpret a visual scene, maintain a coherent conversational context, and engage in interactive dialogue. Current methodologies exhibit limitations under these conditions. A recent study introduced a novel GREC evaluation using dialogues within the context of a Minecraft game, demonstrating current AI models' difficulty in handling such tasks. Additionally, the study highlighted the financial burden associated with commissioning human-generated conversational data, resulting in a paucity of training datasets. The paper proposes an innovative approach to data generation, involving the synthesis of brief descriptions and comprehensive dialogues to enhance AI comprehension. While prior research on AI-driven data generation is relevant, applying it to GREC presents complexities due to the requisite spatial awareness and the necessity of correlating visual information with linguistic expressions.
