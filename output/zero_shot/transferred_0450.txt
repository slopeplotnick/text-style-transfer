The framework's success rates improved to 0.21 for Compound tasks and 0.50 for both Elemental and Complex tasks. These enhancements, reflecting an improvement of 9-30 percentage points, affirm the effectiveness of systematic validation in ensuring structural and semantic accuracy of outputs. The Self Corrective-Agent Architecture exhibits enhanced performance while employing the same few-shot prompting strategy as SMART-LLM. The framework utilizes a total of 700,017 tokens compared to SMART-LLM's 727,649 tokens, representing a reduction of 3.8%, as illustrated in Figure 13, and completes operations in 746 seconds versus 1,075 seconds, denoting a reduction of 30.6%, as depicted in Figure 14. This advancement predominantly arises from its modular prompting design, wherein each component—whether decomposing, assigning, or generating code—employs its specific example file. In contrast, SMART-LLM amalgamates all three example files in each LLM call, irrespective of the component being utilized. Consequently, each Self Corrective-Agent inference utilizes significantly fewer tokens, thereby accelerating performance. This efficient prompting methodology ensures that even with retries prompted by validation, the Self Corrective-Agent consumes fewer tokens overall.
