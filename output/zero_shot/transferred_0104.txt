The task of determining relevance was deconstructed, leading to insights on the requirements for models addressing this challenge. This analysis provides concrete recommendations for the development of future relevance models in the industry. A comprehensive methodology, encompassing model selection, training, and evaluation, is proposed. During the development of LORE, various training strategies were explored, resulting in several key insights that may assist in the fine-tuning of language models for specific applications:  1. Increasing the volume of data does not always yield enhanced performance. Initial improvements occur rapidly; however, the benefits diminish as additional data is incorporated. 2. Incorporating a greater number of features is beneficial. Stable and relevant information, even if somewhat redundant, can enhance model performance. 3. Concise and focused prompts are most effective. Extensive prompts with excessive information are not necessarily advantageous, while prompts lacking critical information can degrade model performance. 4. Simple teacher-consistent distillation (CoT) is suboptimal. It performs less effectively than standard training due to alterations in data distribution. 5. Curriculum learning remains effective. Strategies that structure learning based on complexity outperform random approaches significantly. 6. Extensive chains of thought (CoT) are not required for improved results. The output length did not notably increase during training, indicating that lengthy CoT is not essential for enhanced performance. 7. The rate of entropy reduction constrains model performance. Initially, the model exchanges entropy for improved performance rapidly. However, when entropy becomes too low, the model's exploratory capacity is hindered, and improvement decelerates. 8. Strategic approaches can mitigate the rate of entropy decrease. The report examines various optimization methods, such as clip-higher, on-policy, and entropy loss, concluding that clip-higher is the most effective.  These findings provide valuable guidelines for the advancement of relevance models.
