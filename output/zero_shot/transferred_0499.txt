In this section, we demonstrate the functionality of our interface through the example of "Picture Composition." This feature provides users with detailed information concerning the quantity of objects and events, as well as the types of connections, thereby facilitating the creation process. Figure 5.a presents a diagram relevant to picture composition, while Figure 5.b illustrates how Participant 4 (P4) constructs a narrative using TaleFrame. The process begins with P4 dragging the event "Jack shoves Ryan," the initial component of the diagram, into the workspace (Figure 6.f). Subsequently, P4 incorporates the two individuals involved in this event into the designated area (Figure 6.g). Following this, P4 establishes a connection between these individuals (Figure 6.h1), selects the line representing their interaction, and modifies the relationship parameters (Figure 6.e). These parameters include emotion type, action type, relationship strength, and the dynamics of the relationship. The "action_direction" of the relationship is indicated by the "connect node," typically directed from origin to destination (e.g., "from Jack to Ryan"). In contrast, for the event labeled "confront each other" in the subsequent segment, where the action is reciprocal, there exists a toggle labeled "is bidirectional." Activating this switch adjusts the action_direction to "bidirectional." By applying this method to all components, P4 completes the final three segments, linking the four event boxes to finalize the narrative structure (Figure 6.h2). Subsequently, P4 selects the "Modify Story" button, altering the title and description and assigning the four events to their respective components of the narrative. The narrative is then evaluated across seven dimensions according to established criteria (Figure 6.1). Detailed recommendations are provided based on feedback received (Figure 6.m). Consequently, P4 revises elements with lower scores, such as by incorporating additional connections among certain events. Thereafter, P4 employs the "Regenerate" button to produce an updated version. Ultimately, P4 utilizes the "Export" button to acquire the completed narrative, the diagram, and the corresponding JSON file.
