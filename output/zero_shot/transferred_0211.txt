In the third stage of the study, the emphasis is placed on training the model to adhere to specific instructions across various tasks within the Infinity-MM framework. A comprehensive dataset is utilized, encompassing tasks such as document comprehension, reasoning with charts, optical character recognition (OCR), and interactive dialogues. The Infinity-MM methodology assigns differential weights to these tasks: 35% for general visual question answering, 25% for document comprehension, 20% for reasoning with charts, 15% for OCR-related tasks, and 5% for dialogues and specialized domains. The training parameters are set with a learning rate of 1e-5 and the process spans one epoch with a batch size of 512. The objective is to predict subsequent words employing standard language modeling techniques.  The model, optimized for Neural Processing Units (NPU), is evaluated against contemporary vision-language models through five distinct testing protocols. Comparative analysis is conducted with recent models such as InternVL2 and Qwen2-VL, which are tailored for diverse systems and exhibit varying configurations. The AutoNeural model, upon completion of this third training stage, demonstrates a superior balance between accuracy and training velocity.  Evaluation is performed across five benchmarks, each designed to assess different competencies. MMStar evaluates the model's capability to answer multi-step visual queries requiring common sense reasoning. HallusionBench examines the model's proficiency in handling linguistic and visual ambiguities. MathVista_MINI tests the model's ability to resolve mathematical problems through the interpretation of diagrams and charts. AI2D_TEST assesses the model's comprehension of textbook science and diagrammatic representations. OCRBench evaluates the model's effectiveness in reading text from images encompassing diverse scenes, documents, and signage.  Performance metrics are collected on each benchmark, and an aggregate score is computed to ascertain the model's overall proficiency in managing a breadth of task types.
