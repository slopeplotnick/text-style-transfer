In our endeavor to enhance code reuse and expedite the delivery of new applications, we aim to transition our codebase to a superior-level Software Product Line (SPL), specifically Levels 4 or 5. To facilitate this transition, we have initiated discussions with personnel within the organization to ascertain their perspectives on SPL engineering. These dialogues are designed to address specific research questions, which will be elaborated upon subsequently. A subsequent series of interviews will be conducted at the project's conclusion to refine our migration strategy.  The initial phase of the migration strategy involves identifying potential changes, starting with the delineation of feature locations to enable the creation of feature models. We will then analyze the interaction and interdependencies of these features to develop a comprehensive understanding of the system.  The interviews were conducted to garner insights into current practices and to evaluate the perceived advantages, risks, and concerns associated with transitioning to an SPL. Research Question 1 (RQ1) seeks to understand employees' opinions regarding current best practices in software development, focusing on what is currently effective. Research Question 2 (RQ2) examines their perceptions of the potential benefits the new SPL could offer, particularly how it could enhance application development. Research Question 3 (RQ3) investigates their views on the risks associated with adopting the new SPL, following discussions on its advantages. Research Question 4 (RQ4) explores the apprehensions stemming from these risks, aiming to comprehend the concerns employees may have regarding the migration. These apprehensions include potential pitfalls, challenges that may arise, and the necessity for strategic planning and risk management.
