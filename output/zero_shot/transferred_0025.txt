The project focuses on the integration of new features and updates within the framework, addressing technological advancements and agricultural improvements. The primary objective is to expedite development processes, such as reducing the time required to create new applications relative to previous projects. This endeavor integrates concepts such as code reuse, innovation, and rapid development. Initially, the project commenced as a reusable codebase, facilitating the migration project. Through analysis, seven effective practices were identified, with Factorization and Reuse being the most prevalent, occurring nine and eight times, respectively, as illustrated in Figure 1. Understanding these benefits is crucial as it serves to motivate the team. Customization emerges as a significant component of Software Product Lines (SPL), frequently cited by IT, UX, and agriculture specialists. An SPL is perceived as a platform capable of generating customized software from pre-existing components. The second most frequently mentioned concept is speed, as noted by IT, Product Owners (PO), and UX professionals, highlighting the swift development of new software. Automation, which involves the automatic creation of software, is also highlighted, with IT and PO professionals viewing it in conjunction with speed. Industrialization, discussed by IT and UX experts, pertains to the ability to produce applications on demand and cost-effectively. The improvement of code quality, solely referenced by IT personnel, emphasizes the enhancement of consistency, testability, and reliability in code. The strategic advantage of increased sales, as noted by IT and PO participants, involves the ability to market more software or respond promptly to market opportunities. These seven identified benefits are depicted in Figure 2, with customization, cited ten times, being the most frequently discussed concept, followed by speed.
