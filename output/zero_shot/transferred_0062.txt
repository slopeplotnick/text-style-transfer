Adopting a Software Product Line (SPL) approach necessitates substantial organizational transformation. The complexity inherent in managing software products has been documented in a survey conducted by Springer and Miler. Within the SPL framework, collaborative efforts among teams are emphasized, involving the utilization of a shared set of tools and resources across various projects. This strategy facilitates time and resource optimization, accelerates the time-to-market for products, and enables teams to concentrate on innovation. Key elements such as component reuse, configuration-based customization, and comprehensive documentation contribute to smoother, more efficient operations. The transition to SPL requires meticulous planning and management; however, it ultimately enhances organizational cohesiveness and adaptability.  At ITK, discussions were held with the development team regarding outcomes and future plans. Their desire to remain informed and integral to the transition process led to close collaboration throughout the migration. A comparative analysis of ITK’s experiences with other documented SPL adoption strategies reveals that ITK’s approach aligns with the IDEAL improvement model. The initiative commenced with a decisive step by the team to pursue migration, beginning with an evaluation of existing practices and project perceptions. Subsequent stages involve formulating a strategic action plan, its implementation, and applying agile methodologies to iteratively refine processes based on feedback and results. The guidelines developed serve to structure the action plan effectively. Challenges encountered during this transition differed from those reported elsewhere, notably the absence of issues such as insufficient skilled personnel or lack of managerial support, due to the company's prevailing mindset, which did not perceive these factors as problematic.
