Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) was employed using the Qwen-1.5 model as the foundational framework to enhance the model's general linguistic capabilities and adapt them to e-commerce-specific tasks. To facilitate domain-specific learning, knowledge graphs were utilized alongside carefully constructed prompts that incorporated e-commerce-related information, enabling the model to comprehend domain-specific patterns and relationships. The model's performance was evaluated through standardized assessments and relevance metrics to ensure accuracy and its ability to address diverse query types effectively. After the training phase, the model was leveraged for labeling tasks, yielding improvements in efficiency and data quality by reducing errors and enhancing the overall labeling process. Additionally, the model demonstrated its capacity to address complex tasks, such as interpreting intricate queries, identifying implicit meanings, and performing logical reasoning for constructing pathways. Given the intricacy of these tasks, simple instructions were insufficient, necessitating the implementation of the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) methodology. CoT enabled the model to systematically decompose problems, analyze relationships, and compare pathways step-by-step, significantly improving its performance in handling challenging queries. Furthermore, a diagnostic framework was developed to identify difficult cases and iteratively refine the model through user feedback. Subsequently, CoT data was utilized for tasks such as attribute extraction and matching, followed by retraining with the SFT methodology using the Qwen-2 model. This iterative approach enhanced the model's reasoning capabilities while maintaining a task-specific focus.
