A Python script was implemented in Jupyter Notebook to analyze word frequency and assess lexical similarity within textual data. Following the extraction and sorting of words based on their frequency within the analyzed book, key terms were manually selected for deeper analysis. The dataset and corresponding results are accessible online at [https://cite.lirmm.fr/tgeorges/interviewartefact](https://cite.lirmm.fr/tgeorges/interviewartefact), which includes interview questions and responses, an analytical overview, topic lists, the script notebook, and visual representations, such as bar charts, illustrating response distributions. This study specifically addresses findings related to each research question, including current best practices, identified benefits, risks, and apprehensions regarding the future of Software Product Line (SPL) methodologies.   Table 3 presents a quantitative summary of the identified practices, benefits, risks, and fears that were subsequently subjected to detailed analysis. Results are organized according to topics and roles, adhering to the design process approach. The distribution of participant responses was relatively consistent across varying levels of professional experience. Notably, responses did not show significant variation based on years of professional experience; for instance, developers with 4 and 7 years of experience provided similar insights when questioned about good practices derivable from the proposed approach. Bar charts illustrating these response patterns, categorized by years of professional experience, are included in the online repository linked in the article.   The interviews aimed to identify current best practices considered essential for inclusion in the proposed methodology. The most frequently cited practice was "Factorization and Reuse," which was reinforced by team discussions where new features were evaluated for their applicability across multiple applications. Additionally, "Innovation" emerged as a prominent theme, reflecting the team's commitment to maintaining contemporary skills and fostering technological progress. This focus on innovation allows participants to regularly update the codebase, ensuring the approach remains adaptable to evolving industry standards.
