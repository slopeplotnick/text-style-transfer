We developed a system designed to identify conspicuous errors that are easily recognizable, rather than to conduct in-depth scientific analysis or assess kinematic details. Our dataset is primarily focused on complex physical phenomena and scientific principles. Consequently, utilizing specific lists for deconstructing physics yields superior outcomes compared to employing general explanations, highlighting the necessity for specialized methodologies to evaluate intricate physical reasoning in video production. VideoScore2 is unsuitable for our evaluation due to its reliance on rigorous scientific validation, which does not adequately align with perceptions regarding this dataset.
