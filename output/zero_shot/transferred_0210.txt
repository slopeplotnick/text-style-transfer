The methodology section delineates the participant selection, interview setup, execution, and data analysis processes. Participants were strategically chosen to represent diverse groups, encompassing varying roles, levels of experience, and educational backgrounds within the project. The roles included Product Owners, Developers, Agronomists, and User Interface/Experience Designers. The educational qualifications of these participants ranged from engineering degrees to doctorates. Selection criteria also considered the participants' varying degrees of experience with the project. The final sample comprised 16 individuals: nine Developers (current or former project contributors), two Agronomists, three Product Owners, and two User Interface/Experience Designers, all actively involved in the project. Table 1 provides a detailed breakdown of the educational backgrounds and experience levels for each role. Ensuring participant motivation to provide meaningful responses was paramount. Participants were informed that their feedback would contribute to optimizing the Software Product Line (SPL) process, which could be advantageous for their roles. Structured interviews were selected, featuring a standardized set of questions. The questions adhered to best practices, utilizing clear and precise language, and each question focused on a singular concept. Sensitive topics, queries about historical events, and questions beyond the participants’ expertise were deliberately avoided, contributing to an actor-driven interview approach. The interviews were structured into three segments: an introductory segment for personal introductions, a main segment divided into two sub-sections addressing the current process and potential modifications, and a concluding section that provided an overview of the interview.
