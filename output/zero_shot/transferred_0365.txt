The platform developed by ITK has been operational for approximately five to six years. Utilizing the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK's platform is classified as a "Level 3: Software Platform," characterized by a standardized configuration applicable across all products. Presently, the configuration encompasses both shared components and specific allocations for new versions. The objective is to delineate the commonalities and variances within the domain, thereby establishing a uniform configuration for the entire software product line. This entails the strategic reuse of components from a parts library, distinct differentiation of these components, and the implementation of a standardized configuration that governs permissible modifications within application setups, while incorporating numerous quality solutions within the software product line. The management of variances is systematically addressed within the software product line configuration, delineating permissible setups for applications and specifying the scope and constraints of allowable modifications, including the imposition of rules that specific versions must adhere to. The primary aim is to furnish an automated software setup that facilitates the generation of diverse versions. According to the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK is positioned at the fourth level at the organizational scale, indicating a collaborative integration and coordination of domain engineering and application engineering. The project benefits from robust collaboration between agronomists and software developers. The decision to transition and collaborate with the research team was the result of a collective discussion among project leaders and stakeholders. Prior to commenced planning for interviews, a definitive research methodology for the remainder of the project had not been established. Initial efforts were directed toward comprehending the perspectives of company personnel regarding the transition to Software Product Line (SPL) architecture. Based on insights garnered from the interviews, a definitive methodology for the migration project will be subsequently developed.
