The transition to increased reliance on electric energy has intensified the focus on the exploration of essential minerals. Despite significant demand, profitability in mineral discovery remains elusive. Over the past two decades, investment in mineral exploration has increased, yet the rate of new discoveries has diminished. This paper proposes the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to enhance the scientific rigor of mineral exploration, reduce errors, and lower costs. The current methodologies employed in mineral exploration, encompassing disciplines such as geology and geophysics, have proven insufficient for identifying covert mineral deposits effectively. A major drawback of existing methods is their inadequate assessment of uncertainty, which results in suboptimal decision-making and financial losses in drilling operations that frequently yield insufficient returns.  This paper advocates for a novel approach grounded in Bayesianism and the principle of falsification, emphasizing data collection as a means to critically evaluate human hypotheses. The decision-making process concerning subsequent data acquisition is guided by explicit metrics and informed decisions. A practical framework is provided, applicable to diverse mineral exploration scenarios. The successful implementation of this framework necessitates the deployment of various AI technologies, particularly innovative unsupervised learning methods that collaborate with domain experts to enhance data interpretation and generate alternative geological hypotheses. Additionally, human-in-the-loop AI systems are pivotal in orchestrating data collection efforts in geology, geophysics, geochemistry, and drilling, thereby diminishing uncertainty regarding geological hypotheses prior to addressing the quantity and quality of mineral deposits. The efficacy of this approach will be demonstrated through its application in contemporary mineral exploration and resource evaluation case studies.
