This case study examines the challenges in assessing the risk associated with an individual's suicidal ideation. Specifically, it addresses an error made by the Menta model in classifying a supportive Reddit post as indicative of problematic behavior. This misclassification arose due to the post's considerable length and the presence of formatting anomalies, such as unusual symbols, HTML elements, and placeholder terms like "Hyperactive behavior," which misled the model. The content of the post was, in fact, a message encouraging the recipient to persevere for the sake of their younger siblings and to continue efforts to prevent paternal abuse. Furthermore, the post advised the recipient to disregard disparaging comments from trolls and affirmed that the commenter did not perceive them as worthless or deserving of self-harm. The model's erroneous behavior classification appeared to be influenced by themes of excessive burden and difficulties related to academic and social environments. However, the individual described learning to set boundaries by declining excessive responsibilities, which contributed to their improved well-being and social integration.
