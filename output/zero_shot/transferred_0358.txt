DeepSeek-V3.2 demonstrates comparable performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across a range of reasoning assessments. It significantly enhances the capability of open models in task execution, particularly excelling in specialized agent tasks as outlined by EvalSys and other studies conducted in 2025. Serving as a cost-effective alternative for agent tasks, DeepSeek-V3.2 narrows the performance disparity between open-source and leading private models, while remaining economically viable. To advance the reasoning capabilities of open models, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale was developed by extending length limits, achieving parity with the top-tier closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro, and demonstrating outstanding performance in competitions such as IOI, ICPC World Final, IMO, and CMO in 2025.  DeepSeek-V3.2 retains the foundational architecture of DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, with the principal innovation over the previous iteration, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, being the integration of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) and additional training. The DSA mechanism comprises two core components: a rapid indexer and a comprehensive token selection system. Beginning with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which features an extended context length of 128K, the development of DeepSeek-V3.2 involved further training across two phases utilizing datasets akin to those used for DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp was evaluated against various benchmarks and compared with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, yielding analogous results. Importantly, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp exhibits superior proficiency in managing long sequences efficiently, maintaining performance levels relative to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus across both short and long-duration tasks.
