In this study, we present Story2MIDI, a sequence-to-sequence Transformer-based model engineered to generate music that aligns with the emotional nuances of a given text. To develop this model, we assembled the Story2MIDI dataset by integrating existing datasets focused on sentiment analysis in text and emotional classification in music. Consequently, the dataset comprises pairs of brief text excerpts and musical compositions that are intended to evoke similar emotional reactions from the reader or listener. Despite the challenges posed by our dataset's limited scale and constrained computational resources, our findings suggest that the model effectively discerns emotion-relevant features in music and incorporates these into its generative process, resulting in compositions with varied emotional responses. We assessed the outputs using objective musical metrics, complemented by a human listening study, which verified the model's proficiency in capturing the intended emotional cues. We exist in an era characterized by an escalating demand for entertainment and multimedia content, a trend significantly accelerated by the proliferation of social media and streaming platforms for music, audiobooks, and podcasts. Central to many forms of this entertainment is the narrative, a story that propels the experience, whether it is embedded in a film, a game, a podcast, or a documentary. Narratives are potent in their ability to evoke emotion; as audiences engage with them, they frequently embark on a dynamic emotional journey influenced by the characters, plot developments, and underlying themes. This emotional progression within a narrative is crucial to the enduring impact stories have on audiences. Similarly, music is renowned for its capacity to influence listeners' emotional states and is widely acknowledged as a powerful medium of emotional expression. Therefore, creative producers often incorporate background music into narratives to enhance audience connection and augment the overall engagement of the experience. With the swift advancements in artificial intelligence across various modalities, novel models are being continuously developed, enabling the generation of diverse content forms and thereby broadening the capabilities of automated systems in creative endeavors.
