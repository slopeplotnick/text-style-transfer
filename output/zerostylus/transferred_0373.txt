This paper seeks to elucidate the recent advancements in large language models (LLMs), which integrate task-specific functionalities with open-ended conversational systems, thereby enhancing the dialogue capabilities within artificial intelligence frameworks. In particular, this section of the tutorial examines technological advancements, providing illustrative examples of potential future trajectories. We, therefore, explore approaches such as developing novel LLMs trained on conversational data, designing dialogue systems that are trained end-to-end, and enabling dialogues to refine themselves through self-practice. Moreover, the development of chat systems that integrate information from a variety of sources, including web pages, lists, forms, tables, scholarly articles with figures and equations, as well as documents accessible online, represents another significant concept for the future. In addition to incorporating vision, their approach encompasses the modeling of knowledge, emotion, and communicative expressions, including facial movements and gestures.
