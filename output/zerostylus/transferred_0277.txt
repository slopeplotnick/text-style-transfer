System and Strategy Updates: The integration of advanced AI mechanisms enables significant upgrades to both online strategies and system configurations. From a strategic perspective, the enhanced model incorporates more reliable query-item relevance scores into the downstream ranking module, optimizing performance through a trade-off derived using Pareto optimization principles. Moreover, supported by the model's strong performance, previously employed heuristic rules are systematically retired or replaced, leading to notable improvements in system efficacy. The cumulative effect of these modifications has demonstrated substantial gains in performance while maintaining a manageable level of resource expenditure. For instance, research such as DeepSeek-R1 has identified emergent phenomena, including the model's ability to engage in long chains-of-thought and its occasional "Aha Moments." Similarly, we conducted a dynamic analysis of the evolution of model output length during the reinforcement learning process, with results depicted in Fig. 17. These findings indicate that as model proficiency improves, output length tends to decrease and eventually stabilizes, suggesting that extensive reasoning steps are not essential for model capability enhancement but are rather an accompanying phenomenon during training. Further analysis reveals that reasoning length requirements differ significantly across task types. For example, mathematical and programming tasks often necessitate detailed, stepwise reasoning processes. In contrast, tasks such as identifying user intent and aligning it with appropriate items benefit from straightforward and concise reasoning approaches. Redundant reasoning in such cases fails to improve performance, underscoring the importance of tailoring reasoning processes to task-specific demands. Additionally, image-related tasks are more effectively addressed by Vision Language Models (VLMs), which inherently possess visual comprehension capabilities and thus eliminate the need for additional processes such as caption generation. To explore this approach, we employed the teacher model Qwen2.5-VL-72B to sequentially perform two key steps: generating chains-of-thought for knowledge and reasoning and subsequently applying rules in a structured manner.
