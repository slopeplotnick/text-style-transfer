LLaMA-Factory primarily focuses on addressing text-generation tasks and does not emphasize the possibility of reformulating these challenges into classification-based frameworks. To overcome the limited number of available PEFT methods and facilitate the adaptation of large language models to sorting tasks, we introduce PEFT-FACTORY as a solution. However, there are alternative configurations for training large language models that, while not as user-friendly as LLaMA-Factory, present distinct advantages. FastChat is optimal for the training of large-scale models aimed at conversational applications. Additionally, frameworks such as LitGPT and LMFlow are broadly adaptable to various generative models and training methodologies. Furthermore, Axolotl is extensively utilized in the terminal for the effective post-training of large-scale models, maintaining functional integrity and scalability. In essence, Open-Instruct centers on the refinement of instructional methodologies for large-scale models and introduces a range of model variations and approaches to achieve this objective. Since its introduction, H2O LLM Studio has emerged as a business-oriented tool featuring a graphical interface for the development and deployment of large-scale models. Moreover, GPT4All provides a user-friendly interface designed to enhance the efficiency of training processes around LLaMA, facilitating a more accessible experience for users. ColossalAI focuses on delivering a framework for fine-tuning across multiple nodes for distributed computing. Additionally, LLaMA-Adapter and LLaMA-Accessory constitute more lightweight configurations, with LLaMA-Adapter incorporating trainable adapters into LLaMA models, while LLaMA-Accessory provides a comprehensive toolkit for the development of large language models. Moreover, LLaMA-Adapter is frequently employed within configurations such as LitGPT, providing enhanced adaptability for model setups. Our work addresses three key limitations observed in related approaches: enabling the training of large models using techniques beyond reparameterization-based PEFT methods, introducing new PEFT methods in a modular and dynamic framework, and facilitating the training and evaluation of large models for sorting task applications.
