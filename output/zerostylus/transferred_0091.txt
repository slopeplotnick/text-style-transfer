To evaluate the efficacy of pre-training, we examine how closely the token type distributions in the generated samples align with those in the original pre-training data. Specifically, we analyze the occurrence of each token type within the pre-training dataset and compare these with their occurrence in generated outputs at various stages of training. Notably, samples generated at the 250-epoch stage exhibit distributions that most closely resemble those observed in the original dataset. While the reduction in training and validation losses persists until 300 epochs, the fidelity of the generated samples to the original data diminishes, as policy entropy declines and the entropy regularization loss comes to dominate over time. Therefore, we select the 250 epochs stage for fine-tuning on EMOPIA to effectively balance the reduction in loss while maintaining fidelity to the original dataset. Moreover, to assess whether the decoder can generate MIDI using AV quadrants, we conducted an experiment where quadrant labels were provided directly as input, replacing the text embeddings typically produced by the encoder. This method demonstrated an ability to produce MIDI samples that closely aligned with EMOPIA's valence-arousal metrics, effectively reflecting its intended emotional and arousal characteristics, as discussed in Section IV-B. Furthermore, Section IV-A outlines the procedure employed in conducting a listening test with four participants, and the perceptions are summarized in Table I. The model's music was reported to align more closely with the arousal dimension, achieving an accuracy of 0.70, compared to its alignment with valence, which had an accuracy of 0.53, or with both valence and arousal, which demonstrated a 0.40 accuracy. In general, one would anticipate an accuracy of 0.5 for either valence or arousal, whereas an accuracy of 0.25 is expected when considering both dimensions simultaneously. Consequently, the performance of the model's music exceeded that of the baseline metrics, particularly in terms of arousal (0.70) and the combined valence-arousal dimensions (0.40). Participants noted that the emotional tone conveyed by the music aligned with the sentiment of the text to a degree that exceeded random chance. A larger sample size would enhance the reliability of the results and enable an examination of statistical significance. Therefore, particular attention should be directed towards incorporating a broader spectrum of listeners in subsequent research endeavors to ascertain whether these preliminary findings remain consistent.
