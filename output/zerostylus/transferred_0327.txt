Following the training phase, we developed a comprehensive and challenging evaluation benchmark designed to rigorously assess model capabilities (Section 3.5). Recognizing the advanced capabilities of large language models (LLMs) and the inherent diversity of e-commerce data, the evaluation benchmark was carefully designed to ensure sample comprehensiveness while incorporating targeted hard sample mining, aligned with the core competencies outlined in Section 2. To establish the foundations of this evaluation, we conducted a series of preliminary steps, including feature construction, model selection, and prompt optimization, as illustrated in Fig. 6. Guided by the theoretical framework introduced in Section 2, effective relevance modeling requires a systematic construction of the attribute space for each item. Consequently, we selected features that enable the model to perceive and reason across both visual and textual modalities. To address the need for visual attributes, such as stylistic details, cuts, and patterns, the item's Main Product Image was utilized as a key input. This visual component plays a crucial role for queries heavily reliant on image-based information, where textual descriptions alone may prove insufficient. Furthermore, to enrich the representation of item attributes, we incorporated category-property-value (CPV) triples and Stock-keeping-unit (SKU) identifiers. However, CPV data often contains redundant information that is irrelevant for relevance judgment. To mitigate this issue, we introduced a filtering pipeline that combines domain-specific prior knowledge and the capabilities of LLMs. Specifically, CPV data is presented as key-value pairs, with shared keys frequently observed among similar products. For instance, both "eye cream" and "face cream" products typically share the key "brand." Therefore, we conducted statistical analyses on a large volume of product data to identify medium-to-high frequency keys. Subsequently, the powerful LLM Qwen3-235B-Instruct was prompted with the relevance task definition, the attributes under consideration, and each key to evaluate whether each attribute contributes to relevance. To ensure the accuracy of the model's judgments, this process was repeated across eight independent iterations, retaining only those attributes for which the model consistently affirmed "true." This iterative approach enhances the reliability of the filtered attribute set, thereby improving the overall quality of relevance modeling.
