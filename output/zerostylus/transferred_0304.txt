**Refined Paragraph:** Datasets. In line with established methodologies (Zeng et al. 2025; Liu et al. 2025; Wu et al. 2025), we have curated a collection of 5,500 Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset (Hendrycks et al. 2021), incorporating insights from prior research. Evaluation Benchmarks. We conduct performance evaluations on a range of benchmarks, including AIME 2024 (Li et al. 2024a), AMC (Li et al. 2024a), MATH500 (Hendrycks et al. 2021), GSM8K (Cobbe et al. 2021), Minerva (Lewkowycz et al. 2022), OlympiadBench (He et al. 2024), and College Math (Tang et al. 2024). Furthermore, for out-of-domain evaluation, ARC-C (Clark et al. 2018) and GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al. 2024) are also included. Inference is performed through greedy decoding, adhering to a token limit of 3,000 (Liu et al. 2025; Wu et al. 2025). Baselines. We assess CAPO using four principal reinforcement learning algorithms: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. For each baseline, we additionally evaluate its CAPO-enhanced variant, allowing a direct comparison of CAPO's contribution as an enhancement tool. This setup enables a thorough investigation of both the standalone efficacy of the baselines and the improvements facilitated by CAPO integration. Implementation. To ensure fairness and consistency across different baseline algorithms and CAPO variants, we standardize the implementation details. Given that each method necessitates unique parameter settings, comprehensive training configurations are outlined in Appendix B. Datasets. We utilize the GUI-R1-3K dataset (Luo et al. 2025), derived from OS-Atlas (Wu et al. 2024b), which encompasses 3,000 examples of vision-language-action across web and mobile platforms. This dataset aligns visual states with natural language instructions and action sequences, serving as a diverse testbed for evaluating various reasoning and planning methodologies. Evaluation Benchmarks. For planning, the configurations include GUI-Act-Web (Luo et al. 2025), OmniAct-Web (Kapoor et al. 2024), and AndroidControl-Low/High (Li et al. 2024b). In terms of perception, Screenshot Pro (Li et al. 2025) is employed. Baselines. We undertake a comparative analysis of GRPO and its CAPO-augmented variant using the primary GUI benchmark configuration (Wu et al. 2024b). This supplementary evaluation underscores CAPO's adaptability in multimodal reasoning contexts, extending its application beyond the realm of mathematical reasoning tasks.
