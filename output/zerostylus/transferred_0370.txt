When engaged in Referring Expression Comprehension (REC), the task involves identifying an entity based on a description articulated in colloquial language and is a pivotal area in vision-language research. Recent advancements have shown that individuals have enhanced their abilities not merely by aligning words but by comprehending their significance and employing supplementary knowledge. Generalized Referring Expression Comprehension (GREC), which involves interpreting descriptions that may correspond to no objects or multiple objects, such as "Pick up those three boxes," extends these principles to better emulate real-world communication dynamics, where the referent or intended meaning may remain ambiguous or entirely absent. However, investigating these dynamics necessitates a considerable quantity of authentic images with descriptive annotations, posing significant financial challenges for researchers. To address this, online platforms provide a cost-effective and efficient means for testing innovative concepts. In scenarios where models must analyze a scene, maintain dialogue continuity, and engage in reciprocal communication, current methodologies encounter difficulties. A recently proposed study introduced a novel GREC test within a Minecraft gaming environment, demonstrating that this task poses significant challenges for existing AI models. The research indicates that generating conversational datasets requires substantial financial resources, resulting in a scarcity of training data. Consequently, we propose an innovative framework that generates additional data by synthesizing concise descriptions and comprehensive dialogues, thereby enhancing AI's comprehension capabilities. In prior research, models have been implemented to generate data using AI; however, applying this methodology to GREC presents challenges, as it necessitates an understanding of spatial relationships and their connection to visual perception. Nonetheless, these challenges underscore the importance of developing frameworks that can effectively integrate spatial and linguistic data.
