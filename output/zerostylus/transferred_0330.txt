An overview of participants' characteristics is presented in Table B.3 in the Appendix. The analysis revealed no significant differences in participant characteristics across the experimental groups. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups: (1) In the first group, participants were informed that the music excerpts they would hear were composed by artificial intelligence (AI); (2) in the second group, participants were told that the music excerpts originated from movie soundtracks; and (3) in the third group, participants were instructed that they would hear music composed by both humans and AI and were required to identify the composer for each piece. Prior to the music rating, participants in Groups 1 and 3 received explanations concerning AI and its use in music tools. Subsequently, after receiving the specific instructions, participants were tasked with evaluating 18 music excerpts, which were randomly selected to ensure a balance across subgenres and AI-generated elements. Before listening to the first song, participants were instructed to adjust the volume to a comfortable level and familiarize themselves with all the emotion-related terms. Although the order of terms varied randomly among participants, it remained consistent across all songs for each individual. Participants were further instructed to rate the emotions they personally experienced, rather than those they believed the music conveyed. Each music excerpt was presented twice. During the initial presentation, participants were instructed to listen attentively without responding. Subsequently, the rating questions were displayed, and the music excerpt was repeated to minimize potential memory-related biases in participants' evaluations. Following the completion of music ratings, participants proceeded to complete a questionnaire, providing information on their personality traits, musical preferences, and socio-demographic background. Furthermore, participants' attitudes toward artificial intelligence were assessed. After completing the questionnaires, participants viewed a debrief screen, which included a description of the study and the intended use of their data. Participants were then invited to share additional thoughts on AI-generated music, as well as their overall experience of the study, through an open-ended question. The survey concluded with the presentation of individualized feedback, aiming to uncover subjective insights and themes from their personal reflections.
