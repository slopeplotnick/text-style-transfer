The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence, particularly in its capacity for language processing, is significantly enhancing its ability to generate complex narratives. Specifically, the creation of intricate storylines has become a central focus of development efforts, with the objective of constructing narratives that are coherent and engaging, contingent upon the input provided. In future endeavors, it is anticipated that challenges such as maintaining narrative consistency, enhancing character development, and refining narrative control mechanisms will be progressively addressed. This will be done to ensure a more coherent and engaging storytelling experience. However, ensuring that the narrative aligns with user expectations while maintaining coherence in the plot, character development, and structural integrity presents a significant challenge in story generation. To address this, we established a set of critical evaluative principles. These principles ensure the coherence and alignment of narrative elements with user expectations, emphasizing the importance of maintaining logical consistency and fostering a balance between believability and creative expression. Furthermore, large-scale language models, such as GPT-4 and Llama-3, have demonstrated significant proficiency in generating high-quality text. Nevertheless, due to their training on broadly generalized datasets, they exhibit limitations in producing narratives that are sufficiently controlled and structurally coherent. This observation holds particular significance in scenarios where users require granular details and a well-defined narrative framework to ensure clarity and coherence in the storytelling process. Therefore, future work will focus on enhancing model capabilities to effectively regulate narrative generation in alignment with user expectations while simultaneously fostering creativity, thereby underscoring the significance of this endeavor. To enhance the efficacy of story generation, fine-tuning these models and employing structured prompts serve as promising approaches to optimize their performance. This is particularly beneficial as it enhances the quality of the narratives and ensures that the models exhibit greater stability and reliability when addressing complex tasks. Moreover, integrating fine-tuning with structured input is instrumental in enhancing the predictability and consistency of narratives, thereby addressing control-related challenges during the generation process.
