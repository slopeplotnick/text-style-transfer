The methodology section is structured to encompass the presentation of participants (Sect. 3.1), the design of the interviews (Sect. 3.2), the procedures for conducting them (Sect. 3.3), and the approach utilized for analyzing the responses (Sect. 3.4). To ensure a representative sample, participants were carefully selected to reflect a diverse population [KP08]. Specifically, selection criteria included the consideration of distinct roles within the project, varying levels of experience, and diverse educational backgrounds. The four primary roles represented in the project are Product Owners (PO), Developers (IT), Agronomists (AGRO), and User Interface/Experience (UX) designers. Furthermore, participants exhibit varying levels of educational attainment, with individuals either holding an engineering degree or a PhD. Moreover, the selection process accounted for participants' differing levels of experience within the project. Ultimately, a total of 16 participants were chosen to ensure representativeness: nine Developers who are either currently involved in or have previously contributed to the project, two Agronomists, three Product Owners, and two UX designers, all of whom are actively engaged in the project. Motivating participants to provide relevant and informative responses was a critical consideration [KP08]. In this regard, participants were informed that they would receive feedback on their responses and that their input would contribute to the development of an effective strategy for implementing the SPL process. Structured interviews were employed, with a predefined set of questions. To enhance clarity and precision, the interview design adhered to the primary recommendations outlined in [KP08], ensuring the use of unambiguous and straightforward language. Each question was designed to address a single concept, avoiding sensitive topics, references to past events, and questions beyond the respondent’s scope of knowledge. Additionally, actor-driven interviews were integrated into the methodology to address the specific research inquiries outlined in the subsequent section. The interview questionnaire was organized into three main sections to facilitate logical progression. The first section served as an introduction, allowing interviewees to present themselves. The main section, divided into two subsections, focused first on the current process and then on the proposed changes. Finally, the concluding section provided an overall summary of the interview, allowing for a holistic discussion of the responses.
