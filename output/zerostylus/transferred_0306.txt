Our development of Menta signifies a substantial advancement in the design of specialized models for predicting mental health issues through social media text analysis. The model is inherently designed to facilitate precision and user-friendliness, thus enabling systematic and effortless operation across diverse applications. Importantly, Menta employs a training methodology that effectively addresses data imbalance and demonstrates versatility across a range of clinical applications. Moreover, it maintains an adept balance between accuracy and efficiency, surpassing certain alternative models and rivaling large language models across six mental health tasks. Its design further facilitates ease of use and sharing, incorporating features that ensure privacy and minimize resource consumption. Historically, the evaluation of mental health through social media platforms relied on conventional machine learning techniques and models requiring extensive manual configuration. However, the utilization of sophisticated word models by Jiang et al. has enhanced the identification of mental health concerns within the Reddit platform, demonstrating superior efficacy over traditional methodologies. Similarly, Sarkar et al. developed a model utilizing shared features to forecast depression and anxiety from Reddit posts more effectively than models concentrating on singular aspects. In alignment with this approach, recent research has developed deep learning-based tools to assess suicide risk, facilitating proactive intervention strategies. Furthermore, to evaluate recent applications within this domain, we employ large language models as instrumental tools. For instance, a study utilized ChatGPT to analyze and categorize mental health concerns, including stress, depression, and suicidality, based on social media content. This illustrates the model's capacity to infer psychological states without requiring prior contextual familiarity. The detection of depression through online text has become increasingly sophisticated, with contemporary models demonstrating superior performance compared to their predecessors, particularly when fine-tuned. This is particularly relevant as it demonstrates that incorporating prompts with emotional cues and illustrative examples can enhance the ability of these models to interpret depressive symptoms and emotional states effectively.
