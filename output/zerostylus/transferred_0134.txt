The advent of AI agents has facilitated autonomous reasoning, strategic planning, and effective tool usage. Recently, the introduction of medical agents capable of executing complex medical tasks has been observed. For instance, MMedAgent utilizes a diverse array of tools to manage medical tasks across various modalities, including the generation of radiology reports for chest X-rays. MedRAX, on the other hand, is specifically designed for chest X-ray interpretation, integrating advanced analysis tools. Similarly, CT-Agent demonstrates proficiency in interpreting 3D CT scans and enhances reasoning capabilities. However, these agents often rely on a singular tool for radiology report generation, resulting in suboptimal performance due to limited inter-tool collaboration. In contrast, the Radiologist Copilot employs a comprehensive set of integrated tools that enable rich collaboration and support more complex planning and execution processes. The drafting of radiology reports is crucial yet time-consuming and prone to errors, particularly with 3D imaging. Furthermore, rigorous quality control, a critical step in ensuring clinical qualification of reports, is often neglected in previous research. To address these issues, we introduce the Radiologist Copilot, an AI assistant equipped with a coordinated set of tools for automated radiology reporting and quality assessment, aiming to support radiologists and improve operational efficiency. The system is designed to assist radiologists throughout the entire radiology reporting process. Initially, the Tool Library is initialized, followed by the systematic utilization of the Action Planner and Executor to formulate and execute tasks. The system iteratively monitors actions and their outcomes, enabling the verification of task completion. The Action Executor generates the appropriate command for the selected tool, executes it, and obtains the resultant outcome of the action, thereby ensuring the completion of planned tasks.
