This tutorial is specifically designed for individuals engaged in the study or application of conversational agents and spoken information systems. We assume that participants possess foundational knowledge in natural language processing and speech processing. While prior experience in system deployment is advantageous, the instructional material will be introduced at a gradual pace to ensure comprehensive understanding for all attendees. The tutorial draws upon a collection of pertinent academic works, including notable papers such as the tagging and recognition of conversational speech (Stolcke et al., 2000), the nuanced nature of spoken language and its implications for human-machine interaction (Moore, 2017), methodologies for instruction and feedback in task-oriented dialogue systems (Liu et al., 2018), the application of acoustic models for time series classification (Yang et al., 2021), the integration of generative approaches in speech recognition utilizing large-scale language models (Chen et al., 2023a), and the exploration of SpeechGPT in facilitating cross-modal conversational capabilities (Zhang et al., 2023). Although numerous academic papers will be referenced throughout the tutorial, emphasis will be placed on 7-8 key works, of which only 1-2 are authored by the presenter.
