**Refined Paragraph:** Data sampling. Drawing upon Taobao search logs, we conceptualize the task of data categorization as organizing information into distinct industry sectors, such as consumer electronics and apparel. To ensure representativeness, stratified sampling is conducted in proportion to the distribution of each industry, capturing key attributes and features across all industry categories. Subsequently, for each sector, the data is further divided into high-, medium-, and low-frequency categories based on query frequency. Representative samples are selected from these frequency-based strata, ensuring a balanced dataset that encompasses both commonly searched high-frequency queries and infrequent long-tail queries. This process guarantees comprehensive coverage of diverse query types. Data annotation and cleaning. Following data sampling, the selected data is assigned to domain experts for annotation, generating labeled datasets for downstream applications. However, errors in manual annotation primarily arise from two sources. First, annotators occasionally overlook critical details, particularly in long and complex multi-attribute queries. Second, ambiguities in the annotation rules contribute to inconsistencies, as the subjective nature of user preferences often leads to edge cases that existing guidelines fail to address comprehensively. To mitigate these issues, robust language models are employed for data filtering. Specifically, the attribute matching model adheres to strict matching criteria and demonstrates superior fine-grained feature capture capabilities compared to manual annotation, particularly in multi-attribute scenarios. Moreover, by embedding comprehensive annotation rules into model prompts, the system is guided to perform reasoning and judgment in alignment with instructional guidelines. This approach effectively identifies and excludes corner cases that fall outside the scope of predefined rules. Model design and reasoning. To further enhance data filtration and reasoning, two specialized models are employed. One model is optimized for processing extended textual content, leveraging its long-context comprehension capabilities, while the other is tailored for multimodal data interpretation. To achieve a systematic and in-depth reasoning process, we propose a progressive chain-of-thought (CoT) synthesis pipeline, as illustrated in Fig. 8. This pipeline employs a stagewise construction strategy comprising three progressive stages: Knowledge Injection and Reasoning, Multimodal Comprehension, and Rule Awareness. The Knowledge Injection and Reasoning stage forms the foundation of the Path Construction phase by decomposing user preferences into attributes that can be directly compared with item characteristics. Subsequently, the Multimodal Comprehension and Rule Awareness stages constitute the Path Following phase, where the alignment between item attributes and query requirements is rigorously evaluated based on item-specific details and established discrimination rules. This sequential framework ensures a robust and systematic evaluation process capable of addressing complex scenarios.
