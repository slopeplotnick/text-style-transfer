In order to evaluate model performance in mathematical problem-solving, we employ a comprehensive suite of five benchmarks: AMC, Minerva (Lewkowycz et al., 2022), MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021), and Olympiad-Bench (He et al., 2024). To semantically verify the correctness of responses, we utilize GPT-4o as a programmatic judge, adhering to methodologies established in prior studies. For general reasoning capabilities, we assess model performance across disciplines such as physics, biology, business, economics, and law, using benchmarks including MMLU-Pro, SuperGPQA, GPQA-Diamond, and BBEH. These assessments are more challenging and thus facilitate a stricter evaluation of the models' proficiency in complex reasoning. We determine accuracy using the Exact Match method, evaluating alignment with expected answers, and results are obtained via greedy decoding. To assess the significance of various components within the Qwen3-8B-Base model, we conduct ablation studies by selectively deactivating specific features to evaluate their impact on performance across both mathematical and general reasoning tasks. The results, presented in Table 2, indicate that disabling the challenger training mechanism significantly impacts performance metrics, resulting in a reduction of 1.9 points in mathematics scores and 1.0 points in general scores. Similarly, the exclusion of challenger warm-up and curriculum learning adversely affects performance, underscoring the critical role of these components. Notably, the differential impact observed in mathematical tasks, as opposed to general tasks, aligns with evidence presented in Table 1, where variability in math scores is contingent upon the employed methodology. Our findings further suggest that while integrating diverse human data generally enhances performance, concentrating on specific data types yields superior improvements in those particular domains, though the mechanisms underlying these enhancements remain unexplored. This observation is particularly significant, as it allows developers to influence the model's improvement by selecting specific types of data, thereby ensuring the model remains aligned with its intended objectives and mitigating the risk of deviation.
