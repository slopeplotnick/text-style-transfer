In the audio domain, both OMNIGUARD-7B and OMNIGUARD-3B demonstrated commendable performance across audio safety benchmarks, as indicated in Table 4. Given the limited exploration of audio guardrails, our model offers a robust solution to the field and demonstrates that reasoning-based safety training can effectively generalize to the audio modality. Furthermore, OMNIGUIDARD was evaluated in cross-modal safety scenarios to assess its capability to reason across modalities. As illustrated in Figure 3, the OMNIGUIDARD family consistently excelled across all evaluated cross-modal safety benchmarks, including MM-SafetyBench, Video-SafetyBench, and AIAH. Compared to Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, OMNIGUIDARD-7B consistently achieved significant improvements in accuracy across all benchmarks, highlighting the generalization of our omni-modal safety alignment in unifying multimodal reasoning. Moreover, despite having significantly fewer parameters, the lightweight OMNIGUIDARD-3B performs comparably to large-scale general-purpose models such as Qwen3-VL-235B on both MM-SafetyBench and Video-SafetyBench. These results further confirm that OMNIGUIDARD effectively generalizes safety reasoning across modalities, offering a scalable and parameter-efficient solution for cross-modal alignment.
