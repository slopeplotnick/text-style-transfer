**Benchmarks.** OMNIGUARD is evaluated across a comprehensive range of public safety benchmarks, encompassing both unimodal and cross-modal configurations. In the unimodal setting, the system's performance is assessed across four distinct modalities: text, image, video, and audio. For text, we utilize a diverse set of tools, including BeaverTails (Ji et al., 2023), ToxicChat (Lin et al., 2023), WildGuardMix (Han et al., 2024), Aegis2.0 (Ghosh et al., 2025), and the OpenAI Moderation dataset (Markov et al., 2023), ensuring robust and systematic evaluations. Image evaluation is conducted using UnsafeBench (Qu et al., 2024), VLGuard (Zong et al., 2024), and LlavaGuard (Helff et al., 2025a), while video assessments leverage SafeSora (Dai et al., 2024a) and SafeWatch-Bench (Chen et al., 2025). For audio, we incorporate the MuTox English split (Costa-jussà et al., 2024) and WildGuardMix-TTS, which transforms WildGuardMix test samples (Han et al., 2024) into audio using a text-to-speech pipeline consistent with the dataset construction methodology. In the cross-modal configuration, OMNIGUARD’s capabilities are evaluated on three setups: image-text, video-text, and audio-text. These assessments are conducted using MM-SafetyBench (Liu et al., 2024b), VideoSafetyBench (Liu et al., 2025a), and AIAH (Yang et al., 2025), respectively.
