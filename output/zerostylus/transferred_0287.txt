Over the past decade, significant advancements have been made in the development and deployment of voice-activated artificial intelligence assistants, beginning with Apple's Siri, followed by Microsoft's Cortana, Google Assistant, Amazon's Alexa, and Samsung's Bixby. Consequently, this segment of the tutorial elucidates the technological innovations underpinning these AI assistants, such as their capabilities to discern user intent, supplement details through specific neural network architectures, and orchestrate dialogue utilizing adaptive learning methodologies. Additionally, they leverage data sourced from online searches, exploit the semantic web, and employ knowledge graphs for comprehending spoken language. Currently, advancements in AI assistants are achieved through the utilization of language models, which enhance the comprehension and tracking of conversations, thereby improving the quality of spoken language. This tutorial will present an overview of contemporary language models and their adaptation mechanisms for specific tasks, employing a methodical approach to ensure precision in task-specific applications. The discussion also centers on recent advancements in systems designed to manage multiple conversations, encompassing both general-purpose and task-specific applications, while examining the datasets used in these systems and the methodologies employed for their evaluation. Furthermore, new challenges in advancing language models are outlined, alongside the growing demand for systems capable of managing multiple simultaneous conversations.
