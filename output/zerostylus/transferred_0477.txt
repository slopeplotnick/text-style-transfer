To evaluate the efficacy of our system, we conduct experiments across four simulation benchmarks comprising 64 tasks and five real-world tasks, specifically examining three categories of VLA policies. For the simulation benchmarks, we utilize Simpler (Li et al., 2024b), Libero (Liu et al., 2023a), Robotwin1.0 (Mu et al., 2025), and Robotwin2.0 (Chen et al., 2025). Simpler is a real-to-sim benchmark that leverages the SAPIEN simulator integrated with the ManiSkill2 framework. It provides simulated task environments for both the WindowX and Google Robot platforms; however, our study primarily focuses on tasks within the WindowX platform. Libero is a benchmark designed for lifelong learning in decision-making (LLDM), consisting of four distinct task suites. Among these, we concentrate on the most challenging suite, as prior studies have already achieved near-optimal performance on the remaining suites. Furthermore, Robotwin1.0 and Robotwin2.0 emphasize dual-arm gripper manipulation, offering a diverse assortment of assets and task types, as well as scripted policies facilitating autonomous data collection. In the real-world experimental setup, we utilize the REALMAN75 dual-arm robotic system within an office-like environment to evaluate our framework under practical conditions. The experimental tasks incorporate commonly encountered objects, including books, pens, a cabinet, a charger, a laptop, and a paper ball. Specifically, we define five operational tasks: (1) *Receive Book*, where the robot receives a book handed over by a human and places it on the bookshelf; (2) *Storage Charger*, where the robot retrieves the charger head, secures it inside the cabinet, and closes the cabinet door; (3) *Paper and Pen*, where the robot disposes of a paper ball into a trash bin and places a pen into the appropriate holder; (4) *Laptop*, where the robot closes the laptop lid, unplugs the charger, and stores both items within the cabinet; and (5) *Pick Books*, where the robot's left and right arms simultaneously lift and relocate a book.
