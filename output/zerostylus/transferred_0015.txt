Nearswap, as introduced by Goddard et al. (2024), is a merging method designed to enhance interpolation strength when parameters are analogous and to weaken it when they differ. To compare different model architectures, we selected three families of large language models (LLMs) for our experiments: GPT, LLAMA, and QWEN. Specifically, the GPT family, as outlined by Radford et al. (2019) and Gao et al. (2020), includes GPT2-small, GPT2-medium, GPT2-large, GPT2-x1, and GPT-neo-2.7B. Meanwhile, the LLAMA family, developed by Touvron et al. (2023) and expanded by Dubey et al. (2024), consists of LLAMA-2-7B, LLAMA-3-8B, LLAMA-3.1-8B, LLAMA-3.2-1B, and LLAMA-3.2-3B. In contrast, the Qwen family, introduced in 2024, comprises QWEN2-0.5B, QWEN2-1.5B, and QWEN2-7B. These models are accessible on the Hugging Face platform, with URLs provided in Appendix A. Furthermore, the computational resources necessary for the continual pretraining of biased LLMs are detailed in Appendix C, while hyperparameter configurations are outlined in Appendix D. To evaluate social bias in these LLMs, we utilize three benchmarks: the Bias Benchmark for Question-Answering (BBQ) (Parrish et al., 2022), the Bias in Open-Ended Language Generation Dataset (BOLD) (Dhamala et al., 2021), and HONEST. For BBQ, the dataset consists of approximately 58,000 templated question-answer pairs across nine social dimensions relevant to U.S. English speakers. It measures the models' reliance on stereotypical priors versus explicit evidence by contrasting "underspecified" with "fully specified" versions of each question. The BOLD dataset encompasses 23,679 prompts, categorized into 43 demographic subgroups covering occupation, gender, race, religion, and political ideology. To ensure that debiasing methods do not adversely affect performance on downstream tasks, we evaluate both the debiased and pre-trained LLMs using the SuperGLUE benchmark, which includes eight tasks: BoolQ, CB, COPA, MultiRC, ReCoRD, RTE, WiC, and WSC. All evaluations are conducted employing the Language Model Evaluation Harness framework.
