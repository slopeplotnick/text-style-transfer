Despite considerable advancements in computational sound and music generation, a standardized methodology for evaluating these systems remains absent. Researchers have proposed objective metrics to assess the fidelity of audio reproduction and the capability of neural networks to categorize music into pre-existing genres, extending beyond mere auditory evaluation. In this investigation, we utilized auditory assessments alongside objective metrics from prior studies to appraise the technical proficiency and musical innovation of our symbolic music generation system. To evaluate the efficacy of our approach using the GoEmotions dataset, we generated 100 MIDI samples for each segment of our test set and analyzed the outcomes. During our preliminary auditory evaluation, we aimed to determine the extent to which the music produced by our model corresponded with the emotional content of the text; hence, we solicited feedback from individuals who listened to the compositions. This task requires listeners to perceive the narrative's emotional nuances and subsequently evaluate the music's congruence with these emotions, a process considerably more subjective than conventional musical assessments. Initially, each participant was presented with a text and tasked with categorizing it into one of four emotion categories. Subsequently, participants assessed two musical selections accompanying the narrative and chose the one they deemed most effectively conveyed the story's emotional essence. The two-part experimental design enabled us to determine whether participants agreed on the emotional content of the text and whether the accompanying music aligned with that emotion. Since not all musical selections precisely corresponded to the emotion category identified by the participant, our analysis emphasized the congruence of music with emotions delineated by positive/negative valence and high/low arousal, rather than strict categorical matches. The music selections were examined concerning their alignment with participants' perceptions of narrative emotions, classified by positive or negative valence and high or low energy levels. This provided an evaluation of the congruence between the emotional content of the music and the narrative, utilizing metrics of positivity/negativity and energy levels, thereby offering a more adaptable framework to assess their emotional alignment.
