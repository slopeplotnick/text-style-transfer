**Refined Paragraph:** **Baselines and Models.** In constructing our baseline models, we utilize Gemini-2.0-Flash, which functions as an LLM-only model devoid of external knowledge and operates solely based on the original question. To evaluate the performance of CAIRNS, we experiment with two ablations: (1) *Literature-only*, which retrieves information exclusively from the literature subset; and (2) *Literature+Data*, which combines retrieval from both the literature subset and climate data, leveraging a two-stage API reasoning approach. Notably, Gemini-2.0-Flash serves as the foundational model for CAIRNS and all its variations. To ensure a fair comparison, all models are evaluated using identical corpora, prompt structures, and evaluation settings. **Impact of ScholarGuide Prompt (SGP).** The ScholarGuide Prompt significantly enhances readability-related dimensions, including contextual richness, structural coherence, and linguistic fluency. Furthermore, its citation constraint effectively addresses inconsistencies in cited sources. While this constraint marginally reduces the overall citation frequency, it improves the faithfulness of the generated content, as evidenced by a higher FaithRate in comparison to the 'SGP' ablation. Moreover, by enforcing a system of unified reference numbering and grounding each factual statement in explicit evidence, SGP enables the model to produce responses that are both clearer and more verifiable. These improvements hold consistently across both zero-shot and few-shot settings, underscoring the critical role of structured prompting in driving CAIRNS's performance. **Impact of Multi-turn ReAct (MTR).** Multi-turn ReAct introduces an iterative reasoning mechanism that allows the model to refine its intermediate outputs by repeatedly aligning retrieved literature and climate data. Consequently, this approach enhances the comprehensiveness and contextual relevance of the generated responses, as demonstrated by higher comprehensiveness scores. Although its overall contribution is modest compared to SGP, MTR complements the structured generation process by improving the model's robustness, particularly for multi-source or data-intensive queries. **Location-Weighted Retrieval (LWR).** The inclusion of Location-Weighted Retrieval enables the model to provide region-specific responses, making it particularly effective for geographically focused queries. Ablating this component results in a slight reduction in specificity; however, other performance metrics remain largely unaffected. This finding suggests that LWR positively contributes to the model’s ability to generate geographically sensitive answers without significantly compromising its overall output quality.
