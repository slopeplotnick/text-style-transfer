We present *promptsolution*, a unified and modular open-source Python framework specifically designed for automatic prompt optimization. The framework accommodates both practitioners focusing on single-task prompt optimization and researchers conducting large-scale benchmark studies across diverse datasets. Notably, *promptsolution* distinguishes itself within the prompt optimization tool landscape by emphasizing extensibility, modular design, and a low abstraction level tailored to optimizing prompts effectively. Moreover, the framework facilitates seamless comparison of optimizers, requiring minimal manual effort for systematic benchmarking experiments. While not every optimizer within *promptsolution* performs optimally across all tasks, the library consistently provides at least one effective optimizer per specific application, ensuring robust performance. This modularity allows users to substitute components or explore alternative optimizers effortlessly when the current configuration proves suboptimal. Additionally, we demonstrated the operational mechanisms of the framework, elucidating the interactions between its components and verifying its utility and competitiveness through comparative evaluations against alternative tools. Looking ahead, we aim to expand the framework's interoperability by developing interfaces compatible with higher-level systems, such as DSPy, thereby enabling users to integrate functionalities from both ecosystems. Furthermore, to simplify the management of complex, large-scale experimental setups, we plan to introduce compatibility with configuration management tools, such as Hydra (Yadan, 2019). To enhance accessibility, we intend to implement a lightweight graphical interface that enables real-time tracking of experiments and visual analysis of optimization processes. In response to the growing prevalence of multi-agent systems, we aim to support prompt optimization and interaction protocol configurations across multiple agents. Inspired by principles of AutoML, we are also exploring ensembling strategies for optimizers, akin to ELPO (Zhang et al., 2025), to improve outcomes further. The extensible design of *promptsolution* ensures its adaptability, allowing the incorporation of emerging methods as advancements in the field continue to evolve.
