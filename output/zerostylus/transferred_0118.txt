The refinement of reasoning processes and verification of intermediate steps is crucial for enhancing model efficacy. Specifically, certain studies adopt methodologies that decompose reasoning into discrete steps, systematically evaluating each one while employing reward models to optimize mathematical reasoning performance. For instance, one study investigates the performance improvements associated with the use of a final outcome verifier, known as the GV-Gap, particularly when both the solver and verifier utilize the same model architecture. However, the scope of this research was restricted to fundamental models, deliberately excluding those subjected to additional training post-initial configuration. Additionally, the scaling of computational resources during test phases is a topic of active exploration. Researchers have explored various approaches, such as random sampling with self-checking, parallel sampling with error correction, and the comparative effectiveness of Self-Consistency (SC) versus a generative verifier. The focus of optimization efforts lies in discerning the most effective strategies for leveraging computational resources to enhance verification processes during testing. Nonetheless, verifiers possess inherent limitations; they may erroneously validate incorrect responses, dismiss valid reasoning, or select unsuitable solutions. A significant area of research centers on the methodologies through which models can improve their capabilities and autonomously assess their performance. This line of inquiry has sparked skepticism among some researchers; however, others have put forth theories and evidence suggesting that refining their understanding can indeed enhance model performance. For example, a study conducted in 2023 examines the necessity of employing a more robust verifier to facilitate performance improvements in small models and analyzes how these enhancements are influenced by the verifier's capacity. At the onset of self-improvement endeavors, utilizing feedback articulated in straightforward language or developing models specifically designed to rectify their errors emerges as an effective strategy, although this approach may evolve as proficiency increases. Finally, the progressive enhancement of responses through computational tools, such as code interpreters, allows for continuous improvement via iterative refinement.
