The rapid advancement of large language models (LLMs) is fostering the development of new methodologies aimed at leveraging their capabilities for beneficial applications. As LLMs become increasingly integrated into online interactions, their potential to enhance mutual understanding and facilitate more constructive conversations warrants further exploration. This research examines whether these models can transcend the basic task of identifying harmful content in dialogues, exploring their ability to actively participate in de-escalating tensions and resolving disputes online. Rather than merely evaluating the fairness of individual messages, this approach involves determining whether a model can assess the overall fairness of a conversation, analyze emotional dynamics, and generate responses that facilitate conflict resolution and emotional regulation. To evaluate the effectiveness of these models, researchers have constructed a comprehensive dataset sourced from Reddit and developed an assessment framework that incorporates various methodologies, including rule-based scoring, user simulation, and alignment comparisons with human decision-making processes. Empirical evaluations demonstrate that proprietary models exhibit superior performance compared to free models in terms of reasoning capabilities and efficacy in resolving arguments. However, we also explore whether these models, despite their potential, encounter limitations in facilitating harmonious online interactions. The influence of large language models is transforming how individuals communicate, acquire knowledge, and collaborate effectively. Moreover, in addition to functionalities such as language translation and text summarization, these models are increasingly deployed in educational settings, healthcare services, and mental health interventions. Rather than solely automating tasks, we investigate the potential for LLMs to be deployed in online discussions to encourage more considerate and equitable interactions. Consequently, online interactions can escalate into contentious exchanges, where individuals persist in mutual antagonism, allowing tensions to intensify. This phenomenon frequently occurs due to the inability of individuals to perceive facial expressions or interpret social signals.
