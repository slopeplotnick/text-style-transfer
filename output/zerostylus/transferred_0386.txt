We present **Radiologist Copilot**, a system designed to assist radiologists in generating automated radiology reports with integrated quality control, achieved through a structured framework that coordinates multiple tools. The agentic system, underpinned by a reasoning backbone, autonomously selects appropriate tools, devises actionable plans, and performs tasks akin to radiologist workflows, thereby streamlining the reporting process. Furthermore, the toolset encompasses functionalities such as region localization, analytical planning, strategic template selection, quality evaluation, and iterative refinement driven by feedback, all of which collectively ensure the creation of detailed and high-quality reports. Experimental results further highlight that **Radiologist Copilot** significantly outperforms other state-of-the-art approaches in radiology reporting. Automated radiology reporting with embedded quality control mechanisms is indispensable, as interpreting 3D medical imaging modalities, such as CT and MRI, is inherently time-intensive and resource-demanding. For instance, **CT2Rep** employs an auto-regressive causal transformer architecture and relational memory to produce radiology reports for 3D chest CT images, while **Reg2RG** utilizes a region-guided referring and grounding framework tailored for chest CT report generation. However, it is important to note that report generation, while a critical component, represents only one stage of the overall radiology process and often lacks robust quality assurance mechanisms. To address broader challenges in medical image analysis, **Medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** have been proposed for tasks such as radiology report generation (RRG) and visual question answering (VQA). Specifically, 3D medical VLMs have demonstrated promising performance across 3D imaging modalities, including CT and MRI. For example, **RadFM** introduces a generalist vision-language foundation model that integrates both 2D and 3D medical data, whereas **M3D** contributes **M3D-Data**, a large-scale multimodal medical dataset, alongside **M3D-LaMed**, a multi-modal large language model designed for 3D medical image analysis. Additionally, **Merlin** develops a 3D VLM that incorporates both structured and unstructured data to enhance the interpretation of abdominal CT scans. Similarly, **CT-CHAT** combines the **CT-CLIP** vision encoder with a pre-trained large language model to facilitate the analysis of 3D chest CT volumes. Moreover, **Med3DVLM** advances multimodal representation through the adoption of an efficient encoder, a contrastive learning strategy, and a dual-stream projector. In contrast, **Hulu-Med** unifies text, 2D and 3D imagery, and video within a single architecture, presenting a generalist medical VLM designed for diverse medical tasks.
