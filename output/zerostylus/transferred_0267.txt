**Evaluation Metrics.** To evaluate the functionality and clinical utility of the Radiologist Copilot, we conducted a comprehensive assessment encompassing both task-level and agent-level evaluations. For task-level evaluation, we employed natural language generation (NLG) metrics, such as BLEU-1, ROUGE-L, METEOR, and BERTScore, to measure the quality of the generated text. Additionally, clinical efficacy (CE) metrics, including F1-RadGraph and GREEN, were utilized to assess clinical accuracy. At the agent level, we adopted the LLM-as-a-Judge framework, leveraging OpenAI GPT-5.1 to systematically evaluate the agent's operational processes. This evaluation framework focused on four key dimensions: Analysis Process, Tool Selection, Action Planning, and Action Execution. Each dimension was scored on a scale from 1 to 5, with 5 indicating excellence and 1 representing poor performance. Specifically, Analysis Process assessed the agent’s ability to perform thorough, logical, and complete task analysis; Tool Selection evaluated the appropriateness and accuracy of the tools chosen; Action Planning examined the coherence and feasibility of the planned actions; and Action Execution measured the successful implementation of these actions. To holistically assess the Radiologist Copilot, we conducted evaluations of both its individual components and its overall functionality in handling radiology reporting tasks. The results of the tool evaluation revealed that most scores were 5, indicating excellence, while the remaining scores were 4, denoting a good standard. These findings highlight the agent's thorough analytical processes, accurate tool selection, effective planning strategies, and successful execution. Moreover, the evaluation provided critical insights into the Copilot’s ability to address user inquiries, demonstrating that its planning and execution processes are cohesive, efficient, and capable of generating high-quality radiology reports. This workflow closely mirrors the practices of professional radiologists, thereby contributing to enhanced clinical efficiency.
