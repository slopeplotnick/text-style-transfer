Numerous studies have explored more structured prompting paradigms. For instance, Plan-and-Solve (Wang et al., 2023c) explicitly decomposes tasks into planning and solution phases, which yields stronger results in mathematical and logical contexts. Moreover, Prompt Chaining (Chen et al., 2023) investigates whether the integration of intermediate summaries or steps can enhance performance in text summarization tasks. Complexity-based Prompting (Fu et al., 2023) proposes that the complexity of a task should guide the selection of prompts, an idea that closely aligns with our motivation to analyze prompt specificity. While much of the existing research emphasizes task format or exemplar inclusion, our study uniquely focuses on the degree of prompt specificity as a continuous dimension, systematically varying detail and structure. Recently, methods for automatic or human-in-the-loop prompt refinement have been introduced. For example, black-box optimization techniques (Diao et al., 2023) adjust prompts using task feedback without accessing model specifics. Furthermore, the study "Prompt Engineering a Prompt Engineer" (Zhou et al., 2023) demonstrates that large language models can be prompted to refine their own prompts, a metaprompting strategy that parallels our generalization loop. Additionally, Prompt Refinement with Image Pivots (Lu et al., 2023) exemplifies cross-modal prompt tuning by utilizing visual features to refine textual prompts for image generation. Although our work is text-only, it shares the central aim of adjusting prompt representations to improve downstream fidelity. In contrast to these studies, DETAIL emphasizes the role of textual specificity as a modulator of LLM behavior in reasoning tasks, rather than focusing on semantic drift or multi-modality. Comprehensive reviews, such as those conducted by Liu et al., have surveyed reasoning-focused prompting techniques, revealing a growing taxonomy of prompt formats, strategies, and task types. However, few studies explore how the phrasing detail or granularity of prompts influences model behavior across various models and domains. While methods like CoT and Plan-and-Solve underscore the significance of prompt content, they do not delve into the detailed exploration of this aspect.
