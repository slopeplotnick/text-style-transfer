The model loader, through the init_adapter method, creates the PEFT model. From this point, the tasks of loading, training, and saving the model are entrusted to PEFT provider libraries and the LLaMA-Factory framework. However, unlike LLaMA-Factory, PEFT-FACTORY adopts a dynamic loading mechanism for custom PEFT methods, thereby enhancing its extensibility and modularity. This design allows researchers and users to seamlessly integrate custom PEFT methods without needing to change the core codebase of PEFT-FACTORY. To exemplify our Custom PEFT Interface, we replicated two PEFT methods that are absent from traditional PEFT frameworks: BitFit (Ben Zaken et al., 2022) and SVFT (Lingam et al., 2024). During the dynamic loading process, the peftloader module automatically identifies and loads PEFT methods from an organized directory hierarchy. Each custom PEFT method is situated in its own subdirectory, comprising two essential files: a config.py file, which outlines a PeftConfig subclass, and a model.py file, which implements a BaseTuner subclass. The loader verifies each implementation by checking for necessary attributes, such as peft_type for configurations and prefix for model classes, prior to registration. Subsequently, the loader dynamically loads the config and model subclasses, registering them through the register_peft_method function, which integrates the config and model into the constants of the Hugging Face PEFT library. Moreover, this process is further detailed by Algorithm 1 in Appendix B.1. Following the dynamic loading, the custom PEFT method is handled in a manner similar to off-the-shelf methods, as discussed in Section 3.1. To add a new method, it is essential to adhere to the directory structure within the PEFT methods directory, indicated by the environment variable PEFT_DIR, with ./peft as the default directory, to ensure consistency in organizational structure and class inheritance. Further details about example method templates are provided in Appendix C as well as in the PEFT-FACTORY documentation<sup>2</sup>.
