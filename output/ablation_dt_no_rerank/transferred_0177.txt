The MODOMA system creates a laboratory environment for language acquisition experiments with full parametric control. This allows users to manage the properties of each experiment through predefined settings. Importantly, all facets of language acquisition, including the adult, the daughter, and the executed language procedures, are integrated into the system. As such, the parameters can refer to any of these components. Table 1 offers an overview of the relevant parameters for the current experiments. The MODOMA system is capable of executing several language acquisition processes, similar to the procedure described by Shakouri et al. in 2025. The first parameter listed in Table 1 concerns the acquisition of functional categories, indicating whether this aspect is enabled, and this applies to all experiments discussed herein. The other parameters outline key features of the experimental framework. Specifically, the parameter controlling the type of phrases generated by the mother agent defines the structure of Delilah's verbal outputs, deciding whether they are sentences or noun phrases. Both the training and test experiments presented in this paper systematically vary this parameter. Likewise, the minimum volume of data the daughter agent must process before acquiring functional and content categories is varied across all experiments. Combined, these two parameters set the experimental configuration. Within the training experiments, the acquisition of functional and content categories is initiated following the processing of 1,000 noun phrases (NPs) and 1,000 sentences and subsequently progresses to 10,000 noun phrases and 10,000 sentences, respectively. This allows assessment of the number of input exemplars generated by Delilah necessary to discern this statistical pattern. Moreover, the acquisition of functional categories parameter defines the type-token ratio used to transform distributional differences between function and content words into distinct linguistic knowledge. The training sessions outlined in this paper serve to determine the appropriate values for these parameters, while the test experiments validate the chosen settings with new datasets generated by Delilah.
