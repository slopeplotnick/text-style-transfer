Udio is capable of transforming natural-language prompts into completed music tracks across various styles, incorporating lyrics in multiple languages. Regarding the perception of AI-generated music, research in computational creativity and applied machine learning has examined the creative value of these models through subjective listening tests. Participants are tasked with rating samples produced by these models in comparison to alternative baseline models or human-composed music. Frequent evaluation metrics include overall quality, creativity, or novelty, as well as specific musical elements such as melody, harmony, and rhythm. A critical metric in this assessment is the perceived naturalness or humanness of the music. This is often directly assessed via rating scales or examined using a musical Turing Test, where participants judge whether a musical composition was created by a human or an AI system, sometimes with varying degrees of disclosure about the source. In tasks such as generating Bach chorales, even foundational models like Deepbach produced results that were difficult to distinguish from authentic compositions. To assess the emotional dimensions of AI-generated music, it is customary for participants to identify the targeted emotions using categorizations like "happy" or "sad," or through dimensional emotion models. As AI-generated music evolves in complexity and sophistication, studies have explored whether listeners' evaluations are influenced by their knowledge or beliefs regarding the music's origin. Nevertheless, the empirical evidence remains inconclusive. Earlier investigations, which manipulated awareness of the composers' identities, revealed no significant bias when participants rated live performances of string-quartet compositions generated by either human or computer composers. Similarly, Zlatkov et al. (2023) did not detect systematic bias against AI-composed works when using pop music samples as stimuli. Moreover, consumer marketing-oriented research by Moura and Maw (2021) did not identify any distinct preference based on authorship, regardless of whether the music was classical or pop in nature.
