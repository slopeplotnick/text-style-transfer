Reviewing countless documents quickly and constructing summaries and query systems that align with decision timelines are crucial tasks. Discovering patterns in high-dimensional multivariate and spatial datasets is essential to this process. Avoiding cognitive bias and realistically quantifying uncertainty allows for rational decision-making amidst uncertain conditions. Therefore, one of the most significant potentials lies in an AI system that interacts with humans, applying novel data science techniques to high-dimensional space-time data. This collaboration generates plausible hypotheses regarding mineralization, its extent, and quality, which are decision-ready. Such hypotheses must be falsifiable; alongside stating a hypothesis, it is critical to specify the data or outcomes that would refute it. This approach is key in preventing cognitive bias. The readiness for decision-making is imperative, as deterministic mapping alone fails to achieve this readiness. Underpinning this system are foundational data science tools. As noted, the principal challenge is not the selection of a machine learning algorithm, but rather the careful curation and comprehensive analysis of data to form hypotheses. Humans tend to concentrate on known indicators of mineralization. For instance, in exploring massive nickel sulfide deposits, geologists use a scatterplot between MgO and Ni to assess potential for sulfide mineralization. However, this bivariate plot, potentially supplemented by additional data, can be enhanced using data science and AI methods to discover previously unnoticed high-dimensional relationships. Thus, advancing and deploying data science and AI-based or enabled modeling and analysis tools is instrumental in assisting domain experts. For example, these tools aid in detecting anomalies within high-dimensional geological and geochemical datasets, and facilitate high-dimensional pattern analysis of geophysical data for comparative evaluations against known deposits. They leverage non-linear adaptations of fundamental data science methodologies, such as PCA, MDS, and FA. Moreover, spatial clustering techniques effectively delineate complex spatial relationships, while advanced geostatistical methods enable the creation of complex surfaces constrained by diverse datasets. Emphasizing data science tools oriented towards falsification rather than mere data fitting, and implementing multi-physics stochastic inversion across scales—from borehole to country-scale—further enhances analytical efforts in this field.
