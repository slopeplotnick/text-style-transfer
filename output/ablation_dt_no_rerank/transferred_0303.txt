Visual misperceptions typically manifest in distinctive patterns. Once identified by the detection mechanism, these occurrences are promptly communicated to the fine-tuned system, thereby initiating a feedback loop. In this loop, the system learns from its errors and iteratively validates its outputs against observed instances. If discrepancies are identified, the original input, erroneous output, and the corrected output are reintroduced to facilitate the learning process. The system then reevaluates the same input to achieve the desired accuracy. Lang2Logic dissects paragraphs into individual sentences, translating each into a logical expression and converting it to CNF form. Additionally, Lang2Logic simplifies the CNF expression for downstream use. Figure 1 presents the input paragraph, analyzed and translated by Lang2Logic into four CNF clauses with four variables. Thereafter, Lang2Logic performs simplifications, with the final expression displayed in the figure's lower section. The operation of Lang2Logic is as follows: the initial text is processed to yield a concise logical expression. First, the grammatical framework, transformer, and NLP environment are established, including syntax for function-call expressions. A LogicTransformer is then implemented to convert textual tokens into Sympy symbols and logical function calls, such as And, Or, and Not. Following this, LangToLogic modifies sequences using a designated model. For each line in the model response, a simplified output is produced. Initially, the model misinterpreted "implies" as a logical operator rather than its lexical meaning, leading to overuse; however, the identification mechanism corrected this misapplication. An illustrative statement is: "Dragon fruit thrives in the forest ecosystem, whereas peanuts fail to adapt when moved there, indicating the forest's unsuitability for peanut cultivation." Utilizing predefined rules, the system transforms the statement into logical expressions and simplifies them to produce the final outcome.
