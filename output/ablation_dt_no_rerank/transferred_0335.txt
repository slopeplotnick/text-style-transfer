To achieve distinct spatial cues and overcome the constraints of fixed viewpoints, we diversify camera rotation trajectories. Each trajectory starts from a random pitch, yaw, and roll orientation, followed by random drifts to mimic natural movements. Additionally, we ensure that the main visual cues corresponding to the audio stay within the field of view for a certain duration. Specifically, we compute the direction of maximum audio energy using spherical harmonics decomposition and adjust the camera's initial orientation so that the trajectory is roughly centered around the primary sound source. After obtaining the binaural audio, we discard samples with a normalized left-right channel difference below a specific threshold, ensuring that the retained samples exhibit distinct spatial auditory cues. BiAudio stands out as the largest FoV video dataset with binaural audio format. Previous real-world binaural datasets are much smaller in scale. While SimBinaural offers a substantial amount of content, its synthetic video and audio limit its reliability for real-world applications. Moreover, these datasets typically focus on narrow sound environments: OAP on street scenes, and Fair-Play, SimBinaural, and MUSIC-21 on music. In contrast, BiAudio is large-scale and open-domain, offering diverse real-world sound environments. Spatial Cue Enhancement further improves spatial perception and dynamics, making it ideal for video-to-binaural spatial audio generation. The overall architecture of ViSAudio is depicted in Figure 2. Our approach builds upon parts of the MMAudio architecture, fine-tuning the pretrained MMAudio to inherit its robust audio generation capabilities, ensuring strong generalization across open-domain acoustic environments. To generate binaural audio that ensures both channel coherence and spatial distinctiveness, we adopt Dual-Branch Audio Generation, where two dedicated branches independently predict left and right flows. Additionally, we introduce the Conditional Spacetime Module, which extracts spatio-temporal features from the video and integrates them into the generation process, further enhancing the audio's spatial realism.
