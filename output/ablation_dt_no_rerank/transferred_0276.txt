Case 3: Suicidal Risk Level Detection. This case highlights a misclassification where a Reddit post with a supportive level of suicidal risk was mistakenly predicted as behavior by the Menta model (Figure 12). The primary cause was the excessive input length, which encompassed a long multi-speaker thread with formatting noise—such as list syntax, HTML artifacts, and placeholder tokens like 'Hyperactive behavior.' Furthermore, these tokens acted as misleading cues, thereby biasing the model toward behavior-related conclusions. For instance, one user on the thread advised another to remain alive for their younger siblings, emphasizing the need to protect them from their father's potential harm. Moreover, the individual cautioned against internalizing a troll comment, reassuring the recipient that they were not perceived as worthless or deserving of self-harm. However, the categorization of the post under 'behavior' was partly due to the excessive burdens of academics and social interactions identified, which the model misinterpreted. Nonetheless, the user reported that practicing assertiveness by declining unwelcome demands led to improved overall well-being and facilitated more friendships, illustrating the positive aspect of behavioral change.
