Procedure. The experiment lasted approximately 20 to 35 minutes. Initially, volunteers were asked to select a picture of their interest for a writing task, ensuring they fully understood the content of the chosen picture. Following this, participants were familiarized with the operational features of TaleFrame. Subsequently, they were tasked with constructing a narrative utilizing their chosen image. Throughout the experiment, we meticulously documented their actions and verbalizations. Upon completion of the task, participants were requested to provide feedback through a structured questionnaire, and each participant received a monetary compensation of $5 for their contribution. User Feedback. TaleFrame's usability was evaluated using a 5-point Likert scale based on user experience. ① Framework Construction (Q1-Q3): TaleFrame facilitates the construction of a comprehensive narrative framework through its user-friendly interface and options for delineating components of the narrative, enabling users to organize elements interactively. ② Narrative Visualization (Q4-Q6): It visually represents the interconnections among these components, making the structure comprehensible while offering tools to refine the narrative alongside its development. ③ Narrative Timing and Coherence (Q7-Q9): Furthermore, TaleFrame maintains a coherent timeline and consistency within the narrative, providing specific recommendations for enhancing emotional expression and thematic depth. ④ Evaluation and Improvement Suggestions (Q10-Q12): Finally, it systematically evaluates the quality of the narrative framework, offering targeted suggestions for enhancement, thereby improving the overall quality of the story.
