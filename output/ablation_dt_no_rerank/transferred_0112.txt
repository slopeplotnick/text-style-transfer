Our samples were compared against EMOPIA MIDI files, where the major-key ratio serves as an indicator of valence, while average note length and note velocity indicate arousal. For the valence metric, our samples resemble EMOPIA across three quadrants; however, in Q2, our model produces a much higher major-key ratio than EMOPIA, reflecting a possible major-key bias from the dataset's predominance of major-key tracks combined with a limited fine-tuning set. Regarding arousal, the fine-tuned model accurately captures the note-length pattern across quadrants: shorter notes appear in high-arousal quadrants (Q1 and Q2) and longer notes surface in low-arousal quadrants (Q3 and Q4), closely aligning with EMOPIA. Meanwhile, variations in average note velocity are less pronounced, likely due to REMI's simplification of MIDI velocities into fewer categories, thereby compressing the dynamic range and complicating separability between quadrants. These quantitative results partially align with listener feedback from listening tests, demonstrating that the model captures certain musical attributes related to arousal and valence. Qualitative assessments suggest that while the model is more proficient at capturing arousal, valence results are closer to random, possibly due to the model's propensity to generate an excessive number of major-key tracks. Therefore, additional quantitative metrics may help identify discrepancies in positive valence generation. Within this project, we introduced Story2MIDI, a transformer-based model designed to generate emotionally congruent symbolic music from textual inputs. To achieve this, we constructed a comprehensive text-music dataset and implemented an encoder-decoder framework to map emotional expressions in text to musical elements. The encoder was meticulously fine-tuned to yield distinct emotion representations across varying levels of energy and positivity, while the decoder was initially pretrained on a large corpus of symbolic music and later fine-tuned on a smaller, emotion-annotated dataset for emotion-conditioned music generation. Story2MIDI was assessed through both subjective listener evaluations and objective musical metrics, collectively affirming the model's efficacy while highlighting several challenges that warrant further investigation.
