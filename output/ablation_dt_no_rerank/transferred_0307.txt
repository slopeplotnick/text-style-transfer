In this paper, we introduce Story2MIDI, a sequence-to-sequence Transformer-based model designed to generate music that aligns with the emotional content of a given piece of text. To develop this model, we constructed the Story2MIDI dataset by integrating datasets pertinent to sentiment analysis in both text and emotion classification in music. As a result, we compiled a dataset containing pairs of text excerpts and corresponding musical pieces, both eliciting analogous emotional responses from their respective consumers. Despite the small scale of our dataset and restricted computational resources, our results demonstrate that our model effectively discerns emotion-relevant attributes within music and integrates them into its generative process, thereby producing outputs with diverse emotional tones. We validated the generated results by employing objective musical metrics alongside a human listening study, thus corroborating the model's efficacy in encapsulating the intended emotional cues. We live in an era with an ever-growing demand for multimedia content, including music, audiobooks, and podcasts, particularly amplified by the rise of social media and streaming platforms. At the heart of many forms of entertainment lies a narrative that drives the experience, be it in cinematics, interactive media, audio series, or documentaries. Narratives are powerful tools for evoking emotions as audiences engage with the arcs set by character development, plot progression, and thematic exploration. This emotional progression is integral to the lasting impact that narratives have on audiences. Similarly, music profoundly influences emotional states and is widely recognized as a medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creative producers often integrate background music into narratives, significantly enhancing emotional engagement and the immersive experience of the audience. With the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence, new models capable of generating diverse content across different modalities are continually being developed.
