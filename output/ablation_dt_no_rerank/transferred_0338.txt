The left segment of Figure 2 shows the prompt used alongside an example of the extracted triples for an individual training image, illustrating the semantic organization of various attributes. The output, as presented, consists of multiple ontology-aligned triples covering diverse facets of the garment, including category (depicts), shape (has_silhouette), material (has_material_type), pattern (has_pattern), and additional relationships defined by the Fashionpedia ontology. Each triple adopts the (image_id, relation, attribute) format, thus facilitating a clear interpretation of the represented image's semantics. This structure supports the construction and comparison of concept-based graphs at a downstream level. To ascertain the degree of influence, we systematically compared the knowledge graph of each generated image against those of all training images by calculating node-level overlap. For each prompt, we selected the training images that exhibited the greatest node-level overlap with the graph of the generated image. The prompt used was: "Create triples for the fashion item in the image based on the relationships of Fashionpedia Ontology, provided at: \\({ONTOLOGY_PATH} = \). Focus exclusively on the fashion item of the image with image_id: \\)([IMAGE_ID=)} and generate triples that describe the image (image_id, relationship, attribute_value)." For instance, in the case of image 58810.jpg, the extracted triples included: 58810.jpg, depicts, Shirt; 58810.jpg, belongs_to_category, Upperbody; 58810.jpg, has_nickname, None; 58810.jpg, has_silhouette, Slim-fit; 58810.jpg, has_waistline, Straight; 58810.jpg, has_length, Long-sleeved; 58810.jpg, has_collar_type, Classic; 58810.jpg, has_lapel_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_neckline, Crew-neck; 58810.jpg, has_sleeve_type, Long Sleeves with Cuffs; 58810.jpg, has_pocket_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_opening_type, Button-front Closure; 58810.jpg, has_material_type, Cotton-blend Fabric; 58810.jpg, has_leather_type, None; 58810.jpg, has_fabric_treatment, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_pattern, Plaid; 58810.jpg, has_animal_pattern, None. Subsequently, graphs characterized by the highest node-level overlap with the graph of the generated image were utilized in the next phase of the unlearning process. Moreover, Neo4j was employed for an extensive exploration of the graph, enabling the identification of prevalent attributes and the distribution of concepts across the dataset. In the unlearning process, the generative model was retrained following the removal of specific training samples, allowing for an assessment of the extent to which these samples influenced the model's output. A series of 10 unlearning tests were conducted using both the baseline method and our proposed approach. In each experiment, images were generated for 15 prompts before and after the unlearning process. To maintain consistency with the baseline configuration, a comparable proportion of training images identified as the most influential for each prompt were systematically removed.
