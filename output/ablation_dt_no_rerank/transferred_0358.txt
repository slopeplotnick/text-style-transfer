DeepSeek-V3.2 achieves similar levels of performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across multiple reasoning benchmarks. Moreover, it significantly advances the agentic capabilities of open models, demonstrating exceptional proficiency in long-tail agent tasks as introduced in EvalSys (2025); Li et al. (2025); Luo et al. (2025). Furthermore, DeepSeek-V3.2 emerges as a highly cost-efficient alternative for agent scenarios, notably narrowing the performance gap between open and frontier proprietary models while incurring substantially lower costs. To push the boundaries of reasoning capabilities in open models, we developed DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale by relaxing the length constraints. Consequently, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale achieves performance parity with the leading closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro, and shows gold-medal performance in the IOI 2025, ICPC World Final 2025, IMO 2025, and CMO 2025. Both versions of DeepSeek-V3.2 use identical architecture to DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp. Compared with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, the only architectural modification in DeepSeek-V3.2 is the introduction of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) through continued training. The DSA prototype primarily consists of two components: a lightning indexer and a fine-grained token selection mechanism. Beginning with a base checkpoint of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, whose context length is extended to 128K, we perform continued pre-training followed by post-training to create DeepSeek-V3.2. This continued pre-training involves two stages, both utilizing dataset distribution aligned with the 128K long context extension data from DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, we evaluate DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp on a suite of benchmarks focusing on diverse capabilities and compare its performance with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, revealing similar outcomes. Although DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp significantly improves computational efficiency on long sequences, we do not observe substantial performance degradation when compared to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, in both short- and long-context tasks.
