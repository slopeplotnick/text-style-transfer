Explanations in XAI are expected to meet specific standards, ensuring both clarity and fidelity to the model. Firstly, it is essential that explanations are comprehensible to the intended recipients, and secondly, they should accurately represent the operations of the model. Understandability is receiver-dependent but generally demands meaningful content and a direct presentation. Fidelity requires that the explanation truthfully reflects the model's behavior, including no bias, which may arise from intentionally constrained explanations. While clarity and fidelity are independent qualities, maximizing each in isolation poses significant challenges. For instance, explanations that align with individuals' expectations may be perceived as more comprehensible; however, this predisposition can engender bias if it informs the selection of explanations. Moreover, a legitimate explanation that is inaccurately construed can equally mislead the explainer. This paper introduces a post-hoc explanatory framework for arbitrary models designed to enhance both clarity and fidelity within a specified framework. Importantly, explanations should use straightforward language and illustrate causal relationships, as this mirrors the conventional structure employed in human discourse. Such an approach ensures that the intended interpretation aligns with the actual interpretation, thereby removing the need for supplementary guidance on understanding the explanation and facilitating a more concise presentation. The use of accessible vocabulary is critical due to the potential discrepancy between the data attributes used by the model and the optimal features for elucidation. This notion constitutes a fundamental aspect of methodologies collectively referred to as Concept-based Explainable Artificial Intelligence (C-XAI). The assumption underlying this approach is that, although models are adept at processing various forms of data such as images and graphs, human communication predominantly relies on high-level, semantically meaningful concepts.
