Datasets. In line with previous studies (Wu et al., 2025), we have curated 5.5K Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset (Hendrycks et al., 2021). Evaluation Benchmarks. We perform evaluations on a comprehensive suite of tests including AIME 2024, AMC, MATH500, GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math to assess their efficacy in complex problem-solving scenarios. For evaluations that extend beyond conventional benchmarks, we additionally incorporate ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond to tackle advanced problem-solving in non-standard contexts. Methodology. Our approach utilizes greedy decoding constrained by a 3,000 token limit and evaluates CAPO in combination with four prominent reinforcement learning algorithms: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. Furthermore, each algorithm’s CAPO-enhanced variant is incorporated, allowing us to conduct a direct assessment of CAPO's potential as a universal augmentation. This setup enables the evaluation of the independent efficacy of baseline methods alongside the enhancements afforded by CAPO integration. To ensure consistency across the various baseline algorithms and CAPO variants, we adhere to a standardized description of all implementation details. Recognizing that different methods require distinct parameter settings, the complete training configurations are detailed in Appendix B. We also utilize the GUI-R1-3K dataset, originating from OS-Atlas, encompassing 3,000 instances of vision-language-action across both web and mobile platforms. This dataset integrates visual states with natural language directives and action sequences, thereby serving as a robust testbed for evaluating multimodal reasoning and planning capabilities. In our planning framework, we assess using GUI-Act-Web, OmniAct-Web, and AndroidControl-Low/High modules. For perceptual evaluation, Screenshot Pro is employed, whereby GRPO is compared with its CAPO variant under the primary GUI benchmark configuration. This supplementary evaluation aims to demonstrate the effectiveness of CAPO across various reasoning frameworks, extending well beyond traditional mathematical reasoning challenges.
