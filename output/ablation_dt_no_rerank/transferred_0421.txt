Efforts are focused on maximizing code reuse and accelerating the delivery of new applications. The goal is to transition the code base to a higher-level Software Product Line (SPL), specifically Level 4 or 5. To ensure this transition occurs smoothly, initial steps involve conducting interviews with company personnel to assess their perceptions of SPL engineering. These interviews are designed to answer the research questions presented in the following section. Furthermore, another series of interviews is planned at the project's end to refine the guidelines for a successful migration. The upcoming migration phases will include variability analysis, starting with establishing a process for feature localization to synthesize feature models. Subsequently, studying the interactions and dependencies among these features will help achieve a comprehensive understanding of the system. The conducted interviews aim to elucidate perceptions regarding current practices, benefits, risks, and concerns anticipated before transitioning to a SPL. Research Question 1 (RQ1) explores the perceptions of company stakeholders regarding existing development practices, highlighting their views on current efficacies. Research Question 2 examines the perceived advantages of the prospective SPL from the standpoint of company stakeholders, focusing on how it will enhance application development value. RQ3 investigates stakeholder perceptions regarding risks associated with the future SPL, analyzing potential risks in their routine work. Finally, RQ4 delves into the fears experienced by company actors, arising from the risks identified previously. This inquiry aids in understanding the apprehensions held by company personnel about the migration's implications, regarding potential adverse outcomes, anticipated obstacles, and necessary strategic planning and mitigation efforts to navigate these challenges effectively.
