Our findings indicate that attitudes toward AI, accompanied by qualitative themes such as ethical considerations and functional use, shape music perception beyond aesthetic criteria. Therefore, it is essential for future research to employ instruments specifically designed for AI music and develop multi-factor rating scales accordingly. With AI-generated music increasingly featured on streaming platforms, however, it is vital to explore socioeconomic issues related to fairness and transparency in its production and distribution. Given the substantial impact of attitudes toward AI on music appreciation and emotional experiences, experimental designs that manipulate information about the creation and distribution process should be considered. For example, modeling the transparency of training datasets or revenue sharing intentions of AI music pieces as experimental conditions could provide valuable insights. Moreover, examining diverse production and usage contexts is crucial. The perception of songs created collaboratively between humans and AI systems, particularly as advanced text-to-music systems facilitate co-creation, is an area of growing interest. Additionally, building on the research by Moura and Maw (2021), it is pertinent to study the perception and acceptance of AI music in various functional applications. Our findings emphasize that attitudes toward AI significantly influence how listeners perceive and evaluate AI-generated music, surpassing other factors such as composer identity, genre preferences, or personal listening habits. Looking forward, it will be crucial to monitor how societal attitudes toward artificial intelligence evolve, as these attitudes are likely to significantly impact acceptance or resistance in cultural domains like music. Ultimately, the debate is not merely about the recognizability or enjoyment of AI-generated music as human-like, but about redefining what constitutes "authentic" and "valuable" creativity. By critically engaging with audience responses, we can delve into questions such as what forms of (artificial) creativity deserve acknowledgment, and under what conditions.
