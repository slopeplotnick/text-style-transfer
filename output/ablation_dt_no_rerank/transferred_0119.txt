Benchmarks. OMNIGUARD is evaluated using a diverse suite of public safety benchmarks across both unimodal and cross-modal settings. For the unimodal setting, performance is assessed across four modalities — text, image, video, and audio. In the text domain, datasets such as BeaverTails (Ji et al., 2023), ToxicChat (Lin et al., 2023), WildGuardMix (Han et al., 2024), Aegis2.0 (Ghosh et al., 2025), and the OpenAI Moderation dataset (Markov et al., 2023) are utilized. Meanwhile, for image analysis, UnsafeBench (Qu et al., 2024), VLGuard (Zong et al., 2024), and LlavaGuard (Helff et al., 2025a) are employed. Video evaluations are conducted using SafeSora (Dai et al., 2024a) and SafeWatch-Bench (Chen et al., 2025), whereas the audio analysis makes use of the MuTox English split (Costa-jussà et al., 2024) and WildGuardMix-TTS. The latter is constructed by transforming WildGuardMix (Han et al., 2024) test samples into speech via a text-to-speech pipeline consistent with our dataset construction methodology. For cross-modal settings, OMNIGUARD is tested in three configurations: image-text, video-text, and audio-text, corresponding to MM-SafetyBench (Liu et al., 2024b), VideoSafetyBench (Liu et al., 2025a), and AIAH (Yang et al., 2025), respectively. We compare OMNIGUARD against a comprehensive suite of baselines encompassing models across all modalities, including LLMs, VLMs, and audio LLMs. More importantly, these comparisons involve both large-scale and compact state-of-the-art models to gauge their safeguarding capabilities, with details presented in Table 7. Uni-Modality. OMNI-GUARD's performance is benchmarked against both proprietary and open-source state-of-the-art models in four unimodal safety scenarios: text (Table 1), image (Table 2), video (Table 3), and audio (Table 4). Both variants, OMNI-GUARD-7B and OMNI-GUARD-3B, consistently achieve top results across all modalities. Though OMNI-GUARD-7B excels as the superior model, the smaller OMNI-GUARD-3B also outperforms or matches larger models like GPT-4o, Qwen3-235B, and Qwen3-VL-235B, underscoring the efficacy of our omni-modal safety alignment strategy.
