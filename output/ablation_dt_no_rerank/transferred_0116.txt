Data sampling. Utilizing Taobao search logs, we begin by categorizing the data into industries such as consumer electronics and apparel, then conduct stratified sampling according to the proportion of each industry. This step is crucial to ensure that the training data comprehensively covers attributes and features across all industries. Moreover, within each industry, the data is divided into high, medium, and low-frequency categories based on query occurrence rates, allowing for sampling that reflects these inherent patterns and ensures inclusion of both prevalent high-frequency queries and rare long-tail samples. The data is then allocated to specialized annotators for labeling. Data cleaning. Errors in manual annotation are primarily due to two aspects: first, annotators occasionally overlook certain details, particularly with lengthy multi-attribute queries. Second, the annotation rules themselves are often insufficiently defined, as they fail to accommodate all scenarios, reflecting user preferences ambiguously. To address these challenges, powerful language models are leveraged for data filtration. Specifically: (1) the attribute matching model adheres to strict matching protocols, capturing fine-grained details more effectively than manual annotation in complex multi-attribute scenarios; (2) by integrating comprehensive annotation rules within prompts and directing the model to engage in reasoning and judgment as instructed, corner cases beyond the predefined scope are identified and filtered. Two models are employed due to their complementary strengths: one excels in comprehending extended textual content, while the other shows proficiency in interpreting diverse data types. Therefore, to enhance the reasoning capability of the model, a progressive pipeline is implemented, encompassing three sequential stages: knowledge injection and reasoning, multimodal comprehension, and rule awareness. Firstly, user requirements are deconstructed into attributes for systematic comparison with items. Subsequently, the degree to which items fulfill search requirements is assessed through comprehensive evaluation based on item characteristics and predefined criteria.
