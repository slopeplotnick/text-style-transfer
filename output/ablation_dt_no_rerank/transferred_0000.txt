Long-Tail Hard subset This subset is crafted to challenge advanced language models by providing complex examples that are difficult to resolve. We concentrated on identifying these hard cases, categorizing them into explicit demands characterized by Multi-Attribute (MR) requests, and implicit demands. The implicit demands comprise four distinct types: Negation (NE), Alternatives (AL), Knowledge-dependent (KD), and Reasoning-dependent (RD). Each category required a tailored mining process. NE and AL queries were efficiently filtered using predefined keywords and regular expressions. For the more elusive KD and RD queries, we first undersampled low-frequency logs and then applied a robust language model, Qwen3-235B, to ensure self-consistency by requiring four identical results across eight inferences. In identifying MR queries, we utilized an NER model on length-filtered queries to detect those with more than five attributes. To validate the complexity of the final dataset, all candidates were filtered through a baseline relevance model; consequently, we retained only samples where the model correctly predicted the outcome five or fewer times across eight attempts, indicating considerable model uncertainty. Visual Salience subset This subset identifies cases where the primary product image offers crucial, fine-grained visual details, such as color and style, essential for accurate interpretation. We implemented a keyword-based retrieval strategy using a detailed visual taxonomy. To ensure the necessity of visual attributes and reduce noise from false recalls, we applied two essential filters: initially, a predetermined blacklist and subsequently retaining only query-ad combinations where the prominent visual keyword was absent from the product's textual description. An LLM was then employed to confirm that the remaining keywords genuinely represented visual properties. Ultimately, to spot inherently difficult examples, we trained a naive multimodal relevance model, retaining candidates that revealed high prediction uncertainty, with consistency of less than four in a majority vote across eight predictions.
