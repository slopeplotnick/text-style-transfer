Our methodology employed label-driven Chain-of-Thought (CoT) generation combined with hardcase mining. By integrating label-constrained CoT synthesis with the excavation of difficult samples, we enhanced the model's reasoning capabilities through structured multi-step inference patterns that adeptly navigated challenging edge cases. Moreover, we implemented hardcase-focused evaluation protocols specifically designed to rigorously assess performance in complex semantic scenarios, ensuring robust handling of intricate reasoning requirements. These innovations facilitated the online distillation deployment, where the CoT-based multi-dimensional knowledge system was successfully distilled and transferred to smaller models. This achievement marked a significant milestone in scaling deep reasoning capabilities within production environments, ultimately realizing substantial performance improvements in online A/B testing scenarios. Stepping deeper into the realm of complex challenges, our strategy confronted two paramount issues: first, the reliance on visual information for product attribution understanding, especially when user-uploaded images are often characterized by insufficient textual context; and second, during the path selection process, judgment outcomes must strictly adhere to formats encompassing numerous fine-grained rules and detailed business standards aligned with observable attributes. To address these challenges, we pursued a comprehensive technical strategy that integrated multimodal understanding capabilities with rule-aware reasoning mechanisms, enabling the model to process visual information while maintaining stringent adherence to business constraints. We employed a hybrid paradigm, combining CoT Generation, SFT, and Reinforcement Learning built upon both Qwen-2.5 LLM for text reasoning and Qwen-2.5 VL for multimodal understanding. This dual-model architecture facilitated seamless integration of visual and textual reasoning pathways, effectively breaking through single-modality limitations. Furthermore, our methodology leveraged multimodal and rule-aware CoT generation, synthesizing image-text paired understanding with standard-oriented prompt engineering. Business rules and exemplar cases were embedded into CoT reasoning chains to establish robust rule comprehension logic. Thereafter, reinforcement learning was introduced to significantly enhance the model's generalized reasoning capabilities, thereby improving the application of active rules to path judgments under complex attribute scenarios.
