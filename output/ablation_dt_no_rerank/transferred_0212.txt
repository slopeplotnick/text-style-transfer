**Training-free, schedule-based early exit.** We propose SchED (Schedule-based Early Decoding) for diffusion language models, which enables early termination without additional training. SchED employs a schedule, such as linear or cosine, to determine stopping points based on the model's confidence level, ensuring compatibility with diverse architectural configurations. By evaluating the model's evolving confidence, it becomes evident that instructional enhancements promote stabilization, aligning with the schedule and facilitating early terminations without sacrificing quality. SchED operates by applying a dynamic confidence threshold that evolves during text generation, exploiting the diffusion models' inherent advantages to enhance efficiency without retraining. The implementation code is publicly accessible. **Diffusion language models.** These models represent a contemporary alternative to autoregressive models, capable of both de novo training and adapting existing autoregressive checkpoints. LLaDA employs a bidirectional strategy, masking text in one direction and correcting it in the reverse, leading to impressive pretraining and fine-tuning results. In efforts to transform autoregressive models into diffusion models for fair comparisons across varied model sizes and tasks, methodologies have been developed. Meanwhile, Dream-7B focuses on refining recipes and releasing different model variants. **Architectural advancements.** For example, LLaDA-MoE leverages sparse Mixture-of-Experts to achieve optimal performance with fewer active components. Recent advancements, such as UltraLLaDA, showcase diffusion models' capacity to handle extensive textual data, incorporating RoPE scaling to support up to 128K tokens effectively. Concurrently, Seed Diffusion emphasizes enhancing throughput in code models. Moreover, formal analyses of these models' scaling properties reveal an expansion rate equivalent to autoregressive models. Scholarly reviews highlight advantages such as parallelization, filling informational gaps, and managing broader contexts, while also addressing unresolved questions.
