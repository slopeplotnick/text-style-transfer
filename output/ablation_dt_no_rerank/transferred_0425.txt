In this section, we discuss the limitations of TaleFrame related to interaction modes and advanced narrative structures, as well as future research directions. TaleFrame currently operates on desktops and laptops, utilizing a combination of mouse and keyboard inputs for precise control, which is essential for connecting entities, identifying relationships, and linking events to recognize sequences. Despite positive feedback for the drag-and-drop feature, its effectiveness diminishes on mobile devices like smartphones and tablets due to their smaller screens. Though touch pens and finger inputs can substitute the mouse, long interaction processes and input complexities can lead to accidental touches that impair the user experience on these devices. To address these challenges, we have optimized information visualization through color coding, icons, and connecting lines while implementing adaptive layout resizing to accommodate various screen sizes. Looking ahead, we plan to explore and define a broader range of interaction styles—such as touchscreens, keyboard inputs, and voice commands—to create a flexible framework that facilitates diverse user engagement across different devices. In terms of narrative structures, TaleFrame struggles to handle complex event relationships that transcend linear temporal frameworks, particularly with causal links, flashbacks, and other intricate storytelling elements. Moreover, entity relationships add layers of complexity that impact the coherence and accuracy of event connections. Although we have attempted to improve the modeling of event dependencies, handling multi-layered event interweaving and interactions among various entities remains challenging. As the complexity of story plots and entity relationships increases, the model faces greater difficulties in understanding and generating event sequences, leading to incoherent or logically unclear narratives. Therefore, in future work, we aim to enhance entity relationship modeling and optimize strategies for generating non-linear narrative structures. These efforts are intended to improve the model's performance in complex story generation, ultimately providing users with a richer and more coherent narrative experience.
