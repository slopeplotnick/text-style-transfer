In our experimental framework, we employed seven methodologies to integrate models, namely Linear interpolation, Karcher Mean, SLERP, NuSLERP, TIES, DELLA, and Nearswap. These techniques facilitated the amalgamation of a bias-inverted model with a pre-trained Large Language Model. The Linear method, as delineated by Wortsman et al. (2022), offers the simplest approach by aggregating and averaging weights from fine-tuned models, providing a cost-effective and efficient solution. In contrast, the Karcher Mean, introduced by Goddard et al. in 2024, adopts a different paradigm by considering the curved geometry of model parameter spaces, thereby enhancing performance in non-Euclidean domains. Moreover, SLERP (Spherical Linear Interpolation), also described by Goddard in 2024, navigates model weight interpolation through a curved trajectory on a spherical manifold, preserving the geometric configuration of the space. Expanding on this concept, NuSLERP, introduced by Goddard in 2024, adapts SLERP by enabling differential integration within model components. This focused approach highlights the most significant elements, optimizing balance and enhancing performance. Alternatively, TIES, suggested by Yadav in 2023, merges models by emphasizing discrepant task-specific parameters, mitigating interference while preserving the individual strengths of each model. Similarly, DELLA, proposed by Deep in 2024, reduces interference by selectively pruning less critical parameter updates through magnitude-aware rescaling, thus preserving essential components and sustaining the original model's performance.
