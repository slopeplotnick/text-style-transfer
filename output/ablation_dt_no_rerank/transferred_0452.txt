Experts in numerical modeling transform high-level hypotheses into numerical representations, where the overarching conceptual hypothesis is translated into multiple quantitative hypotheses, each annotated with specific model parametrization details. Hypotheses are assigned discrete probabilities, while intricate details necessitate probability distributions. This prior distribution model must be tested against data, an endeavor that involves numerical modeling—often termed forward modeling—by domain experts familiar with these data sets. Statistical falsification tests, utilizing statistical metrics and p-values, are employed for this validation. Moreover, domain experts in Monte Carlo simulation utilize Bayesian inversion to refine model hypotheses and variables in light of the data. Decision-making hinges upon Bayesian uncertainty quantification, emphasizing the need for comprehensive interdisciplinary collaboration among all domain experts to achieve optimal outcomes.
