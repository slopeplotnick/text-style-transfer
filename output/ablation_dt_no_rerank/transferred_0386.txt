We propose Radiologist Copilot, a sophisticated tool that aids radiologists in achieving automated radiology reporting with integrated quality control through an agentic framework with coordinated tools. This system, leveraging a reasoning backbone, autonomously selects the appropriate tools, formulates plans, and executes tasks, thereby emulating the radiologist’s role to complete the entire reporting process. The tool library includes functionalities such as region localization, analysis planning, strategic template selection, quality assessment, and feedback-driven adaptive refinement, enabling comprehensive radiology reporting. Experimental results demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms other leading methodologies in this domain, highlighting the necessity of automated reporting with robust quality control mechanisms, particularly given the time-intensive nature of 3D medical imaging modalities. For instance, CT2Rep utilizes an advanced auto-regressive causal transformer architecture alongside relational memory to generate reports for 3D chest CT images. Similarly, Reg2RG employs a region-guided framework for the generation of chest CT reports. However, report generation is merely one facet of the broader radiology reporting process and often lacks essential quality control measures. To address these gaps, Medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have been proposed to facilitate various medical tasks, including report generation and visual question answering. Notably, 3D medical VLMs have shown effectiveness in processing 3D medical imaging techniques such as CT and MRI. RadFM introduces a versatile vision-language foundation model that integrates both 2D and 3D medical data for radiological applications. Furthermore, M3D presents a comprehensive multi-modal medical dataset, M3D-Data, alongside a multi-modal large-language model, M3D-LaMed, designed for analyzing 3D medical imagery. Merlin constructs a 3D Vision-Language Model (VLM) that effectively integrates both structured and unstructured data to support the interpretation of abdominal CT scans. Meanwhile, CT-CHAT serves as a specialized vision-language foundation model, designed specifically for 3D chest CT volumes and integrating the CT-CLIP vision encoder with a pretrained large language model. Additionally, Med3DVLM is a 3D VLM that enhances multimodal representations through an efficient encoder, a contrastive learning strategy, and a dual-stream projector. Lastly, Hulu-Med offers a comprehensive medical vision-language model that seamlessly incorporates text, 2D and 3D imagery, as well as video, within a unified architecture.
