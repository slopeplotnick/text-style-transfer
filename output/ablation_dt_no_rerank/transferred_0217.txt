In Section 3, we explained how the questions align with four primary themes relevant to the research. Since most interviewees were unfamiliar with SPLE, we clarified its concepts and significance during the interviews. This explanation might have introduced some bias; however, we trust our interviewees to form their own opinions independently and the interviewer to maintain minimal influence. The questions were intentionally open-ended to encourage comprehensive responses and capture diverse ideas. Each question was carefully designed to ensure they did not limit the scope of potential answers. Subsequently, metrics were established to optimize the data collected and facilitate the extraction of pertinent information. These metrics quantify the frequency of significant terms, both in aggregate and by specific roles. While the tables provide exhaustive information, the graphs illustrate the distribution of these values. Additionally, even unique responses, not echoed by others, were considered in our analysis. We meticulously analyzed each response. Terms extracted from the interviews were systematically aligned with those prevalent in the Software Product Line Engineering (SPLE) domain, such as "personalisation" and "making specific some parts of the code," akin to "customization." This alignment enhanced the consistency of our findings. Moreover, aligning these terms aided in identifying supporting arguments within the scholarly literature, effectively addressing stakeholders' concerns. Engaging in this iterative process with various participants gradually improved the fluidity of the interviews. Although the questions themselves remained unchanged, our method of presenting them evolved over time. For instance, we began offering additional hints and elaborating on certain aspects when participants appeared uncertain and enhancing the transitions between questions for a smoother flow. Nonetheless, we assert that the quantity and quality of data obtained from both the initial and final questionnaires remained consistent.
