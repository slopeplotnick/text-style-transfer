RQ1. Process-centric Metrics. What are the GRAPHETORY characteristics of programming agents concerning process-centric metrics? What factors impact these metric values? RQ2. Analysis of Problem-solving Strategies. What are the most common problem-solving strategies utilized by programming agents? Additionally, what factors affect their strategic behavior, and how consistently do agents adhere to expected planning? Moreover, to what extent do agents evolve or alter their problem-solving strategies during execution? RQ3. Inefficiency Pattern Analysis. What are the common GRAPHORY (anti-)patterns prevalent among the agents' behaviors, and how frequent are these patterns within their operations? Our study focuses on two autonomous, open-process, and widely used programming agents: SWE-agent (SA) and OpenHands (OH) for this evaluation. Accordingly, we adhere to each framework's default configurations for our experiments: SWE-agent operates under a per-instance cost cap of $2, while OpenHands runs for a maximum of 100 trajectory iterations, with both utilizing their default file-viewing and code-editing tools. Furthermore, we pair the agents with four LLMs: DeepSeek-V3 671B MoE (DSK-V3) [14], DeepSeek-R1 (DSK-R1) [18], Devstral-small-2505 24B coding-specialized open weights (Dev) [7], and Claude Sonnet 4 (CLD-4) [9]. Our choice of LLMs includes both general-purpose and reasoning models, as well as frontier and open-source models, resulting in eight distinct ⟨agent, model⟩ configurations for analysis, thereby enabling a comprehensive investigation of various training strategies.
