In the context of online shopping, consumers often express complex requests involving attributes such as brand specificity, pricing constraints, functional characteristics, and intended recipient demographics. Therefore, it is crucial for the system to simultaneously ascertain and systematically organize these multiple parameters. This integrative approach allows for the effective curation and presentation of selections that align with the user’s preferences and requirements. However, determining the extent to which different data modalities, such as textual content or imagery, are used should not be based solely on the user’s query. Instead, the system must analyze the specifics extracted from the inquiry and align them with item characteristics, subsequently using these details strategically to determine the content presented to the user. Essentially, the system connects a user's query to corresponding items by deconstructing the question into specific components and then using these to filter and select relevant items. This process unfolds in two stages: first, ensuring the system accurately interprets the question and associates it with pertinent item information, and second, ensuring that appropriate items are selected based on this interpretation. The complexity of these steps presents challenges and may lead to ambiguity. For instance, when a query like "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set" is submitted, the system must discern the user’s intent and match it with the appropriate product attributes. Initially, this entails correlating the query’s specifications with item descriptions, with the primary challenge lying in accurately aligning these components.
