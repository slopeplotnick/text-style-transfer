To refine the results, we repeated the analyses using the ATTARI subscales. Specifically, only the behavioral subscale (BEH) emerged as a significant predictor, whereas the affective and cognitive subscales fell short when entered simultaneously (see supplements S3). This suggests that the predictive strength of AI attitudes is primarily driven by participants' practical willingness to engage with AI, rather than overarching beliefs or feelings. To complement these quantitative findings and understand the participants' perceptions more deeply, we conducted a thematic analysis of 178 open-ended responses. An overview of the main themes is presented in Table 2, with the full codebook available in the Supplementary Materials. Participants frequently categorized AI-generated music by its technical and musical quality, originality, and its capacity to evoke emotions. Humanness emerged as the most frequent theme, transcending mere sound characteristics. Many respondents emphasized the importance of the human element, pointing out that an artist's personality and the story behind a song influenced their judgments, and described AI-generated music as lacking soul and authenticity. However, some participants expressed indifference toward the music's origin, as evidenced by one participant's remark, "If it sounds good then I don't mind if it was artificially generated" (ID926). Furthermore, the open-ended responses offered additional evidence that attitudes toward AI play a significant role in how listeners evaluate AI-generated music. Many comments focused on normative stances rather than the songs' perceived quality or characteristics, with one participant stating they "don't like anything to do with AI-generated music" (ID914). Beyond these dimensions, participants highlighted ethical considerations influencing their evaluations. These included concerns about replacing musicians, potential economic harm to artists, and the fairness of training data usage ("is built upon data without the consent of the artists that have dedicated decades of their lives to the craft", ID615). For instance, a participant noted, "I think it undermines and abuses artists" (ID446), showing that listeners are not only concerned with the aesthetic aspects of AI music but also with the equity of its production and distribution processes.
