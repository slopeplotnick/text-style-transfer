This method aims to enhance fairness across tasks and groups by directly optimizing balanced accuracy. In contrast to conventional CE loss, it specifically addresses disparities in performance between dominant and minority cohorts, thereby improving consistency in multi-task fine-tuning. An ablation study was conducted to evaluate the comparative effectiveness of various training methodologies. Specifically, we examined the effects of single-task versus multi-task fine-tuning methodologies on mental health prediction capabilities. Additionally, we developed a mental health assessment application for iOS utilizing llama.cpp<sup>1</sup> as the inference backend, leveraging GGUF V3-quantized models optimized for mobile devices. The transformation of models from PyTorch to the GGUF format was implemented via a specialized conversion pipeline, facilitating efficient execution on both CPUs and GPUs. The application was developed using Swift/SwiftUI and integrates llama.cpp through a C++ bridge layer to perform direct inference within the iOS runtime, eliminating the need for external servers. At runtime, matrix operations are accelerated using Apple Metal, with automatic CPU fallback when GPU utilization reaches saturation. Moreover, thread-level parallelism is employed through llama.cpp's integrated thread scheduling API, supporting up to eight threads to ensure optimal performance across Apple's efficiency and performance cores. Furthermore, model weights are strategically memory-mapped to minimize RAM utilization and facilitate the lazy loading of tensors. System stabilization is achieved through a 4,096-token context window, combined with efficient cache management and batched inference processes, thus mitigating fragmentation issues during extended input processing. Additionally, long social media posts are truncated to a maximum of 6,000 characters. Three models, namely Menta (2.33 GB), Phi-4 Mini (2.40 GB), and Qwen-3 (2.30 GB), were tested on an iPhone 15 Pro Max utilizing open-source code. The deployment framework is compatible with various quantization methods, providing a balance between latency and precision. For the Menta model, we employed four high-quality corpora for the detection of depression, stress, and suicidal ideation sourced from the social media platform Reddit. In this case, disorder-related samples were systematically excluded, and the remaining data underwent annotation by domain experts. Finally, the investigation utilized the aforementioned datasets.
