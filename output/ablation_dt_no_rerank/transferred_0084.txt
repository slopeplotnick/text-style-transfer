The coherence dimension of our system was designed to identify obvious errors that are recognizable by the general population, rather than undertaking deeper scientific analyses or evaluating motion dynamics. In contrast, our dataset prioritizes complex physical phenomena and scientific principles. Therefore, employing explicit physics decomposition using domain-specific checklists yields more reliable results than general-purpose rationales, emphasizing the need for specialized evaluation frameworks in assessing sophisticated physical reasoning within video production. VideoScore2 is consequently not ideally suited for our benchmark due to its requirement for stringent, scientifically grounded evaluations, which leads to weaker alignment with human judgments on this dataset.
