All prompts were crafted in the first language (L1) of each language team, despite the English interface of the prompt creation platform, ensuring no translation was needed for generating unscripted prompts. Once a team member completed a domain path, they formulated the prompt in their first language. Prior to this task, all team members engaged in a preliminary sample project to familiarize themselves with the assignment requirements. Subsequently, each language team produced at least 2,000 unscripted prompts upon understanding the project objectives. Scripted prompts came from a variety of culturally pertinent and domain-specific sources, enriching the content. These included Vukuzenzele, a government publication detailing public services; Wikipedia entries in South African languages; a comprehensive database for precise vocabulary selection, such as African Wordnet; and translated manuals concerning animal health and agricultural practices. The data for prompts underwent meticulous cleaning and preparation to optimize it for training speech recognition models. Each phase enhanced the dataset by systematically removing extraneous elements, categorizing text according to specific themes, and identifying or annotating any instances of personal information. Upon acquisition, preliminary quality assessments were executed, encompassing reviews to detect and address structural anomalies. The text was filtered to retain only the desired South African languages, and while instances where the top-ranked language did not align with the expected category were excluded, confidence scores were preserved as supplementary information.
