This section delves into models that enable seamless, high-quality translations by integrating both speech and text modalities across languages. It provides a comprehensive exploration of how state-of-the-art voice-interfaced language models process and comprehend speech and conversational signals. Over the past decade, there has been significant progress in the creation and deployment of large-scale voice-powered AI virtual assistants, beginning with Apple's Siri, followed by Microsoft's Cortana, Google Assistant, Amazon's Alexa, and Samsung's Bixby. This part of the tutorial elaborates on the technological innovations underpinning these AI virtual assistants, such as intent detection and slot filling facilitated by specific neural network architectures, and dialogue management through reinforcement learning methodologies. Moreover, it discusses the exploitation of web-scale query-click data, the semantic web, and the integration of knowledge graphs for enhanced spoken language understanding. Presently, the advancement of AI virtual assistants is characterized by leveraging large language models to improve the precision of spoken language comprehension, dialogue state tracking, and language generation capabilities. This tutorial also provides an exhaustive overview of existing large language models and outlines strategies for their adaptation to specialized downstream applications. It highlights recent progress in multi-conversational systems, covering both general and domain-specific tasks, including a comprehensive analysis of relevant datasets and evaluative methodologies. Furthermore, emerging research challenges in refining language models are explored, underscoring the increasing demand for systems capable of managing simultaneous conversational threads.
