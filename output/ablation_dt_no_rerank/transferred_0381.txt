Our methodology seamlessly integrates with cutting-edge high-throughput data processing frameworks, such as vLLM (Kwon et al., 2023) and SGLang (Zheng et al., 2024), and proves effective for both real-time service and offline batch processing. In Section 4.2, the two-stage vLLM framework is primarily employed due to its superior processing efficiency and ability to manage reasoning trace. THINKMERGE is tested under two distinct scenarios. First, for tasks characterized by a single definitive solution, we evaluate on AIME 2025 (MAA, 2025) and GPQA Diamond (Rein et al., 2024), allowing the aggregation of various responses through majority voting. Next, for tasks lacking a singular correct answer, our evaluation includes LiveCodeBench v5 (2024.10-2025.02) (Jain et al., 2024), BrowseComp-en (Wei et al., 2025), BrowseComp-zh (Zhou et al., 2025), GAIA (Mialon et al., 2023), and xBench-DeepSearch (Chen et al., 2025a), focusing on generating coherent and quality resolutions. In terms of closed-ended tasks, we performed five experimental trials assessing models such as Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-14B (Yang et al., 2025a), and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B (Guo et al., 2025). Conversely, for open-ended coding tasks, our methodology incorporates models like DeepCoder-14B-Preview (Luo et al., 2025), Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-Coder-30A3B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B-Thinking (0725), and Qwen3-Think-30A3B (Yang et al., 2025a). For open-ended deep research agent tasks, we apply WebSailor models, specifically WebSailor-3B, WebSailor-7B, and WebSailor-32B (Li et al., 2025a). The maximum sequence length is established at 32,768 tokens, with detailed sampling hyperparameters available in Appendix A.1. Moreover, we explore THINKMERGE's applicability in research-intensive contexts where integrating reasoning and tool use is essential before response generation. Specifically, we evaluate three Tongyi-WebSailor models—namely, WebSailor-3B, WebSailor-7B, and WebSailor-32B—across four demanding web-based benchmarks: BrowseComp-en, BrowseComp-zh, GAIA, and XbenchDeepSearch. BrowseComp-en and BrowseComp-zh focus on complex, elusive factual inquiries in English and Chinese, respectively. To ensure size comparability with BrowseComp, 200 questions were randomly sampled from BrowseComp-en's extensive dataset of 1,266 questions. For GAIA, robust tool usage is required for real-world multi-step tasks; hence, following previous work (Li et al., 2025b), we evaluate the 103 text-only validation cases. XbenchDeepSearch targets professional-style, deep information retrieval. We utilize the WebSailor agent pipeline with recommended decoding hyperparameters: a temperature of 0.6, a top-p of 0.95, and a context length of 32,768 tokens.
