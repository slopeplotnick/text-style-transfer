The interviews will focus on the following questions: ① Which aspects of your thinking are the most challenging to articulate during the creative process? ② Which of the four foundational units is the most helpful in facilitating your creative process? ③ Do you encounter any challenges, and how do you address them? ④ Do new insights emerge during the creative process? Participants (P1-P4) demonstrated a preference for narrative development using a drag-and-drop card interface over text-based outlining methods, attributing increased enjoyment to the former. However, some participants expressed concerns regarding the iterative nature of editing unit attributes (P1, P4). P1 noted, "When constructing a narrative, my initial approach is to concentrate on the overarching framework, starting with event cards and entity cards to outline the narrative's structure. Then I incorporate specific attributes into the event and entity cards." P4 added, "During the narrative development process, multiple revisions were necessary. If attribute editing were more convenient, I would be more inclined to employ this method." Moreover, several volunteers suggested that certain semantic information within the narrative might be effectively conveyed through interactive methods (P2, P3). P2 said, "I prefer positioning entity cards involved in the same event above the event cards. This method allows me to easily identify which entities participate in the event." P3 proposed, "Using relationship cards to connect different entity cards could be beneficial. If event cards were organized with connecting lines, it would more effectively demonstrate the sequence and flow of events." Building on these insights, we identify significant interaction patterns (Figure 2) and propose three design goals: DG1: The system should facilitate input of attributes for each foundational unit, aiding the user in constructing the initial narrative framework. DG2: The system should assist users in comprehending the overarching narrative structure, facilitating the analysis of relationships between entities and interconnections among events. DG3: The system should provide assessments and specific recommendations aimed at enhancing the existing story framework.
