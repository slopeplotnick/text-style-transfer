This plugin-style architecture promotes code reusability and enables fast prototyping of novel PEFT methods. Researchers can develop and test new methods separately, with the framework automatically integrating them at runtime. Moreover, the dynamic loading approach has proven particularly valuable for comparative studies, allowing researchers to evaluate multiple PEFT variants under identical experimental conditions without code duplication or version control conflicts. Building on the SuperGLUE benchmark, we include seven datasets divided into four tasks, namely natural language inference (NLI) - CB; question answering - MultiRC, ReCoRD, BoolQ, and COPA; word sense disambiguation - WiC; and coreference resolution - WSC. Furthermore, we have integrated text generation datasets, which are commonly used to benchmark generative LLMs. Additionally, we cover six datasets for reasoning and natural language understanding, separated into three tasks: question answering – MMLU, PIQA, SIQA, and OBQA; natural language inference (NLI) – Hellaswag; and commonsense reasoning – WinoGrande. Furthermore, three datasets are included for mathematical problem solving, each categorized into distinct tasks: question answering – MathQA; mathematical word problems – GSM8K; and simple mathematical problems – SVAMP. In parallel, there are three datasets for code generation: Conala, CodeAlpacaPy, and APPS. However, some datasets may require minor format modifications to be compatible with the input formatting of PEFT-FACTORY. Further details on this subject are provided in Appendix B.2.
