The advent of AI agents has brought forth capabilities like autonomous reasoning, planning, and tool usage. Recently, medical agents have been introduced to tackle complex medical tasks. For instance, MMedAgent employs a multi-modal approach to handle tasks such as radiology report generation for chest X-rays (CXR), leveraging various tools. MedRAX offers a specialized framework for interpreting chest X-rays by integrating advanced CXR analysis tools. CT-Agent is a token-efficient and anatomy-aware agent for 3D CT scan visualization, which promotes effective reasoning. However, when it comes to radiology report generation (RRG), these methods generally rely on a single tool and lack cooperation among tools. In contrast, our Radiologist Copilot provides a diverse and comprehensive toolset, facilitating robust inter-tool collaboration and enabling more complex tool planning and execution. Radiology reporting is crucial yet time-consuming and prone to errors, especially with 3D imaging modalities. Furthermore, rigorous quality assurance is a critical, albeit laborious, step to ensure clinical validity, often neglected in previous studies. To address these challenges, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an AI agentic assistant equipped with a systematically orchestrated set of tools for automated radiology reporting and quality control, aimed at supporting radiologists and boosting clinical efficiency. As depicted in the accompanying figure, Radiologist Copilot is designed to assist radiologists through the comprehensive radiology reporting process. Initially, it initializes the extensive tool library, engaging both the action planner and the executor to strategically plan and execute necessary actions. The memory module precisely records executed actions and their outcomes, facilitating the verification of task completion. The action executor is responsible for generating and executing commands based on the next action determined by the planner; it formulates the appropriate command for the selected tool, executes it, and evaluates the resultant outcome for each succeeding action.
