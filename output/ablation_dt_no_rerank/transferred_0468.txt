We introduced promptsolution, a unified and modular open-source Python framework for automatic prompt optimization. It is specifically designed for practitioners requiring single-task prompt refinement and researchers engaging in large-scale benchmark studies. Our framework is distinguished by its user-friendly, flexible architecture and low abstraction level, with a particular focus on optimizing prompts. Although not every optimizer included in promptsolution performs optimally on every task, notably with examples such as OPRO on GSM8K, the library consistently offers at least one effective optimizer per task. This modular design facilitates easy switching to an alternative optimizer if the current one yields unsatisfactory results. Moreover, the framework's inherent design supports systematic benchmark experiments, requiring minimal manual effort for optimizer comparisons. Through a detailed demonstration, we elucidate the interaction between components in the framework, showcasing how they collectively bolster effective prompt optimization. In our comparative evaluation, we confirmed the utility and competitiveness of promptsolution against other prompt optimization tools. Looking to the future, we aim to enhance interoperability with high-level frameworks like DSPy, harnessing strengths from both ecosystems. Additionally, to simplify the management of complex experimental setups, we plan to integrate with configuration management tools such as hydra. To improve user accessibility, we are developing a lightweight graphical interface for real-time tracking and visual analysis of experiments. In response to the growing prominence of multi-agent systems, we seek to optimize system prompts and interaction protocols across multiple agents. Inspired by AutoML, we are exploring ensembling strategies for prompt optimizers, akin to ELPO. More broadly, the framework's extensibility ensures our capability to incorporate innovative optimization methods as the field progresses.
