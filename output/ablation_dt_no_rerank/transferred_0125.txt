OMNI-GUARD-7B consistently surpasses strong baselines in executing safety tasks across the modalities of text, vision, and audio. It notably outperforms various recent multimodal large language models (MLLMs) and omni-modal language models (OLLMs) that have been developed with safety alignment, as well as state-of-the-art specialized guardrail models. The more compact OMNI-GUARD-3B also demonstrates competitive performance, achieving results comparable or superior to recent MLLMs or OLLMs such as GPT-4o and Qwen3. Moreover, empirical analysis indicates that the use of reasoning-based omni-modal guardrails enhances the reliability, consistency, and explainability of moderation processes, marking a significant advancement toward achieving safe and reliable omni-modal AIs. In summary, our contributions are as follows: We introduce OMNIGUARD, the first family of omnimodal guardrail models that can perform unified safety moderation across text, images, videos, and audio with deliberate reasoning. Additionally, we establish a unified training framework that employs omni-modal targeted distillation, endowing the model with deliberate and explainable omni-modal safety reasoning capabilities. Extensive experiments further demonstrate that OMNIGUARD achieves state-of-the-art accuracy, robust generalization, and enhanced explainability compared to prior guardrails. The development of MLLMs has fostered increased interest in OLLMs, which can process and generate outputs across multiple modalities simultaneously. Unlike earlier practices involving separately pretrained unimodal components, OLLMs are trained end-to-end on multimodal data, thereby acquiring native proficiencies for unified interpretation and production of content across text, audio, images, and video modalities. This prevailing paradigm involves mapping heterogeneous inputs into a shared latent space, which aligns different modalities and facilitates cross-modality reasoning. Models such as Qwen2.5-Omni and LLaMA-Omni offer real-time, end-to-end streaming generation of both text and speech, while NExT-OMNI extends capabilities further into comprehensive cross-modal generation and understanding. Nevertheless, these OLMs face safety issues due to parameter misalignment, which can lead to potentially hazardous scenarios.
