Mathematical Reasoning. Our investigation focused on the performance of models in solving mathematical problems using five distinct benchmarks: AMC, Minerva (Lewkowycz et al., 2022), MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021), and Olympiad-Bench (He et al., 2024). To evaluate these benchmarks, we employed GPT-4o, in line with prior methodologies, to verify the correctness of final answers against the ground truth. General Domain Reasoning. To assess general reasoning capabilities, we evaluated model performance on a spectrum of disciplines, including physics, biology, business, economics, and law, using benchmarks such as MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024), SuperGPQA (Du et al., 2025), GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al.), and BBEH (shoaa kazemi et al., 2025). These benchmarks encompass challenging tasks that provide a stricter evaluation of complex reasoning abilities. We measured accuracy using the Exact Match (EM) metric, derived through greedy decoding. To evaluate the significance of individual model components, we conducted ablation studies on the Qwen3-8B-Base model by selectively deactivating features and observing performance impacts on both mathematical and general reasoning tasks. The results, summarized in Table 2, reveal that disabling challenger training leads to the most significant performance decline, reducing Math and General averages by 1.9 and 1.0 points, respectively. Similarly, removing challenger warm-up and curriculum learning detrimentally affects performance, highlighting their critical importance. Notably, mathematics tasks demonstrate greater sensitivity to variability than general tasks, a pattern consistent with the observations in Table 1, where mathematical scores show greater variance depending on the methods employed. Overall, these findings underscore that while integrating heterogeneous human data offers general performance improvements, domain-specific sampling is particularly effective in strengthening the corresponding domain. This insight is crucial as it empowers developers to strategically steer model calibration by selecting specific data types, thereby mitigating the risk of deviation from its intended learning trajectory.
