Other promising prompt optimizers not yet implemented in promptsolution include GEPA (Agrawal et al., 2025), TextGrad (Yuksekgonul et al., 2025), MIPRO (Opsahl-Ong et al., 2024), and PromptWizard (Agarwal et al., 2024). Most of these tools are primarily accessible in research environments characterized by infrequent updates, inadequate documentation, and insufficient validation. Therefore, they are inherently challenging to use for both scientific benchmarks and practical applications. In contrast, the landscape of prompt optimization has seen the rise of numerous actively maintained libraries and tools, encompassing both open-source and commercial offerings. Among open-source solutions, several libraries are designed to work only with specific optimizers, such as TextGrad, AdalFlow, Microsoft's PromptWizard, and PromptIM, each adopting unique optimization strategies. While the prompt-ops library supports two optimizers, its focus is on enhancing prompt efficiency for Llama models. Conversely, promptsolution offers a versatile framework enabling the integration of various optimizers for diverse LLM applications. One of the most prominent utilities for constructing LLM configurations is DSPy, which provides a dedicated segment for optimizing prompts, incorporating optimizers like MIPROv2 and GEPA. This synergy integrates LLM training processes with prompt optimization methodologies. While DSPy serves as a general-purpose tool for AI application development, promptsolution is specifically focused on prompt optimization, making it particularly advantageous for researchers and specialists engaged in comprehensive studies. On the other hand, promptomatix, derived from DSPy, simplifies the process and introduces a distinct methodology for prompt optimization.
