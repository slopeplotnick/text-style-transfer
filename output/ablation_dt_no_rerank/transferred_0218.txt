Baselines and Models. Gemini-2.0-Flash serves as the base model for LLM-only baselines, operating without external knowledge and utilizing just the original question. We explore multiple CAIRNS configurations: (1) Domain RAG (literature-only), which retrieves from literature exclusively; and (2) Domain RAG (literature+data), which enhances retrieval with both literature and climate data through a two-stage API reasoning process. In all configurations, Gemini-2.0-Flash forms the foundation, and the models are evaluated on identical corpora, prompt structures, and settings to ensure fairness. Impact of ScholarGuide Prompt (SGP). The SGP improves dimensions related to readability, such as contextual richness, structural coherence, and language fluency. Its citation management effectively corrects inconsistencies across cited sources. Though this approach slightly reduces the overall citation rate, it enhances faithfulness, as evidenced by a higher FaithRate when compared to the SGP ablation. By utilizing unified reference numbering and grounding each factual assertion in explicit evidence, the SGP enables clearer and more verifiable responses. This consistency is maintained in both zero-shot and few-shot scenarios, demonstrating the importance of structured prompting for CAIRNS's performance. Impact of Multi-turn ReAct (MTR). The iterative reasoning loop allows for the refinement of responses by continuously aligning retrieved literature and climate data, leading to more comprehensive and context-aware answers, as shown by improved comprehensiveness scores. Although MTR's impact is modest compared to SGP, it complements the structured generation process and enhances robustness for multi-source or data-intensive queries. Location-Weighted Retrieval (LWR). The removal of LWR results in a slight reduction in specificity, although other metrics largely remain constant. This suggests that LWR positively contributes to the model's ability to produce geographically nuanced answers, particularly in queries with a geographic focus.
