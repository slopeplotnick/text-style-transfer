Stage 3: Instruction-specific fine-tuning. This advanced phase refines the model's ability to follow targeted instructions across various tasks within Infinity-MM. We train using a comprehensive dataset that includes document comprehension, chart reasoning, OCR-centric tasks, and multi-turn dialogues. Consistent with the methodology prescribed by Infinity-MM, we apply task-specific mixture weighting, allocating 35% to general visual question answering, 25% to document comprehension, 20% to chart reasoning, 15% to OCR-centered tasks, and 5% to conversational exchanges and specialized domains. The training is conducted with a learning rate of 1e-5 for one epoch with an effective batch size of 512, aiming for next-token prediction via standard autoregressive language modeling loss. We then evaluate our NPU-native architecture against state-of-the-art vision-language models across five diverse multimodal benchmarks. Our comparative analysis includes recent models such as InternVL2 and Qwen2-VL, which represent different architectural families and parameter scales. The AutoNeural model, refined to this tertiary stage, achieves an optimal balance of precision and training efficiency. We assess the model using a benchmark suite targeting distinct multimodal capabilities. MMStar evaluates core vision-language reasoning through multi-step visual questions that require commonsense knowledge. Additionally, HallusionBench assesses the model's handling of language hallucination and visual illusions. MathVista_MINI tests mathematical reasoning over diagrams and charts, checking the model's ability to extract quantitative information and perform numerical computations. Furthermore, AI2D_TEST evaluates the model's capabilities in diagrammatic comprehension and scientific reasoning on textbook illustrations. Finally, OCRBench assesses the model's proficiency in text recognition within diverse visual contexts, including scenes, documents, and signage. We report performance for each test and calculate an average score to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the model's proficiency across various task categories.
