Guardrails function as external moderation systems to ensure safety during human-AI interactions. Initially, rudimentary rule-based filtering mechanisms were employed, proving effective only in constrained settings. However, as the landscape evolved, these systems struggled to comprehensively address emerging challenges and maintain precision across diverse scenarios. Recent advancements have integrated sophisticated AI models into guardrail systems, enhancing flexibility and the capacity for generalization. For instance, the Llama Guard model from Meta AI, refined on curated safety data, exemplifies efforts to secure interactions between users and AI systems. Further extending safety measures, models such as Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard address visual-related concerns, thereby broadening the scope across multiple modalities. Despite these advancements, several challenges persist. Much of prior research concentrated predominantly on text and visual data, whereas video and audio have been processed simplistically, lacking contextual comprehension. Moreover, many systems are limited to single media types or specific scenarios, lacking the capability for multi-modal integration and reasoning. To address these challenges, OMNIGUARD was developed as the inaugural framework capable of processing various media formats while employing deliberate reasoning for safety assurance. By implementing omni-modal reasoning, OMNIGUARD offers comprehensive and interpretable safety assurance across all media modalities.
