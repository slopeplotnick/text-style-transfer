The swift advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) technology has substantially enhanced its capabilities in handling language, with story generation emerging as a significant focal point within the field. Story generation aims to produce coherent, engaging, and contextually appropriate narratives based on the provided input. However, these systems continue to face challenges, particularly in maintaining consistency, developing characters effectively, and controlling narrative progression. Specifically, ensuring that the narrative adheres to user expectations while effectively controlling plot development, character actions, and overall structure presents a significant challenge. Therefore, fine-grained control remains a critical issue—avoiding logical inconsistencies in generated content while ensuring alignment with user expectations to preserve both plausibility and creativity in the storyline. The authors are affiliated with the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University of Technology and the Zhejiang Key Laboratory of Visual Information Intelligent Processing in Hangzhou, China. Large language models like GPT-4 and Llama-3 have demonstrated proficiency in generating high-quality text; however, their training on extensive generic datasets often results in difficulties related to producing narratives that are sufficiently controlled and structured. This limitation is especially evident when users demand storytelling with precise details and an unambiguous framework. Thus, enhancing the ability of these models to control story creation in alignment with user expectations while maintaining creative output is crucial. The application of fine-tuning techniques combined with structured input prompts has emerged as an effective strategy to advance story generation capabilities. This approach not only improves the quality of the generated narratives but also augments the stability and dependability of the models in executing complex story generation tasks. Ultimately, integrating fine-tuning methodologies with structured input effectively enhances the predictability and consistency of generated narratives, thereby addressing control challenges inherent in the story generation process.
