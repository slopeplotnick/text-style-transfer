Therefore, when test coverage is insufficient, certain issues may remain undetected, resulting in false negatives. We address this limitation by employing multiple failing test cases alongside lightweight static analysis. Regarding bounded model checking (BMC), we rely on bounded encodings of programs. Constraints such as loop unrolling and depth bounds may exclude feasible behaviours, potentially leaving otherwise valid diagnoses unreachable. Moreover, when iterations are truncated by these bounds, diagnoses applicable to the bounded model might not generalize to the comprehensive program. We judiciously select these bounds and report them alongside results, acknowledging the inherent limitation of incompleteness due to bounding. Concerning pointers, arrays, and undefined behavior, as articulated in Section 5.1, CFAULTS leverages static analysis tools like CPPCHECK and CLANG-TIDY to identify issues, including uninitialized variables and potential runtime errors like division by zero. Upon detecting such issues, CFAULTS immediately terminates execution and reports them to the user. Additionally, CFAULTS utilizes CBMC's memory-safety checks, for instance, --bounds-check and --pointer-check, to detect invalid memory operations, such as out-of-bounds accesses and unsafe pointer arithmetic. However, CFAULTS adheres to CBMC's compliance with ANSI C standards, meaning behaviors considered valid by CBMC's memory model are likewise deemed acceptable within our framework, whereas violations unflagged by CBMC may not be detected. This underscores the need for future efforts to refine CBMC's configuration to better align with the anticipated C semantic rules, particularly concerning pointers and arrays. Regarding external validity, our methodology was evaluated using benchmark and student programs that compiled successfully and possessed explicit specifications such as assertions or expected outputs. Extending the application to larger programs involving extensive input/output operations, concurrency, or complex libraries—or addressing specifications articulated in more sophisticated manners—might require additional effort. This could likely alter the outcomes observed in this study.
