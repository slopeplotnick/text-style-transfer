Radiology reporting is an indispensable part of radiological examinations, demanding radiologists to meticulously interpret medical images and formulate comprehensive reports that convey key findings. This task is often time-consuming and prone to errors, especially with volumetric imaging modalities like CT and MRI. Moreover, rigorous quality control is critical to ensure that reports meet clinical accuracy and completeness standards, though such verification is labor-intensive, requiring thorough checks of various report elements. However, existing automated approaches largely concentrate on the report generation phase, frequently disregarding essential quality control, thus limiting their effectiveness in fully supporting radiologists. To address these challenges, we introduce Radiologist Copilot, an AI-driven assistant equipped with coordinated tools for automating radiology reporting and quality control. This system uses large language models as its reasoning foundation, autonomously selecting tools, planning, and executing actions that mirror radiologists' practices throughout the reporting process. The array of tools includes region localization, image-directed analysis planning, strategic template selection for report creation, and adaptive refinement based on feedback-driven quality assessments. Therefore, Radiologist Copilot facilitates precise, complete, and efficient radiology reporting, bolstering radiologists' productivity and enhancing clinical efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot markedly outperforms other leading methodologies in radiology reporting effectiveness. The source code will be released upon formal acceptance.
