The methodology section comprises the presentation of participants (Sect. 3.1), the interview design (Sect. 3.2), our approach to conducting interviews (Sect. 3.3), and the interview analysis method (Sect. 3.4). Participants were selected to ensure a representative population, encompassing different roles within the project, varying levels of experience, and diverse educational backgrounds. There are four roles involved: Product Owners (PO), Developers (IT), Agronomists (AGRO), and User Interface/Experience (UX) designers. These participants have different educational qualifications, distinguishing holders of engineering degrees from those with doctoral qualifications. Moreover, we considered varying levels of project experience among participants. Ultimately, 16 individuals were selected: nine Developers currently or previously engaged in the project, two Agronomists, three Product Owners, and two UX designers, all actively contributing to the project. Table 1 provides a detailed overview of their educational backgrounds and experience levels within the organization. Ensuring participant motivation is crucial for obtaining insightful responses. Therefore, participants were informed they would receive feedback, and their contributions would aid in developing an effective SPL process strategy. We opted for structured interviews, guided by a list of questions. Efforts were made to adhere to primary recommendations, employing clear and precise language. Each question was designed to address a singular concept, deliberately avoiding sensitive topics or inquiries into historical events. Additionally, questions that participants might lack the knowledge to answer were excluded, prompting the incorporation of actor-driven interviews. The interview questions were structured into three segments, beginning with an introduction where participants presented their backgrounds. The main section followed, divided into two subsections: one examining the current process and the other exploring prospective modifications. Finally, a concluding section offered a comprehensive discussion of the entire interview process.
