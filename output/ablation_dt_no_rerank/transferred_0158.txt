Llama-4 Maverick showcases superior performance compared to the conventional Bayesian reliability-weighted combiner, which is a standard methodology used in biological studies for information integration. Humans also employ this approach, as demonstrated by Ernst & Banks (2002). Notably, the enhanced performance of Llama-4 Maverick suggests that it may be leveraging additional mechanisms beyond the traditional method. As illustrated in Figure 7, the comparative analysis of Llama-4 Maverick's responses to multimodal input versus unimodal input indicates that a non-linear integration approach outperforms a linear one under these conditions. However, in scenarios involving noise, the Bayesian method emerges as superior, making it an effective benchmark for improving the performance of less proficient language models. Furthermore, Figure 8 demonstrates that models with higher accuracy align more closely with Bayesian methodologies. Although Gemma 3 4B and Phi 4 Multimodal exhibit lower overall accuracy, their performance in this domain remains competent. An analysis of task and model performance is presented in Appendix A.9. Our study reveals that language models display diverse behaviors when subjected to tasks designed to mirror human perceptual processes. Models with higher accuracy, such as GPT-5 Mini, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, and Llama-4 Maverick, provide stronger evidence of employing Bayesian methods, especially in image processing (see Appendix A.11 for comprehensive details). These advanced models can adjust their behavior in line with Bayesian principles when confronted with variables like noise or additional context, harnessing prior knowledge despite the absence of explicit training for such integration. This mirrors the functioning of the human brain, where Bayesian models explain a substantial range of perceptual phenomena without explicit training (Petzschner et al., 2015; Knill & Pouget, 2004). It is crucial to emphasize that the current analysis focuses on behavioral observations, and a comprehensive mechanistic elucidation of this behavior would require further investigation.
