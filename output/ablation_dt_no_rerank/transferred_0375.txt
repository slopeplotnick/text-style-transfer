Training Dynamics Analysis. In Figure 3, we present the training dynamics of the 7B model when employing CAPO and GRPO methodologies. Initially, both methods display similar improvement rates; however, CAPO consistently outperforms once imitation stabilizes. The gray vertical line marks the phase transition from imitation to independent problem-solving. Beyond this point, CAPO's entropy trajectory continues to rise steadily, unlike the plateau observed in GRPO, while rewards persistently improve. This suggests that CAPO not only achieves stronger rewards later but also sustains higher entropy, indicative of more diverse exploratory reasoning. Moreover, the gradual increase in entropy ensures CAPO avoids early predictability induced by premature negative example incorporation, thus stabilizing imitation and enhancing adaptability across varied tasks. Comparing Static and Dynamic Curriculum Strategies. We compare the static curriculum method with our dynamic strategy. In the static setting, task difficulty is gauged by assessing model performance on each example, arranging the training sequence accordingly. Conversely, our dynamic approach adjusts the focus during training without necessitating data reordering. As illustrated in Table 3, ADARFT and the static curriculum GRPO(+SC) provide limited gains over baseline GRPO. However, our dynamic method GRPO(+Ours) demonstrates significant superiority across most tasks, underscoring that conjectures about task difficulty are inadequate and that dynamic adjustments yield better results.
