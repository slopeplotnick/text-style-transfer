Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) unify task-oriented and open-domain dialogue systems. This section of the tutorial will cover this technology shift and highlight promising future directions. Examples of these advancements include developing new foundation LLMs pre-trained with dialogue-specific data, end-to-end training of dialogues, and implementing self-dialogue practices. Furthermore, a significant future development involves integrating dialogue systems with multifaceted content, such as web pages, lists, forms, tables, scholarly papers with figures and equations, and online retrieved documents. Moreover, these systems incorporate vision, knowledge, emotion, and expressive capabilities, including facial movements and gestures.
