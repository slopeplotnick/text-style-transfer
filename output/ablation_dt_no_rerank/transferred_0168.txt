Several studies have investigated more structured prompting paradigms. For instance, Plan-and-Solve (Wang et al., 2023c) explicitly decomposes tasks into a planning phase and a solution phase, demonstrating enhanced efficacy for mathematical and logical problems. Prompt Chaining (Chen et al., 2023) explores whether chaining intermediate summaries or steps improves text summarization performance. Similarly, Complexity-based Prompting (Fu et al., 2023) advocates that task complexity should dictate prompt construction, aligning with our investigation into prompt specificity. While most existing research focuses on task format or exemplar inclusion, our study uniquely emphasizes the degree of prompt specificity as a dynamic continuum, systematically varying detail and structure. Moreover, recent scholarly work has introduced methodologies for either automatic or human-assisted prompt refinement. For example, black-box optimization methods (Diao et al., 2023) adjust prompts using task feedback without accessing model gradients. Prompt Engineering a Prompt Engineer (Zhou et al., 2023) illustrates that large language models can be prompted to refine their prompts, adopting a metaprompting approach akin to our generalization loop. Prompt Refinement with Image Pivots (Lu et al., 2023) employs cross-modal tuning, utilizing visual features to refine textual prompts for image generation. Although our focus is exclusively on text, our objective remains to refine prompts to enhance model performance. However, unlike earlier studies, DETAIL underscores the impact of specificity within text on the reasoning capabilities of models, rather than on semantic drift or the integration of textual data with other modalities. Comprehensive reviews, such as that by Liu et al., further elucidate these aspects. Recent scholarship has systematically investigated various prompting methodologies to enhance reasoning capabilities, illustrating the vast array of available prompts and strategic frameworks. Nevertheless, research exploring how the detail or granularity of prompt phrasing influences model behavior across diverse tasks and domains remains limited. Approaches such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT) and Plan-and-Solve highlight the importance of prompt content; however, they do not extensively address this critical aspect.
