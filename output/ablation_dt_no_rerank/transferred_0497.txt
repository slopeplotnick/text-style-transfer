Objective Evaluation. We report ViSAudio's superior effectiveness across multiple objective metrics on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution datasets, as illustrated in Table 2. It is systematically compared with several state-of-the-art video-to-spatial-audio generation methods, detailed in Section 5.1. For both the BiAudio and MUSIC test sets, video and caption inputs are utilized, whereas for FAIR-Play, which lacks captions, only video inputs are employed. Models without text-processing capabilities are evaluated using sole video inputs. As demonstrated in Table 2, ViSAudio outperforms other binaural-audio generation methodologies in terms of overall audio quality, spatial channel distribution accuracy, audio-visual synchrony, and semantic alignment. Therefore, this underscores the model's proficiency in generating realistic binaural audio while maintaining synchrony between auditory and visual elements under varied acoustic environments. Moreover, its robust performance on the out-of-distribution FAIR-Play dataset, despite not being trained on it, further indicates its adaptability to new scenarios. We present a comparative analysis of video-conditioned binaural spatial audio generation in Figure 3. For instance, the scenario shows a person playing the sitar, with the camera panning from left to right, causing the sound source to shift from right to left in the video. We conducted a comparative analysis of the spectrograms generated by ViSAudio, the ground truth, and alternative methodologies. The audio produced by ViSAudio exhibits the highest fidelity to the ground truth, accurately illustrating spatial auditory dynamics. Specifically, boxed regions indicate heightened energy in the left-channel spectrogram corresponding with the sound source's location on the left, effectively replicating natural loudness perception by the left ear. This agreement with the ground-truth spatial audio underscores our model's adeptness in accommodating viewpoint changes. In contrast, alternative methodologies fail to accurately reproduce the rhythmic structures and misrepresent disparities between the left and right audio channels.
