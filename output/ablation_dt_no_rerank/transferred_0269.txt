The individual is a leading figure in technology-focused artificial intelligence and currently serves as the Executive Director of the Machine Learning Center at the Georgia Institute of Technology. He holds the distinguished Rhesa S. Farmer Chair of Advanced Computing Concepts and is recognized as a Georgia Research Alliance Eminent Scholar. Furthermore, he received his PhD in Electrical Engineering from the same institute and is acknowledged as an IEEE Fellow. His extensive career includes positions at esteemed organizations such as SRI International, Nuance, Yahoo!, Microsoft, Google, Viv Labs, and Samsung. Throughout his career, his research has spanned fields such as acoustics, active noise and vibration control, speaker recognition, web search, AI virtual assistants, natural language processing, and conversational artificial intelligence. This tutorial is designed for researchers and practitioners at the intersection of conversational agents and spoken information interactions. We assume participants will have a foundational understanding of NLP and speech processing; however, prior system deployment experience, although beneficial, is not required. The tutorial will offer a carefully paced introduction to accommodate a broader audience. Key papers providing essential insights and foundations for this session include: "Dialogue act modeling for the automatic tagging and recognition of conversational speech" (Stolcke et al., 2000), "Is spoken language all-or-nothing? Implications for future speech-based human-machine interaction" (Moore, 2017), "Dialogue Learning with Human Teaching and Feedback in End-to-End Trainable Task-Oriented Dialogue Systems" (Liu et al., 2018), "Voice2series: Reprogramming acoustic models for time series classification" (Yang et al., 2021), "Hyporadise: An open baseline for generative speech recognition with large language models" (Chen et al., 2023a), and "SpeechGPT: Empowering Large Language Models with Intrinsic Cross-Modal Conversational Abilities" (Zhang et al., 2023). While we will reference many relevant papers throughout, the focus will be on 7-8 pivotal studies, of which only 1-2 are authored by the presenter.
