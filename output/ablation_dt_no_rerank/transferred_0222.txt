Research in truthful AI aims at developing systems that accurately reflect real-world facts. Previous studies have introduced various interventions to enhance "truthfulness," such as identifying learned activations that align with truth or adjusting model activations during decision-making processes. Moreover, there has been a discussion regarding the trade-off between truthfulness and utility in AI systems. Honest AI emphasizes that systems should not deliberately communicate information that contradicts their internalized beliefs merely to gain rewards, thus addressing concerns related to AI deception. On the contrary, a less explored domain is truth-seeking AI, focused on weighing evidence and discovering novel insights. As AI systems gain autonomy, the importance of truth-seeking mechanisms is poised to rise, especially given that these systems must decide which information to acquire based on their interpretations of situations. While truth-seeking AI explicitly aims to uncover truth, optimizing for "truth-seeking" may not align with a single metric. Potential objectives include adhering to Bayesian principles or maintaining coherence in analytical processes. The concept of belief entrenchment describes the tendency to cling to existing beliefs even in the face of contradictory evidence, closely related to confirmation bias, where individuals tend to seek, recall, or interpret information that confirms their pre-existing beliefs. The intricacies of this bias pose significant challenges for measurement. In psychology, assessing confirmation bias is inherently task-specific and characterized by low reliability. Accurate assessment requires not only reliable access to an individual's existing beliefs but also a robust measure of these beliefs, presenting considerable methodological challenges.
