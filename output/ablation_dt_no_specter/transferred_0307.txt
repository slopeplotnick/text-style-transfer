In this paper, we introduce Story2MIDI, a sequence-to-sequence Transformer-based model designed to generate music that aligns with the emotional content of a given text. To develop this model, we constructed the Story2MIDI dataset by merging existing datasets for sentiment analysis in text and emotion classification in music. The resulting dataset consists of pairs of text excerpts and musical compositions that evoke congruent emotional responses in readers and listeners. Although our dataset is relatively small and our computational resources limited, our results demonstrate that the model effectively learns emotion-relevant features in music and incorporates them into its generative process, producing samples with diverse emotional responses. We evaluated the generated outputs using both objective musical metrics and a human listening study, which confirmed the model's ability to capture the intended emotional cues. In today’s world, there is an ever-increasing demand for entertainment and multimedia content, driven by the rapid expansion of social media and streaming platforms for music, audiobooks, and podcasts. At the core of many of these forms of entertainment lies a narrative—a story that shapes the experience, whether in films, games, podcasts, or documentaries. Narratives are powerful tools for evoking emotion, as audiences often embark on a dynamic emotional journey influenced by characters, plot developments, and underlying themes. This emotional progression is essential to the lasting impact that stories have on their audiences. Similarly, music exerts a profound influence on listeners’ emotional states and is widely recognized as a powerful medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creative producers frequently pair narratives with background music, which has been shown to enhance emotional engagement and amplify the immersive quality of the experience. With the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence across multiple modalities, new models are continually being developed to generate diverse forms of content, offering exciting possibilities for the future of multimedia storytelling.
