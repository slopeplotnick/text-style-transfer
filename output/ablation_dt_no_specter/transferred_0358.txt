DeepSeek-V3.2 achieves performance comparable to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across a range of reasoning benchmarks. Moreover, it significantly enhances the agentic capabilities of open models, demonstrating exceptional proficiency in specialized agent tasks introduced in EvalSys (2025) and related studies. As a highly cost-efficient alternative for agent scenarios, DeepSeek-V3.2 effectively narrows the performance gap between open-source and leading proprietary models, all while maintaining substantially lower costs. To further advance the reasoning capabilities of open models, we developed DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale by relaxing sequence length constraints. Consequently, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale achieves performance parity with the leading proprietary system, Gemini-3.0-Pro, and demonstrates outstanding results in prestigious competitions such as the IOI 2025, ICPC World Finals 2025, IMO 2025, and CMO 2025. Notably, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale retains the same foundational architecture as DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp. Compared to its predecessor, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, the sole architectural enhancement in DeepSeek-V3.2 is the incorporation of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) through continued training. The DSA framework consists of two key components: a lightning indexer and a fine-grained token selection mechanism. Building on the base checkpoint of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which features an extended context length of 128K, we conducted continued pre-training followed by post-training to develop DeepSeek-V3.2. The continued pre-training process involved two distinct stages, both utilizing training data aligned with the extended context data used for DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp was evaluated on a comprehensive suite of benchmarks and compared with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, showing similar performance. However, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp demonstrated significantly improved computational efficiency for processing extended sequences, while maintaining robust performance on both short- and long-context tasks relative to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus.
