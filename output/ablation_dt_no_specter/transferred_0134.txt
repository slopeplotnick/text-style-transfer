The advent of AI agents enables autonomous reasoning, planning, and tool utilization. Recently, medical agents have been developed to tackle complex medical tasks. For example, MMedAgent employs a multi-modal approach, leveraging various tools to perform tasks across different modalities, such as generating radiology reports for chest X-rays (CXR). Similarly, MedRAX provides a specialized agent framework for chest X-ray interpretation, integrating state-of-the-art tools for CXR analysis. CT-Agent, on the other hand, is an anatomy-aware and token-efficient agent designed for effective reasoning in 3D CT visual question answering. However, in the context of radiology report generation, these agentic methodologies often rely on a single tool and exhibit limited collaboration among multiple tools. In contrast, our Radiologist Copilot introduces a diverse and comprehensive toolset, enabling rich inter-tool collaboration and supporting significantly more complex tool planning and execution. Radiology reporting is essential to clinical practice but remains time-consuming and prone to errors, particularly for 3D medical imaging. Furthermore, rigorous quality control, a critical step to ensure the clinical reliability of reports, is often overlooked due to its labor-intensive nature in previous studies. To address these challenges, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an AI agentic assistant equipped with an orchestrated suite of tools for automated radiology reporting and quality assurance, aimed at supporting radiologists and enhancing clinical efficiency. As illustrated in Fig. 1, Radiologist Copilot is designed to assist radiologists throughout the entire radiology reporting process. It begins by initializing the tool library, followed by iterative utilization of the action planner and executor to systematically plan and execute tasks. The memory module records actions and their outcomes, facilitating the verification of task completion. The action executor is responsible for generating and executing commands based on the next action determined by the action planner. For each subsequent action, it formulates the appropriate command for the designated tool, executes the command, and evaluates the resulting outcome.
