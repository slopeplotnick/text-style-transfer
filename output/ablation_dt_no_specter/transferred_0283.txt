To ensure data quality, the survey included an attention check similar to the one used by Shank et al. (2023) ("Who was the composer of the music you just heard?") before the music rating. Participants were then required to self-report the honesty and care of their responses on a 5-point scale, followed by a question about their listening setup, specifically whether they used headphones or speakers. Importantly, participants were informed that their responses would not affect feedback or compensation. An overview of the study design is presented in Figure 1. All participants were asked to indicate their level of familiarity with the music excerpt and their degree of liking on a scale from 1 (not at all) to 5 (very). If participants reported a familiarity level of 4 or higher, they were subsequently asked whether the music evoked memories of a specific person or experience. Furthermore, participants in the "AI or human" condition were additionally required to indicate whether they believed the music excerpt was generated by an AI or a human, as well as to rate their confidence in this judgment on a 6-point scale ranging from 1 (definitely AI) to 6 (definitely human). Participants' emotional responses to the music excerpts were assessed using the Geneva Emotion Music Scale (GEMS; Zentner et al., 2008), which categorizes emotional experiences into three overarching dimensions: Sublimity (e.g., Wonder, Tenderness, Nostalgia), Vitality (e.g., Joy, Power), and Unease (e.g., Sadness, Tension). For this study, a condensed version of the GEMS was employed, featuring one representative label for each emotion alongside a selection of related descriptors. Emotion ratings were provided for each dimension on visual analog scales ranging from 0 to 100, with initial values set at 0. If no selection was made, the rating remained at 0. Additionally, an open text box was provided, allowing participants to introduce up to three supplementary emotion terms not encompassed by the GEMS framework, accompanied by intensity ratings for each.
