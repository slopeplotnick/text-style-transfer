In this section, we discuss the limitations of TaleFrame regarding interaction modes and advanced narrative structures, as well as directions for future research. TaleFrame currently operates on desktop and laptop environments, utilizing a combination of mouse and keyboard for precise control, which is crucial for connecting entities to identify relationships and linking events to recognize sequences. User studies indicate a preference for drag-and-drop interactions; however, this mode may be less effective on popular mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, due to their smaller screen sizes. Although touch pens and fingers can replace the mouse, the complexity of prolonged interactions and input of relationship information can lead to accidental touches, thus impairing the user experience on mobile devices. To reduce cognitive load, we have optimized information visualization through color coding, icons, and connecting lines, and implemented adaptive layout resizing to accommodate different screen sizes. In future work, we plan to explore and define a broader range of interaction styles, including touchscreens, keyboard input, and voice commands, to create a flexible framework that allows for diverse user engagement across various devices. TaleFrame also faces challenges in handling advanced narrative structures in story generation, particularly with complex event relationships beyond linear temporal control, such as causal relationships, flashbacks, and other intricate narrative forms. Moreover, the relationships between entities complicate the connections between events, impacting the coherence and accuracy of the generation process. While we have attempted to improve the modeling of event dependencies, results have been less than ideal when dealing with multi-layered event interweaving, temporal reversals, and interactions between different entities. The relationships between entities, especially in events involving multiple entities, often lead to difficulties in generating the correct event sequence or capturing causal chains, which can result in incoherent or logically unclear storylines. As the complexity of the story plot and the relationships between entities increases, the model faces greater challenges in understanding and generating these event relationships. In future work, we will gradually attempt to improve entity relationship modeling and optimize strategies for generating non-linear narrative structures to enhance the model's performance in complex story generation, ultimately providing users with a richer and more coherent narrative experience.
