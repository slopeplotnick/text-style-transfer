Data sampling. Using Taobao search logs, we begin by categorizing the data into distinct industries, such as consumer electronics and apparel, and then perform stratified sampling based on the representation of each industry. This approach ensures that the training dataset comprehensively covers attributes and features across all industries. Following this, the data within each industry is further divided into high, medium, and low frequency categories according to query frequency, with sampling conducted proportionally. This ensures that the dataset includes both commonly occurring high-frequency queries and less frequent long-tail queries. Subsequently, the sampled data is assigned to domain experts for annotation. Data cleaning. Errors in manual annotation primarily arise from two sources: first, annotators may overlook specific details, particularly when handling long, multi-attribute queries; second, the annotation guidelines may lack sufficient clarity or comprehensiveness, making it challenging to account for all edge cases. To address these issues, we utilize robust language models for data filtering. Specifically, (1) the attribute matching model employs strict matching strategies, demonstrating superior capability in capturing fine-grained features compared to manual annotation in complex scenarios; and (2) by embedding comprehensive annotation rules into prompts, the model is guided to reason and make judgments, effectively identifying and filtering out cases that fall outside the scope of existing rules. To further enhance the filtering process, we adopt a dual-model approach: one model excels in understanding long textual contexts, while the other specializes in interpreting diverse data types. Reasoning enhancement. To equip the model with advanced reasoning capabilities, we propose a progressive chain-of-thought (CoT) synthesis pipeline, as illustrated in Fig. 8. This pipeline follows a stagewise construction strategy, encompassing three sequential stages: Knowledge Injection & Reasoning, Multimodal Comprehension, and Rule Awareness. In the Knowledge Injection & Reasoning stage, user intents are parsed into attributes that can be compared with item characteristics. The subsequent stages, Multimodal Comprehension and Rule Awareness, focus on evaluating how well items meet the search requirements by leveraging item-specific details and predefined rules.
