Model Paradigm & Backbone: We employed a Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) paradigm utilizing the Qwen-1.5 model as the foundational framework. This approach facilitated the effective transfer of general language comprehension capabilities to domain-specific e-commerce applications. Data & Prompt: Our methodology incorporated knowledge graphs, wherein domain-specific information was systematically integrated through meticulously designed contextual prompts. This strategy enabled the model to discern e-commerce-specific semantic patterns and entity relationships with enhanced precision. Evaluation: To evaluate the model's efficacy, we employed standardized assessments and relevance metrics to ensure its precision and capability in handling diverse query types, thereby ensuring both accuracy and generalizability. Application: Upon completion of the training phase, the model was deployed to facilitate labeling tasks, successfully expediting the process and enhancing data quality by identifying errors. Moreover, the model demonstrated proficiency in addressing more complex tasks, such as interpreting intricate queries, discerning implicit meanings, and executing logical reasoning for constructing pathways. Simple instruction-following proved insufficient for these multi-step reasoning tasks. To address these challenges, we implemented the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) methodology. This approach facilitated the model's ability to systematically decompose problems, discern intricate relationships, and perform step-by-step path comparisons, significantly improving its capacity to process complex queries. Furthermore, we developed a data synthesis framework centered on the identification of challenging cases, enabling iterative refinement of the model through feedback-driven optimization. Model Paradigm & Backbone: Subsequently, we synthesized CoT data for attribute extraction and matching tasks, retraining the model using the SFT paradigm with Qwen-2 as the backbone architecture. This methodological framework enabled the model to effectively generate intermediate reasoning steps while maintaining a task-specific focus through iterative optimization.
