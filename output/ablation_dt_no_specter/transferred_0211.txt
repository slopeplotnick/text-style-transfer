Stage 3: Instruction-specific fine-tuning. In this third stage, we focus on enhancing specialized instruction-following capabilities across the varied task categories within Infinity-MM. The training utilizes the complete dataset, which includes document understanding, chart reasoning, OCR-centric tasks, and multi-turn conversational interactions. Specifically, we apply task-specific weighting, allocating 35% to general visual question answering, 25% to document understanding, 20% to chart reasoning, 15% to OCR-related tasks, and 5% to conversational and other specialized domains. The learning rate is set at 1e-5, and training is conducted for a single epoch with a batch size of 512. The objective is to predict subsequent lexical items using conventional language modeling techniques. We then evaluate our NPU-optimized model against contemporary vision-language models across five distinct multimodal benchmarks. Our comparison includes recent models such as InternVL2 and Qwen2-VL, which are optimized for different computational architectures and exhibit varying parameter scales. Our AutoNeural model, trained to Stage 3 of the Infinity-MM curriculum, achieves an optimal balance between precision and training efficiency. We assess its performance across five diverse multimodal benchmarks, each designed to evaluate distinct competencies. MMStar evaluates the model's proficiency in addressing visual questions that require multi-step reasoning and the integration of commonsense knowledge. HallusionBench examines the model's ability to navigate linguistic hallucinations and visual illusions, assessing robustness in multimodal contexts. MathVista_MINI focuses on mathematical reasoning tasks involving diagrams, charts, and geometric figures, testing the model's capacity to extract quantitative information and execute numerical computations. AI2D_TEST evaluates the model's proficiency in diagram comprehension and scientific reasoning using annotated illustrations derived from textbooks and educational resources. OCRBench assesses the model's capability in scene text recognition and document understanding across various text-in-image contexts, including natural scenes, documents, and signage. Finally, we report the model's accuracy on each benchmark and compute the average score across all tasks to evaluate its overall multimodal competence.
