(1) Synchronization. We evaluate the temporal alignment between the source and edited videos using four correlation-based metrics: speech, gaze, blink, and pose. Each metric captures a distinct aspect of motion dynamics: speech is assessed through mouth aspect ratio, gaze through iris trajectory, blink through eye aspect ratio, and pose through upper-body joint angles. These metrics, identical to those used for data selection and filtering (subsection 3.2), are employed exclusively for dataset curation and final evaluation. They are not utilized as loss functions or incorporated into the model's training process. (2) Edit Fidelity. To determine whether the intended edits are accurately applied and consistently maintained throughout the video, we employ two complementary CLIP-based metrics. Directional CLIP Score. Adapted from its original use in image editing, this metric evaluates how consistently the per-frame visual transformations align with the overarching edit direction within CLIP's embedding space. We report two complementary variants: an image-based version, which measures alignment with the visual edit implied by the edited first frame, and a text-dual version, which evaluates alignment with the semantic edit described by the text prompt. The text-dual variant ensures fairness for text-based methods, such as LucyEdit, which rely solely on textual guidance without access to the edited first frame. Intuitively, the image-based variant assesses whether the visual modifications across frames resemble the changes observed between the unedited and edited first frames, while the text-dual variant examines whether these modifications align with the transformations suggested by the textual prompt. CLIP-Text Alignment Score. To further evaluate semantic accuracy, this metric quantifies the correspondence between individual frames and the textual description within CLIP's embedding space. By capturing whether the video consistently adheres to the prompt semantics over time, this score complements the Directional CLIP Score and ensures that the video's semantic fidelity to the textual input is maintained throughout.
