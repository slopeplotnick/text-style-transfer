The primary objective is to maximize code reuse and expedite the delivery of new applications. Therefore, the intended approach involves migrating the existing code base to a higher-level Software Product Line (SPL), specifically at Level 4 or 5 as defined in [vdLSR07]. To facilitate this transition, we first decided to conduct interviews with company collaborators to assess their perspectives on SPL engineering. These interviews are structured to address the research questions that will be discussed in the following section. Moreover, another series of interviews is scheduled for the project's conclusion to refine the migration guidelines. The ensuing phases of migration will involve an analysis of variability, commencing with the establishment of a process for feature localization to synthesize feature models. Subsequently, examining the interactions and dependencies among these features will lead to achieving a comprehensive system description. We conducted interviews to ascertain the perception of current practices, benefits, risks, and apprehensions prior to the transition to an SPL. Research Question 1 (RQ1) seeks to elucidate the perception of the company's actors regarding the prevailing good practices in development. Through this inquiry, we aim to highlight their perspectives on the effectiveness of current practices. Research Question 2 explores their perceptions regarding the prospective advantages of the new SPL, engaging in a discussion concerning the added value that the new SPL will contribute to the application development process. Following the discussion of benefits, Research Question 3 (RQ3) seeks to analyze the risks associated with the prospective SPL in the daily work of the company's actors. Finally, Research Question 4 (RQ4) explores the apprehensions that may arise among the company's actors as a consequence of these identified risks. This inquiry facilitates the comprehension of the fears experienced by the company's personnel concerning the migration initiative. Such fears pertain to potential adverse outcomes, anticipated challenges during the transition, and the imperative for meticulous planning and effective risk mitigation strategies.
