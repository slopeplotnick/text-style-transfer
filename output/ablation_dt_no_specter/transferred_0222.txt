Research in the domain of truthful AI focuses on developing AI systems that deliver accurate representations of the real world. Prior investigations have proposed various interventions to enhance "truthfulness," such as identifying learned activations that align with truth [Burns et al., 2022] or modifying model activations during inference [Li et al., 2023]. Moreover, there has been discourse on the trade-off between the truthfulness of AI systems and their utility [Su et al., 2025]. Honest AI pertains to ensuring that AI systems do not deliberately produce assertions that conflict with their internal beliefs in pursuit of extrinsic rewards, thereby addressing concerns related to potential AI deception. Conversely, truth-seeking AI emphasizes the systematic evaluation of evidence and the pursuit of novel insights, underscoring its role in advancing epistemic discovery. As AI systems increasingly operate with greater autonomy, determining which information to seek based on their contextual understanding, the emphasis on truth-seeking becomes more pronounced. Truth-seeking AI endeavors to ascertain and represent truth; however, the evaluation of its efficacy is not reducible to a singular metric. For instance, such systems may prioritize adherence to Bayesian principles or strive for consistency in their reasoning processes. Belief entrenchment refers to the propensity of individuals to adhere to their existing beliefs despite the presence of contradictory evidence. This phenomenon is akin to confirmation bias, wherein individuals selectively seek, recall, or interpret information in a manner that corroborates their pre-existing beliefs. While confirmation bias pertains to the processing of information in specific contexts, it presents challenges in terms of quantification. In the field of psychology, the measurement of confirmation bias is contingent upon the specific task and is characterized by low reliability. Accurately assessing confirmation bias necessitates a comprehensive understanding of an individual's pre-existing beliefs and a robust mechanism to quantify them, which presents a considerable challenge.
