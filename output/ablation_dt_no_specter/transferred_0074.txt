Reasoning has emerged as a widely adopted approach to enhance the performance of large language models (LLMs) across various tasks. Initially introduced through prompting techniques like Chain-of-Thought (Wei et al., 2022), which involve generating intermediate reasoning steps (traces) before arriving at a final answer, reasoning methodologies have since progressed toward reinforcement learning (RL)-based strategies that refine the generation of high-quality reasoning traces. In domains such as mathematics and programming, where outputs can be explicitly verified, RL has shown significant promise by leveraging verifiers as reward signals to improve accuracy (Shao et al., 2024; Lambert et al., 2025). More recently, researchers have extended RL techniques to less deterministic areas, such as narrative generation and comprehension, where correctness is more subjective (Ahuja et al., 2025; Gurung and Lapata, 2025). However, these advancements often come with increased computational costs, as longer reasoning traces result in slower training and inference times (Shen et al., 2025a). Consequently, there is growing interest in optimizing reasoning efficiency to accelerate both RL training and inference-time generation (Sui et al., 2025). One promising direction is latent reasoning, which generates reasoning traces using continuous token embeddings rather than—or in addition to—discrete tokens. This approach is motivated by two key observations: first, not all tokens contribute critical information for deriving correct answers; and second, human reasoning frequently occurs implicitly, without explicit verbalization. By retaining reasoning processes in a latent, non-tokenized form, models can preserve more information while avoiding the lossiness associated with token decoding. For instance, Hao et al. (2025) argue that continuous embeddings can encapsulate multiple reasoning steps simultaneously, effectively enabling parallel resolution of complex problems. Building on this foundation, we propose LiteReason, a novel latent reasoning framework tailored for narrative comprehension and generation tasks, which often involve extensive input and output sequences. LiteReason introduces a specialized Reasoning Projector module that facilitates dual-mode reasoning by generating both discrete text tokens and continuous latent embeddings.
