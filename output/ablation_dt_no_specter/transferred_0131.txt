RQ1. Process-centric Metrics. What are the defining characteristics of programming agents concerning process-centric metrics, and what factors influence these metric values? RQ2. Analysis of Problem-solving Strategies. What are the predominant problem-solving strategies utilized by programming agents? Furthermore, what factors influence their strategic behavior, and to what extent do agents adhere to expected planning? Moreover, to what degree do agents adapt or modify their strategies during execution? RQ3. Inefficiency Pattern Analysis. What are the prevalent inefficiency patterns observed in the agents' behavior, and how frequently do these patterns occur? We conducted our study on two widely used programming agents: SWE-agent (SA) and OpenHands (OH). We adhered to each framework's default configurations for the experiments: SWE-agent operates under a per-instance cost cap of $2, while OpenHands is limited to a maximum of 100 trajectory iterations; both utilize their default tools for file viewing and code editing. We paired the agents with four distinct language models: DeepSeek-V3 671B MoE (DSK-V3), DeepSeek-R1 (DSK-R1), Devstral-small-2505 24B coding-specialized open weights (Dev), and Claude Sonnet 4 (CLD-4). Our selection of language models includes both general-purpose and reasoning-oriented variants, as well as sophisticated and open-source alternatives, resulting in eight distinct agent-model configurations for analysis.
