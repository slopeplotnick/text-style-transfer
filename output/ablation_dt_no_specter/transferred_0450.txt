The framework achieved success rates ranging from 0.10 for Compound tasks to 0.56 for Elemental tasks, demonstrating performance degradation as coordination complexity increased. Executability metrics varied between 0.39 and 0.71, indicating that even when tasks were executed, many did not culminate in valid allocation outcomes. Moreover, Robot Utilization scores, which ranged from 0.56 to 0.66, revealed inefficiencies in coordination across all levels of task complexity. However, the evaluation of the Self Corrective-Agent Architecture demonstrated that systematic validation and hierarchical retry mechanisms could effectively address the reliability challenges identified during SMART-LLM replication. Table 2, located in the section Performance Validation and Evaluation Limitations, documents performance metrics for the same 36-task SMART-LLM benchmark under identical evaluation conditions, thereby isolating the impact of architectural improvements. Notably, the framework achieved success rates ranging from 0.21 for Compound tasks to 0.50 for Elemental and Complex tasks. More significantly, task executability improved substantially across all categories: Elemental (0.75), Simple (0.83), Compound (0.88), and Complex (0.86). These executability gains of 9-30 percentage points demonstrate that validation mechanisms ensured syntactically and semantically correct outputs. Furthermore, the Self Corrective-Agent Architecture demonstrates computational efficiency gains despite employing the same few-shot prompting strategy as SMART-LLM. The framework utilizes 700,017 total tokens compared to SMART-LLM's 727,649 tokens, representing a 3.8% reduction, as illustrated in Figure 13. Additionally, it completes execution in 746 seconds, a 30.6% reduction relative to SMART-LLM's 1,075 seconds, as depicted in Figure 14. This enhancement is primarily attributed to its modular prompting architecture, wherein each stage—decomposition, allocation, or code generation—operates with its respective example file. In contrast, SMART-LLM employs a concatenated prompt comprising all three example files for every LLM invocation, irrespective of the active stage. Consequently, each Self Corrective-Agent inference utilizes significantly fewer tokens, thereby facilitating markedly accelerated reasoning. Notably, this stage-specific prompting structure is so efficient that, even with validation-driven retries, Self Corrective-Agent consistently consumes fewer tokens in total.
