During the parsing process, narratives were deconstructed into elements such as events, characters, and settings, which were then structured into JSON format. Based on this structured data, GPT-4 generated new stories that retained core themes while incorporating innovative elements. We applied a set of predefined criteria to evaluate the quality of these stories, assessing dimensions such as functionality, creativity, readability, and the extent to which they conveyed authenticity and depth of thought. To further ensure quality, alternative AI systems like GPT-4o evaluated both the original and generated narratives, categorizing them as either "Chosen" or "Rejected." Moreover, the metadata associated with these narratives, including their labels, was stored in JSON format to construct a structured dataset. This dataset facilitated the identification of emotional patterns and other narrative attributes, as illustrated in Figure 4. Initially, the dataset was divided into training and testing subsets at a ratio of 9:1, with the majority allocated for training purposes to optimize model performance. The training dataset comprised 8,866 preference pairs aimed at enhancing the model, while the test dataset included 985 previously unseen preference pairs. Subsequently, we refined the Llama-3-8B model utilizing the AdamW optimizer in conjunction with a LoRA-based fine-tuning methodology. This process involved the application of specific hyperparameters, including a learning rate of 5e-6, a batch size of 10, and 10 training epochs, resulting in an evaluation loss of 0.038 on the test set. The selection of these configurations was guided by documented recommendations, empirical testing, and the computational resources available. Furthermore, an ablation study was conducted to evaluate the significance of individual narrative components in generating coherent and high-quality outputs. Beginning with a baseline implementation of the Llama-3-8B model incorporating all narrative elements, components such as "events" were systematically excluded, followed by retraining and fine-tuning the model to adapt to the modified configurations. All models were trained under identical conditions to ensure the fairness and comparability of the experiments. The performance of each modified model was evaluated using an independent test set. Additionally, the stories were assessed based on the same criteria as previously established, encompassing seven dimensions such as creativity and clarity.
