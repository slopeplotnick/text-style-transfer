We propose Radiologist Copilot, an innovative system designed to assist radiologists in achieving automated radiology reporting with integrated quality control through an agentic framework of orchestrated tools. This agentic system, leveraging a sophisticated reasoning backbone, autonomously selects appropriate tools, formulates plans, and executes tasks, thereby emulating the radiologist's workflow to accomplish the entire reporting process. The comprehensive tool library encompasses functionalities such as region localization, analysis planning, strategic template selection, quality assessment, and feedback-driven adaptive refinement, all of which contribute to thorough and precise radiology reports. Empirical evaluations indicate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art methodologies in the domain of radiology reporting. Moreover, the implementation of automated reporting with integrated quality control mechanisms is imperative, given the extensive time demands associated with processing 3D medical imaging modalities. For instance, the CT2Rep framework employs an auto-regressive causal transformer architecture in conjunction with relational memory to facilitate the generation of radiology reports for 3D chest CT images. Similarly, Reg2RG employs a region-guided approach for the generation of chest CT reports. However, the generation of reports represents merely one phase of the comprehensive radiology reporting process and lacks essential quality control measures. To address this, medical vision-language models (VLMs) have been proposed to facilitate tasks such as report generation and visual question answering (VQA). Particularly, 3D medical VLMs have demonstrated efficacy in the analysis of 3D medical imaging modalities, such as computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). For example, RadFM introduces a comprehensive vision-language foundation model that integrates both 2D and 3D medical data for radiological applications. Furthermore, M3D provides an extensive multi-modal medical dataset, alongside M3D-LaMed, a multi-modal large language model designed for the analysis of 3D medical images. Merlin develops a 3D vision-language model (VLM) that leverages both structured and unstructured data for the interpretation of abdominal CT scans. Additionally, CT-CHAT is a vision-language foundation model specifically designed for 3D chest CT volumes, integrating the CT-CLIP vision encoder with a pretrained large language model (LLM). Med3DVLM is a 3D VLM that enhances multimodal representations through the utilization of an efficient encoder, a contrastive learning strategy, and a dual-stream projector. Lastly, Hulu-Med offers a comprehensive medical visual language model (VLM) that integrates text, 2D and 3D images, and video within a unified architecture.
