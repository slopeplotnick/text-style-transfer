This segment of the interviews aimed to identify current good practices that are essential for maintaining continuity in the new approach. The predominant practice identified is Factorization and Reuse, which is substantiated by team discussions wherein each new feature is evaluated to ascertain whether it is exclusive to a single application or can be generalized for use across multiple applications. Moreover, the concept of innovation serves as a significant impetus, encouraging the team to maintain the currency of their skills. This is significant to the participants, as they possess the capability to routinely enhance the code.
