Individuals who are closer to business aspects or less technically inclined may be the most reluctant to embrace change. Although they acknowledge the potential benefits, they might have more concerns about the rapid pace of developments. Since our team is relatively small and accustomed to working with new technologies, and because the adoption of a Software Product Line (SPL) was considered from the project's inception, we anticipated minimal resistance to change. In this context, drawing from our interview results, we have identified strategies to consolidate effective practices, enhance benefits, and address any apprehensions. It is essential to maintain the effective practices already established within the SPL framework to reassure the team. While significant changes to the workflow are anticipated, preserving these established practices will help the team remain in a familiar environment and facilitate a smoother transition. The SPL approach emphasizes accelerated processes, systematic reuse, and modularization—principles that are already being implemented. Therefore, the team can focus on leveraging the associated benefits of this methodology. Transitioning to an SPL is a good way to make reuse and modularization sustainable. Moreover, additional advantages of SPL include customization, automation, and industrialization. Furthermore, it is pertinent to consider other aspects, such as enhancing code quality, which contributes to the mitigation of technical debt. This means that the code base achieves greater consistency, exhibits fewer defects, and mitigates the risks associated with clone-and-own practices. Additionally, the resolution of recurrent bugs contributes to an overall enhancement of code quality. Interestingly, the perceived advantages related to selling are not exclusively recognized by product owners but are, in fact, more frequently emphasized by IT professionals. For instance, employing this form of engineering may enhance and reinforce the company's research and development (R&D) reputation. Furthermore, the adoption of SPL facilitates the accelerated production of new software.
