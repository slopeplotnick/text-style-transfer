The type of padding employed significantly impacts performance: coherent text padding, whether non-relevant or relevant, consistently outperforms random padding, which in turn surpasses multi-task padding. Moreover, the position of padding is crucial; placing context after the task description could degrade model performance more than placing it before. LLM agents are AI systems where pre-trained language models are embedded in a decision-making loop to autonomously execute multi-step, multi-turn tasks using external tools. Context windows have expanded significantly, advancing from 2,000 tokens in GPT-3 to 2 million in Grok 4 Fast, enabled by improved position embeddings and attention mechanisms. However, these models often exhibit recency bias and poor recall of distant tokens. Benchmarks like NIAH and LongBench demonstrate that while expanding context windows improves capacity, it does not inherently enhance capability. Safety assessments for large language models encompass metrics such as toxicity, misinformation dissemination, and system vulnerabilities, with LongSafety specifically evaluating performance on extended inputs of up to 5,000 tokens. In the domain of agent safety, evaluations concentrate on interactive tasks: AgentSecurity and Agent Dojo assess robustness against prompt injection attacks, while RedCode and MobileSafetyBench examine the risks associated with executing code. Most relevantly, AgentHarm serves as a benchmark for evaluating agents' proficiency in managing harmful tasks and executing multi-step workflows. We have extended the AgentHarm benchmark to evaluate agentic capabilities and behaviors under long-context inputs for both benign and harmful tasks. Additionally, AgentHarm tests the efficacy of agents in employing tools within multi-step task scenarios. The framework incorporates tools that are meticulously configured to emulate real-world functionalities, such as Google Search or social media posting APIs, thereby enabling testers to exert comprehensive control over task execution.
