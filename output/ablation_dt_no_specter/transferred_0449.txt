In summary, these studies aim to find ways to diminish hallucinations in text generation, thereby enhancing the credibility and trustworthiness of content produced by LLMs. There is great potential for strategies such as zero-resource detection and self-awareness in combating hallucinations in language models due to the various challenges they present. It is crucial to understand the link between LLMs and hallucinations, as this influences the choice of models that effectively address this issue. Various LLMs exhibit differing degrees of hallucinations, often due to differences in their architectural design and training methodologies. The study [1] provides a comprehensive discussion on hallucinations in language models, outlining the discomfort experienced by these models when generating illogical or non-factual outputs. It also stresses the need to comprehend the underlying causes and classifications of hallucinations encountered in NLP systems, such as the fabrication of facts and the production of nonsensical statements. Likewise, [5] explains that the propensity of these models to generate hallucinations arises from the extensive and intricate nature of their training datasets, which can occasionally lead to the presentation of inaccurate information or the fabrication of non-existent content.
