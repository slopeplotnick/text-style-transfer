This platform is 5-6 years old. According to the criteria of the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK's proprietary platform is classified as a "Level 3: Software Platform" with a reference architecture common to all products. The current architecture contains a collection of shared assets, and explicit variation points for new variants are defined. The migration goal is to reach an architectural state where domain commonality and variability are systematically identified, and a standardized reference architecture is specified for the entire software product line. This includes the systematic and managed reuse of assets from a centralized repository, the explicit representation of variability within these assets, and the definition of a reference architecture that governs permissible variations in application architectures, while incorporating numerous quality attributes into the overarching software product line architecture. Furthermore, variability management is explicitly addressed within the software product line architecture, specifying allowable configurations for application architectures and delineating variation points and constraints for most of these variations, including adherence rules for application-specific variants. The primary objective is to deliver a software configuration that facilitates the automated synthesis of variants. According to the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK operates at the fourth level at the organizational tier. This necessitates the synchronization and coordination of domain engineering and application engineering. The development of the project is executed through robust collaboration between agronomists and software developers. The decision to initiate migration and collaborate with our research team was reached through a collegiate discussion involving project leaders and stakeholders. Prior to the commencement of interview design, a well-defined research methodology for the remainder of the project had not yet been established. Our initial objective was to ascertain the perceptions of the company's stakeholders regarding the transition to a Software Product Line (SPL). Based on the findings of the interviews, we will proceed with the development of a comprehensive methodology for the migration project.
