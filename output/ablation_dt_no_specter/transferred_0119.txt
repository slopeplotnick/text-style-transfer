Benchmarks. We evaluate OMNIGUARD on a diverse suite of public safety benchmarks spanning both unimodal and cross-modal settings. For the unimodal setting, performance is assessed across four modalities — text, image, video, and audio. Specifically, for text, we utilize BeaverTails (Ji et al., 2023), ToxicChat (Lin et al., 2023), WildGuardMix (Han et al., 2024), Aegis2.0 (Ghosh et al., 2025), and the OpenAI Moderation dataset (Markov et al., 2023). For image, UnsafeBench (Qu et al., 2024), VLGuard (Zong et al., 2024), and LlavaGuard (Helff et al., 2025a) are employed. In the video domain, we evaluate using SafeSora (Dai et al., 2024a) and SafeWatch-Bench (Chen et al., 2025). For audio, MuTox English split (Costa-jussà et al., 2024) and WildGuardMix-TTS are used, the latter constructed by converting WildGuardMix (Han et al., 2024) test samples into speech via a text-to-speech pipeline consistent with our dataset construction methodology. In the cross-modal setting, OMNIGUARD is evaluated across three configurations — image-text, video-text, and audio-text — using MM-SafetyBench (Liu et al., 2024b), VideoSafetyBench (Liu et al., 2025a), and AIAH (Yang et al., 2025), respectively. Notably, for benchmarks containing only unsafe samples, accuracy is reported exclusively. Baselines. OMNIGUARD is compared against a comprehensive suite of baselines across all modalities, including LLMs, VLLMs, and audio LLMs. Both large-scale and small-scale state-of-the-art models are included to evaluate their safeguarding capabilities. Moreover, OMNIGUARD is benchmarked against specialized guardrail models, including those based on LLM and VLM architectures. Detailed baseline configurations are presented in Table 7. Uni-Modality. In unimodal evaluations, OMNI-GUARD is compared against leading proprietary and open-source baselines across four safety scenarios: text (Table 1), image (Table 2), video (Table 3), and audio (Table 4). Both OMNI-GUARD-7B and OMNI-GUARD-3B consistently achieve leading results across all modalities. While OMNI-GUARD-7B delivers the strongest overall performance, the smaller variant, OMNI-GUARD-3B, achieves results comparable to or exceeding much larger models such as GPT-4o, Qwen3-235B, and Qwen3-VL-235B, underscoring the effectiveness of our omni-modal safety alignment strategy.
