We fully leverage advanced artificial intelligence tools to upgrade both online strategies and system infrastructure. From a strategic perspective, we incorporate the more reliable query-item relevance scores generated by the enhanced model into the downstream ranking module, achieving the optimal trade-off through Pareto optimization. From an infrastructural perspective, supported by the model's robust performance, we systematically retire or replace the heuristic, rule-based methods previously utilized in the pipeline. The integration of these improvements has demonstrated substantial gains. For instance, studies such as DeepSeek-R1 have observed the emergent phenomenon of long chains-of-thought and the model's capacity for sudden insights or "Aha Moments." Similarly, we conducted a tracking analysis of the variations in the model's response length during the training phase, with findings illustrated in Fig 17. The results reveal that as the model's capabilities improved, the response length exhibited a general reduction and eventually stabilized. This suggests that extensive reasoning steps are not invariably necessary for model capability enhancement but rather emerge as a concomitant phenomenon during the training process. Moreover, further analysis indicates that distinct task types require varying reasoning chain lengths. Specifically, tasks such as mathematical computation and code generation demand more intricate, step-by-step reasoning processes. In contrast, tasks like discerning user intent and aligning it with the appropriate item are better addressed through straightforward and clear reasoning approaches, as additional reasoning steps do not yield further performance improvements. For tasks involving visual data, a more efficient approach is to directly leverage Vision-Language Models (VLMs), which inherently possess image comprehension capabilities, thereby eliminating the need for supplementary steps such as caption generation. To evaluate this methodology, we conducted experiments using the teacher model Qwen2.5-VL-72B, which was employed to sequentially execute two critical steps: generating knowledge and reasoning chains of thought (CoT), followed by applying rule-based CoT reasoning.
