This section will review the current most common applications of artificial intelligence in the field of exploration and point out frequent errors associated with its use. Subsequently, I will conjecture on the potential evolution of AI within exploration, particularly through the lens of decision-making processes. The ultimate goal of exploration remains the discovery of novel findings. Discoveries can occur near known deposits, known as brownfield exploration, or in unexplored areas, referred to as greenfield exploration. In the next decade, it is anticipated that brownfield discoveries will surpass those in greenfield areas. Exploration can be seen as an exercise in refining the focus of investigation by systematically reducing uncertainty regarding the presence of economically viable deposits. Initially, exploration often starts at a continental scale, examining large igneous provinces where geological conditions may favor magma ascent and entrapment in economically viable concentrations. At this stage, geologists employ geological mapping and airborne geophysical data. Anomalies detected within these datasets may indicate subsurface features of interest, prompting a more detailed examination through fieldwork, surveys, and soil analyses. The subsequent phase, which is the most financially demanding, involves drilling to confirm or refute hypotheses about subsurface conditions. Ideally, these decisions are informed by rigorous methodologies, such as decision science and uncertainty quantification, to minimize expenditures by effectively assessing uncertainty and the value of information. Various data sources contribute to understanding mineral system formation. These datasets are utilized not only to predict regions of interest for exploration but also to evaluate geological hypotheses. Data acquisition serves to mitigate uncertainty regarding subsurface conditions. Furthermore, various decisions are undertaken throughout the exploration process, with the objectives of these decisions evolving as the project progresses.
