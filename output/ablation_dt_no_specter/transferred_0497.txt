Objective Evaluation. ViSAudio demonstrates superior efficacy in generating spatial audio from videos, as evidenced by evaluations conducted on various datasets, as illustrated in Table 2. It is benchmarked against several state-of-the-art video-to-spatial-audio generation methodologies, with comprehensive details provided in Section 5.1. For the BiAudio and MUSIC test sets, both video and captions were utilized, whereas for the FAIR-Play dataset, which lacks captions, only video data was employed. Models lacking text-processing capabilities were evaluated using video inputs exclusively. The results presented in Table 2 demonstrate that ViSAudio achieves superior performance in overall audio quality, spatial channel distribution consistency, audio-visual synchronization, and semantic alignment. These findings highlight the model's ability to generate realistic binaural audio while maintaining coherence between auditory and visual modalities, even across diverse acoustic environments. Notably, its strong performance on the out-of-distribution FAIR-Play dataset, which was not included in the training phase, indicates its capability to adapt to novel scenarios. Figure 3 provides a comparative analysis of various methodologies for generating spatial audio from video inputs. A notable instance illustrates an individual playing the sitar while the camera pans from left to right, resulting in an auditory shift from the right to the left channel. Spectrogram analysis was conducted to compare the audio generated by ViSAudio, ground truth audio, and outputs from alternative methods. The results demonstrate that ViSAudio exhibits the highest fidelity to the ground truth audio, accurately replicating spatial sound dynamics. Specifically, when auditory events occur on the left, the left channel exhibits increased amplitude, effectively mirroring real-world auditory perception. In contrast, alternative methodologies failed to accurately reproduce the rhythm and did not effectively demonstrate the distinctions between the left and right audio channels.
