The rapid enhancement of large language models (LLMs) presents novel opportunities for leveraging AI in beneficial applications. As these models increasingly facilitate online interactions, their capacity to enhance empathy and promote constructive discourse emerges as a critical area for responsible AI research. This study investigates the potential of LLMs to transcend mere identification of harmful content in dialogues, exploring their ability to de-escalate tensions and facilitate conflict resolution in online environments. The proposed framework comprises two primary components: first, the model evaluates the fairness of a given conversation while analyzing the emotional states of the participants; second, it generates responses aimed at de-escalating tensions and facilitating conflict resolution. To assess the efficacy of these models, researchers constructed a comprehensive dataset derived from Reddit and developed a multifaceted evaluation methodology. This methodology incorporates rule-based scoring, user simulation, and comparisons with human judgments. Empirical results indicate that commercially available models demonstrate superior performance in reasoning and conflict resolution compared to their open-access counterparts. Nevertheless, the findings of this study indicate that while LLMs demonstrate considerable potential, they also exhibit certain limitations in facilitating harmonious online interactions. These models are transforming the dynamics of communication, education, and collaboration. Beyond applications such as language translation and text summarization, LLMs are increasingly utilized in domains including education, healthcare, and mental health, highlighting their versatility and expanding scope of influence. As the deployment of these models in online discourse environments increases, the potential to extend their utility beyond mere task automation to fostering more civil and equitable interactions becomes evident. However, a significant challenge in digital communication is the prevalence of hostile exchanges, characterized by escalating antagonism and personal attacks. This issue is exacerbated by the absence of visual and social cues, which are typically present in face-to-face interactions.
