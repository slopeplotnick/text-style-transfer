Nearswap, as proposed by Goddard et al. (2024), is a method that enhances interpolation when parameters are similar and diminishes it when they differ. To evaluate various model architectures, we selected three families of large language models for our experiments: GPT, LLAMA, and QWEN. Specifically, the GPT family, initially introduced by Radford et al. (2019) and subsequently expanded by Gao et al. (2020), includes models such as GPT-2-small, GPT-2-medium, GPT-2-large, GPT-2-x1, and GPT-Neo-2.7B. Similarly, the LLAMA series, developed by Touvron et al. (2023) and further refined by Dubey et al. (2024), includes LLAMA-2-7B, LLAMA-3-8B, LLAMA-3.1-8B, LLAMA-3.2-1B, and LLAMA-3.2-3B. In contrast, the Qwen family, introduced by Qwen et al. (2024), comprises QWEN2-0.5B, QWEN2-1.5B, and QWEN2-7B. The aforementioned models are accessible via the Hugging Face repository, with corresponding links provided in Appendix A. Furthermore, the computational resources utilized for continual pretraining to develop biased LLMs are detailed in Appendix C, while the hyperparameter configurations are outlined in Appendix D. We evaluate social bias in LLMs using three established benchmarks: the Bias Benchmark for Question-Answering (BBQ) (Parrish et al., 2022), the Bias in Open-Ended Language Generation Dataset (BOLD) (Dhamala et al., 2021), and HONEST (Nozza et al., 2021). The URLs of these datasets are listed in Appendix E. The BBQ dataset, as delineated by Parrish et al. (2022), comprises approximately 58,000 templated question-answer pairs across nine social dimensions pertinent to U.S. English speakers. By contrasting "underspecified" with "fully specified" versions of each question, it measures the extent to which models rely on stereotypical priors rather than explicit evidence. Similarly, the BOLD dataset, as delineated by Dhamala et al. (2021), comprises 23,679 prompts, systematically categorized into 43 demographic subgroups encompassing occupation, gender, race, religion, and political ideology. To ensure that the debiasing methodologies do not adversely affect performance on ancillary tasks, we assess both the debiased and pre-trained LLMs utilizing the SuperGLUE benchmark, which consists of eight tasks: BoolQ, CB, COPA, MultiRC, ReCoRD, RTE, WiC, and WSC. All assessments are conducted utilizing the Language Model Evaluation Harness.
