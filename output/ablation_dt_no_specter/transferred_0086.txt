Seven model merging techniques were evaluated in this study: Linear (Wortsman et al., 2022), Karcher Mean (Grove and Karcher, 1973), SLERP (Shoemake, 1985), NuSLERP (Goddard et al., 2024), TIES (Yadav et al., 2023), DELLA (Deep et al., 2024), and Nearswap (Goddard et al., 2024). These methods were applied to merge a bias-inverted model with a pre-trained LLM. Linear. Wortsman et al. (2022) proposed the simplest merging technique, which adds and averages the weights of fine-tuned models with scaling parameters. By leveraging straightforward summation, it offers an economical and effective solution for integrating knowledge from multiple models while maintaining consistent performance. Karcher Mean. Goddard et al. (2024) introduced merging methods that compute the Karcher mean (Grove and Karcher, 1973) on a Riemannian manifold, enabling geometric fusion of models. Unlike Linear merging, the Karcher Mean accounts for the curved geometry of the parameter space, ensuring compatibility with non-Euclidean structures. SLERP. Goddard et al. (2024) presented SLERP, which interpolates model weight vectors along a great circle path on the hypersphere (Shoemake, 1985). This approach preserves the curvature of the parameter space by constraining the interpolation path to the unit hypersphere, performing pairwise spherical interpolation. NuSLERP. Extending SLERP, Goddard et al. (2024) introduced NuSLERP, which assigns differential interpolation ratios to layers or tensors, enabling non-uniform spherical interpolation. By weighting critical layers more heavily, NuSLERP balances local expertise with global stability, achieving robust performance through simple rule-based settings. TIES. Yadav et al. (2023) proposed TIES, which merges models by extracting parameter differences that capture task-specific knowledge. By sparsifying these differences, TIES minimizes interference and better preserves the strengths of each model. DELLA. Deep et al. (2024) introduced DELLA, which reduces interference by selectively pruning less significant task-specific parameter updates. Using adaptive pruning with magnitude-aware rescaling, DELLA assigns higher keep probabilities to larger-magnitude parameters within rows, improving retention of essential weights and ensuring the original model's performance is maintained.
