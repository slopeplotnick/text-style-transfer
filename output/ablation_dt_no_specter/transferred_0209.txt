In all three models, the total causal influence of the variable Young on the image and prediction remains consistent, demonstrating its reliability as a measure irrespective of the level of abstraction. However, when an explanation model disregards the causal structure by treating variables as independent, it captures only the direct effect. This direct effect aligns with the total effect in the first model but reduces to zero in the final model, indicating that direct influence is not a universally reliable measure. Section 5.3.1 addresses the challenges that arise when model assumptions are violated, thereby underscoring the necessity for careful selection of models. Whenever assumptions introduce bias into explanations, it is imperative to either modify the model or ensure that the explainer is cognizant of the biased context within which the explanations are applicable. Nonetheless, this should not be construed as an indication that complex models with fewer assumptions are invariably preferable to simpler ones. To ensure that explanations align with their intended interpretation, the causal model must correspond to the level of abstraction inherent in the explainee’s understanding of the world, which is presumed to be approximate but not erroneous. Prioritizing comprehensibility necessitates the omission of certain granular causal mechanisms, thereby consolidating pertinent information into a more streamlined and accessible representation. A notable advantage of the causal explanation model outlined in Section 5.3.1 lies in its reliance on expert knowledge, which enhances its capacity to identify and eliminate spurious correlations more effectively than approaches solely dependent on data-driven modeling. Consequently, the explanation model is capable of identifying spurious relationships acquired by the predictor, even when the same dataset is employed to develop the explanation model. With the increased accessibility of expert knowledge facilitated by large language models (LLMs), as illustrated in the aforementioned example, this approach holds particular promise for enhancing model performance by detecting unintended model behavior.
