Datasets. Following established methodologies (Wu et al. 2025), we curate 5,500 Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset (Hendrycks et al. 2021). Evaluation Benchmarks. We conduct evaluations using several rigorous benchmarks, including AIME 2024 (MAA, 2024), AMC, MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al. 2021), GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math. To extend the evaluation beyond conventional parameters, we also incorporate the ARC-C (Clark et al. 2018) and GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al. 2024) datasets. Inference is performed using a greedy decoding approach with a 3,000-token limit (Wu et al. 2025). Baselines. CAPO is evaluated alongside four principal reinforcement learning methodologies: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. For each baseline, we also assess its CAPO-enhanced variant, enabling a direct comparison of the original methods with their CAPO-augmented counterparts. This approach highlights CAPO's contribution as a general performance enhancer. To ensure a fair comparison, we standardize the experimental setup across all methods. Given the unique requirements of each method, detailed training configurations are provided in Appendix B. Datasets. We utilize the GUI-R1-3K dataset (Luo et al. 2025), derived from OS-Atlas (Wu et al. 2024b), which consists of 3,000 vision-language-action examples spanning web and mobile platforms. This dataset aligns visual states with corresponding natural language instructions and action sequences, offering a robust benchmark for evaluating multimodal reasoning and planning capabilities. Evaluation Benchmarks. For planning tasks, we focus on three specific benchmarks: GUI-Act-Web (Luo et al. 2025), OmniAct-Web (Kapoor et al. 2024), and AndroidControl-Low/High (Li et al. 2024b). For perception tasks, we employ Screenshot Pro (Li et al. 2025). Baselines. We further compare GRPO with its CAPO-enhanced variant using the established GUI benchmark framework (Wu et al. 2024b). This supplementary evaluation underscores CAPO's effectiveness across diverse reasoning scenarios, extending its applicability beyond mathematical reasoning tasks.
