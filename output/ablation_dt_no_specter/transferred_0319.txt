Besides, there is another strategy we employed during the training phase. Since the base model sometimes struggles to follow instructions with longer prompts, we introduced a preliminary warm-up stage using supervised fine-tuning (SFT) to ensure it reliably adheres to the required format. In this process, the Challenger role engages in few-shot grounded generation and computes a corresponding reward. Then, the Solver engages in online curriculum learning, computes its reward, and undergoes iterative updates. This ensures the Solver focuses on problems that are challenging yet solvable, consistent with optimal learning theory. The Solver's reward system integrates both synthetic and empirical data, weighted by curriculum difficulty. R-FeW retains the iterative framework of R-ZERO but now employs a grounded and curriculum-aware data pipeline. The Challenger generates synthetic tasks with few-shot demonstrations derived from human input. The Solver attempts to resolve these tasks and evaluates the frequency of successful outcomes. An online filter selects tasks of moderate difficulty from both synthetic and authentic datasets. We employ Qwen3-4B-Base and Qwen3-8B-Base as our primary models. For comparison, we evaluate against several baselines: (1) Base Model — the pretrained checkpoint without additional training, which is accessible for download; (2) R-Zero — the pioneering ungrounded self-play approach wherein the model autonomously generates its own questions and answers, facilitating self-enhancement; (3) Absolute Zero — a self-play framework confined to code generation tasks with Python execution serving as verification; (4) SPICE — a grounded self-play framework that utilizes extensive document corpora to formulate reasoning tasks with document-grounded responses. We reproduced baselines (1) and (2) using the corresponding configurations from the available setups, ensuring consistency in the training procedure. In contrast, the results for baselines (3) and (4) were derived from the findings reported by Liu et al.
