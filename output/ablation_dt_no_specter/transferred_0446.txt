The interviews will focus on the following questions: ① Which aspects of your thinking are the most challenging to articulate during the creative process? ② Which of the four foundational units is the most helpful in facilitating your creative process? ③ Do you encounter any challenges, and how do you address them? ④ Do you generate any novel ideas during the creative process? Participants (P1-P4) expressed a preference for story creation using a drag-and-drop card interface over traditional text-based outlining methods, citing the former as more engaging and enjoyable. However, some participants also highlighted challenges associated with the repetitive process of modifying card attributes (P1, P4). P1 stated, "In the process of story creation, my initial approach is to prioritize the overarching structure by utilizing event cards and entity cards to establish the narrative framework. Subsequently, I proceed to populate the attributes associated with the event and entity cards." P4 remarked, "Throughout the creation process, I found it necessary to make multiple revisions. Enhancing the convenience of attribute editing would significantly increase my inclination to adopt this method." Moreover, several participants suggested that certain semantic elements of the narrative could be effectively conveyed through interactive mechanisms (P2, P3). P2 articulated a preference for positioning entity cards associated with the same event atop the event cards, thereby facilitating the identification of entities involved in the event. P3 proposed the utilization of relationship cards to interlink various entity cards, suggesting that event cards equipped with connecting lines would more effectively depict the sequence and flow of events. Building on these insights, we identify significant interaction patterns (Figure 2) and propose the following design goals: DG1: The system should provide interfaces for inputting the attributes of each foundational unit, thereby aiding users in constructing the initial framework of the narrative. DG2: The system is designed to assist users in comprehending the comprehensive narrative structure, thereby facilitating the analysis of interconnections between entities and events. DG3: The system is intended to provide assessments and specific recommendations aimed at enhancing the existing story framework.
