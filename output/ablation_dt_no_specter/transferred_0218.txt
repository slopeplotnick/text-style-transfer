Baselines and Models. We employ the Gemini-2.0-Flash model in our LLM-only baselines, which operate without external knowledge and rely solely on the original question. We experiment with multiple ablations of the CAIRNS framework: (1) Domain RAG (literature-only), which involves retrieval exclusively from the literature subset; and (2) Domain RAG (literature+data), which incorporates retrieval augmented with both literature and climate data through a two-stage API reasoning process. Gemini-2.0-Flash remains the principal model for CAIRNS and all its variations. All models are evaluated using identical corpora, prompts, and assessment parameters to ensure methodological consistency. Impact of ScholarGuide Prompt (SGP). The ScholarGuide Prompt enhances readability by improving contextual richness, structural coherence, and linguistic fluency. Moreover, it addresses citation inconsistencies, thereby increasing the reliability of information, despite a marginal reduction in the overall citation rate. By implementing unified reference numbering and grounding each factual statement in explicit evidence, SGP facilitates the generation of clearer, more verifiable, and location-specific answers by the model. This approach demonstrates efficacy across both zero-shot and few-shot scenarios, underscoring the importance of structured prompting in the success of CAIRNS. Impact of Multi-turn ReAct (MTR). The integration of Multi-turn ReAct enhances the model's ability to refine its answers by harmonizing literature with climate data, thereby producing more comprehensive responses with improved contextual relevance. Although its impact is less pronounced compared to SGP, it contributes to a more structured reasoning process, thereby enhancing the model's ability to address complex queries. Location-Weighted Retrieval (LWR). Specifically, Location-Weighted Retrieval facilitates the generation of region-specific responses, particularly for geographically focused questions. Its removal results in a slight reduction in specificity; however, other performance metrics remain largely unaffected.
