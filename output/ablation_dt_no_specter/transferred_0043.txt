Radiology reporting is a critical yet time-intensive and error-prone task for radiologists, particularly when interpreting volumetric medical images such as CT and MRI scans. Moreover, rigorous quality control is essential to ensure that the final report is accurate, comprehensive, and adheres to clinical standards. However, this verification process is often tedious and labor-intensive, requiring meticulous checks of content, structure, and terminology. Existing automated approaches, including radiology report generation methods and medical vision-language models, primarily focus on automating the report generation phase while overlooking the equally important quality control process. This limitation reduces their effectiveness in providing holistic support to radiologists. To address this gap, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an advanced AI assistant equipped with a suite of orchestrated tools for automated radiology reporting with integrated quality control. By leveraging large language models as its reasoning backbone, Radiologist Copilot autonomously selects tools, formulates analytical plans, and executes tasks, closely emulating the decision-making processes of radiologists. Its capabilities include region localization within medical images, region-specific analytical planning, strategic selection of report templates, and iterative quality refinement through feedback mechanisms. Consequently, Radiologist Copilot enables the generation of accurate, detailed, and efficient radiology reports, significantly enhancing clinical workflows and supporting radiologists in their practice. Experimental results demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in radiology reporting. The source code will be made publicly available upon acceptance.
