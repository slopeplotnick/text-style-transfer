As shown in Figure 9, the Menta interface is capable of executing various classification tasks, including the assessment of stress, depression (both binary and severity-level), and suicide risk (both binary and categorical). Upon activation of the "Start Evaluation" button, social media posts are processed through the selected model, and the system displays the predicted class labels, correctness, and associated evaluation metrics. Model efficacy was evaluated across six distinct tasks, utilizing datasets comprising between 10 and 3,000 posts, contingent upon task complexity and data availability. Moreover, our analysis demonstrates a balance between model efficiency and predictive performance when compared to other analogous systems. From a deployment perspective, Menta is engineered to deliver high accuracy, rapid inference speed, and responsiveness, making it an optimal choice for practical applications where both performance and immediacy are paramount. As depicted in Figure 9, the chart delineates three dimensions: inference throughput per second (ITPS), time to first token (TTFT), and accuracy. Each circle represents a distinct model, with the size of the circle indicating the corresponding memory consumption (RAM). Falcon is optimized for speed, demonstrating rapid task initiation and processing times, albeit at the cost of accuracy, as it exhibits the lowest predictive performance among the models evaluated. Conversely, StableLM achieves superior accuracy but is characterized by increased latency and resource consumption, resulting in elevated deployment costs and prolonged response times for users. In contrast, Menta consistently demonstrates high accuracy, with the exception of Task 5, where it is marginally surpassed by StableLM and Phi-4 Mini, while concurrently achieving superior efficiency in terms of latency and throughput compared to StableLM. This implies that Menta produces high-quality outputs expeditiously and with minimal computational resource consumption, which is crucial for systems with extensive user engagement. In summary, although all models exhibit inherent trade-offs, Menta is strategically positioned for optimal deployment in real-world applications, offering robust performance with manageable computational demands. It demonstrates high performance with minimal resource requirements, thereby reducing operational costs and ensuring an optimal user experience.
