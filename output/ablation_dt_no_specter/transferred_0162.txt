Both auditory and visual inputs are utilized to ascertain the modality that predominantly conveys emotions in each sample. By contrasting the model's behavior across these modality combinations, we identify which modality is emotion-dominant in each audio-visual sample. After constructing both the high-quality human-annotated dataset and the FAU-filtered additional dataset, these datasets are amalgamated to create a comprehensive training set. Using this combined data, we extract the mutual information (MI) for every sample. Once the emotion-dominant modality is determined, we reorganize the structure of the reasoning text. Specifically, for each training example, we decompose the reasoning text into two modality-specific segments: an audio-based reasoning text and a video-based reasoning text. To delineate these modality-specific parts, we employ two special tokens, <aud_desc> and <vis_desc>, placing the corresponding token at both the beginning and the end of each audio- and video-based text. We then reorder these texts according to the MI. If audio is the emotion-dominant modality, the audio-based reasoning text is positioned first, followed by the video-based text; conversely, if video is emotion-dominant, the video-based text precedes the audio-based text. For samples where audio and video are equally informative, we include both orderings, i.e., one with audio-first and one with video-first. By leveraging the reasoning text reorganized with the dominant modality, the model learns to initiate its reasoning process from the modality that carries the strongest emotional signal. Following the initial phase, we refine the model's reasoning process through Modality-Aligned Reward Optimization. Prior methodologies typically employ two reward types: an answer reward and a format reward. However, these approaches do not adequately address the emphasis on inputs that most effectively convey emotional content. Therefore, to reinforce MI-guided reasoning throughout optimization, we introduce two novel rewards: the modality-aligned order reward and the modality-grounded reasoning reward.
