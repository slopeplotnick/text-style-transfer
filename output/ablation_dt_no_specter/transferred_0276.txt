Case 3: Suicidal Risk Level Detection. This case highlights a misclassification where a Reddit post with a supportive level of suicidal risk was predicted as behavior by the Menta model. The primary cause was the excessive input length, which included a long multi-speaker thread with formatting noise, such as atypical symbols, HTML artifacts, and placeholder tokens like "Hyperactive behavior." These tokens acted as spurious cues, thereby biasing the model toward behavior. The post involved an individual encouraging another to persevere for the sake of their younger siblings and to continue efforts to prevent harm from their father. Moreover, the individual advised disregarding a provocative comment, emphasizing that they did not perceive the recipient as worthless or deserving of self-harm. However, the rationale for classifying this content as indicative of behavior was linked to themes of overextension and difficulties in managing academic and social responsibilities. The individual reported that learning to establish boundaries, such as declining excessive commitments, contributed to improved well-being and the development of stronger social connections.
