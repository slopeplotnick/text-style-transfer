The transition towards increased reliance on electric energy has necessitated an intensified focus on the exploration of critical minerals. Despite substantial demand, the discovery of these minerals has not translated into financial gains. Over the past two decades, the rate of new discoveries has diminished, notwithstanding increased investment. This paper proposes the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to enhance the scientific rigor of mineral exploration, thereby minimizing errors and reducing costs. Currently, interdisciplinary approaches involving geology and geophysics have proven inadequate in identifying concealed deposits. The prevailing methodologies fail to accurately account for uncertainty, resulting in suboptimal decision-making and financial losses associated with unproductive drilling endeavors. A novel approach is advocated, rooted in Bayesianism and falsification principles, wherein the primary objective of data collection is to critically assess existing hypotheses. Metrics are employed to guide the selection of subsequent data acquisition, ensuring informed decision-making. A practical framework applicable to any mineral exploration is delineated; however, its implementation necessitates diverse AI methodologies. Of particular significance are innovative unsupervised learning techniques that collaborate with domain experts to enhance data interpretation and generate alternative geological hypotheses, alongside human-in-the-loop AI systems that optimize data collection in geology, geophysics, geochemistry, and drilling operations. These systems aim to alleviate uncertainties pertaining to geological theories prior to addressing the quantity and quality of mineral deposits. The efficacy of this methodology will be demonstrated through several ongoing mineral exploration and resource evaluation projects.
