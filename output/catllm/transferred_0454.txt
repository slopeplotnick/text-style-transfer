This illustration underscores the significance of process analysis in elucidating the mechanisms through which agents either accomplish or fail to resolve problems. Recent endeavors have focused on dissecting agent operations by scrutinizing their procedural steps and pinpointing failures, yet such manual analyses are susceptible to bias and incompleteness, proving ineffective for novel systems. Moreover, sequential examination of steps fails to adequately capture agent behavior or problem-solving strategies, specifically in terms of task execution and efficient planning.
