To ensure the applicability of the simulated data for computational research, it was necessary to enable effective comparability across different data groups. Specifically, the dataset was divided into "Male" and "Female" groups to assess their internal consistency, which, if achieved, would validate the comparability of specific subsets of the data. This section outlines the general methodological approach employed across all four studies, except where explicitly noted otherwise within their respective sections. The experimental setup for the studies is illustrated in Figure 1. The simulated dataset was generated using GPT4o-mini, an iteration of the GPT-4 model updated as of October 2023. Default parameters, including a Temperature setting of 1.0 and a Top P value of 1.0, were utilized without the incorporation of frequency or presence penalties. The model was provided with prompts delineating specific characteristics of a hypothetical individual, such as age, gender, and ethnicity, and tasked with responding to a designated questionnaire under evaluation. The prompts were constructed to include the questionnaire items, response options, and explicit instructions to format the responses as a single string of comma-separated values, thereby facilitating data processing. Each prompt was executed independently to ensure that responses were not influenced by prior interactions. For illustrative purposes, an example prompt is as follows: "Pretend to be a [Ethnicity] [Gender] who is [Age] years old and lives in the UK. Answer this [Number of items]-item questionnaire. Each question uses a Likert scale from [Likert scale range]. Use this key to answer: [Response Key]. The questions are: [Questionnaire Items]. Give your answers as a single string of values separated by commas."
