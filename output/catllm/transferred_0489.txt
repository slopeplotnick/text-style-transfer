The duration of the experiment was approximately 20 to 35 minutes, during which participants were required to select a picture that appealed to them for a subsequent writing task. It was ensured that the volunteers comprehended the picture they selected. Subsequently, an introduction to the functionality of TaleFrame was provided. Participants were then tasked with creating a narrative utilizing their chosen picture. Observations were systematically recorded concerning their actions and verbal expressions throughout the experiment. Upon completion of the task, feedback was solicited from the participants through a series of questions. Each participant received a monetary compensation of $5 for their involvement. For the purpose of user feedback, volunteers assessed TaleFrame on a scale ranging from 1 to 5, reflecting their experiential evaluation. TaleFrame facilitates users in constructing a comprehensive story framework with ease, owing to its intuitive interface and the options available for defining various components of the narrative. The platform enables users to arrange the elements of the story interactively and illustrates their interconnections, thereby enhancing the coherence of the narrative. Users are afforded the opportunity to modify the structure of the story dynamically during the process. TaleFrame contributes to the preservation of the story's chronological consistency and proposes enhancements to the emotional and thematic aspects of the narrative. Furthermore, it evaluates the quality of the story and provides recommendations for improvement.
