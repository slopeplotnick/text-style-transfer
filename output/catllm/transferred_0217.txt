In Section 3, the relationship between the interview questions and four central topics pertinent to the research was examined. Since the majority of participants lacked familiarity with Software Product Line Engineering (SPLE), an explanation of its definition and significance was provided during the interviews, which may have introduced a degree of bias; however, reliance was placed on the interviewees to independently form their opinions, and the interviewer was trusted to minimize any influence. Open-ended questions were deliberately employed to enable respondents to provide comprehensive answers and share diverse perspectives. The questions were meticulously crafted to avoid restricting the scope of responses. Metrics were developed to optimize the analysis of the collected data and extract valuable insights. These metrics involved counting significant terms and their frequency, both overall and across specific roles. The data were presented systematically, with tables displaying detailed information and graphs illustrating the distribution of values. Unique responses that were not mentioned by other participants were included and thoroughly considered during the analysis process. Interview-derived terms were categorized and aligned with concepts in SPLE, such as "personalisation" and "making specific some parts of the code," which were conceptually linked to "customization," thereby enhancing consistency in the results. This alignment facilitated the identification of arguments within scholarly literature to address participants' concerns. Iterative engagement with diverse interviewees contributed to refining the interview procedure, ensuring smoother execution over time. While the questions remained unchanged, the approach to administering them was progressively improved, including providing additional clarifications and transitions between questions when required. Despite these procedural refinements, it is posited that the quantity and quality of information obtained from the initial and final questionnaires remained consistent throughout the study.
