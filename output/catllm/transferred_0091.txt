The efficacy of pre-training was evaluated by assessing the similarity of token types in generated samples to those present in the original pre-training dataset. The frequency of occurrence for each token type within the pre-training data was systematically compared to its occurrence within samples generated at various stages of training. It was observed that samples generated at the 250 epochs stage exhibited the greatest resemblance to the original dataset. Despite a continued reduction in training and validation losses up to 300 epochs, samples increasingly diverged from the original data characteristics, thus indicating the 250 epochs stage as optimal for fine-tuning on EMOPIA due to its ability to balance loss reduction with fidelity to the original dataset. Furthermore, an examination was conducted to ascertain the decoder's capability to generate MIDI using AV quadrants by directly providing quadrant labels instead of relying on text embeddings. This straightforward approach yielded MIDI samples that aligned effectively with EMOPIA's valence-arousal metrics. A listening test, as detailed in Section IV-A, was performed with four participants, whose evaluations are documented in Table I. Results indicated that the model's music achieved a superior fit for the arousal dimension, with accuracy at 0.70, compared to valence at 0.53, and combined valence-arousal at 0.40. Typically, expected accuracy would be 0.5 for individual dimensions and 0.25 for both dimensions combined. The model's music surpassed these baseline metrics, particularly in arousal at 0.70 and combined valence-arousal at 0.40. This suggests that participants perceived the music's emotional tone as more congruent with the text than could be attributed to chance. Expanding the study to include a larger participant pool would enhance the reliability of the results and facilitate testing for statistical significance. It is imperative to incorporate a more diverse array of listeners in subsequent studies to ascertain the robustness of these preliminary findings.
