The assessment of the logical and ethical dimensions of AI model responses represents a critical area of inquiry within artificial intelligence research. This paper introduces the Moral Consistency and Progression (MoCoP) system, which evaluates ethical considerations in AI without reliance on pre-existing datasets. Specifically, MoCoP autonomously generates, tests, and refines moral reasoning examples, eliminating the need for external input. Furthermore, it quantifies variations in moral reasoning exhibited by AI models over time, facilitating comparative consistency analysis across different models. The system integrates tools designed to analyze linguistic elements, semantic structures, and reasoning processes, thereby enabling longitudinal tracking of ethical reasoning in AI systems. The organizational structure of the paper is delineated as follows: Section 2 reviews prior research on AI ethics evaluation, Section 3 elaborates on the functional mechanisms of the MoCoP framework, Section 5 presents empirical test results, Section 6 discusses the findings, Section 7 examines methodological challenges, and Section 8 addresses the limitations of the study alongside prospective directions for future research. Previous studies predominantly concentrated on identifying and mitigating bias or harmful content within AI systems. For instance, OpenEthics aimed to enhance the transparency and assessability of AI ethics, while other research efforts focused on promoting fairness and reducing biases against specific demographic groups. Although these approaches contributed to greater transparency in AI operations, their reliance on static datasets and prompts constrained adaptability to evolving scenarios. Consequently, such methods were effective in demonstrating the ethical functioning of AI models at fixed points in time but lacked the capacity to monitor changes in moral reasoning or assess stability across diverse contexts. More recent investigations have extended beyond fairness to explore the mechanisms through which AI systems engage in moral and ethical decision-making processes.
