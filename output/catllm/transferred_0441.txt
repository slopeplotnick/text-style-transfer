The increasing size of large language models necessitates the utilization of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods. Currently, numerous novel PEFT methods present challenges in terms of reproducibility, usability, and comparability. To address these challenges, PEFT-FACTORY was developed as an integrated system designed to facilitate the fine-tuning of large language models through the use of both pre-existing and customizable PEFT methods. It is structured to be both flexible and extensible, and it incorporates 19 PEFT methods, 27 datasets for classification and text generation across 12 tasks, as well as evaluation tools for both general and PEFT-specific metrics. Consequently, PEFT-FACTORY provides a straightforward and reliable framework that enhances the reproducibility of results and the comparison of PEFT methods. The foundation of PEFT-FACTORY is LLaMAFactory, and it is accessible at https://github.com/kinit-sk/PEFT-Factory. Large language models have demonstrated exceptional performance across numerous natural language processing tasks, particularly following the advent of the transformer architecture. However, the scalability of these models has led to a continuous increase in size with respect to trainable parameters. This escalation in parameters renders fine-tuning a costly endeavor, demanding substantial data and posing accessibility challenges for many researchers and practitioners. PEFT methods endeavor to mitigate these issues by confining training to a subset of the model’s parameters, thereby achieving results akin to those obtained by training the model in its entirety. Such a reduction in trainable parameters can be accomplished by introducing new parameters, selecting specific parameters for training, or modifying the model to operate with fewer parameters.
