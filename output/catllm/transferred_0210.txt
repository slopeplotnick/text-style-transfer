The methodology section encompasses an overview of participant selection, interview setup, execution, and subsequent analysis of responses. Participants were selected to represent diverse groups, encompassing variations in roles, levels of experience, and educational backgrounds, thereby ensuring comprehensive representation. Specifically, the roles included Product Owners, Developers, Agronomists, and User Interface/Experience designers, with educational qualifications ranging from engineering degrees to doctoral degrees. Furthermore, individuals were chosen based on varying levels of experience within the project. In total, sixteen participants were involved, comprising nine Developers currently or previously engaged with the project, two Agronomists, three Product Owners, and two User Interface/Experience designers, all of whom were actively involved in project-related activities. Motivation to provide useful and insightful responses was considered significant, and participants were informed that their feedback would contribute to the development of an improved SPL process plan. Structured interviews were employed, characterized by a predefined set of questions, adhering to recommended practices of clarity and precision. Each question was designed to focus on a single concept, avoiding sensitive topics, retrospective inquiries, or questions beyond the participants’ expertise. This approach facilitated the adoption of actor-driven interviews. The interview framework comprised three distinct components: an introductory section wherein participants provided personal introductions, a main segment divided into two subsections addressing the current process and proposed changes, and a concluding section reflecting on the overall interview experience.
