The interviews were structured around the following questions: Firstly, which aspects of cognitive processes are challenging to articulate during creative endeavors? Secondly, which among the four primary components most significantly facilitates creativity? Thirdly, what obstacles are encountered, and what strategies are employed for resolution? Furthermore, do novel ideas emerge during the creative process? Volunteers, denoted as P1-P4, demonstrated a preference for constructing narratives using a drag-and-drop card setup over text outlines, as the former was perceived as more enjoyable. However, certain participants experienced difficulties with repetitive editing of card details (P1, P4). P1 articulated, "During the narrative construction, my initial focus is the overarching framework, commencing with event and entity cards to establish the narrative foundation. Subsequently, I append details to the event and entity cards." P4 observed, "Multiple amendments were necessary during creation. Greater ease in editing details would increase my inclination to utilize this method." Additionally, some volunteers posited that certain narrative elements might benefit from interactive representations (P2, P3). P2 remarked, "I prefer situating entity cards related to a specific event atop the event cards, facilitating immediate recognition of entities involved in the event." P3 proposed, "Utilizing relationship cards to interconnect different entity cards could enhance clarity, and if event cards were linked by lines, it would better illustrate the sequence and progression of events." Consequently, significant interaction patterns were identified (Figure 2), leading to the proposition of three design objectives: DG1 entails enabling users to input detailed information for each primary component to assist in constructing the initial narrative outline. DG2 involves aiding users in comprehending the complete narrative setup, thereby simplifying the visualization of connections between entities and events. DG3 requires the system to provide feedback and specific recommendations to refine the narrative framework.
