The model's improvement is facilitated through the assimilation of errors encountered during its operation, yet the incorporation of an excessive number of unrelated negative examples can disrupt the learning process, leading to instability. It has been observed that Off-Policy Sequence Masking contributes to maintaining stability in certain training contexts that are prone to fluctuations. Keep Routing Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models enhance operational efficiency by selectively utilizing certain expert components during inference. However, discrepancies between inference and training procedures, coupled with policy alterations, may result in inconsistent utilization of experts despite identical inputs. Such inconsistency can abruptly alter active parameters, thereby complicating optimization and exacerbating off-policy challenges. To address this issue, expert routing paths employed during inference are preserved and ensured to be consistently used during training, facilitating the optimization of identical expert parameters. The Keep Routing approach is essential for ensuring the stability of MoE models during reinforcement learning (RL) training, and it has been implemented since the inception of DeepSeek-V3-0324. The DeepSeek-R1 framework demonstrated the efficacy of deliberate reasoning in enhancing the model's capability to resolve complex problems. Based on these findings, there is an intention to integrate reasoning capabilities into tool-calling scenarios. It was identified that the methodology employed by DeepSeek-R1, involving the disposal of reasoning content upon the arrival of subsequent messages, leads to significant token wastage. This necessitates the model to reprocess the entire problem with each new tool invocation.
