In this study, the functionality of the interface is demonstrated using the "Picture Composition" example, which provides users with detailed information regarding the quantity of entities and events as well as the types of connections, thereby facilitating the creative process. Specifically, Figure 5.a illustrates the diagram for picture composition, while Figure 5.b depicts the manner in which P4 constructs a narrative using TaleFrame. Initially, P4 engages with the workspace by dragging the event "Jack shoves Ryan," which constitutes the primary segment of the diagram (Figure 6.f). Subsequently, the two individuals involved in this event are incorporated into the box by P4 (Figure 6.g). Thereafter, the individuals are linked by P4 (Figure 6.h1), and the connection line is clicked to modify the relationship details, including emotion type, action type, relationship strength, and the dynamics of the relationship (Figure 6.e). The directionality of the action within the relationship is represented by the "connect node," typically extending from the initiator to the recipient (e.g., "from Jack to Ryan"). However, for the event "confront each other" in the subsequent segment, where the action is reciprocal, the "is bidirectional" switch is activated, rendering the action direction as "bidirectional." By applying this methodology to all segments, P4 completes the remaining three sections, thereby connecting the four event boxes to finalize the narrative setup (Figure 6.h2). Following this, the "Modify Story" button is clicked by P4, facilitating the alteration of the title and description and the assignment of the four events to appropriate segments of the narrative. The story undergoes evaluation across seven dimensions based on established criteria (Figure 6.1). Furthermore, a comprehensive suggestion is formulated from the feedback received (Figure 6.m), prompting P4 to amend areas with lower scores, such as enhancing connectivity among certain events. Consequently, the "Regenerate" button is activated to produce a revised version. Ultimately, P4 proceeds to click the "Export" button, thereby obtaining the finalized narrative, diagram, and corresponding JSON file.
