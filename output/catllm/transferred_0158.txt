The performance of Llama-4 Maverick surpasses that of a conventional method frequently employed in biological studies for the integration of information, specifically the Bayesian reliability-weighted combiner, which is also utilized by humans. This superior performance suggests the potential incorporation of additional mechanisms beyond those encompassed within the standard approach. As illustrated in Figure 7, a comparative analysis was conducted to evaluate Llama-4 Maverick's responses when employing multimodal sensory inputs versus unimodal inputs, revealing that a non-linear methodology demonstrates enhanced efficacy over a linear approach in this context. Furthermore, under conditions of noise, the Bayesian method consistently exhibits optimal performance, thereby serving as a reliable benchmark for the advancement of less proficient language models. Figure 8 highlights observations indicating that models exhibiting higher accuracy tend to align more closely with the Bayesian method. Although models such as Gemma 3 4B and Phi 4 Multimodal demonstrated relatively lower accuracy, their performance within this domain remained satisfactory. A detailed examination of task and model performance is presented in A.9, underscoring that language models exhibit diverse behavioral patterns when assessed with tasks derived from human perceptual processes. Notably, more accurate models, including GPT-5 Mini, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, and Llama-4 Maverick, provide robust evidence of employing Bayesian principles, particularly in image-based tasks (refer to Appendix A.11 for further details). These models demonstrate adaptive behavior consistent with the Bayesian framework when confronted with variations such as noise or contextual augmentation, effectively utilizing prior information even in the absence of specific training protocols. This phenomenon mirrors human cognitive processes, wherein Bayesian models account for a substantial portion of perceptual mechanisms (Petzschner et al., 2015; Knill & Pouget, 2004) without necessitating explicit training. It is important to note that the present analysis focuses exclusively on observable behavior, with further investigations required to elucidate the underlying mechanisms governing these behaviors.
