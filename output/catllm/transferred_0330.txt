Details concerning the participants are presented in Table B.3 in the Appendix. No significant differences were observed in the characteristics of participants across the groups. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups. The first group was informed that the music they would hear was generated by artificial intelligence. The second group received instructions stating that the music was sourced from movie soundtracks. The third group was briefed that they would listen to music created by both humans and artificial intelligence, necessitating identification of the origin of each musical piece. Groups one and three received explanations regarding artificial intelligence and AI music tools prior to rating the music. Following the provision of instructions, all participants were required to rate 18 music clips, which were randomly selected to incorporate a variety of subgenres and AI tools. Before commencing, participants were instructed to adjust the volume to a preferred level and to review the list of emotion terms. The sequence of terms varied per individual but remained consistent across all songs rated. Participants were advised to evaluate the emotional impact elicited by the music rather than the emotions the music intended to convey. Each music clip was played twice; initially, participants listened attentively without performing any tasks. Subsequently, the rating questions were displayed, and the clip was played again to enhance memory retention. Upon completing the music ratings, participants filled out a questionnaire addressing their personality, music listening habits, and background. Additionally, inquiries were made regarding their perspectives on artificial intelligence. At the conclusion of the study, participants viewed a screen elucidating the study's purpose and the utilization of their data. They were afforded the opportunity to express their thoughts on AI music and their sentiments regarding the study through an open-ended question. The survey concluded with personalized feedback for each participant.
