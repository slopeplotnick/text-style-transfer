In order to enhance comprehension and ensure safety, feedback is incorporated into each example within our data set utilizing advanced models. Specifically, the text is processed using gpt-oss-120b, images and videos are managed through Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct, and sound is addressed with Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct. Each example receives the original content alongside its safety label and any identified problem areas, followed by an explanation of the model's safety decision rationale. Unlike conventional models that merely indicate safety status, OMNIGUARD is designed to provide detailed and transparent safety assessments. It delivers structured feedback comprising several components: (1) Safety judgment, which determines the safety status of the input; (2) Violation categories, which enumerate specific safety concerns if the content is deemed unsafe; and (3) Reasoning critique, which elucidates the model's decision-making process based on established guidelines. This approach enables OMNIGUARD to transcend pattern recognition, facilitating a thorough analysis of potentially unsafe content across various formats. Detailed test results concerning OMNIGUARD's performance are presented herein. The model's efficacy is evaluated in both isolated and combined scenarios across 15 tests encompassing text, images, videos, and audio. Additionally, tests are designed to address two primary inquiries: (1) Whether the incorporation of reasoning enhances the model's capability in safety assessments and in addressing complex safety challenges across multiple formats; and (2) Whether safety knowledge derived from familiar formats can be effectively applied to novel ones, thereby demonstrating the model's ability to comprehend and manage safety considerations in diverse contexts.
