Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) have facilitated the integration of task-focused and open-ended chat systems, marking a significant shift in technological capabilities. This section of the tutorial examines this technological evolution and elucidates potential future directions. Specifically, innovative approaches include the development of novel LLMs trained on chat data, end-to-end dialogue training, and self-dialogue practice. An important future direction involves the creation of chat systems that utilize content from diverse sources, such as web pages, lists, forms, tables, papers with figures and equations, and online documents. These systems will also incorporate vision, knowledge, emotion, and expressions like facial movements and gestures.
