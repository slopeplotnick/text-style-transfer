A method integrating label-driven Chain of Thought (CoT) generation with the identification of difficult cases was employed to enhance the model's reasoning capabilities through step-by-step analysis of challenging examples. The performance was evaluated, specifically focusing on difficult scenarios to assess the model's proficiency in handling intricate reasoning tasks. A significant advancement was achieved by enabling the CoT-based knowledge system for online use and transferring it to smaller models, thereby improving deep reasoning in practical applications. Two primary challenges were encountered: understanding product details from images with minimal text and ensuring decisions adhere to stringent rules and business standards. To address these, a strategy was implemented allowing the model to comprehend both images and text while conforming to business rules. A combination of CoT Generation, Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), and Reinforcement Learning, based on Qwen-2.5 Language Model for text and Qwen-2.5 Vision Language for images, was utilized. This configuration permitted the model to process both visual and textual reasoning, thereby overcoming the limitations of relying solely on a single data type. Multimodal and rule-aware CoT generation was employed, merging the understanding of images and text with prompts adhering to standards. Business rules and example cases were incorporated into CoT reasoning to facilitate the model's comprehension of regulations. Subsequently, reinforcement learning was applied to enhance the model's ability to utilize rules effectively for decision-making in complex scenarios.
