Promptolution is introduced as a simple, open-source Python framework designed to facilitate the automatic refinement of prompts, demonstrating compatibility with diverse language models, natural language processing (NLP) tasks, and prompt improvement methodologies. The framework has undergone testing and exhibited reliable performance, further allowing users to interchange components or incorporate customized additions. This tool is particularly advantageous for individuals focusing on enhancing individual prompts, while simultaneously offering researchers a platform to execute organized and reproducible experimental workflows. Specifically, the study delineates the positioning of the tool within the broader context of prompt optimization technologies, elaborates on its design principles, evaluates its comparative performance against other tools and unoptimized prompts, and proposes future directions for its refinement. Prompt optimization encompasses automated techniques aimed at testing and refining prompts, including their instructional content and embedded examples, and is categorized into continuous and discrete methodologies. Continuous approaches involve numerical representations, whereas discrete methods directly manipulate textual content; the latter is emphasized within this framework due to its accessibility and universal applicability across language models. The iterative process employed by the tool frequently integrates secondary language models to generate improved prompt versions systematically. Three distinct methods are incorporated within Promptolution: OPRO, which leverages language models to propose enhanced instructions based on task descriptions, examples, and preceding outcomes; EvoPrompt, which employs genetic algorithms and differential evolution to refine instructions, with language models facilitating the modifications; and CAPO, an advanced approach utilizing AutoML to achieve cost-efficient improvements across both instructions and examples. CAPO is identified as superior in terms of cost-effectiveness and overall performance relative to the other methods.
