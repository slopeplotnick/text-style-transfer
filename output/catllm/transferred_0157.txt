The evaluation of image safety was conducted through direct observation, and it was determined that all images met the safety criteria, indicating that current safety systems are predominantly focused on singular aspects. Regarding audio, the models OMNIGUARD-7B and OMNIGUARD-3B performed exceptionally well in audio safety assessments. Limited research has been undertaken on audio safety, thus our model emerges as an effective solution, demonstrating that training for safety is equally applicable to audio. Furthermore, OMNIGUARD models were tested in scenarios requiring the integration of various media types, and they exhibited proficiency in all safety evaluations across diverse media platforms such as MM-SafetyBench, Video-SafetyBench, and AIAH. In comparison to the alternate model, Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, OMNIGUARD-7B demonstrated superior accuracy in all assessments, indicating its capability to proficiently manage multi-media integration. Notably, the smaller OMNIGUARD-3B model achieved comparable results to larger models like Qwen3-VL-235B in certain evaluations, despite its reduced complexity, thereby affirming OMNIGUARD's aptitude in safety reasoning across multiple media types and underscoring its suitability for omni-modal media integration. An investigation was conducted into whether training for safety reasoning enhances omni-modal guardrails and how it addresses the added complexity of multi-media interaction. Additional studies on the 7B model encompassing four media types were performed, comparing our OMNIGUARD-7B with the original Qwen2.5-Omni-7B model and another variant fine-tuned solely for safety labels. Several observations were made: firstly, both fine-tuning methodologies enhanced performance relative to the original model across all singular media tests—text, image, video, and audio—thus illustrating that straightforward safety fine-tuning can also improve multi-media handling. Secondly, in comparison to the variant fine-tuned exclusively for safety labels, our model exhibited superior performance.
