A step-by-step process has been incorporated into the PPTPilot to enhance its predictive capabilities over time. Changes to XML files are suggested by the agent, which subsequently generates a temporary PPT file. This file is subjected to verification by a dedicated verifier who evaluates its adherence to the original instructions and provides feedback in cases of discrepancies. Based on the feedback received, the agent updates the PPT and addresses identified errors. Although the design remains relatively straightforward, it demonstrates notable efficiency and effectiveness in facilitating PPT editing compared to existing tools. PPTPilot is intended to serve as a foundational framework for further research into methodologies for PPT editing. The evaluation process utilized advanced vision-language models, including Gemini 2.5 Pro and GPT-5, with the primary results presented in the main comparison (Table 3) derived predominantly from GPT-5, while additional information concerning Gemini 2.5 Pro and other implementation details is provided in the supplementary materials. For testing purposes, PPTPilot was systematically compared with alternative PPT editing tools, such as ChatGPT in extended thinking mode, Gemini-CLI, GPT-Agent, which simulates computer functionality and desktop usage, MiniMax Agent, recognized for its multimodal task capabilities and promoted as an effective PPT tool, as well as PPTAgent and Poster2Agent. Certain tools, including ChatGPT and MiniMax Agent, were constrained by strict usage limits and elevated costs, necessitating the evaluation of a reduced sample set comprising 25 cases to remain within budget constraints. The study emphasized challenging tasks where PPTPilot and ChatGPT exhibited limited performance and included additional cases to ensure coverage of diverse scenarios. Further elaboration regarding these cases is available in the supplementary materials.
