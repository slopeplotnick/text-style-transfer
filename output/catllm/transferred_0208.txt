The relevance of search functionality is a critical aspect of online shopping platforms such as JD and Taobao, as it involves assessing the degree to which products align with user queries and assigning relevance scores to filter out non-matching items. This process significantly enhances the efficiency and user experience of search mechanisms. Large language models (LLMs) demonstrate substantial efficacy in addressing such tasks and are frequently employed in this domain. However, state-of-the-art models, including GPT-5, exhibit limitations, particularly in their lack of domain-specific knowledge and the high operational costs associated with their deployment. Consequently, efforts have been directed towards training smaller language models using shopping-specific datasets to optimize their performance for these specialized applications. Recent advancements in this area have transitioned from foundational training methodologies to more sophisticated approaches that aim to enhance the reasoning capabilities of these models. Despite these developments, challenges persist in managing complex cases that require nuanced decision-making. For instance, the ELLM model, which evaluates product details in relation to user search queries, encounters difficulties in scenarios necessitating the application of specific rules. A pertinent example includes distinguishing between similar color descriptions, such as "lake blue top" versus "sky blue top," where the model lacks predefined rules to make accurate judgments. Alternative models, such as LREF and TaoSR1, attempt to address these limitations by incorporating rule-based mechanisms; however, their reliance solely on textual data results in the omission of visual information. This shortcoming is evident in cases where a product image displays characteristics not explicitly mentioned in the text, such as a "blue top" visually identifiable in an image but not described as blue in the product title. These issues underscore the need to address a fundamental question: what specific capabilities are required for a model to effectively determine relevance in search contexts? This inquiry pertains to the identification and development of essential skills that enable models to evaluate relevance with precision and reliability.
