The interviews were conducted with a focus on creating conversational dynamics rather than adhering to a strict question-and-answer format. Initially, participants were reminded of the principles of Software Product Line Engineering (SPLE) and informed about the questionnaire's purpose. Subsequently, inquiries concerning the interviewee's personal and professional background were posed to gain insight into their role within the organization. This was followed by general inquiries regarding the company's developmental processes, transitioning then to more specific questions about current practices in product development. The aim was to ascertain their perspectives on effective strategies, notable practices, and overall performance. Discussing the current methodology facilitated the introduction of a novel approach that could potentially address existing challenges. At the conclusion of each session, discussions were held about the implications of adopting the new approach, inviting participants to express concerns, perceived risks, and potential benefits. The concluding questions focused on refining the interview process and summarizing the discussion. All interviews were conducted by the project's first author, lasting between thirty minutes to an hour. Feedback indicated that participants appreciated the conversational aspect and felt engaged in the migration project. An additional question, "Was there a question you thought I’d ask but didn’t?" encouraged comprehensive reflection and allowed for the inclusion of overlooked points, contributing to improved subsequent interviews. Consistency across interviews was maintained to ensure reliable data analysis, facilitating participant reflection without altering the questions while enhancing the interviews overall. During the interviews, notes were meticulously documented, and efforts were made to avoid biased or leading questions, ensuring a neutral and comprehensive inquiry. A preliminary interview was conducted with a primary collaborator from the organization to refine the questionnaire and streamline the process; however, data from this initial session were excluded from the analysis.
