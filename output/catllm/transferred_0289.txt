The dataset utilized in this study was generated by GPT-4o, and further details regarding the prompts, datasets, and model settings are available in the supplementary materials. Prompt engineering constitutes a methodological approach aimed at ensuring that language models adhere to specified instructions effectively. Specifically, this research employed prompt chaining, a strategy designed to decompose complex tasks into manageable subcomponents to enhance model performance. For the purpose of story parsing, the task was systematically divided into four distinct phases: identification of entities, identification of events, development of a story outline, and determination of relationships, wherein the outputs of each phase were sequentially integrated into subsequent steps. To ensure adherence to the predefined instructions, prompt frameworks were utilized, with the TIDD-EC prompt framework serving as the primary methodological tool. This framework comprises five essential elements: task definition, detailed instructions, explicit guidelines on permissible and impermissible actions, illustrative examples, and user-provided content, thereby facilitating the generation of accurate and contextually relevant responses. A table is provided within this study to exemplify the specific prompts employed for the "Entity Extraction" task utilizing the TIDD-EC framework. Furthermore, four distinct prompting methodologies were implemented and comparatively analyzed in the context of transforming stories into JSON format. These methodologies include zero-shot prompting, which involved natural language task explanation devoid of examples; the TIDD-EC framework, which established task parameters using the structured framework; TIDD-EC combined with chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning, wherein reasoning processes were explicitly integrated; and TIDD-EC supplemented with prompt chaining, whereby the task was systematically divided into four interconnected components, each utilizing the TIDD-EC framework and linked through prompt chaining.
