The primary objective of this study is to maximize the reuse of code and enhance the efficiency of application delivery through the migration to a higher-level Software Product Line (SPL), specifically levels 4 or 5. To facilitate this transition, the initial phase involved engaging with stakeholders within the organization to gather insights regarding SPL engineering practices, thereby addressing key research questions outlined further in the study. Subsequent consultations are scheduled upon project completion to refine the migration strategy. The migration process will commence with an analysis of feature locations to construct comprehensive feature models, followed by an examination of feature interactions and dependencies to develop a holistic understanding of the system. Interviews were conducted to ascertain perceptions of current practices, potential benefits, associated risks, and concerns prior to the SPL migration. Research Question 1 (RQ1) focuses on organizational perspectives regarding existing development practices, aiming to identify successful methodologies currently in use. Research Question 2 (RQ2) investigates perceived advantages of adopting the new SPL framework, specifically assessing its potential to enhance application development processes. Research Question 3 (RQ3) examines risks associated with the transition, emphasizing how these risks might influence routine operations. Furthermore, Research Question 4 (RQ4) explores fears related to these risks, seeking to understand apprehensions among organizational members regarding challenges and potential adverse outcomes tied to the migration. These concerns encompass the potential for unforeseen complications, the identification of critical challenges, and the necessity of devising robust strategies to mitigate risks effectively.
