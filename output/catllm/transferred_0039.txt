In this study, a collection of datasets encompassing environment, tools, tasks, and verifiers was obtained. Subsequently, the DeepSeek-V3.2 algorithm was employed to conduct reinforcement learning on the aforementioned data, retaining only those datasets that successfully passed the evaluation criteria. Consequently, the analysis resulted in 1,827 environments and their corresponding tasks, culminating in a total of 4,417. An illustrative example can be observed in the context of travel planning, wherein the difficulty of devising an itinerary that adheres to all prescribed constraints is noted, yet the verification of a plan's compliance is relatively straightforward. Specifically, a trip itinerary spanning three days, commencing from October 1st to October 3rd, 2025, in Hangzhou was formulated, necessitating assistance in its development. The itinerary stipulates the avoidance of repetitive visits to identical cities, accommodations, or dining establishments throughout the duration of the trip. Furthermore, it is imperative that the recommended hotels, restaurants, and attractions align with the city scheduled for visitation on each respective day. On the second day, budgetary considerations are to be observed. In instances where a luxury hotel, priced at 800 CNY or more per night, is booked, dining expenditure must be constrained to within 350 CNY for both lunch and dinner, with both venues possessing a minimum rating of 4.0 stars, and the afternoon attraction ticket should not exceed 120 CNY. Conversely, should the hotel cost range between 500 and 800 CNY, increased flexibility is permitted; however, it remains essential for at least one restaurant to achieve a rating of 4.0 or higher, and the attraction ticket should be priced below 180 CNY. For accommodations priced within the 200-500 CNY bracket, only a single restaurant is required to have a rating of 3.2 or above. Assistance in planning this trip is sought.
