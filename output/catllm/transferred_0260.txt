The current system operates utilizing Spring Boot, while the database is administered by PostgreSQL. Agronomists employ Python or MATLAB for the development of simulators. The IT team utilizes a principal platform known as Cross, a comprehensive framework, for the creation of application code. Initially, the platform was designed to develop Proof of Concepts; however, this purpose evolved, and it is now employed as a foundation for finalized products. Specifically, there are seven distinct versions tailored for three Product Owners. New iterations are developed to meet specific client requirements, with a new version typically released annually. The Architecture Dimension levels, as delineated in the FEF framework, address the construction of software and decisively influence the production process within the software product line. At Level 1, architectures cater to individual systems without an emphasis on reuse. Conversely, Level 2 represents a transition towards reusing third-party infrastructure, incorporating common software components such as middleware, albeit without formal utilization of domain-specific assets. Furthermore, at Level 3, domain-specific common features are integrated into a software platform, establishing a reference architecture for all applications, primarily centered around platform utilization. Nonetheless, this platform does not facilitate the management of varying configurations. At Level 4, both common features and domain-specific variations are encapsulated, and a reference architecture is instituted for the entire software product line. Domain assets are developed to assist in product creation, prioritizing the management of variations within the software product line architecture. Finally, at Level 5, the reference architecture assumes a central focus, with application architectures exhibiting minimal deviations from it. Consequently, automated product creation becomes feasible through the use of scripts, tools, and high-level languages, while application development largely involves configuration within the constraints of the reference architecture, thereby enabling automated product setup.
