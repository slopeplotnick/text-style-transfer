TokenPowerBench is a tool designed to evaluate the power and energy consumption associated with the execution of large language models (LLMs) across various hardware configurations, model architectures, and task types. The tool prioritizes simplicity, flexibility, and repeatability in its measurement processes. Specifically, it employs energy-based metrics such as joules consumed per token or per response and real-time power usage, which are intricately linked to distinct stages of LLM operation, including the prefill and decode phases, thereby allowing precise identification of energy expenditure sources. Unlike traditional metrics focusing on speed or throughput, these energy-centric metrics emphasize sustainability and cost efficiency, which are of paramount importance to contemporary AI systems. The methodology underpinning TokenPowerBench's power measurement is notably granular, contingent upon the specific hardware and instrumentation employed. It encompasses three tiers of measurement, ranging from basic GPU data analysis to comprehensive system monitoring facilitated by advanced instrumentation, thus accommodating a spectrum of experimental setups from modest configurations to extensive testing environments. The tool incorporates a framework to ensure experimental repeatability, addressing diverse LLM task types which vary according to system scale, model parameters, and usage scenarios. Factors such as batch size, context length, and system configuration are scrutinized to ascertain their impact on energy consumption. TokenPowerBench is versatile, applicable to configurations from single GPUs to expansive multi-node systems, providing insights into both micro-level details and macro-level energy consumption patterns. As LLM technology evolves rapidly, TokenPowerBench is designed to adapt, with plans to incorporate support for emerging GPUs, chips, and other hardware advancements. Additionally, it will introduce features to assess the implications of energy-saving measures on model output quality, thereby aiding users in optimizing the balance between energy efficiency and model performance.
