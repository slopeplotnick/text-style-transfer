Research on truthful artificial intelligence (AI) is concerned with the development of AI systems capable of providing accurate information about the real world. Previous studies have proposed methods to enhance AI's truthfulness, such as identifying patterns in the AI's behavior that align with factual information or modifying decision-making processes. Furthermore, there has been discourse regarding the equilibrium between an AI's truthfulness and its utility. Honest AI focuses on ensuring that AI systems do not intentionally convey information that contradicts their "beliefs" merely to obtain rewards, thereby addressing the concept of AI deception. Conversely, truth-seeking AI endeavors to examine evidence and discover novel insights. This aspect gains significance as AI systems increasingly operate autonomously, making decisions about the information they pursue based on their situational understanding. Although truth-seeking AI aims at discovering the truth, there exists no singular metric to evaluate its efficacy, as it could strive to be Bayesian or maintain consistency in its reasoning. Belief entrenchment refers to the tendency of individuals to adhere to their current beliefs despite contradictory evidence, closely resembling confirmation bias, wherein individuals seek, recall, or interpret information in ways that reinforce their existing beliefs. Confirmation bias is concerned with information processing in specific contexts, presenting challenges in measurement. In the realm of psychology, the assessment of confirmation bias is contingent upon the task and lacks reliability. Therefore, accurately measuring this bias necessitates an understanding of individuals' pre-existing beliefs and a methodology for assessment, posing significant challenges.
