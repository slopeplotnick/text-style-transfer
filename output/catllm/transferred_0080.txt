The current setup facilitates the reuse of code and expedites the experimentation with new Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods, allowing researchers to independently develop and test novel approaches, which are automatically incorporated into the system during execution. This dynamic loading mechanism proves particularly advantageous for the comparative analysis of diverse PEFT methods, as it enables researchers to assess all methods under identical conditions, thereby mitigating the need for code repetition and circumventing version discrepancies. Specifically, the SuperGLUE benchmark encompasses seven datasets distributed across four tasks: natural language inference (NLI), which includes CB; question answering, comprising MultiRC, ReCoRD, BoolQ, and COPA; word sense disambiguation, incorporating WiC; and coreference resolution, encompassing WSC. Furthermore, datasets for text generation frequently utilized to evaluate generative language models are included, comprising six datasets for reasoning and understanding language, divided into three tasks: question answering, featuring MMLU, PIQA, SIQA, and OBQA; natural language inference (NLI), integrating Hellaswag; and commonsense reasoning, incorporating WinoGrande. In addition, there are three datasets allocated for solving mathematical problems, divided into three tasks: question answering, including MathQA; math word problems, encompassing GSM8K; and simple math problems, comprising SVAMP. Furthermore, three datasets are designated for code generation, namely Conala, CodeAlpacaPy, and APPS. It is noteworthy that certain datasets may require minor format adjustments to be compatible with the input configuration of PEFT-FACTORY, as elucidated in Appendix B.2.
