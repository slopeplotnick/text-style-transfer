The performance of models is not consistent across all topics, as evidenced by the data presented in Table 2, which indicates that models tend to underperform in areas such as Religion and Sport, achieving scores that range from 7.8 to 8.2. Conversely, they exhibit superior performance in domains such as Game and Lifestyle, often surpassing scores of 8.4, thereby suggesting that models exhibit increased caution when dealing with sensitive subjects such as religion and sports. In contrast, topics related to gaming and lifestyle are more straightforward for models, likely due to their neutral nature and extensive coverage in training datasets, thus enabling models to manage them with greater ease. Furthermore, the connection between steering and judgment performances is highlighted in Fig. 3, where it is demonstrated that models excel in steering when they perform well in judgment tasks, indicating that both tasks require similar cognitive processes and alignment capabilities. This observation implies that enhancing foundational skills, such as contextual comprehension and balanced decision-making, can contribute to improved performance across diverse evaluation tasks. Future research endeavors might explore strategies for integrating these skills more effectively to enhance models' abilities in interpreting and managing language. Additionally, the outcomes detailed in Table 3 reveal that mediation influences various language aspects distinctively, as it mitigates toxicity and reduces the usage of exclamations, thereby fostering responses that are more neutral and composed, which can result in conversations that appear more balanced and less emotionally charged. However, mediation does not substantially alter argumentativeness, indicating that the approach does not significantly affect models' handling of disagreements. Notably, models exhibit different behaviors when simulating both sides of a conversation compared to interacting with real users, underscoring the importance of assessing mediation in terms of both procedural mechanisms and its tangible effects in real-world scenarios.
