Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) have predominantly focused on the utilization of enhanced computational resources during the testing phase. For instance, models such as OpenAI's o1 and DeepSeek-R1 are designed to generate extended cognitive steps, whereby ideas are meticulously processed, hypotheses formulated, and errors rectified prior to delivering a definitive response. This methodology, characterized by increased reasoning during the testing phase, has gained prominence due to its propensity to enhance model accuracy and problem-solving capabilities. Nonetheless, elongating the reasoning process does not invariably yield positive outcomes and may, in certain instances, exacerbate issues such as overthinking. Empirical research suggests that accurate responses often emanate from concise reasoning steps. Additionally, an alternative approach involves executing multiple concurrent reasoning processes and synthesizing their outputs, typically by selecting the prevalent answer when the task possesses a definitive solution. This strategy proves efficacious for tasks such as mathematical computations or multiple-choice queries. However, numerous real-world tasks are characterized by the absence of a singular correct answer. For instance, coding tools are required to produce functional programs, while research tools necessitate the integration of supplementary resources, the formulation of comprehensive plans, or the provision of extensive explanations. In such scenarios, the selection of the most frequent answer is untenable, given the lack of a clear solution, despite the method's effectiveness in more simplistic tasks. This observation raises a pertinent inquiry: Is it feasible to leverage the advantages of simultaneous multiple reasoning processes for tasks devoid of a singular correct answer, without resorting to the predominant result selection? In response to this inquiry, we propose THINKMERGE, a novel method that amalgamates the outputs of multiple reasoning processes during testing to generate a cohesive and effective answer.
