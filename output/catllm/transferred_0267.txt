The efficacy of the Radiologist Copilot was assessed through two primary dimensions: the execution of tasks and the performance of the tools utilized. Specifically, tasks such as the generation of radiology reports were evaluated using a range of measures to determine both textual quality and clinical accuracy. Textual quality was assessed using BLEU-1, ROUGE-L, METEOR, and BERTScore metrics, whereas clinical accuracy was measured through F1-RadGraph and GREEN metrics. Furthermore, the functionality of the tools was evaluated using the LLM-as-a-Judge approach, which employs OpenAI GPT-5.1 to systematically review the agent's operational process. This review encompasses four critical domains: task analysis, tool selection, planning, and execution, each rated on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 indicating suboptimal performance and 5 indicating excellence. The task analysis domain evaluates the agent's comprehension of the task, the tool selection assesses the appropriateness of the tools chosen, the planning domain examines the coherence of the strategy, and the execution domain evaluates the successful implementation of the plan. In order to comprehensively evaluate the Radiologist Copilot, both tool performance and task execution in radiology reporting were scrutinized, along with additional tests conducted to assess the efficacy of its core components absent additional features. The tool evaluation revealed predominantly excellent scores, with most evaluations yielding a score of 5, and some instances achieving a score of 4, thereby indicating thoroughness, appropriate tool selection, effective planning, and successful execution. Additionally, the assessment of the Copilot's response to user inquiries demonstrated that its planning and execution phases operate smoothly and efficiently, resulting in high-quality radiology reports. The entire process mirrors the practices of professional radiologists and contributes to time efficiency in clinical workflows.
