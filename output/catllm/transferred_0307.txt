The development of the Story2MIDI model, designed to generate music that aligns with the emotional nuances of textual content, was achieved through the integration of datasets analyzing emotions in both text and music. Specifically, the dataset comprises short textual excerpts paired with music that evokes corresponding emotional responses. Despite limitations in dataset size and computational resources, the model successfully acquired the capability to discern emotional characteristics in music and utilized them to produce new musical compositions with varied emotional expressions. The evaluation of the model's performance involved both quantitative music-related metrics and qualitative assessments through listener feedback, thereby demonstrating its efficacy in accurately matching intended emotional tones. Presently, the demand for entertainment forms such as music, audiobooks, and podcasts is substantial, driven by the rapid expansion of social media and streaming platforms. A significant proportion of this entertainment is narrative-centric, encompassing films, games, podcasts, and documentaries. Narratives are renowned for eliciting emotional engagement, as audiences become invested in characters, plots, and thematic elements. This emotional resonance contributes significantly to the enduring impact of stories. Similarly, music possesses the ability to profoundly influence emotional states, serving as a potent medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creators frequently incorporate background music into narratives to enhance emotional connection and enrich audience engagement. In light of the swift advancements in artificial intelligence, there is a continual emergence of novel models capable of generating diverse content types.
