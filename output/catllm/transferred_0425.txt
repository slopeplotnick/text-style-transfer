The present study examines the limitations of TaleFrame concerning user interaction and its application in advanced storytelling, as well as potential directions for future research. Currently, TaleFrame operates on desktop and laptop platforms utilizing a mouse and keyboard to facilitate the connection of elements and the comprehension of event linkages. User feedback indicates a preference for the drag-and-drop functionality; however, its efficacy diminishes on mobile devices such as phones and tablets due to their reduced screen size. Although touch pens or fingers may substitute for a mouse, prolonged interactions and the manual input of relational information can introduce errors, thereby compromising the user experience on such devices. Consequently, to enhance usability, visual aids including colors, icons, and lines have been implemented, alongside adaptive layouts that adjust to varying screen dimensions. Future investigations will explore diversified interaction modalities, such as touchscreens, keyboards, and voice commands, thereby enabling broader device compatibility for TaleFrame. In the realm of story creation, TaleFrame encounters challenges with complex event relationships extending beyond simple chronological sequencing, including cause-and-effect dynamics, flashbacks, and other narrative techniques. The intricacy of these connections can obscure story clarity and accuracy. Efforts have been made to refine event linkages; however, the task remains arduous in the context of intricate narratives and multifaceted interactions. When events engage multiple entities, accurately sequencing events or depicting causal relationships can lead to convoluted storylines. As narratives increase in complexity, the model's ability to comprehend and construct these connections diminishes. Therefore, future endeavors will focus on enhancing relationship modeling and developing improved strategies for non-linear storytelling, thereby augmenting the model's capability to generate intricate narratives and offering users a more engaging and coherent storytelling experience.
