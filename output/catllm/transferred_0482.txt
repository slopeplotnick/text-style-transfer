Ensuring uniform application of theme colors across all slides, rectifying layouts and formats for related slides, and translating slide content while preserving charts and structural consistency are fundamental objectives in PowerPoint editing workflows. To the best of our knowledge, PPTArena represents the inaugural tool that conceptualizes PowerPoint editing as a sequential process informed by the semantic structure of the deck. It provides detailed information regarding each element, style objectives, and error-check mechanisms to ensure the accuracy of edits, while employing instructions and visual validations to facilitate diverse editing approaches while maintaining task clarity. Complementing this innovation, the development of PPTPilot introduces a sophisticated editing tool specifically designed for intricate PowerPoint modifications. PPTPilot disaggregates individual instructions into granular steps, selectively utilizes APIs such as python-pptx or directly modifies XML files, and systematically evaluates outcomes against predefined task objectives. Its functionality is anchored on two principal methodologies: structural planning, wherein the tool analyzes slide templates, placeholders, shapes, textual elements, and visual data prior to executing edits, and precise implementation, characterized by XML manipulations and stringent rules governing fonts, colors, positions, and template adjustments, while automated systems address repetitive tasks such as translations and style normalization. A cyclical approach encompassing planning, editing, and validation, reinforced by XML and visual checks, enhances its efficacy in managing complex and extensive editing processes. Empirical evaluations of PPTArena included comparisons with advanced PowerPoint tools such as ChatGPT Agent and MiniMax Agent, visual-language models, and simplified iterations of PPTPilot. Despite access to detailed instructions and advanced functionalities, existing systems encountered substantial challenges in maintaining instruction accuracy and achieving aesthetically consistent edits for multi-step, complex tasks. These systems frequently struggled with edits requiring inter-slide connections or template-level style adjustments, areas that PPTPilot is specifically designed to address. Notably, PPTPilot demonstrated superior performance, exceeding advanced tools and models by over 10 percentage points in accuracy for complex editing tasks, while maintaining high efficacy in simpler scenarios. However, it is noteworthy that PPTPilot and other tools exhibited limitations in resolving highly complex tasks reliant on visual elements, underscoring the challenges inherent to PPTArena and the substantial scope for continued advancements in this domain.
