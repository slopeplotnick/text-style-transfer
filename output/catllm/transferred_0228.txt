Within the 10,000 noun phrases, the initial discernible content word, 'forgotten,' appears at rank 54/55, sharing its position with the function word 'at' or 'near.' Beyond rank 55, additional content words are observed. In the evaluation involving 10,000 sentences from Delilah, the most frequent content words are 'forgotten' at rank 11, 'works' at rank 27, and 'in the morning' at rank 66. Subsequent to rank 66, further content words emerge. It is noted that Delilah utilizes 'masses' as a numerical term, and this is accurately identified by the learning process. For the 10,000 examples, distinguishing function words from content words becomes feasible, given that words appearing more than 100 times typically constitute function words, a frequency exceeding 1.7 per thousand within Delilah's sentences. Therefore, from these assessments, MODOMA settings for discerning function and content words were established such that words occurring more than 2 per thousand are classified as function words, whereas others are deemed content words. This learning process is executed once the acquisition of at least 10,000 examples is achieved. The settings are meticulously configured to minimize the likelihood of erroneously categorizing a word as a function word rather than a content word, considering that content words are the default classification. Hence, each word is regarded as a content word unless substantial evidence suggests otherwise. The initial test settings are now default for MODOMA, albeit subject to modification contingent upon further assessments and linguistic theories.
