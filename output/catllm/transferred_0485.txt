In the context of online shopping, users typically request products characterized by multiple attributes simultaneously, such as brand, price range, functionality, and target audience. Consequently, it is essential for the system to analyze these attributes collectively rather than in isolation and to organize them efficiently. This comprehensive analysis assists the system in determining the most appropriate items to present to the user, thereby aligning the user's request with suitable products. It is imperative not to rely solely on the user's inquiry to determine the significance of various data types, including text or images. Instead, the system should examine the attributes identified within the user's question and correlate them with the attributes of the products, subsequently evaluating how this information should influence the selection of items to display. Fundamentally, the system operates by establishing a pathway from the user's query to the products it identifies. Initially, the query is deconstructed into specific attributes, which are then utilized to filter and select items. This process comprises two primary components: first, ensuring the system accurately comprehends the query and aligns it with corresponding product attributes, and second, ensuring the selection of appropriate items based on these attributes. The complexity and ambiguity inherent in these steps present challenges. For instance, when a user searches for "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set," the system must discern the user's intent and correlate it with relevant product features. The initial task involves linking the attributes within the query to the product attributes, with the challenging aspect being the precise alignment of these elements.
