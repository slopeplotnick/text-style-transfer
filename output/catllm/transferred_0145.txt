The methods under investigation were applied to the BrowseComp benchmark, and it was observed that context management during testing facilitates enhanced model performance by leveraging increased computational resources and executing additional steps when necessary. Specifically, the Summary method extends the average steps to 364, resulting in performance enhancement by up to 60.2%; however, its overall efficiency remains suboptimal. Conversely, the Discard-all method, despite its simplicity, demonstrates both efficiency and scalability, achieving a score of 67.6, which parallels the outcomes of parallel scaling while requiring fewer steps. Therefore, test-time computational augmentation can be achieved either through incremental context management or parallel execution, both of which contribute to improved problem-solving capabilities of the model. Nonetheless, the methods exhibit varying degrees of efficiency and scalability, necessitating consideration of computational costs when evaluating model performance. Furthermore, determining the optimal combination of step-by-step and parallel scaling to achieve both efficiency and scalability warrants further investigation. This study introduces DeepSeek-V3.2, a system that harmonizes computational efficiency with advanced reasoning capabilities. Through the application of DSA, complex computational challenges were addressed without compromising the ability to manage extensive contexts. By enhancing computational capacity, DeepSeek-V3.2 achieves performance comparable to GPT-5 in reasoning assessments. Additionally, the integration of our task synthesis system enhances tool utilization, thereby enabling the development of robust and adaptable AI systems utilizing open LLMs. A high-performance variant, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale, has garnered recognition through top awards in competitions such as the IMO and IOI, representing a significant advancement for open LLMs. Nevertheless, it is acknowledged that limitations persist when compared to advanced closed-source models like Gemini-3.0-Pro. For instance, due to fewer training FLOPs, DeepSeek-V3.2 possesses less comprehensive general knowledge relative to leading proprietary models.
