LLaMA-Factory primarily focuses on text generation rather than transforming text generation challenges into sorting tasks. Our framework, PEFT-FACTORY, addresses the limited variety of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods and facilitates the adaptation of large language models for sorting tasks. Other frameworks for training large language models, while not as user-friendly as LLaMA-Factory, offer distinct advantages. FastChat, for instance, is adept at training large models for conversational applications. LitGPT and LMFlow are flexible and user-friendly frameworks compatible with various generative models and training methodologies. Axolotl serves as a command-line tool designed for efficient post-training of large models, maintaining both functionality and scalability. Open-Instruct specializes in instruction fine-tuning for large models, presenting diverse models and methodologies for this purpose. H2O LLM Studio, with its business-centric approach, provides a graphical interface for large model development and deployment. GPT4All enhances usability by creating an interface around LLaMA for efficient training. ColossalAI emphasizes fine-tuning across multiple nodes for distributed computing. LLaMA-Adapter and LLaMA-Accessory are lighter frameworks, with LLaMA-Adapter integrating trainable adapters into LLaMA models and LLaMA-Accessory offering a comprehensive toolkit for large model development. LLaMA-Adapter is often utilized within frameworks such as LitGPT. Upon examining related frameworks, we identified three primary features that are either absent or limited and novel in our approach: training large models using methods beyond reparameterization-based PEFT methods, the modular and dynamic integration of new PEFT methods, and support for training and evaluating large models specifically for sorting tasks.
