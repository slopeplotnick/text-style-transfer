To ensure the efficacy of the simulated data for computational research, it is imperative that various demographic groups within the data are readily comparable. To verify this, the simulated data were divided into "Male" and "Female" categories and assessed for consistency. Successful validation would suggest the feasibility of comparing specific demographic cohorts within the dataset. This section delineates the generalized methodology employed across all four studies, except where specified otherwise in individual sections. Figure 1 illustrates the experimental setup employed for each of the four studies. The simulated data were generated using GPT4o-mini, an iteration of GPT-4 with updates as of October 2023. Default model parameters were utilized, including a Temperature setting of 1.0 and a Top P of 1.0, with no frequency or presence penalties applied. Prompts provided to the model included descriptions of individuals characterized by attributes such as age, gender, and ethnicity, followed by a request to complete a specific questionnaire. The prompts encompassed the questions, answer options, and instructions to compose responses as a single comma-separated string for ease of processing. Each prompt was issued in isolation, with no retention of prior prompts, to prevent contamination of responses. An exemplar prompt is as follows: "Assume the identity of a [Ethnicity] [Gender] aged [Age] residing in the UK. Respond to this [Number of items]-item questionnaire. Each question employs a Likert scale ranging from [Likert scale range]. Utilize this key for responses: [Response Key]. The questionnaire items are: [Questionnaire Items]. Provide your answers in a single comma-separated string."
