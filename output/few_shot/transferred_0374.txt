The findings demonstrate the efficacy of OMNIGUARD in understanding and addressing safety across diverse data modalities. Specifically, OMNIGUARD exhibits strong transferability, leveraging knowledge of safety from one data type to another while maintaining robust identification of unsafe content regardless of modality. The introduction of OMNIGUARD-7B and OMNIGUARD-3B marks a significant advancement, as these models represent the first systems designed to handle a wide spectrum of data types using an extensive safety-oriented training dataset. OMNIGUARD is presented as a comprehensive solution for addressing unsafe content across heterogeneous data contexts. Evaluation results underscore its superior performance, with OMNIGUARD-7B surpassing other state-of-the-art models across all data types and OMNIGUARD-3B achieving competitive performance relative to larger models, such as Qwen3-235B and Qwen3-VL-235B. These outcomes highlight OMNIGUARD's capability for effective unsafe content detection and reasoning, thereby demonstrating the feasibility of developing systems that generalize safety reasoning across data modalities.   Future research is anticipated to focus on more complex safety scenarios to further enhance safety reasoning in evolving language models. However, some limitations of OMNIGUARD must be acknowledged. While the integration of reasoning pathways improves the transparency and reliability of safety evaluations, it also imposes computational overhead, resulting in slower inference times and higher resource demands compared to simpler safety systems. Thus, optimizing the trade-off between the depth of safety reasoning and computational efficiency remains a critical area of improvement. Additionally, the limited availability of publicly accessible datasets for training cross-modal safety systems presents challenges in addressing more intricate mixed data safety scenarios. This underscores the need for continued research to improve the robustness and reliability of such systems, ultimately advancing their potential to mitigate risks across diverse data modalities.
