We developed a framework named PromptSolution, which serves as a Python-based open-source platform designed to facilitate the automatic enhancement of prompts. This tool caters to both individuals seeking to optimize prompts for specific tasks and researchers conducting extensive studies involving multiple benchmarks. PromptSolution is engineered for ease of use and versatility, allowing users to interchange components or experiment with various optimizers if a particular one proves ineffective. While not every optimizer in the library may be ideal for each specific task, there is invariably at least one that demonstrates strong performance in any given context. The framework enables straightforward comparison of different optimizers, as it is configured to conduct such evaluations with minimal additional effort. We have demonstrated the functionality of the framework, elucidated the integration of its components, and validated its efficacy and competitiveness through comparisons with alternative tools. Future developments aim to integrate compatibility with other frameworks such as DSPy, thereby permitting users to amalgamate features from multiple sources. Additionally, plans are in place to introduce functionalities that streamline the management of extensive experiments, such as interfacing with configuration tools like Hydra. To enhance user accessibility, a simplified interface is in development, enabling users to monitor experiments and view results in real-time. Given the rising popularity of multi-agent systems, we are also exploring methods to optimize prompts and communication configurations for systems comprising multiple agents. Drawing inspiration from AutoML, we are considering the incorporation of features that amalgamate multiple optimizers to achieve superior outcomes. Overall, the framework is designed with adaptability in mind, accommodating the introduction of new methodologies as the field continues to evolve.
