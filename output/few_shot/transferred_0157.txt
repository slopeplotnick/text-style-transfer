We evaluated the safety of images through manual inspections, determining that all images complied with established safety criteria, which highlights the narrow focus of current safety systems. For audio-based safety evaluations, our models, OMNIGUARD-7B and OMNIGUARD-3B, demonstrated superior performance, achieving notable results in audio safety tests. Given the limited prior work on audio safety, these findings suggest that our models offer a robust solution and substantiate the efficacy of safety-oriented training for audio modalities. Furthermore, we examined OMNIGUARD models in multimodal contexts, assessing their ability to handle safety reasoning across diverse media types. Experimental results on benchmark datasets—including MM-SafetyBench, Video-SafetyBench, and AIAH—indicate that OMNIGUARD-7B achieved superior accuracy in comparison to the competing model Qwen2.5-Omni-7B. Additionally, the smaller OMNIGUARD-3B model delivered competitive results, approaching the performance of larger-scale models such as Qwen3-VL-235B, despite its reduced parameter count. These findings underscore the capability of OMNIGUARD models to effectively manage safety reasoning across multiple modalities, presenting them as viable solutions for multimodal applications requiring robust safety considerations.  In further investigations, we assessed the impact of safety-focused training specifically on omni-modal guardrails and its ability to address the complexities introduced by multimodal integration. Focused comparisons were conducted using the OMNIGUARD-7B model across four media types—text, image, video, and audio—and against both the baseline model Qwen2.5-Omni-7B and a variant fine-tuned exclusively for safety labels. Key observations from these studies include: (1) Both models fine-tuned for safety demonstrated improved performance across single modality tests, confirming that even simple safety fine-tuning enhances multimodal handling capabilities; and (2) relative to the safety-label fine-tuning variant, OMNIGUARD-7B exhibited superior performance, further validating its design for robust safety reasoning in diverse multimodal contexts.
