Our research indicates that public perceptions of artificial intelligence (AI) significantly influence the reception of AI-generated music, beyond mere auditory characteristics. Therefore, there is an imperative to develop dedicated evaluative frameworks and tools specifically for AI-composed music. As AI-generated music increasingly populates streaming platforms, considerations of fairness and transparency must be prioritized. Given that perceptions of AI influence enjoyment and emotional response to music, further inquiry into dissemination and disclosure practices is warranted. For instance, empirical studies could examine transparency regarding the data sets used for AI training or the distribution of financial remuneration from AI music revenues. Moreover, research should explore varied AI music creation and application methodologies, including collaborative compositions between humans and AI, facilitated by advancing technologies. This is akin to the practical analyses undertaken by Moura and Maw (2021). Our findings suggest that attitudes towards AI are more influential in shaping judgments of AI music than the origin of the music, its genre, or individual listening habits. Future investigations should monitor evolving perceptions of AI, as these will profoundly impact acceptance and integration of AI in both music and broader cultural domains. The fundamental inquiry extends beyond the aesthetic appreciation or anthropomorphic evaluation of AI music; it interrogates our understanding of authentic and valuable creativity. Through attentive engagement with public sentiment, we can discern which forms of creativity warrant recognition and how they should be categorized.
