Recent advancements in the domain of large language models (LLMs) have notably integrated task-specific and open-ended conversational systems. This segment of the tutorial addresses this technological evolution and presents possible future directions. Notable developments include the creation of novel LLMs trained on dialogue data, the end-to-end training of conversational models, and the self-supervised learning of dialogues. One significant prospective direction is the development of conversational systems capable of synthesizing content from diverse sources such as web pages, lists, forms, tables, academic papers with figures and equations, and various digital documents. Additionally, these systems may incorporate multimodal inputs, including visual, knowledge-based, emotional, and expressive cues such as facial movements and gestures.  Huck Yang, a Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA Research with a Ph.D. from Georgia Tech, focuses on speech and language modeling, enhancing speech recognition, and working with multimodal models. He delivered a tutorial on "Parameter-Efficient Language Modeling for Speech Processing" at ASRU and Interspeech 2023 and conducted a series of tutorials at ICASSP on "Prompt Learning for Speech-Language Models" from 2022 to 2024. His contributions also include service as an area chair at Interspeech, SLT, and ICASSP, along with professional experience at Amazon AGI.  Andreas Stolcke, a distinguished AI Scientist and Vice President at Uniphore, holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Berkeley. His career includes positions at Amazon, SRI International, and Microsoft. His research interests encompass language modeling, speech recognition, speaker identification, and speech translation. As an IEEE Fellow and a Fellow of the International Speech Communication Association (ISCA), his longstanding research pursuits involve language modeling for speech processing, paralinguistics, and conversational AI.
