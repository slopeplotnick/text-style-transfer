We conducted a comparative analysis of our samples against the EMOPIA MIDI files. The proportion of major key usage is indicative of the perceived positivity in music, while metrics such as average note length and note velocity offer insight into its energetic quality. In terms of positivity, our samples largely correspond with EMOPIA across three quadrants, except in Quadrant 2 (Q2), where our model generates a significantly higher number of major-key tracks compared to EMOPIA. This discrepancy is likely attributable to the dataset's composition, which contains more major-key tracks than minor-key ones, coupled with the limited size of the fine-tuning set. Regarding energy, the fine-tuned model accurately replicates the note-length pattern: shorter notes in high-energy quadrants (Q1 and Q2) and longer notes in low-energy quadrants (Q3 and Q4), mirroring the patterns found in EMOPIA. The variations in note velocity across quadrants are less pronounced, likely due to the simplification of MIDI velocities by REMI into fewer categories, thus narrowing the range and obscuring quadrant-specific distinctions. The quantitative findings partially align with listener feedback, suggesting that the model effectively captures certain musical features related to energy and positivity. However, the model demonstrates greater proficiency in capturing energy, while the results for positivity appear almost random. This may be due to the model's propensity to produce an excessive number of major-key tracks. Incorporating additional metrics may facilitate the identification of deficiencies in positivity generation. In this study, we developed Story2MIDI, a transformer-based model designed to generate symbolic music from emotional text input. This involved constructing a text-music dataset and designing an encoder-decoder system to link textual emotions with musical output. The encoder was fine-tuned to produce distinct emotion representations across varying levels of energy and positivity, while the decoder was initially trained on a large corpus of symbolic music before being fine-tuned on a smaller, emotion-labeled set to generate emotion-based music. We evaluated Story2MIDI using listener feedback and musical measurements, concluding that while the model performs well, certain areas require further refinement.
