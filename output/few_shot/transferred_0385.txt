The Menta interface is capable of managing a variety of tasks, including stress detection, depression assessment (both binary classification and severity estimation), and suicide risk evaluation (binary and detailed levels). Upon activation of the "Start Evaluation" function, the system processes social media posts through the selected model, providing results that encompass predicted categories, their accuracy, and other evaluative details. An empirical evaluation was conducted on six distinct tasks using datasets with sizes ranging from 10 to 3,000 posts, contingent upon task complexity and data availability. The findings suggest a trade-off between model speed and accuracy relative to comparable systems. Menta is engineered for precision, speed, and responsiveness, rendering it suitable for practical applications that demand high performance and swift feedback. As illustrated in Figure 9, the chart delineates three metrics: processing speed (ITPS), time to first result (TTFT), and accuracy. Each circle represents a model, with its size indicating memory usage. The Falcon model prioritizes processing speed, offering rapid task execution and initiation but with compromised accuracy. Conversely, StableLM achieves higher accuracy at the expense of slower response times and greater resource consumption, thus increasing operational costs and latency for users. Menta, however, maintains high accuracy levels (with the exception of Task 5, where StableLM and Phi-4 Mini exhibit marginally superior performance) and concurrently achieves greater speed and efficiency compared to StableLM. This capability enables Menta to deliver prompt, accurate results while minimizing memory usage—an essential attribute for systems deployed in high-demand environments. In summary, while each model exhibits distinct advantages and limitations, Menta is well-positioned for deployment in real-world scenarios, offering commendable performance without excessive resource consumption, thereby optimizing cost-efficiency and enhancing user experience.
