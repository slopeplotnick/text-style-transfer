Gemini-2.0-Flash is employed as the baseline model, operating independently of external information and receiving only the original question. We explore variations of CAIRNS, including a version that solely utilizes literature and another that incorporates both literature and climate data, employing a two-step API reasoning process. Gemini-2.0-Flash serves as the principal model for CAIRNS and all its iterations. To ensure fairness, all models are assessed using identical corpora, prompts, and evaluation settings. The ScholarGuide Prompt enhances readability by improving context, structure, and language, while also addressing citation issues to increase information reliability, albeit with a slight reduction in the overall citation rate. This prompt employs unified reference numbers and connects each fact to clear evidence, thereby enabling the model to deliver clearer and more specific responses. The efficacy of this approach is evident in both zero-shot and few-shot scenarios, underscoring the significance of structured prompts for the success of CAIRNS. Multi-turn ReAct facilitates the model's ability to refine responses by aligning literature with climate data, thereby producing more comprehensive and contextually enriched answers. Although its impact is not as pronounced as that of the ScholarGuide Prompt, it contributes to the structured reasoning process and enhances the model's capacity to address complex inquiries. Lastly, Location-Weighted Retrieval supports the model in providing region-specific responses, particularly for geographically focused questions, and its removal slightly diminishes specificity without significantly affecting other metrics.
