Other methodologies include CoT2, a system designed to produce token mixtures analogous to training-free methods, which is applied to GPT-2 for tasks involving logical reasoning. CODI employs a teacher-student framework to enhance the performance of GPT-2 and Llama 3.1 1B models on GSM8k tasks. Additionally, Token Assorted innovates by generating novel token embeddings via VQ-VAE and applying them to train the Llama 3.1 8B model for mathematical and logical reasoning tasks. Our LiteReason method distinguishes itself by not utilizing extensive datasets like GSM8k-Aug for reasoning traces, as such datasets are unavailable for our specific tasks. Instead, we leverage traces generated autonomously by the model. Furthermore, we limit training to a small Reasoning Projector for reasoning purposes, rather than extensively training the entire model, to prevent potential disruption to the model's token generation capabilities. We also permit the model to integrate latent and discrete reasoning during reinforcement learning training, hypothesizing that it will discover an optimal balance over time. Unlike other methods, which often benchmark against Chain-of-Thought prompting and occasionally employ reinforcement learning, we observe a lack of comparison to non-latent RL-trained models, which we argue provides a more accurate measure of performance for complex tasks. Consequently, we include this comparison in our evaluations as a benchmark for peak performance and computational limits. Regarding narrative tasks, despite substantial reasoning research in areas such as mathematics and coding, significant investigations have also explored the use of large language models for story comprehension and generation. Some studies concentrate on specific aspects of story creation, such as event planning or character development, representing diverse reasoning styles. However, these tasks present challenges for reinforcement learning applications due to their lack of clear reward signals. In this study, we focus on two tasks that assess reasoning based on narratives.
