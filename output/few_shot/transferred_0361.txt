In this experiment, we investigated the effects of substituting different models for Stage 1 and Stage 2 while maintaining a constant number of function evaluations. The employment of a smaller model for Stage 1 did not significantly influence the general Frechet Inception Distance (gFID), indicating that predicting the basic layout with some noise is largely unaffected by model size. However, utilizing a less robust model for Stage 2 resulted in a substantial decline in the quality of generated outputs, demonstrating the importance of increased capacity and training for detail refinement. Our previous discussions have addressed this two-stage approach, and we are now examining prevalent practices within this context. We aim to elucidate why these methods are effective and how we can enhance them by considering the distinct roles of navigation and refinement.  Subsequently, we analyze the impact of selecting various latent spaces on model behavior within a two-stage framework. Figure 9 illustrates the progression of oracle loss during training when employing VA-VAE and SD-VAE latent spaces. Throughout the navigation stage, both latent spaces exhibit a gradual increase in oracle loss, suggesting that the complexity of predicting the basic layout intensifies irrespective of the latent space. Conversely, the refinement stage reveals significant variations in loss patterns. Although the absolute values are not directly comparable, observable trends in the oracle loss curves are evident. For VA-VAE, which aligns its latent space with semantic structure via DINO-based VF loss, the oracle loss smoothly adopts a parabolic trajectory as training advances. This indicates that the latent space is well-structured, with semantic modes clearly delineated. In contrast, SD-VAE, which emphasizes low-level reconstruction, results in a flatter, more oscillatory curve, reflecting a less organized latent space and the model's challenges with consistent denoising. Overall, latent spaces that effectively align with high-level concepts prove advantageous for diffusion modeling, as they facilitate clear mode organization under Gaussian marginals, thereby simplifying both navigation and refinement processes.
