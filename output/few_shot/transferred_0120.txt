TokenPowerBench is a benchmarking tool designed to quantify energy consumption and power usage associated with executing large language models (LLMs) across various hardware configurations, model architectures, and task types. Its primary goal is to provide a simple, flexible, and reproducible framework for energy measurement. The tool employs energy-centric metrics, such as energy consumption per token or per response and instantaneous power usage, aligning these measurements with specific operational phases of LLMs, such as the prefill and decode stages. These stage-specific insights enable users to pinpoint energy expenditure within the model's workflow. Unlike conventional performance metrics, such as latency or throughput, TokenPowerBench emphasizes energy sustainability and cost-efficiency, which are increasingly critical considerations in the deployment of contemporary AI systems.  TokenPowerBench adopts a detailed power measurement methodology that varies depending on the hardware and monitoring tools employed. It offers three levels of measurement granularity, ranging from GPU-level monitoring to system-wide analyses via advanced profiling tools, thereby catering to both small-scale setups and extensive testing infrastructures. The tool is specifically designed to ensure reproducibility, enabling consistent experimental setups across use cases. It accommodates diverse LLM workloads, accounting for variables such as batch size, context length, and system configuration to analyze their impact on energy consumption. It supports a broad spectrum of execution environments, from single GPUs to large-scale, multi-node systems, offering both granular insights and overarching analyses of energy utilization.  Given the rapid evolution of LLM technologies, TokenPowerBench is designed for adaptability and future-proofing. It is structured to integrate emerging hardware, such as advanced GPUs and custom chips, while incorporating features that evaluate the trade-offs between energy efficiency and model performance. This enables users to make informed decisions regarding the optimization of energy usage relative to the quality of the generated outputs. As a result, TokenPowerBench serves as a critical tool for promoting sustainable and cost-effective practices in the development and deployment of AI systems.
