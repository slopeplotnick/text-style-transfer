The experimental session lasted approximately 20 to 35 minutes. Initially, participants selected an image of their preference for a narrative composition task, ensuring comprehension of their chosen visual stimulus. Subsequently, an instructional briefing on the functionality of the TaleFrame system was provided. Participants then engaged in the creation of a narrative utilizing their selected image, with their interactions and verbalizations systematically documented throughout the process. Upon completion, participants were solicited for evaluative feedback. Each participant received a remuneration of $5 for their involvement. For the purpose of user feedback, participants evaluated TaleFrame, rating it on a Likert scale ranging from 1 to 5 based on their experiential engagement. TaleFrame is designed to facilitate users in constructing a comprehensive story framework through its intuitive interface and customizable options for defining narrative components. It enables users to interactively organize story elements, elucidating their interconnections to enhance narrative coherence. Furthermore, users can modify the structural aspects of the narrative iteratively. TaleFrame aids in maintaining temporal consistency throughout the narrative and provides recommendations for enhancing the emotional and thematic dimensions. It also evaluates narrative quality and offers constructive suggestions for improvement.
