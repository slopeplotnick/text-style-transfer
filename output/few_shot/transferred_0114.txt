PEFT-FACTORY computes a range of classification and efficiency metrics during prediction phases, encompassing token accuracy and semantic similarity metrics such as Rouge and Bleu. For classification tasks, it employs standard Accuracy and F1 metrics. To assess efficiency in evaluating PEFT methods, the PSCP metric—known for its flexibility—is utilized, examining factors such as parameter count, memory usage, and prediction time. These metrics are configured within the train/sft/metric.py file designed for supervised fine-tuning, based on the framework established by LLaMA-Factory. Each metric is organized into separate data classes named ComputeAccuracy, ComputeF1, and ComputePSCP. For classification, a configurable option in the training settings labeled compute_classification.metrics allows for the inclusion or exclusion of classification metrics. A separate setting, compute_pscp, is available for efficiency metrics, with further details provided in Appendix B.3. PEFT-FACTORY is engineered for flexibility and user-friendliness, effectively supporting research in PEFT methodologies. It demonstrates enhancements in reproducibility and benchmarking with two illustrative examples. The modular architecture of PEFT-FACTORY facilitates the creation and testing of novel PEFT methods by researchers and developers. Currently, the development of new PEFT methods is often hindered by shared code that lacks reliability or is challenging to reproduce. Additionally, developers typically need to independently write training and evaluation code, leading to redundancy, potential errors, and inconsistent outcomes.
