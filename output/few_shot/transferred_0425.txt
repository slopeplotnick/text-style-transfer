The present discussion addresses the limitations of TaleFrame concerning user interaction and advanced storytelling, as well as prospective research directions. Currently, TaleFrame operates on desktop and laptop platforms utilizing a mouse and keyboard interface to facilitate the connection of events and the understanding of their interrelations. User feedback suggests a preference for drag-and-drop functionality; however, this method may be suboptimal on mobile devices such as phones and tablets, due to their reduced screen size. Alternative interaction methods, such as touch pens or fingers, are available, but extended interactions and the input of relational information are prone to errors, potentially degrading the user experience on mobile platforms. To enhance usability, we have incorporated color schemes, icons, and lines to convey information and implemented responsive layouts that adapt to various screen dimensions. Future research aims to explore additional interaction modalities, including touchscreens, keyboards, and voice commands, to enhance the accessibility of TaleFrame across diverse devices.   In terms of narrative construction, TaleFrame encounters challenges in managing complex event relationships that extend beyond linear timelines, encompassing causal links, flashbacks, and other narrative techniques. The intricacy of these connections can compromise the clarity and accuracy of stories. Although improvements have been made to enhance the representation of event connections, these efforts are hindered by the complexity of interactive narratives involving multiple entities. Such complexity can result in difficulties in maintaining event sequence or illustrating causality, thereby generating confused narrative structures. As narratives grow in complexity, the model's capacity to comprehend and effectively establish these connections diminishes. Future endeavors will focus on refining relationship modeling and developing advanced non-linear storytelling strategies to enhance the model's proficiency in crafting intricate narratives, ultimately providing users with a more engaging and coherent storytelling experience.
