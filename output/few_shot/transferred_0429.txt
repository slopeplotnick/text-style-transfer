Artificial Intelligence (AI) is advancing rapidly, particularly in the domain of natural language processing and generation, where the creation of coherent and engaging narratives is a focal area (Zhejiang University of Technology, Hangzhou, China). The primary objective remains the generation of narratives that are both logical and captivating, derived from user-provided inputs. However, challenges persist in maintaining story coherence, character development, and narrative control. Ensuring that the narrative aligns with user intent, while preserving plot, character dynamics, and structural integrity, is crucial for maintaining both believability and creativity in storytelling.  Large-scale language models such as GPT-4 and Llama-3 have demonstrated proficiency in generating text. Nevertheless, due to their training on extensive, general datasets, these models often struggle with producing narratives that are sufficiently controlled and structured. This limitation becomes pronounced when users require narratives with specific details and a clearly defined framework. Enhancing these models' capability to manage story creation in a manner that aligns with user specifications, while retaining creativity, is an area of significant importance.  A promising approach involves the fine-tuning of these models in conjunction with the use of structured prompts. This methodology not only enhances the quality of narrative generation but also contributes to the models' stability and reliability in executing complex tasks. The integration of fine-tuning with structured input ameliorates predictability and consistency issues, thus addressing control challenges within the storytelling process.
