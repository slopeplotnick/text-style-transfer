The discussion focuses on DeepSeek-V3.2, a model noted for its rapid and intelligent performance. Its distinct features include: (1) DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA), which enhances processing speed without sacrificing the model's capacity to manage extensive data inputs; (2) a Scalable Reinforcement Learning Framework, wherein a robust learning methodology is augmented with additional computational resources post-training, enabling DeepSeek-V3.2 to perform at par with GPT-5. Moreover, a specialized version, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale, surpasses GPT-5 and rivals Gemini-3.0-Pro in addressing complex problems, achieving accolades at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad and the International Olympiad in Informatics. (3) A Large-Scale Agentic Task Synthesis Pipeline has been developed to generate extensive training datasets, facilitating the model's capability to utilize tools and enhance reasoning in challenging scenarios, thereby improving its proficiency in understanding and executing instructions. The release of reasoning models such as DeepSeek-AI and OpenAI during 2024 and 2025 marked a significant advancement for Large Language Models (LLMs). However, a divergence has emerged, wherein open-source models like MiniMax, MoonShot, Qwen, and ZhiPu-AI show progress, yet closed-source models such as Anthropic, DeepMind, and OpenAI exhibit a more accelerated enhancement, widening the performance gap especially on complex tasks. Three primary challenges in open-source models were identified: the reliance on traditional vanilla attention mechanisms, which inadequately address extensive data handling; insufficient computational resources allocated post-training, leading to suboptimal performance on demanding tasks; and a comparative deficiency in generalization and instruction-following capabilities, limiting their practical applicability.
