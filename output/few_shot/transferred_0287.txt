This section addresses models that facilitate simultaneous translation of speech and text to ensure seamless and high-quality interlingual translations. It elucidates the mechanisms by which contemporary voice-interfaced language models process and interpret speech and conversational cues. Over the past decade, significant advancements have occurred in the development and deployment of voice-powered AI assistants, commencing with Apple's Siri, followed by Microsoft's Cortana, Google Assistant, Amazon's Alexa, and Samsung's Bixby. This segment of the tutorial will explore the technologies underpinning these AI assistants, such as neural networks tailored for intent detection and detail completion, conversational management through machine learning techniques, leveraging web search data, exploiting the semantic web, and utilizing knowledge graphs for enhanced spoken language comprehension. Presently, AI assistants are advancing through the integration of language models to improve comprehension, conversation tracking, and the refinement of spoken language. This tutorial aims to provide a comprehensive overview of current language models and their adaptation for specialized tasks. It highlights recent advancements in systems managing multiple dialogues, applicable to both general and specialized tasks, and includes a discussion on datasets and their evaluation methodologies. Furthermore, it addresses emerging research challenges in the evolution of language models and the growing demand for systems capable of handling multiple concurrent conversations.
