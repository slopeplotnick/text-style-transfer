UnresolvedRetry denotes scenarios wherein an individual repeatedly attempts modifications on a file without success. As exemplified in Figure 12-a, a software engineering agent (SWE-agent) named Dev attempts to employ `str_replace` on `utils.py` 183 times without success, ultimately reaching the cost limit. StrNotFound arises when attempted modifications fail due to the absence of the targeted text within the file, as illustrated in Figure 13-a. This occurrence is typically attributed to formatting discrepancies or minor textual variations. The phenomenon of NoEffectEdit is observed when the original and new text are identical, resulting in no actual modifications; Figure 13-b exemplifies this, where a string is redundantly replaced with itself, leading to resource wastage. AmbiguousTarget occurs when the target text appears multiple times in the file, creating ambiguity regarding the specific instance to be altered. Figure 13-c depicts this scenario with multiple identical `if` statements, complicating the editor's decision on which instance to modify, thereby highlighting a limitation of string-based editing. Notably, inefficiencies persist even in resolved cases, underscoring the necessity of process analysis to comprehend agent behavior. While advantageous for complex problem-solving—as evidenced by increased success rates—this extensive processing can become inefficient when additional steps fail to contribute to improved solutions.
