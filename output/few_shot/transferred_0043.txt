Radiology reporting constitutes a critical component in the evaluation of medical images, yet it is a time-intensive process prone to errors, particularly with three-dimensional modalities such as computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Ensuring the high quality of these reports is of paramount importance, though it necessitates meticulous verification, a task perceived as onerous. Contemporary automated methods predominantly emphasize the generation of reports while neglecting essential quality control, thereby limiting their utility for radiologists. In response, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant designed to automate both report generation and quality assurance. Leveraging large language models for decision-making, tool selection, planning, and execution, Radiologist Copilot emulates the cognitive processes of a radiologist. Its functionalities include identifying regions of interest in images, planning analyses, selecting appropriate report templates, and enhancing quality via feedback mechanisms. This innovative system aims to expedite the production of precise and comprehensive reports, ultimately augmenting radiologist efficiency. Empirical evaluations indicate that Radiologist Copilot surpasses existing state-of-the-art methodologies in report generation. The codebase will be made publicly available upon final approval. The radiology reporting process demands that radiologists scrutinize medical images and document significant findings in detail, but this task is frequently encumbered by delays and susceptibility to error, especially with 3D imaging. Rigorous quality checks are indispensable to ensure the accuracy and completeness of these reports, yet they are labor-intensive due to the need to evaluate nuances in content and language. Automating the synthesis of reports while incorporating quality controls is, therefore, essential for enhancing operational efficacy and supporting radiologists.
