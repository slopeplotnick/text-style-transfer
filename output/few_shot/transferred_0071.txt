Cov_tgsp and CSP demonstrate strong performance overall, as evidenced by Table 2 and Figure 3. However, performance varies across datasets. Specifically, on individual datasets, CSP and cov_tgsp typically rank among the top performers but exhibit reduced effectiveness on the PhysionetMI dataset compared to methods from Cho2017 and Zhou2016. This discrepancy may stem from differences in experimental protocols, such as sampling frequencies, electrode configurations, and participant numbers. Our findings suggest that the efficacy of these methods for decoding motor imagery from EEG signals is highly dataset-dependent. For instance, within the Zhou2016 dataset, CSP with StandardScaler and logistic regression achieved the highest performance score of 0.87, closely followed by cov_tgsp with RobustScaler and logistic regression at 0.84. Conversely, on the Cho2017 dataset, cov_tgsp surpassed CSP, achieving a superior score of 0.74 compared to 0.68. Methods incorporating instantaneous functional connectivity and non-linear features consistently underperformed across datasets; svd_entropy with RobustScaler and logistic regression scored 0.68 on Zhou2016, 0.59 on Cho2017, and 0.54 on PhysionetMI. In the PhysionetMI dataset, overall scores were notably lower, with cov_tgsp achieving the best setup at 0.64, and CSP averaging 0.62 across frequency bands. These results suggest that although non-linear and connectivity-based features can capture intricate EEG signal patterns, they may not always be optimal for motor imagery classification in this context. Analysis of individual subjects reveals that CSP and cov_tgsp are generally superior across datasets, as depicted in Figures 5 and 6 for Cho2017 and Zhou2016. However, this trend is less pronounced in the PhysionetMI dataset (Figure 7), where these methods do not consistently outperform others. Notably, alternative methods occasionally achieve the highest scores: for instance, Hjorth parameters outperform CSP and cov_tgsp for subjects #57 and #59, while the instantaneous connectivity method with TGSP yields the best results for subjects #17 and #73.
