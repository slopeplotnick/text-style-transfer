The experimental setup was as follows: For both solvers and verifiers, a generation framework was employed utilizing a temperature of 0.7, top-p set at 0.9, and a maximum output length of 8192 tokens. Outputs lacking a boxed answer were discarded. All evaluations were conducted using vLLM on H200 GPUs. Further information on prompt configurations and output filtering processes is available in Appendix F.    Regarding solver performance, 37 models were evaluated across 9 datasets, with performance averaged across tasks (Figure 1) and detailed results for individual tasks presented in Appendix G. In general, models with larger capacities exhibited superior performance. Models from the Qwen3 and DeepSeek groups emerged as top performers, while Llama3-Base underperformed due to its unfamiliarity with the question-answering format. Within specific groups, Qwen2.5-Base and DeepSeek demonstrated significant improvements as model scales increased, a trend observed across other groups as well.    Subsequently, the relationship between solver performance and verifier performance was examined (see Figure 2). For all 21 post-trained models and across all datasets, verifier performance was assessed on the corresponding solver models, employing metrics including verifier accuracy, F1-score, precision, false positive rate (FPR), false negative rate (FNR), and gain for each solver-verifier pairing. Verifier metrics were segmented into three verification setups, with averages computed across solvers and datasets for each setup. Verifier accuracy generally improved with higher solver accuracy, although other metrics displayed more intricate patterns. For instance, FPR increased during self-verification and when verifying models from within the same group but slightly decreased when verifying outputs from different groups. This indicates that verifiers with enhanced solver capabilities are more prone to erroneously classify their own outputs or those from their group as correct. Further details on F1-score and precision metrics are provided in Appendix H.
