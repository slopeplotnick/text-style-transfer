Synthesizing related events alongside a retrospective analysis elucidates a coherent narrative, integrating preceding occurrences into a comprehensive framework. This approach surpasses mere summarization, which often results in fragmented accounts devoid of coherent causality and chronological context. Retaining detail, such as specific technological aspects in a "new tech release," enhances clarity compared to overgeneralization, which risks obscuring critical insights pertinent to business impact. Precise dating is instrumental in evaluating temporal durations and effects; absent these dates, it becomes challenging to discern event-induced market dynamics, thereby compromising predictive accuracy. Our investigation underscored the significance of event monitoring and information assimilation. Removal of event tracking components revealed that even minor updates can substantially influence market dynamics if revealed progress unexpectedly. Conversely, in the absence of supplementary information, such as a major shareholder divesting shares without prior announcements, market volatility remains minimal. While individual events may have limited immediate impact, the evolution of expectations over time, apparent through systematic event tracking, proves crucial in fortifying predictive models. Comparative analysis across four methodologies affirmed the superiority of our approach. For instance, reliance on a company's historical data may suffice, yet proves inadequate with limited historical precedence or in novel ventures, where identifying analogous past occurrences is challenging.
