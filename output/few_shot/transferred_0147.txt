This section details the methodology employed in the creation and validation of the dataset. A cohort of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) experts sourced samples from clinical cases, historical case records, and examination questions. These samples underwent a meticulous cleaning process to eliminate duplicates and ensure the removal of any personal information. Annotation was conducted in a tripartite manner: initially by the experts themselves, followed by cross-checking among the experts, and finally reviewed by an independent third party. The resultant benchmark, TCM-BEST4SDT, comprises 600 questions across four domains: TCM Basic Knowledge, Medical Ethics, Large Language Model (LLM) Content Safety, and Syndrome Differentiation and Treatment (SDT). For evaluation, three methods were devised to assess model performance in authentic TCM clinical scenarios. The first method, selected-response evaluation, involves repetitive assessment of each question with randomized answer choices, implementing differential scoring based on the question type. The second method, judge model evaluation, utilizes an LLM with prompts developed by experts to evaluate the model's responses. The third method, reward model evaluation, features a customized model to assess the alignment of prescriptions with syndromes. TCM-BEST4SDT comprises two categories of tasks: SDT tasks and general evaluation tasks. SDT tasks incorporate data from clinical cases and historical records, while general tasks encompass TCM Basic Knowledge, Medical Ethics, and LLM Content Safety. TCM Basic Knowledge questions are derived from public examinations such as the National Qualification Exam for Medical Practitioners, the National Postgraduate Entrance Exam: Comprehensive Clinical Medicine (TCM Integrated), and the Chinese Herbal Medicine Title Exam. Medical Ethics questions are sourced from reputable references and manually annotated by experts for context-specific scenarios. LLM Content Safety questions were independently constructed by experts based on practical requirements.
