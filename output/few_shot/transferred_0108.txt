The interviews were conducted with an emphasis on creating a conversational rather than a rigid question-and-answer environment. Initially, participants were provided with a brief overview of the SPLE and the purpose of the questionnaire. The interview commenced with queries related to the participants’ personal backgrounds and their roles within the company, aiming to establish a contextual understanding of the interviewees. Subsequently, the discussion transitioned to general inquiries regarding the company’s developmental procedures, followed by more focused questions concerning the current strategies employed for new product development. The aim was to ascertain effective practices, identify areas of success, and evaluate the overall efficiency of existing processes. This discussion facilitated the introduction of a novel approach designed to address any identified challenges. The conclusion of the interviews involved discussions on the potential transition to the new method, encouraging participants to express any concerns, perceive risks, and possible benefits. Furthermore, feedback was solicited on the interview process itself to enhance future implementations. The interviews were consistently conducted by the first author of this study, with each session lasting between 30 and 60 minutes. Participants reported a positive experience, feeling integrated into the migration project. Each session concluded with the question, "Was there a question you thought I’d ask but didn’t?" which prompted participants to reflect comprehensively on the topics discussed and contribute any overlooked insights. This approach also optimized subsequent interviews. A standardized set of questions was maintained across all sessions to enable consistent data analysis; however, the aim was to facilitate comprehensive reflection while improving the interview process. Detailed notes were taken during the interviews. Additionally, based on received guidance, the interviewer ensured that questions were unbiased and non-leading, maintaining neutrality while adequately probing without directing responses. A preliminary interview was conducted with a key collaborator from the partner company to refine the questionnaire and streamline the procedure. The data from this initial interview was excluded from the final analysis.
