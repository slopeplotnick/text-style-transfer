Recent advancements in language models with capabilities to process voice inputs have garnered significant scholarly attention. Notably, proprietary models such as GPT-4o and Gemini-1.5-pro demonstrate superior performance in tasks involving speech recognition, translation, and spoken language understanding, surpassing earlier publicly accessible models. Despite these achievements, the integration of speech processing with language models remains underexplored. Additionally, the synergistic operation of speech and language models, particularly within autonomous intelligent systems, is an emergent field of inquiry.  This tutorial aims to explore the historical development of language models in speech processing, offering insights into the design of interoperable systems. The discussion will encompass advanced methodologies for multimodal data integration and foundational techniques for aligning speech and text modalities. Participants will be introduced to accessible examples illustrating these concepts. Furthermore, the tutorial will examine novel approaches to simultaneous speech and text processing, employing iterative data generation methodologies that integrate both modalities.  By juxtaposing traditional and contemporary methodologies, this tutorial seeks to illuminate current advancements and persistent challenges in the fusion of speech and language models. The three-hour session will address the evolution of these models within voice interfaces and intelligent systems. Each primary topic will be allocated 35 minutes, followed by 10 minutes for questions and another 10 minutes for a break. Each segment will commence with an overview, progressing to an in-depth analysis of pivotal studies currently influencing the field. Concluding the session, we will deliberate on challenges and emerging research opportunities in this dynamic area of speech and language modeling integration.
