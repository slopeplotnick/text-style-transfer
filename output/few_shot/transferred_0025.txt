The project focuses on integrating new features and updates into the existing framework, emphasizing advancements in technology and agricultural improvements. The primary objective is to accelerate development processes, such as expediting the creation of new applications compared to previous iterations. The initiative leverages concepts like code reuse, innovation, and rapid execution, originating from a reusable code base that facilitates the migration project. Seven effective practices were identified, with Factorization and Reuse emerging prominently, cited nine and eight times respectively, as illustrated in Figure 1. Understanding these benefits is crucial as it serves to motivate the team. Customization is a significant aspect of a Software Product Line (SPL) and was frequently highlighted by professionals in IT, User Experience (UX), and agriculture (AGRO). An SPL is perceived as a platform capable of generating customized software from existing solutions. The notion of speed, the second most frequently mentioned concept, was acknowledged by IT, Product Owners (PO), and UX professionals, signifying the swift development of new software. Automation, which entails generating new software automatically, was also noted, with IT and PO professionals perceiving automation and speed as interconnected. Industrialization, discussed by IT and UX experts, refers to producing applications on demand and cost-effectively. Improving code quality, exclusively mentioned by IT personnel, involves ensuring the code is consistent, testable, and reliable. Competitive advantages were noted by IT and PO professionals, indicating the potential for increased software sales or rapid response to market opportunities.
