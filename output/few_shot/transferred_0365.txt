The platform has been operational for approximately 5–6 years. Leveraging the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK's platform is categorized as a "Level 3: Software Platform," characterized by a standardized setup employed across all products. The current configuration includes shared components and designated areas for accommodating new versions. The objective is to achieve a state where commonalities and variabilities within the domain are clearly delineated, enabling the establishment of a unified, standardized setup for the entire software product line. This involves strategically reusing components from a parts repository, distinguishing domain-specific variations, and implementing a cohesive standard setup that regulates permissible modifications to application configurations. Moreover, the setup integrates numerous quality-oriented solutions within the software product line infrastructure.   The management of domain-specific differences is systematically addressed by defining allowable application configurations and establishing boundaries for modifications, including rules governing compliance for specific versions. The overarching aim is to create a software setup that facilitates the automated generation of diverse versions. According to the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK operates at the fourth maturity level at the organizational scale, signifying a coordinated integration of domain engineering and application engineering.   The project is being implemented through extensive collaboration between agronomists and software developers. The decision to migrate to an SPL approach and engage the research team was reached collectively during discussions involving project leaders and stakeholders. Prior to initiating the planning of interviews, no definitive research methodology had been established for subsequent phases of the project. The preliminary focus was to understand the perceptions of company personnel regarding the transition to SPL. Insights gained from the interviews will guide the development of a comprehensive methodology for the migration project.
