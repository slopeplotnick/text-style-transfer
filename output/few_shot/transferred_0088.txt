Figure 1 illustrates the operational mechanism of the fine-tuned model. Initially, the model extracts critical details from both the query and the item descriptions, including product names, brands, model specifications, and target demographic information. Subsequently, it assesses the congruence of these details between the query and the item description to determine relevance. The CoT tuning enhances the model's capability not only to accurately predict relevance labels but also to provide explanatory insights. Analysis of the fine-tuned model's outputs reveals a tendency towards stringent relevance criteria, frequently categorizing products as "Irrelevant" if certain query details are absent from the product description, irrespective of their actual significance in purchasing decisions. This stringent approach results in an increased rate of false negatives, thereby reducing the model's recall rate and adversely affecting the platform's transactions and revenue. To address this issue, we ensure alignment between the model's predictions and real user behavior and feedback. The fine-tuned model is employed to predict query-item pairs based on historical purchase data, selecting those labeled "Irrelevant" to construct a training dataset. These conservative predictions are treated as suboptimal instances, while GPT-40 is used to generate "Relevant" rationale results for the original query-item pairs, treated as optimal instances. Subsequently, the KTO algorithm is applied to align the model with these preferences. In contrast to other alignment methodologies, the KTO algorithm utilizes straightforward binary labeled data for model alignment, thereby simplifying and expediting the setup process.
