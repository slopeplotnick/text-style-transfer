Concerns regarding migration and its potential adverse effects are prevalent. These concerns primarily revolve around possible negative outcomes. Discussions with stakeholders about the advantages revealed their apprehensions concerning perceived risks. Two pivotal inquiries that facilitated the identification of these fears included: - Is this approach beneficial for the company's existing processes? - What are your thoughts on a platform capable of rapidly developing analogous software? Seven distinct concerns were identified, as illustrated in Figure 4. The principal apprehensions among IT professionals pertain to the potential ineffectiveness, complexity, and resistance to change (refer to Table 7). Ultimately, all stakeholders, irrespective of role, expressed a desire for training and demonstrations. They seek to comprehend the utility of a Software Product Line (SPL) in their work and the transition to this novel methodology. Drawing from established best practices, benefits, risks, and fears, we have devised guidelines to facilitate the transition to an SPL. These guidelines emerged from deliberations and resolutions within the company. This section elaborates on anticipated outcomes (Sect. 5.1), the implementation of best practices (Sect. 5.2), strategies to foster future benefits (Sect. 5.3), risk mitigation (Sect. 5.4), addressing fears (Sect. 5.5), specific actionable steps (Sect. 5.6), and a comprehensive discussion (Sect. 5.7). Prior to the interviews, expectations regarding potential outcomes were uncertain. It was hypothesized that individuals familiar with or favorable towards reusability would exhibit greater enthusiasm and interest. These individuals were expected to be proactive in initiating and supporting the project. In our observations, more experienced developers emphasized mindset, whereas less experienced developers concentrated on technical aspects. However, this variance in experience did not significantly affect the quantity or quality of responses obtained.
