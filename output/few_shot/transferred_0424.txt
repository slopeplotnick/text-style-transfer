We aim to develop a system capable of training policies to exhibit natural movement without reliance on the original data. This system should exhibit versatility across various tasks and policies without necessitating retraining. To achieve this, we introduce Score-Matching Motion Priors (SMP), which facilitate the training of control policies to perform naturally across diverse tasks. The process begins with training a model to comprehend motion data without being constrained to any specific task or policy. Once trained, this model is employed to compare simulated motions against behaviors in the original dataset. By utilizing the learned SMP as a reward mechanism, we can train new policies to execute tasks with natural behaviors. The primary objective is to establish a system that generates motion priors, which are reusable for character animation by integrating reinforcement learning with score distillation. Our system generates high-quality motions comparable to the best existing methods and enhances their reusability and modularity. SMP enables training policies without the need for the original data. We demonstrate that a motion prior can be derived from a model trained on extensive motion datasets without task specificity. Subsequently, this motion prior can be adapted into style-specific variants through prompts and guidance. Furthermore, we illustrate the capability to amalgamate different style priors to create novel styles absent from the original data.
