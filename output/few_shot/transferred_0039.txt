The approach involves the construction of datasets comprising environmental conditions, tools, tasks, and verifiers. Subsequently, DeepSeek-V3.2 is employed to perform reinforcement learning on these datasets, retaining only the entries that successfully pass validation criteria. This process results in a curated dataset containing 1,827 unique environments and their associated tasks, yielding a total of 4,417 task-environment pairs. As an illustrative example, the problem of planning a trip is utilized to demonstrate the complexity of satisfying all imposed constraints, while simultaneously emphasizing the relative simplicity of verifying the feasibility of a proposed itinerary.  One specific example involves a three-day trip originating in Hangzhou from October 1 to October 3, 2025. The constraints for the trip include avoiding repeated visits to the same cities, accommodations at the same hotels, and dining at the same restaurants. Furthermore, the hotels, restaurants, and attractions must align with the city visited on any particular day. Budget constraints are placed specifically on the second day of the trip. For hotels classified as "luxury" (costing 800 CNY or more per night), dining expenses must not exceed 350 CNY across both lunch and dinner. Furthermore, both dining establishments must possess a minimum rating of 4.0 stars, and any afternoon attraction must have an entry fee below 120 CNY. For "moderate" hotels (costing 500–800 CNY per night), a more relaxed budget is allowed, requiring at least one restaurant to maintain a rating of 4.0 stars or higher, with the afternoon attraction ticket capped at 180 CNY. Finally, for "budget" hotels (costing 200–500 CNY per night), the only requirement is that at least one restaurant achieves a minimum rating of 3.2 stars. This specific example underscores the intricate constraints and considerations involved in automated itinerary planning.
