We compiled a collection of 5,500 Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset, following methodologies established in prior research. Our evaluation includes performance assessments on AIME 2024, AMC, MATH500, GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math. In addition to conventional tasks, we also investigate performance on ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond. We employ a technique known as greedy decoding, constrained by a 3,000-token limit.   Our experimental framework includes testing CAPO in conjunction with four principal reinforcement learning methodologies: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. For each method, we evaluate a variant paired with CAPO to determine the extent of its enhancement. This methodology allows us to compare the standalone performance of the original methods against their CAPO-enhanced versions. To ensure methodological rigor and fairness, we maintain consistent experimental conditions across all methods, with comprehensive training details provided in Appendix B.  We utilize the GUI-R1-3K dataset from OS-Atlas, comprising 3,000 instances of vision-language-action interactions across web and mobile platforms. This dataset aligns visual states with natural language instructions and action sequences, serving as an effective benchmark for evaluating reasoning and planning capabilities across diverse scenarios. For planning assessment, we focus on GUI-Act-Web, OmniAct-Web, and AndroidControl-Low/High; for perception analysis, we employ Screenshot Pro. We specifically compare GRPO and its CAPO variant using the primary GUI benchmark configuration. This supplementary evaluation aims to demonstrate CAPO's efficacy across varied reasoning contexts, extending beyond purely mathematical applications.
