In 2023, Zakharov discussed mechanisms of perceptual change detection and probabilistic inference under uncertainty. Petzschner and Glasauer (2011) proposed that such processes align with a "best guess" heuristic employed in uncertain conditions. Recent computational models suggest that inferential errors, particularly regarding temporal estimation, are underpinned by memory-based predictive mechanisms (Fountas et al., 2022). When estimating magnitudes or quantities, individuals integrate ambiguous information with prior beliefs, resulting in predictable behavioral patterns. Figure 1 illustrates that both Llama-4 Maverick and humans exhibit a tendency to regress toward the mean, suggesting convergence in their inferential strategies despite differences in underlying mechanisms. Components of Large Language Models (LLMs) appear to operate analogously to such heuristic processes. For instance, contextual learning can be interpreted as an approximate inference strategy, as posited by Xie et al. (2021), with pedagogical applications of this approach enhancing reasoning capabilities (Qiu et al., 2025). Moreover, LLMs decompose sequences based on prediction-driven surprise in a manner consistent with human event perception (Kumar and Fountas, 2023, 2025). However, extant research predominantly emphasizes explicit reasoning or task-specific learned behaviors in LLMs, often neglecting the emergent reasoning modalities arising naturally through training. Rapid advancements have been observed in the development of LLMs capable of integrating diverse information modalities, with evaluation frameworks such as MMbench and SEED-bench (Liu and Li, 2024) assessing their reasoning efficacy. Nonetheless, such benchmarks rarely address the impact of noise within information modalities on inferential processes. Our novel dataset facilitates a granular examination of how LLMs integrate uncertain information from disparate sources. This represents a nascent yet critical area of inquiry towards designing models that remain robust under conditions of uncertainty.
