Individuals who are primarily engaged in business activities or possess limited technical proficiency may exhibit reluctance to adapt, even when they recognize the potential advantages, due to concerns about the rapid pace of technological advancement. Our team is relatively small and accustomed to emerging technologies; consequently, having contemplated the adoption of a Software Product Line (SPL) from the outset, significant resistance was not anticipated. Through interviews, we have identified strategies to sustain successful practices, augment benefits, and address any apprehensions. It is imperative to maintain effective practices currently employed with SPL to ensure collective comfort. Although our operational methodologies will undergo significant transformation, preserving beneficial habits will facilitate team acclimatization to novel developments. SPL emphasizes accelerated processes, reuse, and modularity, which we are already implementing, thereby allowing us to concentrate on the benefits. Transitioning to an SPL model supports sustainable reuse and modular decomposition. Additional advantages of SPL include enhanced customization, automation, and a shift towards a factory-like process. Furthermore, SPL adoption contributes to improved code quality, aiding in alleviating technical debt by promoting consistency, reducing bugs, and minimizing redundant code duplication. Addressing recurrent bugs further enhances code quality. Notably, the advantages of SPL are not solely recognized by product owners; IT professionals frequently acknowledge these benefits more extensively. For example, this engineering approach can enhance the company's research and development (R&D) reputation. Moreover, the adoption of SPL facilitates expedited software development.
