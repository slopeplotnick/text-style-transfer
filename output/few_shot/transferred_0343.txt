Guardrail systems play a crucial role in ensuring safety in interactions between humans and artificial intelligence. Initially, these systems employed rudimentary rule-based approaches for content filtering, which proved inadequate as the technological landscape evolved, presenting novel challenges. These early guardrails lacked comprehensive coverage and precision. Contemporary guardrail systems, however, leverage advanced AI models, enhancing their adaptability and generalization capabilities. Notably, Llama Guard, based on specialized safety datasets from Meta AI, safeguards dialogues between users and AI. Additionally, models such as Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard address visual safety concerns. Despite these advancements, limitations persist. Previous efforts predominantly concentrated on textual and visual content, with video and audio being managed in a rudimentary manner, often resorting to simplistic methods lacking contextual understanding. Moreover, existing systems predominantly target single media types or specific contexts, falling short in handling multimodal media or performing cross-modal reasoning. In response to these challenges, OMNIGUARD has been developed as a pioneering system capable of multimodal integration and cross-modal reasoning to ensure safety. OMNIGUARD aspires to deliver comprehensive and coherent safety assessments across diverse media forms.
