We modify the motion of the camera to generate diverse viewpoints and prevent it from remaining stationary. Each camera trajectory is initialized randomly, with variations in parameters such as pitch, yaw, and roll, allowing for naturalistic movement. To ensure audiovisual coherence, the primary sound sources are aligned with the visual scene and maintained in focus for a sufficient duration. The loudest sound in each clip is identified using a decomposition-based method, after which the camera is adjusted to emphasize that sound source. Once binaural audio is processed, samples with insufficient interaural differences—indicating minimal spatial audio cues—are discarded, retaining only those with distinct spatial auditory features.   BiAudio represents the largest video dataset with binaural audio to date. Other existing real-world binaural audio datasets are limited in scale, while synthetic datasets, such as SimBinaural, provide significant duration but lack realism, diminishing their utility in practical scenarios. Additional datasets often concentrate on specific environments, such as urban streets or musical performances, whereas BiAudio provides comprehensive coverage of diverse real-world auditory contexts. The dataset enhances the perception of spatiality and movement, making it a robust resource for synthesizing binaural audio from video inputs.  The architecture of ViSAudio is depicted in a proposed schematic, which draws inspiration from elements of the MMAudio design. Specific adaptations are implemented to retain MMAudio's robust sound generation capabilities while ensuring its applicability across varied acoustic environments. Binaural audio is generated with clearly differentiated left and right channels by employing a Dual-Branch Audio Generation framework, where distinct components independently predict the two channels. Furthermore, a Conditional Spacetime Module is integrated to extract spatiotemporal features from the video, incorporating them into the audio synthesis process to achieve a more immersive and realistic experience.
