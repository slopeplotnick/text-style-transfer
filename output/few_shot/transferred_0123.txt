The performance of models on mathematical problem-solving was evaluated using five benchmarks: AMC, Minerva, MATH-500, GSM8K, and Olympiad-Bench. To assess the correctness of responses, we employed GPT-4o to compare generated answers against ground truth, aligning with methodologies from prior studies (e.g., Zhang et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024). For general reasoning, model performance was analyzed across a range of domains, including physics, biology, business, economics, and law, using benchmarks such as MMLU-Pro, SuperGPQA, GPQA-Diamond, and BBEH. These benchmarks were specifically designed to assess models' capabilities in complex reasoning tasks. Accuracy was measured using the Exact Match (EM) metric, which evaluates the perfect alignment of generated answers with reference answers, while results were generated using greedy decoding.  To investigate the functional relevance of specific model components, an ablation study was conducted on the Qwen3-8B-Base model, wherein certain features were disabled to observe their impact on mathematical and general reasoning tasks. The findings, summarized in Table 2, reveal that disabling challenger training resulted in the most significant reduction in performance, with math and general reasoning scores dropping by 1.9 and 1.0 points, respectively. Additionally, the removal of challenger warm-up and curriculum learning led to further performance deterioration, underscoring the importance of these components. The observed impact was more pronounced on mathematical tasks compared to general reasoning, corroborating findings in Table 1, where mathematical performance exhibited greater sensitivity to methodological variations.  Overall, the results demonstrate that incorporating diverse human-labeled data enhances general performance, while targeted data types yield greater improvements in specific areas. These findings highlight the importance of data selection in guiding model fine-tuning to prioritize desired competencies and prevent unintended deviations from the intended learning objectives.
