In the third stage, we concentrate on refining the model to adhere to specific instructions across various tasks within the Infinity-MM framework. The comprehensive dataset employed encompasses document comprehension, chart-based reasoning, OCR-centric tasks, and interactive dialogues. Adhering to the Infinity-MM methodology, we assign distinct task weights: 35% to general visual question answering, 25% to document understanding, 20% to chart reasoning, 15% to OCR, and 5% to dialogues and specialized domains. The learning rate is established at 1e-5, with training conducted over a single epoch utilizing a batch size of 512. The objective is the next-word prediction via standard language modeling techniques. Evaluation of our model, optimized for NPU, is conducted against contemporary vision-language models through five distinct assessments. Comparison is made with recent models such as InternVL2 and Qwen2-VL, designed for disparate systems and with varying parameters. Our AutoNeural model, advanced to this third stage, exhibits an optimal balance of accuracy and training efficiency. It is subjected to five benchmarks that assess diverse competencies: MMStar evaluates the model's capability in multi-step and commonsense visual question answering; HallusionBench examines the model's handling of linguistic and visual illusions; MathVista_MINI assesses mathematical problem-solving using diagrams and charts; AI2D_TEST evaluates diagrammatic and textbook science comprehension; OCRBench investigates text recognition from images across diverse settings, documents, and signage. Performance is quantified through accuracy on each test, with an aggregate score calculated to determine overall proficiency in handling varied task typologies.
