Social media has become an integral part of contemporary life, facilitating rapid communication, seamless information sharing, and the exchange of opinions across geographic boundaries. However, this freedom also propagates hate speech and offensive language. Unfortunately, there has been limited research aimed at detecting hate speech in Bangla social media, and a high-quality dataset for this purpose is lacking. The lexicon used in social media often diverges significantly from that found in traditional print media, complicating the automated detection of Bangla hate speech. Historically, hate speech detection has been approached as a supervised classification task. Initial studies relied on lexicon-based and rule-based methods, which frequently failed to capture implicit or context-specific hate speech. Contemporary research has shifted towards employing machine learning techniques, such as logistic regression and SVMs, for identifying online threats or harmful content through traditional supervised learning frameworks.
