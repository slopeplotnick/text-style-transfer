Predicting stock prices remains a challenging task due to the inherent unpredictability of markets and their rapid reactions to real-time events. Recently, advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs) [Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2022; Liu et al., 2023] have introduced novel methodologies for stock price prediction through text analysis. While LLMs excel in textual comprehension, their application to complex, real-world tasks often necessitates specialized memory systems capable of consistently leveraging historical information. Despite considerable research on memory systems, existing approaches encounter significant obstacles, particularly in processing the vast and noisy corpus of online news, which often contains irrelevant information and lacks direct explanations for price changes. The primary challenge lies in extracting salient details and logical connections from unstructured textual data that directly influence stock market movements.  To address this challenge, this study proposes a novel two-layer memory system designed to enhance stock price prediction. The first layer organizes events in two dimensions: (1) aggregating daily event details to construct a coherent snapshot and (2) tracking temporal changes in events to build a knowledge base that encapsulates the evolution of market expectations. The second layer establishes a knowledge base that identifies and analyzes the relationship between event patterns and stock price fluctuations. This dual-layer structure enables the system to autonomously acquire, organize, and utilize pertinent knowledge.  The proposed framework operates through two primary stages: retrieval and reasoning. During the retrieval stage, the system leverages recent events to query its memory for analogous past scenarios, facilitating more informed decision-making. Subsequently, in the reasoning stage, it integrates recent events, newly acquired data, and historical examples to generate predictions regarding stock price movements. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that this system outperforms existing models in prediction accuracy. Furthermore, its inherent interpretability allows it to elucidate the connections between events and price changes, thereby offering a clear rationale for its predictions.
