The initialization of the PEFT model is facilitated by the model loader through the `init_adapter` method. Subsequently, the PEFT provider libraries, in conjunction with the LLaMA-Factory framework, are responsible for the processes of loading, training, and saving the model. In contrast to LLaMA-Factory, PEFT-FACTORY employs a flexible approach for loading bespoke PEFT methods, enhancing its adaptability and extensibility. This configuration allows researchers and practitioners to integrate custom PEFT methods without modifying the core codebase of PEFT-FACTORY. To demonstrate the functionality of our Custom PEFT Interface, we replicated two PEFT methods not incorporated within any conventional PEFT framework, specifically BitFit and SVFT. During dynamic loading, the `peftloader` module identifies and imports PEFT methods from a systematically organized directory structure. Each custom PEFT method resides in its designated directory, comprising two principal components: a `config.py` file defining a `PeftConfig` subclass, and a `model.py` file containing a `BaseTuner` subclass. The loader verifies each setup by inspecting essential attributes such as `peft_type` for configurations and `prefix` for model classes before proceeding with their incorporation. It then loads the config and model subclasses, registering them via the `register_peft_method` function, which incorporates the configuration and model into the constants of the Hugging Face PEFT library. This process is further elucidated in Algorithm 1, located in Appendix B.1. Upon successful loading, the custom PEFT method is managed analogously to standard methods, as described in Section 3.1. For the introduction of a new method, adherence to the directory structure within the PEFT methods directory is required, configurable through the environment variable `PEFT_DIR`, defaulting to `./peft`, to ensure consistent organization and class inheritance. Detailed templates of example methods are provided in Appendix C and the PEFT-FACTORY documentation.
