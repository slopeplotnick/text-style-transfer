We evaluate the performance of OMNIGUARD utilizing a series of public safety tests encompassing both singular and composite data modalities. For individual modalities, we examine text, images, videos, and audio. Text evaluations employ benchmarks such as BeaverTails, ToxicChat, WildGuardMix, Aegis2.0, and OpenAI Moderation. Image assessments utilize UnsafeBench, VLGuard, and LlavaGuard. Video analyses involve SafeSora and SafeWatch-Bench, while for audio, examinations include MuTox English split and WildGuardMix-TTS, which converts WildGuardMix test samples into speech. In the mixed modality context, we assess OMNIGUARD using image-text, video-text, and audio-text configurations through MM-SafetyBench, VideoSafetyBench, and AIAH. The primary performance metrics are Accuracy and F1 score, with accuracy solely reported for tests containing unsafe samples. Comparative analyses involve evaluating OMNIGUARD against other models, both large and small, to assess protective efficacy, including specialized models based on LLM and VLM frameworks. Both OMNI-GUARD-7B and OMNI-GUARD-3B exhibit exceptional performance across all domains.
