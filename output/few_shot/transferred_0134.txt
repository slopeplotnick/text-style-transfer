AI agents have demonstrated the capability to autonomously engage in cognitive processes such as thinking, planning, and tool utilization. In recent developments, medical AI agents have undertaken complex medical responsibilities. For instance, MMedAgent employs an array of tools to execute medical tasks, including the generation of reports for chest X-rays. MedRAX, a specialized agent for interpreting chest X-rays, utilizes cutting-edge tools for this purpose. CT-Agent excels in the comprehension and reasoning of 3D CT scans. However, in the domain of radiology report generation, these agents often rely on singular tools, resulting in suboptimal collaborative functionality. Our proposed Radiologist Copilot distinguishes itself by integrating multiple tools that operate synergistically, thereby enhancing the capability to orchestrate and execute more intricate tasks. The generation of radiology reports is a critical, yet time-intensive, process that is prone to errors, particularly with 3D imaging. Evaluating the quality of these reports is crucial but commonly neglected in other research. To address these issues, we developed Radiologist Copilot, an AI assistant equipped with an ensemble of tools for the creation and quality assessment of radiology reports, aimed at aiding radiologists and optimizing efficiency. As illustrated in the accompanying figure, Radiologist Copilot supports radiologists throughout the entire report generation process. Initially, it configures the tool library, followed by utilizing the action planner and executor to strategize and execute tasks. It includes a memory module that records executed actions and their outcomes, facilitating task completion verification. The action executor generates and executes commands aligned with the planned subsequent actions. For each task, it formulates the appropriate command for the selected tool, executes the command, and captures the action's outcome.
