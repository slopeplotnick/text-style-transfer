In the left section of Figure 2, an illustration demonstrates the prompt configuration and the extraction of triples for a single training image. This arrangement delineates how varying details pertaining to the image are systematically organized. The resultant output consists of multiple triples aligned with the Fashionpedia ontology, encompassing attributes such as clothing type (depicts), shape (has_silhouette), material (has_material_type), pattern (has_pattern), among other relationships specified by Fashionpedia. Each triple adheres to the format \( (image\_id, relation, attribute) \), thereby facilitating a clearer interpretation of the image's representation. This configuration aids in the construction and comparison of concept-based graphs. To assess influence, we contrasted the graph of each generated image with those of all training images by evaluating the extent of shared nodes. For each prompt, the training images exhibiting the greatest overlap with the generated image’s graph were retained. The employed prompt was: "Create triples for the fashion item in the image based on the relationships of Fashionpedia Ontology, provided at: \({ONTOLOGY\_PATH}\). Focus exclusively on the fashion item of the image with image\_id: \([IMAGE\_ID\)]\) and provide the triples that describe the image \((image\_id, relationship, attribute\_value)\). Please provide no additional comments." For instance, regarding image 58810.jpg, the triples included: 58810.jpg, depicts, Shirt; 58810.jpg, belongs_to_category, Upperbody; 58810.jpg, has_nickname, None; 58810.jpg, has_silhouette, Slim-fit; 58810.jpg, has_waistline, Straight; 58810.jpg, has_length, Long-sleeved; 58810.jpg, has_collar_type, Classic; 58810.jpg, has_lapel_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_neckline, Crew-neck; 58810.jpg, has_sleeve_type, Long Sleeves with Cuffs; 58810.jpg, has_pocket_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_opening_type, Button-front Closure; 58810.jpg, has_material_type, Cotton-blend Fabric; 58810.jpg, has_leather_type, None; 58810.jpg, has_fabric_treatment, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_pattern, Plaid; 58810.jpg, has_animal_pattern, None. These graphs, demonstrating the greatest node commonality with the generated image's graph, were subsequently employed in the ensuing step of unlearning. Beyond identifying similar images, Neo4j was utilized to interrogate the entire graph, identifying prevalent attributes or visualizing the distribution of concepts across the dataset. For the unlearning process, the generative model was retrained following the exclusion of specific training samples to verify their impact on the output. Ten unlearning experiments were conducted using both the baseline and our methodology. In each experiment, images were generated for 15 prompts both pre- and post-unlearning. Consistent with the baseline configuration, a comparable number of the most influential training images were removed for each prompt.
