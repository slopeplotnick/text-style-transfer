We developed a tool named Promptolution, an open-source Python framework designed to automate the enhancement of prompts. This tool has been empirically validated and demonstrated efficacy. It operates seamlessly across various language models, natural language processing (NLP) tasks, and prompt improvement methodologies, allowing for modular interchangeability and user-defined augmentations. Promptolution is advantageous for individuals aiming to refine singular prompts and equally beneficial for researchers as it facilitates organized and replicable experimental processes. Figure 1 depicts the operational framework of Promptolution. The objectives of this paper are as follows: to position Promptolution within the landscape of existing prompt optimization tools, to elucidate its architectural design, to assess its performance relative to alternative tools and non-optimized prompts, and to propose future enhancements. Prompt optimization entails the application of automated techniques for testing and refining prompts, encompassing both instructions and examples. This domain can be bifurcated into continuous methods, which are numerical, and discrete methods, which engage directly with textual data. Our focus is on discrete methods due to their intuitive comprehensibility and broad applicability across diverse language models. The tool enhances prompts incrementally, frequently utilizing auxiliary language models to suggest improvements. The methodologies embedded within Promptolution include: OPRO, harnessing a language model to propose improved instructions informed by task descriptions, examples, and prior outcomes; EvoPrompt, which applies genetic algorithms and differential evolution for instruction enhancement, with modifications managed by a language model; and CAPO, an advanced method employing AutoML for more efficient improvements, applicable to both instructions and examples. CAPO is distinguished by its cost-effectiveness and superior performance relative to alternative methods.
