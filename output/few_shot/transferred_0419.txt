This study investigates Lang2Logic, a novel framework that bridges natural language and computer logic. Lang2Logic translates everyday language into Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF), a representation essential for logical reasoning tasks. By transforming natural language into CNF, Lang2Logic facilitates the interpretation of human communication into a format comprehensible to computational systems. The framework employs specialized computational tools and a customized parsing methodology to ensure the accurate conversion of natural language into CNF. Notably, this process enables the translation of natural language into SAT (Boolean satisfiability problem) formulations, a class of logical problems solvable by SAT solvers, thereby supporting the application of SAT solvers in practical scenarios.   The primary objective of Lang2Logic is to establish a seamless connection between natural language comprehension and computational logic by systematically converting English into CNF, the requisite input format for SAT solvers. The Lang2Logic framework is structured into three core components: (1) transforming natural language into logical expressions, (2) converting these expressions into CNF, and (3) optimizing the resulting CNF for efficiency. Each of these components is critical to ensuring the accuracy and efficacy of the transformation process.   The initial step involves utilizing the ChatGPT o1-mini model API to convert natural language into logical expressions. The o1-mini model is specifically configured to identify redundancies, generate logical expressions compatible with auxiliary tools, and conform to the syntactic requirements of the Sympy conversion utility. Leveraging the ChatGPT API obviates the need for manually defined complex grammar rules, thereby simplifying the transformation process. However, this approach is constrained by higher computational costs and potential inefficiencies with larger inputs. To mitigate these challenges, the framework incorporates the Natural Language Toolkit (NLTK) to preprocess input text. NLTK’s Punkt tokenizer segments the text into individual sentences, enabling the o1-mini model to process each sentence independently. This sentence-by-sentence processing reduces errors, such as sentence omission or misinterpretation, and enhances the accuracy of the generated logical expressions.
