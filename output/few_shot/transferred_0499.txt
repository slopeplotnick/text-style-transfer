In this section, we present an illustration of our interface via an example named "Picture Composition." This feature provides users with explicit information, such as the number and type of entities and events, as well as their interconnections, thereby facilitating content creation. Figure 5.a presents a schematic of picture composition, whereas Figure 5.b illustrates the process by which P4 constructs a narrative using TaleFrame. The procedure begins with P4 dragging the event "Jack shoves Ryan," the initial element of the diagram, into the workspace (refer to Figure 6.f). Subsequently, P4 incorporates the two individuals involved in this event into the designated box (Figure 6.g). Following this, P4 establishes a connection between the two characters (Figure 6.h1), interacts with the connecting line, and modifies the specifics of their relationship (Figure 6.e), including the type of emotion, type of action, the strength of the relationship, and its evolution. The "action_direction" of the relationship is denoted by the "connect node," typically extending from the initiator to the recipient (e.g., "from Jack to Ryan"). However, in the event "confront each other," where the action is reciprocal, an option labeled "is bidirectional" is available. Activating this option renders the action_direction "bidirectional." By replicating this process across all segments, P4 completes the remaining three sections, linking the four event boxes to finalize the narrative structure (as shown in Figure 6.h2). Subsequently, P4 selects the "Modify Story" button, amending the title and description, and aligns the four events with the appropriate segments of the narrative. The narrative is evaluated across seven dimensions based on identical criteria (Figure 6.1). Additionally, a comprehensive recommendation is provided from the feedback (Figure 6.m). In response to this feedback, P4 revises segments receiving lower scores, such as adding more interconnections to specific events. Thereafter, P4 activates the "Regenerate" button to generate a revised version. Ultimately, P4 selects the "Export" button to obtain the final narrative, corresponding diagram, and JSON file.
