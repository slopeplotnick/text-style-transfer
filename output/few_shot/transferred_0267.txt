We evaluated the efficacy of the Radiologist Copilot by assessing two key aspects: task performance and tool functionality. For tasks such as radiology report generation, we utilized several metrics to evaluate textual quality and clinical accuracy. The textual quality metrics included BLEU-1, ROUGE-L, METEOR, and BERTScore, while clinical accuracy was assessed using F1-RadGraph and GREEN. Tool functionality was evaluated using the LLM-as-a-Judge framework, leveraging OpenAI GPT-5.1 to comprehensively review the agent's operational process. This evaluation encompassed four dimensions: task analysis, tool selection, planning, and execution, each scored on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Task analysis assessed whether the agent accurately comprehends the task; tool selection evaluated the appropriateness of tools chosen by the agent; planning examined the coherence of the devised strategy; and execution evaluated the agent's adherence to the plan. To ascertain the Radiologist Copilot's overall effectiveness, we examined both tool performance and radiology task handling, alongside ablation tests to assess core component performance without additional features. In tool evaluation, the majority of scores were 5 (excellent), with some scores of 4 (good), indicating thoroughness, appropriate tool selection, strategic planning, and successful execution by the agent. Furthermore, we analyzed the Copilot's handling of user queries and found that its planning and execution phases operate smoothly and efficiently, culminating in high-quality radiology reports. The entire process aligns closely with real radiologists' workflows, thereby enhancing efficiency in clinical practice.
