When users engage in online shopping, their queries often encompass multiple attributes simultaneously, such as brand, price range, functionality, or target audience. Consequently, it is imperative for the system to process and integrate these attributes collectively, rather than in isolation, and to structure them coherently. This enables the system to determine appropriate responses to user queries and effectively align their requests with relevant items. Furthermore, it is essential that the system does not solely depend on the user’s query to calibrate the weighting of various data modalities (e.g., textual or visual data). Instead, the system must analyze the extracted attribute-level details within the query and align them with the corresponding details of the items, subsequently determining how to utilize this alignment in generating recommendations.   In essence, the system operates by establishing a correspondence between the user’s query and the retrieved items. Initially, the system deconstructs the query into discrete attributes and employs these attributes to filter and select relevant items. The process is composed of two primary stages: (1) ensuring accurate interpretation of the query and its alignment with item attributes and (2) selecting appropriate items based on this alignment. A significant challenge lies in the complexity and ambiguity inherent in these stages. For instance, in a search for "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set," the system must discern the user’s intent and accurately map the query components to the relevant product features. The core challenge resides in the precise alignment of the attributes within the query to those of the items, ensuring an optimized recommendation process.
