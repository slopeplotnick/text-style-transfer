Research on truthful artificial intelligence (AI) is centered on developing AI systems capable of providing accurate information about the real world. Previous studies have proposed methodologies to enhance AI truthfulness, such as identifying behavioral patterns in AI that align with factual accuracy and modifying AI decision-making processes. Discussions have also emerged around balancing the trade-off between AI truthfulness and utility. The concept of honest AI focuses on ensuring that AI systems do not intentionally convey falsehoods contrary to their "beliefs" merely to achieve rewards, addressing issues related to AI deception. Conversely, truth-seeking AI involves analyzing evidence and discovering novel information, a feature likely to gain significance as AI systems become more autonomous, determining the information to pursue based on situational comprehension. The objective of truth-seeking AI is to ascertain truth, which can be assessed through various methodologies, including Bayesian approaches or maintaining consistency in reasoning.  Belief entrenchment refers to the tendency to adhere to pre-existing beliefs, even when faced with contradictory evidence. This phenomenon is akin to confirmation bias, where individuals tend to seek, recall, or interpret information in a manner that corroborates their pre-existing beliefs. However, confirmation bias, which pertains to the information processing in specific contexts, presents challenges in measurement. In psychological research, the quantification of confirmation bias is highly task-dependent and often lacks reliability. Accurately measuring confirmation bias necessitates an understanding of an individual's existing beliefs and a robust method of assessing them, posing significant challenges in the field.
