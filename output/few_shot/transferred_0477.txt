The system's performance is evaluated across four simulated test scenarios comprising 64 tasks and five real-world tasks, examining three categories of VLA policies. For the simulations, the benchmarks include Simpler, Libero, Robotwin1.0, and Robotwin2.0. Specifically, Simpler employs the SAPIEN simulator and ManiSkill2 framework, focusing primarily on tasks designed for the WindowX platform, despite also encompassing tasks related to the Google Robot platform. Libero addresses lifelong learning within decision-making tasks, consisting of four task sets; this work concentrates on the most challenging task set, as prior research has achieved notable success on the others. Robotwin1.0 and Robotwin2.0 involve dual-arm gripper systems for task execution and automated data collection.  In the real-world evaluation, the experiments are conducted using a REALMAN75 dual-arm robotic system within an office-like environment, incorporating five tasks utilizing commonly encountered objects such as books, pens, a cabinet, a charger, a laptop, and a paper ball. The tasks include: (1) **Receive Book**, wherein the robot retrieves a book from an individual and replaces it on a shelf; (2) **Storage Charger**, involving the collection of a charger, placing it within a cabinet, and closing the cabinet door; (3) **Paper and Pen**, which requires disposing of a paper ball into the trash bin and placing the pen in the pen holder; (4) **Laptop**, whereby the robot closes a laptop, unplugs its charger, and stores it in the cabinet; and (5) **Pick Books**, necessitating the simultaneous use of both arms to pick up and lift a book.
