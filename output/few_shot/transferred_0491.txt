Our approach integrates label-driven Chain of Thought (CoT) generation with the identification of challenging cases, thereby enhancing the model's capacity for step-by-step reasoning and tackling difficult scenarios. We evaluated the model's efficacy by concentrating on complex cases to assess its capability to navigate intricate situations and perform advanced reasoning tasks. Crucially, we developed an online CoT-based knowledge framework with the capability to transfer to smaller models, marking a significant advancement in enhancing deep reasoning in practical applications.  The project encountered two primary challenges: firstly, deciphering product information from images with minimal textual content, and secondly, ensuring decision-making processes adhered to stringent regulations and business standards. To address these issues, we employed a strategy enabling the model to comprehend both images and textual data, while maintaining alignment with business regulations. Our methodology amalgamated CoT Generation, Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), and Reinforcement Learning rooted in the Qwen-2.5 Large Language Model (LLM) for textual reasoning, and Qwen-2.5 Vision-Language (VL) model for visual analysis. This configuration empowered the model to perform both visual and textual reasoning, thus overcoming the limitations inherent in utilizing a single data modality.  Our deployment of multimodal and rule-aware CoT generation involved integrating the comprehension of images and text with standardized prompts. We embedded business regulations and exemplar cases into the CoT reasoning process to enhance the model’s rule comprehension. Subsequently, reinforcement learning was employed to optimize the model's proficiency in applying rules to decision-making processes in complex scenarios.
