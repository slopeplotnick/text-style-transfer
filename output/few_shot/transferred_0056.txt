The model improves by learning from its mistakes; however, incorporating an excessive number of unrelated negative examples can destabilize the learning process. It has been observed that utilizing Off-Policy Sequence Masking contributes to maintaining stability in training scenarios that would otherwise be precarious. Keep Routing Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models operate more efficiently by selectively employing certain expert components during inference. Nevertheless, discrepancies between inference and training procedures, alongside policy modifications, may lead to inconsistent expert utilization even with identical inputs. This inconsistency can abruptly alter active parameters, disrupting optimization and exacerbating off-policy issues. To address this, expert routing paths utilized during inference are preserved and ensured to be consistent during training, optimizing the same expert parameters. This Keep Routing technique is vital for maintaining the stability of MoE models during reinforcement learning (RL) training and has been implemented in our RL training since the advent of DeepSeek-V3-0324. DeepSeek-R1 demonstrated that deliberation significantly enhances a model's ability to solve challenging problems. Consequently, this insight prompted the integration of deliberative skills into tool-calling scenarios. Adhering to DeepSeek-R1's methodology—discarding reasoning content upon receiving subsequent messages—results in substantial token wastage, forcing the model to reprocess the entire problem for each new tool invocation. To mitigate this, a specialized context management system was developed for tool-calling scenarios, as depicted in Figure 4: - Old reasoning content is discarded only when a new user message is received. If solely tool-related messages (such as tool outputs) are appended, the reasoning content is retained throughout the interaction. - When reasoning traces are removed, the history of tool calls and their outcomes is maintained within the context.
