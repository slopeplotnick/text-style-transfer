We developed a model termed Story2MIDI, capable of generating music that aligns with the emotional undertones of textual narratives. The construction of this model involved the integration of datasets that evaluate emotional content in both text and music, with each dataset associating brief textual excerpts with corresponding musical pieces that evoke similar emotional responses. Despite the constraints of a limited dataset and computational resources, the model effectively learned to identify emotional features within music and utilize these features to generate new compositions exhibiting diverse emotional contexts. The results were evaluated using music-specific metrics and subjective human assessment, confirming the model’s proficiency in accurately reflecting intended emotional cues. In contemporary times, the demand for entertainment mediums such as music, audiobooks, and podcasts has surged, driven by the rapid expansion of social media and streaming platforms. A significant portion of this entertainment revolves around narratives, as observed in films, games, podcasts, and documentaries. Narratives excel at evoking emotional responses by engaging audiences with characters, plots, and thematic elements, thus enhancing their memorability. Similarly, music possesses a profound ability to influence emotions, serving as a well-established medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creators frequently incorporate background music into narratives to deepen emotional engagement and enhance the audience's immersive experience. With the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence, novel models are continuously being developed to generate diverse content forms.
