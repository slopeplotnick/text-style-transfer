DeepSeek-V3.2 demonstrates comparable performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across a range of reasoning benchmarks. Furthermore, it significantly enhances open model performance in specific agent-centered tasks, particularly those evaluated by EvalSys and other studies conducted in 2025. Notably, DeepSeek-V3.2 is a cost-effective alternative for agent tasks, narrowing the performance disparity between open-source and top-tier private models while remaining economically advantageous. To further improve reasoning capabilities in open models, the DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale variant was developed through the relaxation of length constraints. This enhanced version achieves parity with the leading closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro, and excels in prestigious competitions such as the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI), International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) World Finals, International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), and Canadian Mathematical Olympiad (CMO) in 2025.  Structurally, DeepSeek-V3.2 shares the same foundational framework as DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, diverging only from its predecessor, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, through the incorporation of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) and extended training procedures. DSA comprises two integral components: a high-speed indexer and an advanced token selection mechanism. Building on the longer context length architecture of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which allows a contextual window of up to 128K, additional training was undertaken to refine the model. This training involved two stages, utilizing datasets akin to those employed for the extended context data of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp was rigorously evaluated against a variety of benchmarks and compared to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, revealing comparable outcomes. Notably, DeepSeek V3.2-Exp demonstrated superior efficiency in processing extended sequences while maintaining competitive performance across both short- and long-context tasks.
