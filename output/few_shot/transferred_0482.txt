Ensuring consistency in theme colors across all slides, standardizing layouts and formats for related slides, and translating slide content while preserving charts and overall structure are critical tasks in PowerPoint editing. As we understand, PPTArena is the pioneering tool that conceptualizes PPT editing as a step-by-step process anchored in the semantic understanding of the deck. It offers detailed information for each element, style objectives, and error checks to ensure accuracy in edits, utilizing instructions and visual assessments to provide diverse editing methodologies while maintaining task clarity. Accompanying this tool is PPTPilot, an intelligent editing tool designed for intricate PPT modifications. PPTPilot decomposes each instruction into granular steps, selecting between using APIs like python-pptx or editing XML files directly, and verifies outcomes against task objectives. Its functionality is rooted in two principal concepts: structure-based planning, which involves analyzing slide templates, placeholders, shapes, text, and visual data before editing, and precise execution, where XML edits and strict regulations dictate fonts, colors, positions, and template alterations while automated tools address repetitive tasks such as translations and style normalization. A cyclical process of planning, editing, and validation, along with XML and visual assessments, enhances its capability in managing complex and extended edits. We evaluated various tools on PPTArena, including sophisticated PPT tools like ChatGPT Agent and MiniMax Agent, visual-language models, and simpler iterations of PPTPilot. Despite having detailed instructions and tool access, existing systems encounter difficulties in maintaining instructional fidelity while executing aesthetically pleasing and functional edits for complex, multi-step tasks. They frequently experience errors when edits require the integration of slide-specific links or template-level style modifications, which our tool effectively underscores. Conversely, PPTPilot demonstrates superior performance, surpassing advanced tools and models by over 10 percentage points on complex edits, while also excelling in simpler tasks. Nevertheless, even PPTPilot and other tools encounter challenges with highly complex tasks reliant on visual elements, highlighting the demanding nature of PPTArena and the significant potential for future advancements.
