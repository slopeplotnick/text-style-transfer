The use of reasoning strategies to enhance the performance of large language models (LLMs) across diverse tasks is becoming increasingly prevalent. Initially, this enhancement was achieved through methodologies such as Chain-of-Thought (CoT) techniques, wherein models articulate their thought processes prior to providing answers (Wei et al., 2022). More recently, reinforcement learning (RL) has been employed to further refine these cognitive processes, particularly in domains such as mathematics and coding where solution verification is feasible. In these scenarios, RL frameworks reward models for accurate outcomes (Shao et al., 2024; Lambert et al., 2025).   Emerging studies have extended the application of RL to more subjective tasks, such as narrative generation and comprehension, where answer validation is less straightforward (Ahuja et al., 2025; Gurung & Lapata, 2025). However, a significant drawback of RL implementation is its potential to slow down processes and increase computational costs due to the added cognitive demands on models (Shen et al., 2025a). Consequently, efforts are being directed towards accelerating and optimizing reasoning processes both during training and deployment phases (Sui et al., 2025).  Among these efforts is the concept of latent reasoning, where models generate thoughts in a concise, encoded format rather than explicit textual representations. This approach leverages the principle that not all linguistic expressions are necessary for deriving solutions, akin to the human cognitive capability of abstract thinking. Encoded representations enable models to retain extensive information without the loss that accompanies verbalization. For instance, Hao et al. (2025) illustrate that such encoded reasoning can integrate multiple reasoning steps concurrently, effectively solving complex problems in a parallelized manner.  In this context, we introduce LiteReason, a novel latent reasoning framework designed to facilitate complex tasks such as story comprehension and composition, characterized by extensive input-output sequences. LiteReason incorporates a unique module within the model, referred to as the Reasoning Projector, which facilitates the generation of both conventional text and encoded thoughts.
