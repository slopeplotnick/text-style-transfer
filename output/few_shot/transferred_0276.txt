This study examines the assessment of the risk associated with suicidal ideation. A notable challenge emerged when a Reddit post, intended to be supportive and offer assistance, was incorrectly classified by the Menta model as indicative of behavioral issues. This misclassification was attributed to the post's extensive length and formatting irregularities, including unconventional symbols, HTML content, and placeholder terms such as "Hyperactive behavior." These elements contributed to the model's confusion, resulting in the interpretation of the post as behavior-related rather than supportive. The post itself was an exhortation for an individual to persist in life for their younger siblings and continue efforts to prevent paternal abuse. Additionally, the post advised disregarding derogatory comments from trolls and reassured the individual of their worth, explicitly countering notions of self-harm or disposability. The misattribution of a behavior label was linked to the post's discussion of overwhelming responsibilities and difficulties in academic and social contexts, although the individual cited learning to decline excessive obligations as beneficial for their well-being and social integration.
