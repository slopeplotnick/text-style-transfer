Despite significant advancements in the generation of sound and music through computational means, a standardized framework for the evaluation of such systems remains elusive. Beyond subjective auditory assessments, certain researchers have proposed objective methodologies to evaluate the fidelity of audio recreation and the ability of these systems to classify music into predefined categories. In our study, we employed both auditory tests and objective measures derived from prior research to assess the performance of our symbolic music generation system. We generated 100 MIDI samples for each segment of our test set (GoEmotions) and evaluated the outcomes. Our preliminary auditory evaluation aimed to determine the congruence between the music generated by our model and the emotions conveyed in the text. Participants were asked to listen to the music and provide feedback. This task is more subjective compared to typical music evaluation tasks due to the requirement for listeners to first interpret the emotion in the narrative and then assess the emotional alignment of the music. Initially, each participant was presented with a text excerpt and tasked with selecting the most appropriate of four emotional categories. Subsequently, they were presented with two musical options corresponding to the narrative and asked to select the one they perceived as most accurately reflecting the narrative's emotional tone. This dual-phase methodology enabled us to assess both the consensus on textual emotions and the alignment between these emotions and the accompanying music. Given that not all musical selections corresponded precisely to the emotion category identified by the participant, we evaluated the music's emotional alignment based on dimensions of positivity/negativity and energy levels (high/low) rather than discrete categories. Each category was further delineated into dimensions of positive or negative affect and high or low energy. For each participant's selection, we assessed whether the music's emotional qualities aligned with their interpretation of the narrative's emotion along these dimensions. This approach provided a nuanced metric for evaluating the correspondence between the emotional tone of the music and that of the narrative, leveraging positivity/negativity and energy levels for a more flexible assessment of emotional congruence.
