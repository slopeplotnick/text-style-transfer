The methodology section details the participants, the interview setup and process, as well as the analysis of responses. Participants were selected to represent diverse perspectives within the project based on their roles, levels of experience, and educational backgrounds. Four distinct roles were included: Product Owners, Developers, Agronomists, and User Interface/Experience designers. Educational qualifications varied across participants, ranging from engineering degrees to doctoral degrees, and experience levels within the project also differed. A total of 16 individuals participated in the study: nine Developers currently or previously involved in the project, two Agronomists, three Product Owners, and two User Interface/Experience designers. In this study, participants were informed that their feedback would contribute to refining the Software Product Line (SPL) process, thus incentivizing engagement. Structured interviews were conducted, employing a predefined set of questions designed to elicit clear, focused responses. Consistent with established guidelines, questions were articulated using straightforward language, each addressing a specific concept. Sensitive topics, retrospective inquiries, and questions outside the participant’s expertise were consciously avoided. This approach facilitated actor-driven interviews.  The interview design comprised three sections. The initial section involved participant introductions, providing context to their perspectives. The core section focused on two themes: the current process and proposed changes to the workflow. The concluding segment allowed for reflections on the interview process and closing remarks. This structured approach ensured methodical gathering of insights essential for the study.
