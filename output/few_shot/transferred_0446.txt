The interviews encompassed the following inquiries: 1) Which aspects of your cognitive process are challenging to articulate during creative endeavors? 2) Among the four principal components, which one most significantly facilitates your creativity? 3) Are there any obstacles you encounter, and what strategies do you employ to overcome them? 4) Do novel ideas emerge during your creative process? Participants (P1–P4) expressed a preference for constructing narratives using a drag-and-drop card mechanism over textual outlines, as they perceived it to be more engaging. However, some participants encountered difficulties with the repetitive task of editing card details (P1, P4). P1 noted, "When crafting a story, I initially contemplate the overarching structure, thus commencing with event and entity cards to establish the narrative. Subsequently, I append details to the event and entity cards." P4 remarked, "I frequently needed to make revisions during creation. If the process of editing details were streamlined, I would be inclined to use this method more extensively." Additionally, some participants proposed that certain narrative elements could be conveyed through interactive modalities (P2, P3). P2 expressed, "I prefer positioning entity cards associated with the same event atop the event cards. This arrangement allows me to readily identify the entities involved in an event." P3 suggested, "Incorporating relationship cards to connect various entity cards would be beneficial. If event cards were interconnected with lines, it would more effectively illustrate the sequence and progression of events." Drawing from these insights, we identify key interaction patterns (Figure 2) and propose the following three design objectives: - DG1: The system should enable users to input detailed information for each primary component, thereby facilitating the construction of an initial storyline outline. - DG2: The system should assist users in comprehending the overall narrative configuration, enhancing the visibility of connections between entities and events. - DG3: The system should provide feedback and targeted recommendations to refine the narrative architecture.
