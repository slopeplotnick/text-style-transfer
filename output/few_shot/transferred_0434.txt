This section delineates the methodology employed in our system setup, encompassing dataset creation, judge model configuration, and human validation procedures. Our objective is to bridge Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification through a comprehensive examination. Initially, we outline the study design and experimental setup. Subsequently, we address three primary research questions concerning the efficacy of these methodologies, their potential synergistic interactions, and their combinability. To investigate the integration of Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification, we conducted an extensive study aimed at evaluating their performance within a unified framework. Our research questions are as follows:   1. What is the comparative performance of leading Hallucination Detection and Fact Verification methods when applied to identical AI-generated content? Does one method consistently outperform the other? 2. Do these methodologies exhibit distinct proficiencies in identifying factual inaccuracies, or are their capabilities largely overlapping? 3. Can an enhanced system be developed through the integration of both methods, surpassing the effectiveness of individual approaches?  The study was conducted in three phases. Initially, to address Question 1, a range of prominent methodologies from both domains were subjected to direct comparative analysis. This represents the inaugural comparative evaluation of this nature. Following this, insights gained were utilized to explore the collaborative efficacy of the two approaches in Question 2. Our findings demonstrate that integrating Hallucination Detection with Fact Verification yields superior results compared to employing either method in isolation. An in-depth case study was conducted to further elucidate Question 2. Finally, given their complementary nature, we explored Question 3 by developing and assessing two straightforward integration strategies. Experiments were conducted at both the final score level and the system level, revealing that even basic integration techniques can significantly enhance performance.
