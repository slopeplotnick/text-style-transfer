Based on Taobao search logs, we first categorize the data into industries such as consumer electronics and apparel, and perform stratified sampling according to the proportion of each industry. This step ensures that the training data cover a diverse range of attributes and features across all industries. Next, we divide the data within each industry into high, medium, and low-frequency categories based on query frequency, and conduct sampling in proportion to these classifications. This guarantees that the dataset includes both high-frequency queries and long-tail, more challenging samples. The data is then assigned to specialized annotators for labeling. During the data cleaning process, errors typically arise from two sources: first, annotators may overlook certain details, especially in the case of long, multi-attribute queries; second, the annotation guidelines may lack clarity, leaving gaps in their coverage of edge cases. To address these issues, we utilize advanced language models for filtering. The first model enforces strict attribute alignment, excelling at capturing fine-grained details and outperforming manual annotation in complex, multi-attribute scenarios. The second model incorporates comprehensive annotation rules into its reasoning, identifying and eliminating instances that fall outside the scope of rule coverage. By employing both models, we leverage their complementary strengths—one excels at handling extended textual inputs, while the other is proficient in interpreting diverse data modalities. To further enhance the model's reasoning capabilities, we propose a structured, three-stage process: Knowledge Injection & Reasoning, Multimodal Comprehension, and Rule Awareness. The Knowledge Injection & Reasoning stage decomposes user preferences into measurable attributes, which can then be compared with product features. The subsequent stages—Multimodal Comprehension and Rule Awareness—evaluate the extent to which items satisfy the query requirements based on item attributes and predefined criteria.
