In Case 3, the model demonstrates multimodal understanding through joint text-image discrimination. This enables coordinated reasoning between textual and visual information, benefiting from the relevance-oriented caption generation strategy (Section 3.3), which allows the model to extract task-critical information from images. Through rule-aware CoT modeling (Section 3.3), the model has internalized such domain-specific rules, allowing it to identify and appropriately apply them in accordance with business logic. To address the latency bottleneck imposed by real-time computation, we adopted a hybrid deployment strategy combining offline pre-computation with online caching. A batch of high-frequency query-item pairs was sampled from search logs, processed through offline inference using the LLM, and the resulting outputs were retained for subsequent utilization.
