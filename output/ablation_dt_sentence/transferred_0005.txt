We introduce *promptolution*, a modular, lightweight, and extensible open-source framework for automatic prompt optimization in Python. The system has undergone rigorous testing and demonstrates stable performance across a wide range of use cases. It supports multiple language models, NLP tasks, and contemporary prompt optimization methods, allowing components to be flexibly interchanged or extended with custom implementations. While *promptolution* supports single-prompt optimization for practitioners, its low abstraction level and support for systematic, reproducible experimentation also make it well-suited for researchers. Automatic prompt optimization refers to the systematic refinement of prompts using algorithmic strategies, targeting both instructional text and few-shot examples (Cui et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025; Wan et al., 2024). Optimization methods are typically classified as either continuous (Li and Liang, 2021; Lester et al., 2021; Qin and Eisner, 2021), which operate over numerical embeddings, or discrete (Guo et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2023), which modify the textual content directly. For a comprehensive overview, we refer readers to Li et al. (2025) and Cui et al. (2025). *promptolution* focuses on LLM-agnostic discrete optimization, which is both interpretable and broadly applicable. It incrementally refines prompts by employing a meta-language model to propose improved variants. The framework currently supports three optimization strategies: (1) OPRO (Yang et al., 2024), which uses a meta-LLM to generate refined instructions based on task descriptions, examples, and prior candidates; (2) EvoPrompt (Guo et al., 2024), which employs genetic algorithms and differential evolution, with the meta-LLM executing mutation and crossover operations; and (3) CAPO (Zehle et al., 2025), a recent AutoML-based method that improves cost-efficiency while jointly optimizing instructions and examples, outperforming the prior discrete methods in both effectiveness and efficiency.
