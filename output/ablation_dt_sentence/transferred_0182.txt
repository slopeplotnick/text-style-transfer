These languages were selected to represent a diverse range of language families, geographic regions, and levels of resource availability. A key consideration in multilingual tasks is the choice of system prompt language—whether to default to English, the predominant training language for most models, or to match the prompt language with that of the target task. To explore this, we compare two settings. In the English-prompt setting, prompts are written in English for questions in all languages, whereas in the same-language setting, the prompt is translated to match the language of the corresponding question. **Performance Analysis** To better understand how different aspects of model behavior relate, we examine the relationships among the four performance metrics in this section. Specifically, we analyze the impact of different components in the system prompt on model behavior using regression analysis, linking prompt elements and language configurations to multilingual performance metrics. Firstly, some components exhibit consistently positive effects on model behavior. This suggests their utility in guiding the model toward stable and high-quality performance across multilingual contexts. Moreover, upon closer inspection of the model outputs, we find that these components tend to elicit more structured and consistent reasoning, which likely underpins the observed performance gains.
