Objective Evaluation. ViSAudio's performance excels across multiple objective metrics on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution datasets, as evidenced in Table 2. It is compared with several state-of-the-art video-to-spatial-audio generation methods, with detailed descriptions of all compared techniques provided in Section 5.1. For the BiAudio and MUSIC test sets, both video and caption inputs are utilized, while for FAIR-Play, which lacks captions, only video inputs are provided. Models without text-processing capability are assessed using video inputs exclusively. The results in Table 2 show that ViSAudio outperforms other binaural-audio generation methods in terms of overall quality, spatial channel distribution matching, audio-visual synchrony, and semantic alignment. This underscores the model's ability to generate high-fidelity binaural audio while maintaining audio-visual coherence across diverse acoustic conditions. Notably, its strong performance on the out-of-distribution FAIR-Play dataset, which was not included in the training data, demonstrates robust generalization in novel scenarios. Figure 3 presents a comparative analysis of video-conditioned binaural spatial audio generation. The example illustrates a person playing the sitar, with the camera moving from left to right, thereby causing the sound source to shift from right to left in the video. The spectrograms of the generated binaural audio from our method, ViSAudio, are compared with the ground truth and other baselines. Our method produces audio most consistent with the ground truth and effectively reflects spatial changes in the sound source. The boxed regions indicate that when the sound source is located on the left, the left-channel spectrogram exhibits higher energy. This aligns with the increased loudness perceived by the left ear and matches the ground-truth spatial audio, demonstrating our model's capacity to adapt to changes in viewpoint. In contrast, other methods generate incorrect rhythms and fail to reflect any left-right channel differences.
