In this paper, we introduce Story2MIDI, a model capable of generating symbolic music aligned with the emotional content of textual input. To develop this model, we construct a dataset by integrating existing resources for emotion analysis in both text and music. The resulting dataset comprises paired textual segments and musical excerpts designed to elicit equivalent emotional responses in both readers and listeners. Although our dataset is limited in size and our computational resources are constrained, our results indicate that the model effectively captures emotion-relevant features in music and incorporates them into the generation process, producing samples with diverse emotional qualities. We evaluate the generated music using both objective music-related metrics and a human listening study, which together confirm the model’s ability to align the output with the intended emotional cues. In parallel, the growing popularity of social media and streaming platforms has significantly increased the demand for entertainment, including music, audiobooks, and podcasts. This trend reflects a broader societal shift toward heightened consumption of multimedia content. At the core of many of these media forms lies a narrative—a story that drives the experience, whether in films, video games, podcasts, or documentaries. Narratives are powerful in evoking emotional responses through the progression of characters, plot developments, and thematic elements. The emotional arc within a story plays a critical role in shaping its impact on the audience. Similarly, music exerts a profound influence on emotional states and is widely recognized as a medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creative producers often integrate background music into narrative media, as it amplifies emotional resonance and enhances the immersive experience for audiences. With the rapid advancement of AI technologies, novel models are being developed that can generate content across diverse modalities.
