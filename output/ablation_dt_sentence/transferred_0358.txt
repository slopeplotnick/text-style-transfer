DeepSeek-V3.2 demonstrates comparable performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across various reasoning benchmarks. Moreover, it shows significant advancements in the agentic capabilities of open models, particularly excelling on the long-tail agent tasks introduced in EvalSys (2025) and other studies from the same year. As a highly cost-efficient alternative in agentic scenarios, DeepSeek-V3.2 effectively narrows the performance gap between open and leading proprietary models, all while incurring substantially lower costs. In pursuit of pushing the boundaries of open models in reasoning, we developed DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale by relaxing the length constraints. This version achieves performance parity with the leading closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro, and excels in prestigious competitions such as the IOI, ICPC World Final, IMO, and CMO, as observed in 2025. DeepSeek-V3.2 uses the same architecture as DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, with the only architectural modification from DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus being the incorporation of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) through extended training. The DSA prototype consists of two main components: a lightning-fast indexer and a fine-grained token selection mechanism. Starting from the DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus checkpoint, which features an extended context length of 128K, we conducted additional training in two stages to develop DeepSeek-V3.2, using the same data type as the extended context data from DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, we evaluated DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp on a diverse set of benchmarks, comparing its performance with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which yielded comparable results. While DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp significantly enhances computational efficiency on long sequences, it maintains similar performance to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus on both short- and long-context tasks.
