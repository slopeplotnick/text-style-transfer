Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have enabled the unification of task-oriented and open-domain dialogue systems. This section of the tutorial covers this technological shift and highlights several promising future directions. These include building new foundation LLMs pretrained with conversational data, end-to-end training of dialogue systems, and leveraging dialogue self-play techniques. Another important direction is the development of situated dialogue systems, which ground LLM-based conversations in heterogeneous content sources—such as web pages, structured lists, online forms, tabular data, and scientific publications with figures and equations—as well as other web-based documents. Moreover, these systems aim to integrate multimodal signals spanning vision, external knowledge, and affective states, including expressive cues like facial dynamics and gestural behaviors.
