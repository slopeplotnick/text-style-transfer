Several studies have explored more structured approaches to prompt generation. Plan-and-Solve (Wang et al., 2023c), for example, divides tasks into a planning phase and a solution phase, yielding stronger performance on mathematical and logical tasks. Similarly, Prompt Chaining (Chen et al., 2023) examines whether chaining intermediate summaries or steps improves performance in text summarization. Complexity-based Prompting (Fu et al., 2023) argues that task complexity should guide prompt selection, aligning with our focus on analyzing prompt specificity. While most studies emphasize task format or exemplar inclusion, our work uniquely concentrates on the degree of prompt specificity, treating it as a continuous dimension that systematically varies in detail and structure. In recent years, several methods for automated or human-in-the-loop prompt refinement have emerged. Black-box optimization methods (Diao et al., 2023) adjust prompts based on task-specific feedback without requiring access to model gradients. Prompt Engineering a Prompt Engineer (Zhou et al., 2023) demonstrates that large language models can be prompted to refine their own prompts, introducing a metaprompting strategy that parallels our generalization loop. Additionally, Prompt Refinement with Image Pivots (Lu et al., 2023) utilizes visual features to iteratively refine textual prompts, enhancing image generation. Although our work focuses solely on text, we share the aim of adjusting prompt representations to improve output fidelity. In contrast to these studies, DETAIL primarily emphasizes the role of textual specificity in influencing model reasoning, rather than examining semantic drift or integrating textual and non-textual data. Reviews such as those by Liu et al. (2023) have surveyed reasoning-focused prompting techniques, uncovering a growing taxonomy of prompt formats and strategies. However, few studies investigate how the level of detail in prompts influences model behavior across diverse tasks and systems. While approaches like CoT and Plan-and-Solve highlight the importance of prompt content, they do not provide a comprehensive analysis of its specific nuances or implications.
