We explained in Section 3 how the questions map to four main themes corresponding to the research objectives. SPLE was unfamiliar to most interviewees, so we explained its concepts and significance during the interviews. This may have introduced a potential bias in the process; however, we trust our interviewees to form their own opinions on the subject and the interviewer to avoid exerting undue influence. The questions were intentionally open-ended to encourage in-depth responses and capture a broad spectrum of ideas. They were deliberately constructed to avoid restricting the range of potential answers. The metrics were designed to maximize the informational value of the responses and extract meaningful insights. Specifically, they quantify key terms and their frequency, both in aggregate and by role. Accordingly, the tables present complete data, while the figures illustrate the distribution of values. Even unique responses, provided by a single participant, were preserved and factored into the analysis to ensure thorough conceptual coverage. We conducted a comprehensive review of all responses. Terms extracted from the interviews were consolidated and aligned with established SPLE terminology, such as "personalisation" and "making specific some parts of the code," which map to the concept of "customization." This alignment helped to ensure consistency across the results. Moreover, it enabled us to identify relevant arguments in the literature to address participants' concerns. Repeating this process with various individuals improved the overall fluidity of the interviews. Although the questions themselves remained unchanged, the manner in which they were presented evolved over time. For instance, we began offering additional cues and elaborations when interviewees appeared uncertain and refined transitions between questions to enhance coherence. Nevertheless, we believe that the quantity and quality of information collected from both the first and last questionnaires remained consistent.
