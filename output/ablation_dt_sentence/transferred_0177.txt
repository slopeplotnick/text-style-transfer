MODOMA is a fully parametrized system designed to conduct language acquisition experiments, allowing for precise control over each experiment through the specification of predefined settings. It encompasses all aspects of language acquisition, including the adult, the daughter, and the linguistic processes involved, with parameters that can be adjusted for each component. Table 1 presents an overview of the parameters employed in the current experiments. MODOMA is capable of executing various language learning processes, as demonstrated in Shakouri et al. (2025). The first parameter listed in Table 1, *Acquisition of functional categories*, specifies whether functional categories can be acquired by the learner. In all experiments discussed in this paper, this parameter is enabled. The remaining parameters define critical properties of the experiments. For instance, the configuration of the mother agent determines the structure of Delilah’s utterances, specifying whether sentences or noun phrases are generated. This parameter is systematically varied across both training and test experiments. Similarly, the minimum amount of data processed by the daughter agent before acquiring functional and content categories differs across experiments. These two settings define the experimental framework. Specifically, in the training experiments, functional and content categories are acquired after processing 1,000 noun phrases and 1,000 sentences, with reinforcement occurring after exposure to 10,000 noun phrases and 10,000 sentences. This setup enables the determination of how many exemplars from Delilah are necessary to detect the pattern. Furthermore, the threshold for functional category acquisition parameter specifies the type-token ratio used to distinguish between function and content words, transforming these distributional distinctions into linguistic knowledge. The training experiments aim to determine optimal values for these parameters, while the test experiments evaluate the selected settings using new datasets generated by Delilah.
