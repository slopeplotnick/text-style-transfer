As shown in Figure 9, the Menta interface supports a range of classification tasks, including stress, depression (both binary and severity-based), and suicide risk (both binary and categorical). Upon clicking the "Start Evaluation" button, social media posts are processed through the selected model, and the system displays the predicted class labels, their correctness, and the corresponding evaluation results. Model performance was assessed across six distinct tasks, with dataset sizes varying from 10 to 3,000 posts depending on task complexity and data availability. Our analysis highlights a clear trade-off between model speed and accuracy when comparing Menta to other systems. Menta stands out for its balanced performance, offering high accuracy, fast processing speeds, and low latency, making it well-suited for real-world applications where responsiveness is as critical as precision. As depicted in Figure 9, the chart represents three key metrics: task processing speed (ITPS), time to first result (TTFT), and model accuracy. Each circle in the chart represents a model, with the circle size reflecting memory consumption (RAM). Falcon prioritizes speed, achieving rapid task initiation and fast TTFT, but sacrifices accuracy, resulting in lower predictive performance. On the other hand, StableLM delivers superior accuracy but at the cost of increased latency and resource consumption, leading to higher deployment costs and slower response times. In contrast, Menta maintains high accuracy—except for Task 5, where StableLM and Phi-4 Mini marginally outperform it—while achieving faster processing speeds and greater efficiency compared to StableLM. Therefore, Menta provides high-quality results swiftly while maintaining a low memory footprint, which is essential for systems supporting large user bases. Ultimately, Menta is positioned favorably for deployment in real-world environments, offering a balanced trade-off between performance and resource consumption, thus ensuring a cost-effective solution and a satisfactory user experience.
