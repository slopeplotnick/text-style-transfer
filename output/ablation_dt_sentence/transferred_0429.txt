The rapid advancement in artificial intelligence (AI) has significantly propelled developments in natural language processing (NLP), with narrative generation becoming a key area of research. The primary aim of story generation is to craft narratives that are coherent, engaging, and contextually appropriate, based on the input provided. However, existing systems continue to grapple with challenges, particularly in maintaining narrative consistency, developing robust character arcs, and effectively managing narrative progression. Specifically, ensuring that the narrative aligns with user expectations while maintaining control over the integrity of the plot, character development, and structural coherence remains a critical issue. Thus, maintaining logical consistency and aligning with user expectations are paramount for preserving both the credibility and creativity of the narrative. Recent advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-4 and Llama-3 have showcased substantial proficiency in generating coherent textual content. Nevertheless, their training on general-purpose datasets limits their ability to produce stories that are sufficiently controlled and structured. This limitation is particularly evident when specific details and a well-defined narrative structure are desired by users. Therefore, enhancing the control mechanisms of these models to ensure stories adhere to user expectations while maintaining creativity has become crucial. Employing fine-tuning techniques and incorporating structured input prompts appears to be an effective strategy for refining these models' performance in story generation. This approach not only elevates story quality but also fortifies the model's stability and reliability in handling complex story generation tasks. The integration of fine-tuning with structured input prompts significantly enhances the predictability and consistency of narrative outputs, addressing the inherent challenges in controlling the story generation process.
