Although cov_tgsp and CSP consistently lead in performance (see Table 2 & Figure 3), notable differences emerge across datasets. A dataset-wise analysis reveals that, while CSP and cov_tgsp rank among the top performers in most cases, their effectiveness is notably diminished on the PhysionetMI dataset compared to Cho2017 and Zhou2016. This discrepancy can likely be attributed to variations in experimental protocols, such as differences in sampling rates, electrode configurations, and the number of subjects across studies. Our findings underscore that the effectiveness of feature extraction methods for motor imagery EEG decoding is heavily dependent on the dataset. For example, on Zhou2016, the combination of CSP with StandardScaler and logistic regression achieved the highest score (0.87), followed closely by cov_tgsp with RobustScaler and logistic regression (0.84). On Cho2017, cov_tgsp outperformed CSP, attaining a peak score of 0.74 versus 0.68. In contrast, methods based on instantaneous functional connectivity and non-linear features showed weaker performance across all datasets. For instance, svd_entropy combined with RobustScaler and logistic regression yielded scores of 0.68 on Zhou2016, 0.59 on Cho2017, and 0.54 on PhysionetMI. On the PhysionetMI dataset specifically, overall performance was suboptimal, with the best-performing pipeline (cov_tgsp) reaching only 0.64, while CSP averaged 0.62 across frequency bands. These results suggest that, although non-linear and connectivity-based features capture the complex dynamics of EEG signals, they may not offer superior discriminative power for motor imagery classification in this context. A subject-level analysis further supports the general superiority of CSP and cov_tgsp across datasets, as illustrated in Figures 5 and 6 for Cho2017 and Zhou2016. However, this trend becomes less consistent on the PhysionetMI dataset (Figure 7), where these methods do not consistently outperform alternative approaches. In several instances, alternative methods achieve higher performance: for example, Hjorth parameters outperform CSP and cov_tgsp for participants #57 and #59, while the instantaneous connectivity approach combined with TGSP yields the highest scores for participants #17 and #73.
