We propose Radiologist Copilot, a tool designed to assist radiologists in automating radiology reporting with quality control through an agentic framework that orchestrates various tools. The system, powered by a sophisticated reasoning backbone, autonomously selects tools, formulates plans, and executes actions, effectively emulating the behavior of radiologists to complete the entire reporting process. This toolset includes functions such as region localization, analysis planning, strategic template selection, quality assessment, and adaptive refinement based on feedback, all of which contribute to generating comprehensive radiology reports. Experimental results indicate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms other leading methods in radiology reporting. Automated reporting with quality control is particularly crucial for radiologists, given the time-consuming nature of processing 3D medical imaging modalities. CT2Rep utilizes an auto-regressive causal transformer architecture alongside a relational memory system to generate radiology reports from 3D chest CT images. Similarly, Reg2RG employs a region-guided referring and grounding framework for chest CT report generation. However, report generation is just one aspect of the broader reporting process and does not encompass the vital quality control measures. To address such tasks, Medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have been introduced for applications like radiology report generation (RRG) and visual question answering (VQA). In particular, 3D medical VLMs have shown great promise in processing 3D medical modalities such as CT and MRI. RadFM introduces a versatile model that integrates both 2D and 3D medical data, providing a unified approach for radiological analysis. M3D, on the other hand, presents a large-scale dataset and model, M3D-LaMed, for advancing multi-modal 3D medical image analysis. Merlin develops a 3D VLM that integrates structured and unstructured data for interpreting abdominal CT scans. CT-CHAT is a vision-language foundation model designed specifically for 3D chest CT volumes, combining the CT-CLIP vision encoder with a pre-trained language model. Med3DVLM enhances multimodal data representation with an efficient encoder and contrastive learning strategy. Lastly, Hulu-Med provides a generalist medical VLM that combines text, 2D and 3D images, and video within a single architecture.
