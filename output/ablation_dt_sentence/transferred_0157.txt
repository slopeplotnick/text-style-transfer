Moreover, we further evaluate OMNIGUARD in cross-modal safety scenarios, assessing its performance in situations that require reasoning across different media types. As illustrated in Figure 3, the OMNIGUARD family exhibits strong and consistent performance across various cross-modal safety benchmarks, including MM-SafetyBench, Video-SafetyBench, and AIAH. Compared to Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, OMNIGUARD-7B consistently outperforms it across all benchmarks, highlighting the robustness of our model in unified multimodal reasoning. Notably, OMNIGUARD-3B, despite its smaller parameter size, demonstrates competitive performance in several evaluations, approaching the capabilities of much larger models like Qwen3-VL-235B. This reinforces the notion that OMNIGUARD can effectively handle safety reasoning across multiple modalities, offering a viable solution for addressing safety concerns in diverse media contexts.
