The objective is to maximize code reuse and expedite the delivery of new applications by transitioning the code base into a higher-level Software Product Line (SPL), akin to Level 4 or 5. To facilitate this transition, we have initiated interviews with company personnel to evaluate their perception of SPL engineering. These interviews have been structured to address specific research questions, which will be detailed in the following section. Furthermore, another round of interviews will take place at the project's conclusion to refine the migration guidelines. Future phases of the migration will focus on variability analysis, beginning with assessing feature locations to develop feature models. Subsequently, examining the interactions and dependencies among these features will lead to a comprehensive system description. We conducted the interviews to identify perceptions of current practices, perceived benefits, potential risks, and prevailing concerns before migrating to an SPL. Research Question 1 seeks to identify the company's stakeholders' perceptions of current best practices in software development. Here, we aim to highlight views on effective existing practices. Research Question 2 delves into their perceptions of the future SPL's anticipated benefits, discussing its added value from an application development perspective. Research Question 3 addresses perceived risks associated with the new SPL. After discussing benefits, it is crucial to analyze risks encountered in daily professional activities. Research Question 4 focuses on elucidating the concerns induced by these identified risks, enabling an assessment of the company's personnel's fears regarding the migration project. These fears include potential adverse outcomes, challenges, and the necessity to address and manage identified risks systematically.
