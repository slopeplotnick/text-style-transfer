We incorporate a stepwise, iterative refinement procedure into PPTPilot, enabling the system to progressively enhance its predictive performance over successive prediction cycles. Specifically, the agent proposes modifications to the XML files, which are then rendered into a temporary PPT file for further processing. A verifier model evaluates the PPT against the original instructions and provides feedback in case of discrepancies. Based on this feedback, the agent generates an updated PPT revision and systematically corrects the identified errors. Despite the simplicity of our design, we find it to be both effective and efficient in PPT editing, outperforming existing tools in terms of functionality and performance. Consequently, we envision PPTPilot as a foundational tool for advancing research in the field of PPT editing. To ensure reliable evaluation, we rely on the strongest vision-language models, namely Gemini 2.5 Pro and GPT 5. For clarity, the majority of the results presented in the primary comparison (Table 3) are derived from GPT-5, although additional details regarding Gemini 2.5 Pro, along with other implementation-related information, are provided in the supplementary materials. Moreover, to evaluate performance comprehensively, we benchmark PPTPilot against a range of representative PPT editing agents, including ChatGPT operating in extended thinking mode, Gemini-CLI, GPT-Agent—which emulates a full computer environment with desktop interaction capabilities—MiniMax Agent, which excels at multimodal reasoning and is marketed as a "PPT helper," as well as PPTAgent and Poster2Agent. Due to the strict usage limitations and associated costs of certain tools, such as ChatGPT and MiniMax Agent, we conducted our experiments on a reduced subset of 25 samples to stay within budgetary constraints. This selection procedure targets the most challenging tasks, specifically those in which both PPTPilot and ChatGPT performed poorly, while also incorporating additional cases to ensure comprehensive scenario coverage. Further details regarding these cases are provided in the supplementary materials.
