Our methodology employed label-driven Chain-of-Thought (CoT) generation combined with the identification of challenging cases. Through this approach, we enhanced the model's reasoning capabilities by integrating label-constrained CoT synthesis with the excavation of difficult samples, thereby augmenting the cognitive processes through structured multi-step inference patterns and engagement with complex instances. To evaluate its efficacy, we implemented hardcase-focused evaluation protocols to specifically assess performance on intricate semantic scenarios, ensuring robust handling of complex reasoning requirements. This innovation facilitated the deployment of an online CoT-based multi-dimensional knowledge system, successfully distilled and transferred to smaller models, marking a significant milestone in scaling deep reasoning capabilities to production environments. Moreover, this achievement realized substantial performance improvements in online A/B testing scenarios. However, two paramount challenges were encountered: firstly, the extraction of product attributes from images that often contain minimal accompanying textual information; secondly, ensuring the decision-making process strictly adheres to several fine-grained rules and detailed business standards that align with observable attributes during the Path selection process. To address these challenges, we pursued a comprehensive technical strategy that integrated multimodal understanding capabilities with rule-aware reasoning mechanisms, effectively enabling the model to interpret visual information while maintaining strict adherence to business constraints. We employed a hybrid paradigm of CoT Generation, Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), and Reinforcement Learning (RL) grounded in both Qwen-2.5 LLM for text reasoning and Qwen-2.5 VL for multimodal understanding, thus enabling seamless integration of visual and textual reasoning pathways. Our methodology leveraged multimodal and rule-aware CoT generation, combining image-text paired understanding with standard-oriented prompt engineering. Furthermore, business rules and exemplar cases were embedded into CoT reasoning chains to establish rule comprehension logic. Finally, reinforcement learning was introduced to significantly enhance the model's generalized reasoning capabilities for applying active rules to Path judgments under complex attribute scenarios.
