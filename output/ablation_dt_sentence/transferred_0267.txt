To thoroughly evaluate the effectiveness of the Radiologist Copilot, we perform both agent-level and task-level assessments. For the task-level evaluation of radiology reporting, we utilize a combination of natural language generation (NLG) and clinical efficacy (CE) metrics. The NLG metrics include BLEU-1, ROUGE-L, METEOR, and BERTScore, while the CE metrics—F1-RadGraph and GREEN—measure clinical accuracy. In parallel, we conduct an agent-level evaluation using the LLM-as-a-Judge framework, with OpenAI GPT-5.1 acting as the LLM judge. This evaluation covers the entire task-handling process, considering four key dimensions: Analysis Process, Tool Selection, Action Planning, and Action Execution. Each dimension is rated on a five-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent). The Analysis Process assesses the thoroughness and logical consistency of the agent's task analysis, while Tool Selection evaluates the appropriateness of the tools chosen. Action Planning examines the reasonableness of the agent's plan, and Action Execution measures the effectiveness of the implementation. Furthermore, to gain a deeper understanding of the Radiologist Copilot’s performance, we perform ablation studies on its core components, testing them in isolation. The results from the tool-level evaluation show that most of the scores achieved the highest rating (5, Excellent), with a smaller proportion rated 4 (Good), indicating that the agent excels in task analysis, tool selection, planning, and execution. Additionally, the agent's functionality in addressing user queries demonstrates a seamless and efficient process, particularly in the planning and execution phases, which contribute to the generation of high-quality radiology reports. The entire process mirrors the practices of real-world radiologists, ultimately enhancing clinical efficiency and saving time in clinical workflows.
