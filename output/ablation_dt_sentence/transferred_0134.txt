The advent of AI agents enables autonomous reasoning, planning, and tool usage, with recent advancements seeing the deployment of medical agents to tackle complex and demanding tasks. For instance, MMedAgent utilizes a variety of tools to handle medical tasks, such as generating reports for chest X-rays (CXR). MedRAX presents a specialized framework for chest X-ray interpretation, integrating state-of-the-art CXR analysis tools for more accurate and efficient image assessment. CT-Agent, on the other hand, is an anatomy-aware and token-efficient agent designed for 3D CT analysis, facilitating effective reasoning over volumetric medical imaging data. However, these agents typically rely on a single tool for radiology reporting and lack integration, limiting the collaboration between tools. In contrast, the Radiologist Copilot distinguishes itself by employing a diverse array of tools that seamlessly integrate, enabling sophisticated planning and execution for more complex tasks. Radiology reporting is critical but can be time-consuming and prone to inaccuracies, especially when dealing with 3D imaging modalities such as CT and MRI. Furthermore, assessing report quality is an essential yet often tedious task, frequently overlooked in previous research. To address these challenges, we introduce the Radiologist Copilot, an AI assistant equipped with an orchestrated suite of tools for automated radiology reporting and integrated quality control, designed to support radiologists and enhance clinical efficiency. As illustrated in the accompanying figure, the Radiologist Copilot assists radiologists throughout the entire process of generating reports, improving both accuracy and efficiency. The process begins with the initialization of the tool library, followed by the use of the action planner and executor to formulate and execute tasks. The system incorporates a memory module to record actions and their outcomes, ensuring task completion verification. The action executor then generates and executes commands based on the next action determined by the planner, carrying out the task with the appropriate tool and deriving the result.
