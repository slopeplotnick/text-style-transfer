This paper fully leverages advanced AI tools to enhance both online strategies and system infrastructure. From a strategic standpoint, we incorporate the more reliable query-item relevance scores produced by the upgraded model into the downstream ranking module, deriving the optimal trade-off through Pareto optimization. From an infrastructure perspective, supported by the model's robust performance, we systematically retire or substitute the previously employed heuristic rules. This dual approach has led to substantial gains. For example, studies such as DeepSeek-R1 have observed the emergent phenomenon of long chains-of-thought and the model's "Aha Moment," where the model reaches accurate conclusions in unexpected ways. We similarly tracked the evolution of output length throughout training, with results illustrated in Fig. 17. The findings reveal that as the model improves, the output length exhibits a consistent decreasing trend and eventually stabilizes. This suggests that long chains-of-thought are not a necessary condition for enhancing model capability, but rather an emerging characteristic during training. Further analysis indicates that different task types require varying reasoning chain lengths. Tasks such as mathematical computation and code generation typically demand a more comprehensive, step-by-step approach, while relevance tasks, which focus on accurately identifying query intent and matching it to the right item, benefit from a more intuitive and direct reasoning process. Additional reasoning beyond this point does not yield further performance improvements. For tasks involving images, leveraging Visual Language Models (VLMs), which inherently understand images, offers a more efficient solution than introducing unnecessary complexity, such as caption generation. To explore this, we employed the teacher model Qwen2.5-VL-72B to sequentially execute two steps: knowledge and reasoning CoT generation, followed by rule-aware CoT.
