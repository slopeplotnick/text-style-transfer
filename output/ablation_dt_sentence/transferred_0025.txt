The project centers on integrating new features and updating the existing framework, with a focus on both technological advancements in information technology and improvements in agronomy. It emphasizes the capacity to develop applications more rapidly than their predecessors, embodying the spirit of fast development. This approach combines key principles such as code reuse, innovation, and rapid development. Initially, the project was conceived as a reusable code base, designed to foster adaptability and efficiency in future applications, thereby significantly contributing to the migration process. We identified seven current best practices, with Factorization and Reuse emerging as the most frequently cited, appearing nine and eight times, respectively. As shown in Figure 1, this distribution underscores their prominence. Understanding and articulating the anticipated benefits is crucial, as doing so motivates the team and aligns their efforts toward successful implementation. Customization, a core characteristic of an SPL, was frequently highlighted by IT professionals, UX designers, and agronomists. It was emphasized during team discussions, where each new feature was evaluated to determine whether it should be tailored for specific applications or generalized for broader use across all applications. An SPL is conceptualized as a platform capable of generating mass-customized software from a collection of existing systems. Speed, ranked second by IT professionals, Product Owners, and UX designers, refers to the ability to quickly derive new software. Automation, also mentioned frequently, pertains to the automatic generation of new software. IT professionals and Product Owners view automation and speed as inherently linked. Industrialization, as referenced by IT and UX professionals, describes the on-demand, cost-effective production of applications through automated processes. Improving code quality, a point raised solely by IT professionals, involves ensuring consistency, testability, and reliability within the codebase. Selling advantages, highlighted by IT professionals and Product Owners, refer to the ability to increase software sales or swiftly respond to emerging opportunities.
