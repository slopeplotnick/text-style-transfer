**Mathematical Reasoning.** We evaluate model performance on five mathematical reasoning benchmarks: AMC, Minerva (Lewkowycz et al., 2022), MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021), and Olympiad-Bench (He et al., 2024). For evaluation, we adopt the methodology from prior work (Zhao et al., 2025c; Ma et al., 2025), using GPT-4o as a programmatic judge to compare final answers against the ground truth. **General Domain Reasoning.** To assess the generalization of reasoning ability, we evaluate performance across a diverse range of domains—physics, biology, business, economics, and law—using the following challenging benchmarks: MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024), SuperGPQA (Du et al., 2025), GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al.), and BBEH (Shoaa Kazemi et al., 2025). These benchmarks offer stricter tests of complex reasoning capabilities. For consistency, we follow Ma et al. (2025) and report Exact Match (EM) accuracy, with outputs generated via greedy decoding. To isolate the contributions of individual components within the Qwen3-8B-Base model, we conduct an ablation study based on our R-FEW (5%) experiments. Specifically, we disable three key modules individually and evaluate their impact on both math and general reasoning performance. Results are summarized in Table 2. Among the ablations, removing challenger training leads to the largest performance drop, reducing Math and General averages by 1.9 and 1.0 points, respectively. Similarly, removing challenger warm-up and curriculum learning also degrades performance, suggesting both components are essential for optimal outcomes. Notably, math tasks display higher sensitivity to these changes compared to general tasks. This trend aligns with observations in Table 1, where math benchmarks exhibit greater variance across methods. Overall, while heterogeneous human data contributes broadly to performance gains, domain-specific data sampling proves more effective at boosting performance within its respective domain. This property offers practical value: by curating targeted data domains, developers can steer model development more precisely, prevent unintended drift, and maintain alignment with desired capabilities.
