By integrating related events and examining their antecedents, we can create structured event chains that transform disparate occurrences into a unified and meaningful framework. Unlike mere summaries, this approach mitigates disjointedness and enhances clarity in causal relationships and timelines. Moreover, maintaining specificity within events, such as detailing technological advancements in announcements like a "new tech release," provides a deeper understanding than overgeneralization, which can obscure significant details crucial for business impact. Specifically, precise dates facilitate a comprehensive grasp of temporal durations and their consequential impacts. Without exact temporal markers, the causal influence of events on market fluctuations becomes unclear, thus diminishing the accuracy of predictive models. We explored the significance of event tracking and incorporating incremental information by comparing our complete framework with an ablated version lacking these components. Our analysis revealed that even minor developments in news reports can significantly impact the discourse when they unexpectedly signal progress. Conversely, in the absence of novel information, such as when a major shareholder reduces their holdings without accompanying developments, the market reaction tends to be muted. Therefore, while individual events might exert minimal influence, the progressive shifts in market expectations—achieved through diligent event monitoring—hold substantial predictive power. To assess the efficacy of our historical pattern referencing strategy, we conducted a comparative analysis of four distinct approaches. Our methodology demonstrated superior performance. For instance, examining the historical performance of an individual company can yield satisfactory insights; however, for companies with limited historical data or those pursuing novel ventures, this method proves inadequate due to the difficulty of identifying similar past occurrences.
