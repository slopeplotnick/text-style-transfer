At the conclusion of the study, participants were invited to share any additional thoughts on AI-generated music (see Supplements C for detailed phrasing). Participants' personality traits were assessed using the short form of the Big Five Inventory (BFI-10; Rammstedt and John, 2007), a validated instrument for English- and German-speaking populations. The five personality traits examined included Openness. Basic socio-demographic characteristics were collected to better understand the sample and evaluate the potential influence of these variables on the study outcomes. These variables included participants’ age, gender, nationality, and educational attainment. Although the survey allowed for non-binary responses, all participants identified as male or female; therefore, gender was coded as a binary variable. To ensure participants' ability to discern subtle distinctions in emotional language, their comprehension of the survey language was also assessed. All statistical analyses were performed in Python (v3.9.20), utilizing the pandas library (v2.2.2) for data handling, scipy (v1.12.0) for conducting correlation and ANOVA tests, and statsmodels (v0.14.4) for implementing mixed-effects models. Figures were generated in R (v4.3.3) using the ggplot2 package (Wickham, 2016). Thematic analysis of open-ended responses regarding AI-generated music was conducted following the methodology outlined by Braun and Clarke (2006), using MAXQDA (v.24.10). Additional participant-level robustness checks, averaging ratings across all songs per participant, are reported in Appendix C. These analyses replicated the composer bias effect for music liking but did not reveal significant group differences in emotional intensity. To assess whether the experimental condition influenced ratings of liking and emotional intensity beyond individual differences and music stimuli, we fitted linear mixed-effects models with crossed random intercepts for participants and songs, while controlling for personality traits, listening habits, and the music model used.
