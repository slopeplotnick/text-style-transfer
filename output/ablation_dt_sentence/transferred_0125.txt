Moreover, research shows that reasoning-based omni-modal guardrails contribute to more consistent, explainable, and trustworthy moderation, marking a significant advancement in ensuring the safety and reliability of omni-modal AIs. In summary, we introduce OMNI-GUARD, a pioneering family of models designed for unified safety moderation across text, images, video, and audio, underpinned by deliberate reasoning. Additionally, we propose a novel training framework that incorporates omni-modal targeted distillation to endow the model with explainable, safety-driven decision-making. Our extensive experiments demonstrate that OMNI-GUARD achieves state-of-the-art accuracy, robust generalization, and superior explainability compared to previous models. The rise of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) has sparked increased interest in omni-modal language models (OLLMs), which can simultaneously process multiple modalities and generate flexible outputs across these domains. Unlike earlier models that relied on separately pretrained unimodal components, OLLMs are trained end-to-end on multimodal data, enabling them to develop native capabilities for unified understanding and generation across text, audio, image, and video. These models work by mapping disparate inputs into a shared latent space, facilitating cross-modality reasoning. For example, models such as Qwen2.5-Omni and LLaMA-Omni offer real-time, end-to-end generation of both text and speech. Building on this foundation, NExT-OMNI extends these capabilities to support "any-to-any" cross-modal generation and understanding. However, despite their advances, these models still face safety challenges due to parameter misalignment, which can lead to potentially dangerous outcomes.
