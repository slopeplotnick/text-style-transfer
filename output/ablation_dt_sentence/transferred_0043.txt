Radiology reporting is a critical aspect of medical image evaluation, yet it remains a time-consuming and error-prone process, particularly when dealing with 3D imaging modalities such as CT and MRI. Ensuring the accuracy and completeness of reports is equally important; however, this task is both labor-intensive and prone to human error, requiring careful verification at each stage. Existing automated methods primarily focus on generating reports, overlooking the essential quality control procedures that ensure clinical standards are met, thus limiting their ability to fully assist radiologists in clinical practice. In response, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an agentic AI assistant equipped with an orchestrated suite of tools designed to automate both report generation and quality assurance. By leveraging large-scale language models as the reasoning backbone, the system autonomously selects appropriate tools, plans, and executes tasks, mimicking the decision-making processes of radiologists. These tools include image region identification, strategic analysis planning, report template selection, and iterative feedback-driven quality enhancement. As a result, Radiologist Copilot streamlines the radiology reporting process, improving both accuracy and efficiency while supporting radiologists. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms existing methods in radiology report generation. Upon approval, the source code will be made publicly available.
