Following the training phase, we develop a comprehensive and challenging evaluation benchmark designed to rigorously assess model capabilities (Section 3.5). Given the powerful capabilities of LLMs and the diversity of e-commerce data, we designed our evaluation benchmark to ensure broad sample comprehensiveness, while also incorporating targeted hard sample mining based on the core capabilities identified in Section 2. To establish a solid foundation, we first conducted a series of necessary preliminary explorations, including feature construction, model selection, and prompt optimization, as shown in Fig. 6. Guided by the theoretical framework outlined in Section 2, effective relevance modeling necessitates a thorough construction of the item's attribute space. Accordingly, we select features that equip the model with the essential perception and reasoning abilities across both visual and textual modalities. Specifically, to address the need for visual attributes, such as stylistic details, cuts, and patterns, we incorporate the item's Main Product Image. This visual input is crucial, particularly for "image-heavy" queries, where textual descriptions alone fall short of capturing the underlying search intent. Moreover, to enrich the item-side attribute features, we introduce category-property-value (CPV) triples and Stock-keeping-unit (SKU) data. Given that CPV information can contain redundant or task-irrelevant elements that may hinder relevance judgment, we implement a pipeline that combines prior knowledge with LLMs for effective filtering. In this process, CPV information is represented as key-value pairs, with key co-occurrence observed across semantically similar products. For instance, both "eye cream" and "face cream" products commonly include the key "brand." To identify high-frequency keys, we conducted a statistical analysis based on an extensive product dataset. Subsequently, we prompted the advanced LLM, Qwen3-235B-Instruct, to evaluate whether each attribute contributed to relevance, using the task definition, the considered attributes, and the corresponding keys. To enhance the accuracy of these judgments, we performed eight sampling iterations on the model outputs, retaining only those attributes where the self-consistency result was deemed "true."
