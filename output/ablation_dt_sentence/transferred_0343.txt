Guardrails are external moderation systems designed to enforce safety protocols during interactions between users and AI models. These systems are essential for mitigating risks by imposing safety constraints and preventing harmful content. Initially, simple rule-based filtering methods were employed to exclude undesirable content; however, these approaches proved inadequate as new challenges emerged and interaction complexities increased. Consequently, these systems struggled to address all scenarios comprehensively and ensure consistent accuracy. In response, contemporary guardrail systems now integrate advanced AI models, enhancing their flexibility and ability to generalize. For instance, Llama Guard (Inan et al., 2023) is an LLM-based moderation system fine-tuned on proprietary safety datasets developed by Meta AI, specifically designed to safeguard user-model interactions. Similarly, models like Llama Guard 3 Vision (Chi et al., 2024) and LlavaGuard (Helff et al., 2025b) are tailored to identify and mitigate visual safety concerns in visual-language models (VLMs). Despite these advancements, challenges persist. Prior research has largely focused on textual and visual data, leaving videos and auditory content to be handled with more rudimentary techniques that often fail to capture contextual nuances. Moreover, most existing systems are confined to single modalities or narrowly defined scenarios, limiting their capacity to jointly process heterogeneous media inputs or perform integrated cross-modal reasoning. To address these gaps, the development of OMNIGUARD was initiated. This innovative system is the first to support multi-modal content moderation while utilizing reasoning mechanisms to ensure safety across diverse content types. OMNIGUARD delivers comprehensive, consistent safety evaluations across both unimodal and cross-modal contexts, ensuring robust coverage for a wide range of media forms.
