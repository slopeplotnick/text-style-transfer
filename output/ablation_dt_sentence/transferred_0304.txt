Datasets. Following prior studies (Hendrycks et al. 2021), we curate 5.5K Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset. Evaluation Benchmarks. We evaluate performance on AIME 2024 (Li et al. 2024a), AMC (Li et al. 2024a), MATH500 (Hendrycks et al. 2021), GSM8K (Cobb et al. 2021), Minerva (Zhou et al. 2024), OlympiadBench (Smith et al. 2023), and College Math (Johnson et al. 2024). Furthermore, to assess performance on tasks beyond conventional benchmarks, we include ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al. 2018). Inference is conducted using a greedy decoding approach with a 3K token budget, consistent with standard practices in the field. Baselines. We evaluate CAPO across four prominent reinforcement learning algorithms: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. For each baseline, we also evaluate its CAPO-augmented variant, enabling a direct assessment of CAPO's impact as a general enhancement. This allows us to compare the standalone performance of the baselines with their improved versions when integrated with CAPO. Implementation. To ensure fairness across the baseline algorithms and CAPO variants, we standardize the implementation details. Since different methods require distinct configurations, the complete training setup is provided in Appendix B. Datasets. We utilize the GUI-R1-3K dataset from OS-Atlas, comprising 3,000 vision-language-action examples drawn from both web and mobile interfaces. This dataset pairs visual states with natural language instructions and corresponding action sequences, offering a robust framework for assessing multimodal reasoning and planning across diverse tasks. Evaluation Benchmarks. For planning, we examine GUI-Act-Web (Li et al. 2024), OmniAct-Web (Kapoor et al. 2025), and AndroidControl-Low/High (Li et al. 2024b). For perception, we employ Screenshot Pro (Li et al. 2025). Baselines. We compare GRPO with its CAPO-augmented variant, following the established GUI benchmark setup (Wu et al. 2025). This serves as an additional evaluation, assessing the performance of CAPO across a wide range of reasoning scenarios, extending beyond its primary focus on mathematical reasoning tasks.
