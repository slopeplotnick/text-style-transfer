The model benefits most from learning through its own errors, whereas the inclusion of highly off-policy negative samples can be detrimental, potentially destabilizing the optimization process. We empirically observe that the application of Off-Policy Sequence Masking improves stability in training scenarios that would otherwise exhibit instability. Similarly, Keep Routing Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models enhance computational efficiency by activating only a subset of expert modules during inference. However, discrepancies between the inference and training processes, compounded by policy updates, can result in inconsistent expert routing, even when identical inputs are provided. This inconsistency leads to abrupt shifts in the active parameter subspace, destabilizing optimization and exacerbating off-policy challenges. To address this issue, we preserve the expert routing paths determined during inference and enforce these same routing paths during training, ensuring that identical expert parameters are optimized. This Keep Routing operation has proven essential for maintaining MoE model stability during RL training, and has been a key feature of our RL training pipeline since DeepSeek-V3-0324. Furthermore, DeepSeek-R1 demonstrated that incorporating a structured problem-solving approach significantly improves the model’s ability to solve complex challenges. Building on this, our goal is to integrate cognitive abilities into tool-calling scenarios, thereby enhancing these processes. We observed that replicating DeepSeek-R1's approach—discarding reasoning content after the second round of messages—leads to substantial token inefficiency. This forces the model to reprocess the entire problem with each subsequent tool invocation. To mitigate this, we implemented a context management system specifically designed for tool-calling scenarios, as shown in Fig. 4. Historical reasoning content is discarded only when a new user message is introduced. If only tool-related outputs are appended, the reasoning content is preserved throughout the interaction. Even after the removal of reasoning traces, the history of tool calls and their respective results is retained within the context.
