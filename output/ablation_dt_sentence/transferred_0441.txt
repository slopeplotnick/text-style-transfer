Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) techniques are essential to address the escalating dimensions of Large Language Models (LLMs). Unfortunately, many newly introduced PEFT methods are difficult to replicate, deploy, or compare with each other. Therefore, to tackle these issues, we present PEFT-FACTORY, a unified framework designed to efficiently fine-tune LLMs using both standardized and customized PEFT methodologies. Although its architecture is adaptable and extensible, PEFT-FACTORY natively supports a comprehensive suite of 19 PEFT methods, 27 datasets tailored for classification and text generation tasks across 12 distinct areas, along with evaluation tools for both general and PEFT-specific metrics. Consequently, PEFT-FACTORY provides a streamlined and dependable platform that promotes replicability and enables comparative analysis of PEFT methods. This framework, derived from the foundational LLaMAFactory, is publicly accessible at https://github.com/kinit-sk/PEFT-Factory. Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable results across numerous Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks, particularly following the introduction of the transformer architecture. However, the exceptional scalability of these models has led to a continuous increase in the number of trainable parameters. This proliferation renders the fine-tuning process computationally expensive, data-intensive, and largely inaccessible for many researchers and practitioners. In response, PEFT techniques seek to mitigate these challenges by selectively optimizing a subset of the model's parameters, thereby achieving performance that rivals full model training. This reduction in trainable parameters can be realized through the addition of new parameters, the selection of specific parameters for training, or the reparameterization of the model to use fewer parameters.
