Nearswap, introduced by Goddard et al. (2024), enhances the interpolation process by strengthening the influence of similar parameters and weakening it for dissimilar ones. Specifically, it amplifies the interpolation when parameters are similar and diminishes it when they differ. To compare various model configurations, we selected three families of large language models (LLMs): GPT, LLAMA, and QWEN. The GPT family, as defined by Radford et al. (2019) and Gao et al. (2020), includes GPT2-small, GPT2-medium, GPT2-large, GPT2-x1, and GPT-neo-2.7B. The LLAMA family, as described by Touvron et al. (2023) and Dubey et al. (2024), consists of LLAMA-2-7B, LLAMA-3-8B, LLAMA-3.1-8B, LLAMA-3.2-1B, and LLAMA-3.2-3B. Finally, the QWEN family, defined by Qwen (2024), includes QWEN2-0.5B, QWEN2-1.5B, and QWEN2-7B. These models are available from the Hugging Face repository, with corresponding URLs provided in Appendix A. The computational resources required for continual pretraining to generate biased LLMs are detailed in Appendix C, and the hyperparameter configurations are provided in Appendix D. To investigate social bias in LLMs, we evaluate three established benchmarks: BBQ, BOLD, and HONEST. The URLs for these datasets are provided in Appendix E. The BBQ dataset (Parrish et al., 2022) consists of approximately 58,000 question-answer pairs across nine social dimensions pertinent to U.S. English speakers. By comparing "underspecified" and "fully specified" versions of each question, it assesses the extent to which models rely on stereotypical priors instead of concrete evidence. The BOLD dataset (Dhamala et al., 2021) comprises 23,679 prompts, categorized into 43 demographic subgroups, covering factors such as occupation, gender, race, religion, and political ideology. To ensure that debiasing methods do not negatively affect performance on downstream tasks, we evaluate both the debiased and pre-trained LLMs on the SuperGLUE benchmark, which includes eight tasks: BoolQ, CB, COPA, MultiRC, ReCoRD, RTE, WiC, and WSC. All evaluations are conducted using the Language Model Evaluation Harness<sup>5</sup>.
