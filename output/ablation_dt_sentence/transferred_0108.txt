We planned the interviews to be conducted in a fluid, conversational manner, rather than as rigid Q&A sessions. At the outset, we provided a brief overview of the principles of SPLE and explained the purpose of the questionnaire. The first set of questions focused on the interviewee and their role within the company, aiming to understand their background and profile. We then moved on to general questions about the company’s development process. Following that, we asked more specific questions regarding the current development process for new products, with the goal of understanding the strengths, good practices, and overall state of the process. Discussing the current state naturally led to a transition where we introduced a new approach that could potentially address existing weaknesses. The final part of the interview focused on the migration process, encouraging participants to express any concerns, perceived risks, and potential benefits. The last two questions served to improve the interview process and conclude the discussion. All interviews were conducted by the same interviewer, the first author of this work, and each lasted between 30 and 60 minutes. The interviewees appreciated the process and reported feeling included in the migration project. Each interview ended with the question, "Was there a question you anticipated that I did not ask?" This prompted interviewees to reflect on the entire discussion, allowing them to add any additional points they deemed important, which contributed to the overall fluidity of subsequent interviews. To ensure consistency and comparability in the data analysis, the questions were kept identical across all interviews. The aim was to encourage participants to consider all relevant aspects, without altering the structure of the interview questions, while also improving the overall flow of the interviews. Notes were systematically recorded during each interview. In accordance with recommendations, the interviewer took care to ensure that the questions were neutral and did not unintentionally steer the responses. The interviewer also ensured that enough questions were asked without influencing the participants' answers. As a pilot, the first interview was conducted with our main collaborator at the company, who oversees this research, to refine the questionnaire and ensure a smooth process. The data from this initial interview were excluded from the analysis.
