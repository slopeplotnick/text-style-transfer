Case 3: Suicidal Risk Level Detection. This case involves a misclassification in which a Reddit post intended to provide support was incorrectly labeled as behavior by the Menta model. The primary cause of the error was the excessive input length, which included formatting issues such as extraneous symbols, HTML artifacts, and placeholder phrases like “Hyperactive behavior.” These elements introduced noise and acted as spurious cues, leading the model to misinterpret the post’s intent. Specifically, the post featured a user encouraging another to stay alive for the sake of their younger siblings, emphasizing the need to protect them from potential harm caused by their father. Moreover, the user dismissed a derogatory comment in the thread, asserting that it should not be taken seriously and expressing that they did not believe the individual was worthless or should engage in self-harm. The behavior label appeared to stem from the user’s reflection on personal patterns of overcommitment—taking on more responsibilities than manageable—which previously contributed to academic struggles and social difficulties. However, over time, the user reported learning to set boundaries and decline excessive demands, resulting in improved well-being and enhanced social connectedness.
