We introduced promptsolution, a unified open-source Python framework for automatic prompt optimization, tailored for both practitioners focusing on single-task prompt refinement and researchers engaged in large-scale benchmark studies. Our framework stands out in the landscape of prompt optimization tools due to its extensible, modular design and low abstraction level, specifically geared toward facilitating prompt optimization. Although not every optimizer within promptsolution excels at all tasks—for instance, OPRO on GSM8K—the library consistently offers at least one strong optimizer per task. This modularity empowers users to effortlessly switch to an alternative optimizer if the current one proves inefficient. Moreover, comparing optimizers within promptsolution necessitates minimal manual effort owing to its dedicated support for systematic benchmark evaluations. We demonstrated the operational integration of each component within the framework and how they synergistically enhance prompt optimization. Through rigorous comparative evaluation, we validated the utility and competitiveness of promptsolution against existing tools. Looking ahead, we plan to enhance interoperability with higher-level frameworks like DSPy, thus enabling users to leverage the benefits of both ecosystems. Additionally, to facilitate management in complex experimental setups, we will introduce interfaces to configuration management frameworks such as Hydra. To improve accessibility, we are developing a user-friendly graphical interface for real-time tracking and visual analysis of the optimization process. In response to the rise of multi-agent systems, we aim to optimize system prompts and interaction protocols across multiple agents. Inspired by advancements in AutoML, we will explore ensembling strategies for prompt optimizers, similar to ELPO. More broadly, the extensibility of our framework ensures we can continually integrate new state-of-the-art optimization methods as the field progresses.
