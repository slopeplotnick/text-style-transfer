An overview of participants' characteristics can be found in Table B.3 in the Appendix. No significant differences in participants' characteristics were observed across the experimental groups. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups: (1) In the first group, participants were informed that the music excerpts they would listen to were composed by AI; (2) in the second group, participants were told that the music excerpts were sourced from movie soundtracks; (3) in the third group, participants were instructed that the music excerpts they would hear were composed by both humans and AI, and they were required to identify the composer of each excerpt. Participants in groups 1 and 3 were therefore provided with explanations of AI and AI music tools before beginning the music rating task. After receiving their group-specific instructions, participants were tasked with rating 18 music excerpts, which were randomly selected to ensure a balance across subgenres and AI tools. Prior to the task, participants were instructed to adjust the playback volume to a comfortable level and to familiarize themselves with the emotion terms provided. The order of terms varied randomly between participants but remained consistent across songs for each individual. Participants were also instructed to rate the emotions they personally experienced in response to the music, rather than the emotions they believed the music was intended to convey. Each music excerpt was presented twice. In the first presentation, participants were instructed to listen attentively without engaging in any additional actions. Following this, the rating questions were displayed, and the music excerpt was replayed to minimize potential memory-related biases in participants' evaluations. After completing the music ratings, participants proceeded to the questionnaire, where they provided information about their personality traits, music-related habits, and socio-demographic background. Participants' attitudes toward AI were also assessed. Upon completing the questionnaires, participants were presented with a debrief screen that explained the study's purpose and outlined how the collected data would be used. Finally, participants were invited to share their thoughts on AI-generated music and provide feedback on their overall experience through an open-ended question. The survey concluded with the presentation of individualized feedback to each participant.
