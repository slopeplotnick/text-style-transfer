The application of the KARMA system seeks to improve the functionality and safety of large language models in specialized domains. KARMA is composed of three core components: one facilitates the collection of precise data from diverse sources, another governs the utilization of this data, and the final component ensures the safety and reliability of the responses. Several studies have proposed strategies to enhance the KARMA framework. For instance, one approach involves incorporating both temporal and geographical data to improve the precision of generated responses. Another promising strategy employs a system that ranks and retrieves information, enabling adaptation to temporal changes and enhancing result accuracy. Additionally, a method has been developed that identifies key terms within sentences, thereby improving the system’s ability to discern relationships. Various studies have evaluated specific toolsets for addressing medical queries, showing promising results. Incorporating these tools into the KARMA framework could, therefore, improve its performance in healthcare-related tasks. Furthermore, another approach involves dynamically adjusting safety levels during system operation, thereby enhancing responsiveness to sensitive queries. Additionally, a method exists that integrates heterogeneous data modalities and adaptively adjusts their relative importance, potentially allowing KARMA to better accommodate noisy or mixed data. Collectively, these advancements aim to bolster KARMA’s intelligence, safety, and reliability in real-world deployments.
