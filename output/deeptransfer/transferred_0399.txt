The task is framed as unsupervised fraud detection in journal entry data, where labels for fraudulent activity are scarce and often unavailable, making supervised learning infeasible at scale. Therefore, we aim to detect anomalous entries by analyzing irregular patterns within the data. To simulate real-world auditing conditions, we utilize a synthetic dataset introduced by Gronewald et al. in 2024, which emulates the bookkeeping processes of a medium-sized enterprise. Each entry in this dataset includes features such as a unique identifier, the timestamp of the posting and transaction, classification as a debit or credit, the applicable tax rate, and an associated account identifier. Furthermore, we evaluate our approach using anonymized accounting data provided by an industry partner. However, due to the anonymization process, this dataset lacks the conventional links between debit and credit entries typically present in standard double-entry bookkeeping systems. Consequently, we treat it as a transaction-level fraud detection problem. To address the absence of specific contextual details, we incorporate bookkeeping heuristics, procedural guidelines, and summary statistics into the prompts provided to large language models (LLMs), which enhances their performance relative to traditional machine learning methods. Given the critical importance of safeguarding sensitive accounting data, preference was given to open-weight LLMs, such as LLaMa AI (2025) and Gemma Team et al. (2024), over proprietary LLM API endpoints, such as GPT-4 (OpenAI, 2024), during the benchmarking process. Our proposed approach demonstrates superior performance compared to baseline methods when evaluated on both synthetic datasets, such as those introduced by Gronewald et al., and real-world anonymized ledgers. For the baseline, we employed Isolation Forest, a method originally proposed by Liu et al. [2008], due to its demonstrated efficacy in handling tabular data and its suitability for unsupervised anomaly detection tasks. This approach aligns with the objectives outlined for this study, particularly in addressing the challenges presented by the real-world anonymized dataset.
