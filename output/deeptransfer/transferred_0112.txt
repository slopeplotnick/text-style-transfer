Our samples were compared against the EMOPIA MIDI dataset, where the major-key ratio serves as an indicator of valence and the average note length and note velocity reflect arousal. Regarding valence, our generated samples align well with EMOPIA across three quadrants, except for Q2, where our model produces a significantly higher proportion of major-key tracks. This discrepancy likely stems from a major-key bias due to the dataset's higher proportion of major-key tracks and the limited size of the fine-tuning set. In terms of arousal, the fine-tuned model effectively captures the note-length pattern, with shorter notes in high-arousal quadrants (Q1 and Q2) and longer notes in low-arousal quadrants (Q3 and Q4), closely matching EMOPIA. However, the differences in note velocity across quadrants are less pronounced, likely because the REMI framework simplifies MIDI velocities into fewer categories, compressing the dynamic range and reducing inter-quadrant separability. The quantitative findings partially mirror the listening test outcomes, indicating that the model aligns some musical characteristics with arousal and valence. Nevertheless, the model demonstrates greater proficiency in capturing arousal-related features than those related to valence, with valence results approaching randomness. This may be due to the model's tendency to overgenerate major-key compositions, suggesting that incorporating more quantitative metrics could help identify deficiencies in positive valence generation. In this study, we introduced Story2MIDI, a Transformer-based model designed to generate symbolic music that is emotionally congruent with textual input. To achieve this, we constructed a comprehensive text-music dataset and developed an encoder-decoder framework to map emotional content from text to music effectively. The encoder was contrastively fine-tuned to produce distinct emotion representations across varying levels of valence and arousal, while the decoder was initially pretrained on a large corpus of symbolic music and subsequently fine-tuned on a smaller, emotion-annotated dataset for emotion-conditioned music generation. Story2MIDI was evaluated using both subjective listener assessments and objective musical metrics, demonstrating the model's efficacy while identifying several areas requiring further investigation.
