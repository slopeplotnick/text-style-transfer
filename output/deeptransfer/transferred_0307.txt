Story2MIDI, a sequence-to-sequence Transformer-based model, is introduced to generate music that aligns with the emotional content of a given text. To develop this model, we construct the Story2MIDI dataset by integrating existing datasets focused on sentiment analysis from text and emotion classification in music. The resulting dataset consists of pairs of text excerpts and music pieces designed to evoke congruent emotional responses in the reader or listener. Although the dataset is relatively small and computational resources are limited, our results demonstrate that the model effectively learns emotion-relevant features in music and incorporates them into its generative process, producing samples with diverse emotional responses. To evaluate the generated outputs, we utilize objective musical metrics alongside a human listening study, both of which confirm the model's ability to accurately capture intended emotional cues. In today’s world, the demand for entertainment and multimedia content continues to grow rapidly, fueled by the expansion of social media and streaming platforms. Many forms of contemporary entertainment, such as films, games, podcasts, and documentaries, are centered around narratives. Narratives are powerful tools for eliciting emotional responses, as audiences engage with characters, plot developments, and thematic elements. This emotional journey is integral to the lasting impact that stories have on readers and viewers. Similarly, music exerts a profound influence on emotional states and is widely recognized as a powerful medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creative practitioners often integrate background music into narratives, as it has been shown to enhance emotional engagement and increase the immersive quality of the experience. Furthermore, with the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence across various modalities, new models capable of generating diverse forms of content are being introduced at an unprecedented pace.
