In this paper, we introduce Story2MIDI, a sequence-to-sequence model designed to generate music that aligns with the emotional content of a given text. To develop this model, we constructed a dataset by integrating existing resources for sentiment analysis in text and emotion classification in music. This dataset comprises pairs of text excerpts and corresponding musical pieces that evoke congruent emotional responses in both readers and listeners. Despite the limited scale of our dataset and constrained computational resources, our results indicate that the model effectively learns emotion-relevant features in music and incorporates them into its generative process, thereby producing samples with varied emotional responses. We evaluated the generated outputs using objective musical metrics alongside a human listening study, which confirmed the model's capability to capture the intended emotional cues. In contemporary society, there is a substantial demand for entertainment and multimedia content, including music, audiobooks, and podcasts, particularly due to the rapid expansion of social media and streaming platforms. At the heart of many of these forms of entertainment lies a narrative, a story that drives the experience, whether in films, games, podcasts, or documentaries. Narratives are powerful tools for evoking emotion, as audiences engage with the characters, plot developments, and underlying themes. This emotional trajectory experienced by audiences is crucial to the enduring impact that narratives exert on individuals. Similarly, music exerts a profound influence on the emotional states of individuals and is widely acknowledged as a medium for emotional expression. Consequently, creative producers frequently incorporate background music into narratives to enhance emotional engagement and augment the immersive experience of the content. With the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence across various modalities, novel models are continuously being developed, capable of generating diverse forms of content.
