We propose Radiologist Copilot, an innovative system designed to assist radiologists in achieving automated radiology reporting with integrated quality control through an agentic framework of orchestrated tools. This agentic system, leveraging an intelligent reasoning backbone, autonomously selects appropriate tools, formulates plans, and executes tasks, thereby emulating the cognitive processes of radiologists to accomplish the comprehensive reporting process. The tool library encompasses functionalities such as region localization, analysis planning, strategic template selection, quality assessment, and feedback-driven adaptive refinement, all of which contribute to the generation of detailed and accurate radiology reports. Empirical evaluations indicate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms other leading methodologies in the domain of radiology report generation. Moreover, the implementation of automated reporting with integrated quality control mechanisms is imperative, given the substantial time investment required for processing 3D medical imaging modalities. For instance, CT2Rep employs an auto-regressive causal transformer architecture coupled with relational memory to facilitate the generation of reports for 3D chest CT images. Additionally, Reg2RG utilizes a region-guided framework tailored for generating chest CT reports. However, report generation represents merely one component of the comprehensive radiology reporting workflow and lacks essential quality assurance mechanisms. To address such limitations, medical vision-language models (VLMs) have been introduced to facilitate various radiological tasks, including region-guided reporting (RRG) and visual question answering (VQA). In particular, 3D medical VLMs have demonstrated efficacy in the analysis of 3D medical modalities, such as computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). RadFM introduces a comprehensive vision-language foundation model that integrates both 2D and 3D medical data for radiological applications. Furthermore, M3D presents M3D-Data, an extensive multi-modal medical dataset, alongside M3D-LaMed, a multi-modal large language model (LLM) designed for the analysis of 3D medical images. Merlin introduces a 3D vision-language model (VLM) that integrates both structured and unstructured data to facilitate the interpretation of abdominal CT scans. Meanwhile, CT-CHAT represents a vision-language foundation model specifically designed for 3D chest CT volumes, employing the CT-CLIP vision encoder in conjunction with a pretrained large language model (LLM). Med3DVLM advances multimodal representation learning by incorporating an efficient encoder, a contrastive learning framework, and a dual-stream projection mechanism. Lastly, Hulu-Med offers a comprehensive medical vision-language model that integrates text, 2D and 3D images, and video within a unified framework.
