Radiologist Copilot is a system designed to assist radiologists in achieving automated radiology reporting with integrated quality control through an agentic framework of orchestrated tools. This agentic system, leveraging an advanced reasoning backbone, autonomously selects appropriate tools, formulates plans, and executes tasks, thereby emulating the cognitive processes of radiologists to accomplish the complete radiology reporting workflow. The comprehensive toolset, encompassing functionalities such as region localization, analysis planning, strategic template selection, quality assessment, and feedback-driven adaptive refinement, facilitates the production of detailed and accurate radiology reports. Experimental results demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms other state-of-the-art methodologies in radiology report generation. Automated radiology reporting with stringent quality control measures is essential for supporting radiologists, as the processing of 3D medical imaging modalities is inherently time-intensive. CT2Rep employs an auto-regressive causal transformer architecture alongside a relational memory framework to generate reports for 3D chest CT images. Similarly, Reg2RG utilizes a region-guided framework for chest CT report generation. However, report generation represents only one phase of the comprehensive radiology reporting process and often lacks critical quality control procedures. Medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have been proposed to address various medical tasks, such as radiology report generation and visual question answering. Specifically, 3D medical VLMs have demonstrated effectiveness in processing 3D medical modalities, including computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). RadFM introduces a generalist vision-language foundation model that integrates both 2D and 3D medical data for radiological applications. M3D presents M3D-Data, a large-scale multi-modal medical dataset, alongside M3D-LaMed, a multi-modal language model designed for 3D medical image analysis. Merlin develops a 3D vision-language model that leverages both structured and unstructured data for the interpretation of abdominal CT scans. CT-CHAT represents a vision-language foundation model specifically designed for 3D chest CT volumes, combining the CT-CLIP vision encoder with a pretrained large language model (LLM). Med3DVLM advances multimodal representation learning through the implementation of an efficient encoder, a contrastive learning approach, and a dual-stream projection mechanism. Finally, Hulu-Med introduces a unified medical VLM architecture that integrates textual data, 2D and 3D imaging modalities, and video within a single framework.
