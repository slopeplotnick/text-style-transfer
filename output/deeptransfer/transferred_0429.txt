The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) has significantly propelled progress in natural language processing (NLP), with story generation emerging as a key area of research. Story generation aims to produce narratives that are coherent, engaging, and contextually appropriate based on the given input. However, existing systems continue to face notable challenges, particularly in maintaining narrative consistency, developing characters, and controlling story progression. Specifically, translating user intent into desired story content while ensuring control over plot development, character actions, and structural integrity remains a critical issue. Therefore, achieving fine-grained control is essential to avoid logical inconsistencies and ensure alignment with user expectations, thereby preserving both plausibility and creativity. Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs), such as GPT-4 and Llama-3, have demonstrated their ability to generate high-quality and coherent text. Nevertheless, as these models are trained on broad, general-purpose datasets, they often struggle to produce narratives that are sufficiently controlled and structured. This limitation becomes particularly evident in tasks requiring specific narrative details and well-defined frameworks, where user demands for both precision and creativity are difficult to meet. Consequently, enhancing the control capabilities of LLMs to align story generation with user expectations has become a pressing challenge. The integration of fine-tuning techniques with structured input prompts has shown great promise in addressing this issue. By fine-tuning LLMs and incorporating structured inputs, these models can be better adapted to the story generation domain, producing content that is more structured and aligned with user requirements. This approach not only improves the quality of the generated narratives but also enhances the stability and reliability of the models in handling complex story generation tasks. Thus, the combination of fine-tuning and structured input provides a promising pathway to improving the predictability and consistency of generated content, effectively addressing the inherent control challenges in the story generation process.
