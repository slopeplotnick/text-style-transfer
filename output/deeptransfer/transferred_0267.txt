Evaluation Metrics. To comprehensively assess the effectiveness of the Radiologist Copilot, we conducted evaluations at both the agent-level and task-level. For task-level evaluation in radiology reporting, we employed natural language generation (NLG) and clinical efficacy (CE) metrics. The NLG metrics included BLEU-1, ROUGE-L, METEOR, and BERTScore, while the CE metrics, such as F1-RadGraph and GREEN, measured clinical accuracy. For agent-level evaluation of the agentic tools, we utilized the LLM-as-a-Judge framework, adopting OpenAI GPT-5.1 as the LLM judge. This framework evaluates the agent's task-handling process across four dimensions: Analysis Process, Tool Selection, Action Planning, and Action Execution, with scores ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent). Specifically, the Analysis Process assesses whether the task analysis is thorough and logical, Tool Selection evaluates the appropriateness and accuracy of the tools chosen, Action Planning examines the feasibility and coherence of the proposed plan, and Action Execution measures the success of implementing the plan. To further validate the Radiologist Copilot, we performed ablation studies to investigate the functionality of its core components in the absence of supplementary features. The results of the agent-level evaluation, as shown in Fig. 3, indicate that the majority of scores were 5 (Excellent), with most of the remaining scores being 4 (Good). These findings demonstrate that the agent exhibits thorough task comprehension, accurate tool selection, logical planning, and effective execution. Moreover, Fig. 4 illustrates the Copilot’s workflow in addressing user queries, highlighting that its action planning and execution processes are coherent and efficient. Consequently, the Radiologist Copilot successfully generates qualified radiology reports, emulating professional radiology practices and enhancing clinical efficiency.
