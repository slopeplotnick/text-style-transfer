The methodology section is composed of the participants' presentation (Sect. 3.1), the interview design (Sect. 3.2), our approach to conducting interviews (Sect. 3.3), and the interview analysis method (Sect. 3.4). We selected participants to ensure a representative sample, covering various roles within the project, different levels of experience, and diverse educational backgrounds. Specifically, the project involves four distinct roles: Product Owners (PO), Developers (IT), Agronomists (AGRO), and User Interface/Experience (UX) designers. These participants possess diverse educational qualifications, ranging from engineering degrees to doctoral-level expertise. Moreover, we included participants with varying levels of experience within the project. In total, we selected 16 participants to be representative: nine Developers currently or previously engaged with the project, two Agronomists, three Product Owners, and two User Interface/Experience designers, all actively involved in the project. Therefore, participants were informed that their feedback would contribute to developing an effective plan for the SPL implementation process. We opted for structured interviews, utilizing a predefined set of questions. We adhered to established guidelines, employing clear and unambiguous terminology to ensure consistency and comprehensibility. Each question was designed to focus on a single concept. We refrained from posing sensitive inquiries or questions about past events. Additionally, we avoided questions for which the respondent might lack sufficient knowledge to provide an informed answer. This consideration led us to incorporate elements of actor-driven interviews. The interview framework was structured into three distinct sections. The initial section was dedicated to the participant's self-introduction. The subsequent section was divided into two parts: the first addressed the existing process, while the second examined potential modifications. Finally, a conclusion section was presented, encompassing a comprehensive discussion of the interview in its entirety.
