We employed various datasets: one containing the complete set of foundational units, one excluding events, one excluding relationships, one excluding entities, and one without the story outline. Both qualitative and quantitative metrics were used for evaluation. Specifically, the same test dataset, comprising 196 samples, was utilized to assess the fine-tuned models. Qualitative metrics focused on evaluating the functionality and artistic quality of the generated stories. For quantitative analysis, stories from the preference dataset ("Chosen") were used as a baseline, enabling a comparison of the similarity between outputs from different fine-tuned models. This analysis examined the impact of the four foundational components on the generated narratives. In the quantitative evaluation, we primarily assessed the similarity between the narratives generated by different fine-tuned models, using identical JSON inputs, and the original text. Each fine-tuned model generated three distinct narratives per input. The original text served as the benchmark, with similarity measured using three metrics: ROUGE-L, BERTScore, and METEOR Score. ROUGE-L evaluates sequence similarity by identifying the length of their longest common subsequence (LCS). BERTScore captures semantic similarity, avoiding limitations of surface-level matching. METEOR Score integrates multiple matching techniques, offering greater flexibility and robustness. As illustrated in Figure 7, the Full Units configuration outperformed others, demonstrating superior content control, semantic consistency, and linguistic naturalness in story generation. To further evaluate TaleFrame's usability, a study was conducted involving 2 experts and 17 volunteers, as described in Section 3. To support the creative process, volunteers were provided with a diverse set of images and allowed to select topics based on their interests. They then used TaleFrame to generate narratives. If dissatisfied with the generated story, volunteers could modify the attributes of the four foundational components to produce a narrative that aligned with their preferences. Throughout the study, volunteers were required to engage with all interactive features of TaleFrame.
