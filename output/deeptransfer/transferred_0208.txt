Search relevance is crucial for online shopping platforms like JD and Taobao. It involves evaluating how well products align with user queries and assigning relevance scores to filter out mismatched items. This process is essential for enhancing the search experience and improving ease of use for consumers. Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable proficiency in various tasks and are widely applied in these domains. However, state-of-the-art models, such as GPT-5, face limitations, including insufficient domain-specific knowledge and high operational costs. Consequently, there is a shift towards training smaller language models with domain-specific data, such as shopping data, to improve their effectiveness in specialized applications. Recent advancements have been made in enhancing the capabilities of smaller models, moving from basic training techniques to more sophisticated methodologies for problem-solving. Despite efforts to teach models systematic reasoning, challenges persist in managing complex scenarios. For example, the model known as ELLM evaluates product specifications and aligns them with user search queries; however, it struggles with intricate situations requiring specific rules. In cases where a query like "lake blue top" is matched with an item labeled "sky blue top," the model lacks the necessary rules to distinguish between these color variations. Models such as LREF and TaoSR1 attempt to address this limitation by incorporating rule-based chains of thought, thereby enhancing rule-awareness. Nevertheless, their inability to integrate visual information leads to deficiencies in discrimination. For instance, if a query specifies a "blue top" and the product title does not explicitly mention the color blue, yet the image depicts it, the model fails to recognize this due to its lack of image analysis capabilities. These challenges arise from neglecting a fundamental inquiry: What specific capabilities must a model possess to effectively address these tasks? This inquiry pertains to the essential competencies required for a model to accurately evaluate relevance.
