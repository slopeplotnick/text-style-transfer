We evaluated these methodologies on the BrowseComp benchmark. Effective context management during testing significantly enhances model performance by enabling the utilization of increased computational resources and facilitating additional execution steps when necessary. For example, the Summary method extends the average number of steps to 364, resulting in a performance enhancement of up to 60.2%. However, its overall efficiency remains relatively low. In contrast, the Discard-all method, despite its simplicity, demonstrates commendable efficiency and scalability, achieving a score of 67.6, comparable to parallel scaling while requiring significantly fewer steps. Therefore, test-time computation can be enhanced either through sequential context management or parallel execution, both of which augment the model's problem-solving capabilities. However, the efficiency and scalability of each approach vary, necessitating careful consideration of computational costs when evaluating model performance. Moreover, determining the optimal combination of sequential and parallel scaling to achieve both efficiency and scalability remains an area for future investigation. In this study, we introduced DeepSeek-V3.2, a framework that effectively bridges the gap between computational efficiency and advanced reasoning capabilities. By utilizing Dynamic System Architecture (DSA), we addressed critical computational complexity without compromising long-context performance. By augmenting the computational budget, DeepSeek-V3.2 achieves comparable performance with GPT-5 on reasoning benchmarks. Furthermore, the incorporation of our large-scale agentic task synthesis pipeline substantially enhances tool-use proficiency, thereby unlocking novel opportunities for the development of robust and adaptable AI systems utilizing open LLMs. Additionally, our high-compute variant, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale, has achieved gold-medal recognition in prestigious competitions such as the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) and the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI), signifying a significant advancement for open LLMs. Nonetheless, we acknowledge certain limitations of this variant in comparison to state-of-the-art closed-source models, such as Gemini-3.0-Pro. For instance, due to a reduced number of total training FLOPs, the extent of general knowledge encompassed by DeepSeek-V3.2 remains inferior to that of the leading proprietary models.
