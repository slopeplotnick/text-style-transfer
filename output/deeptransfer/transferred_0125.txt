OMNI-GUARD-7B consistently outperforms strong baselines in addressing safety tasks across text, vision, and audio modalities, including various recent MLLMs and OLLMs developed with safety alignment, as well as state-of-the-art specialized guardrail models. Meanwhile, the more compact OMNI-GUARD-3B also achieves competitive or superior results compared to recent models such as GPT-4o, Qwen3-235B, and Qwen3-VL-235B. Analyses further indicate that the integration of reasoning-based omni-modal guardrails significantly enhances the consistency, explainability, and trustworthiness of moderation processes, marking a substantial advancement toward the development of safe and reliable omni-modal AI systems. Overall, our contributions can be summarized as follows: We introduce OMNIGUARD, the first family of omni-modal guardrail models capable of performing unified safety moderation across text, images, videos, and audio with deliberate reasoning. Additionally, we have developed a unified training framework that employs omni-modal targeted distillation to endow the model with deliberate and explainable omni-modal safety reasoning capabilities. Extensive experiments demonstrate that OMNIGUARD achieves state-of-the-art accuracy, robust generalization, and enhanced explainability compared to prior guardrails. The progression of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) has spurred growing attention toward omni-modal language models (OLLMs), which are capable of simultaneously processing inputs from multiple modalities and flexibly generating outputs across these modalities. Unlike earlier practices that assembled separately pretrained unimodal components, OLLMs are trained end-to-end on multimodal data, enabling them to acquire native capabilities for unified understanding and generation across text, audio, image, and video modalities. The prevailing paradigm of these models involves mapping heterogeneous inputs into a shared latent space, which aligns different modalities and allows cross-modality reasoning. Models such as Qwen2.5-Omni and LLaMA-Omni feature real-time, end-to-end streaming generation of both text and speech. NExT-OMNI even extends these capabilities further into "any-to-any" cross-modal generation and understanding. However, these OLMs face safety challenges due to parameter misalignment, which may lead to potentially dangerous use cases.
