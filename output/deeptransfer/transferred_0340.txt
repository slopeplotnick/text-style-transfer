We evaluate the performance of episodic memory retrieval across diverse temporal scales of events using temporal intersection over union (tIoU), a metric that quantifies the overlap between retrieved and ground truth segments as the ratio of their intersection to their union duration. To this end, we compare WorldMM with various models across tasks such as temporal grounding, single-modality retrieval, long-form egocentric video retrieval, and keyframe selection. Further details on the baseline methods are provided in Sec. C.1. As shown in Tab. 3, WorldMM achieves significantly higher tIoU scores compared to strong baselines. Notably, reasoning-based retrieval and keyframe selection methods exhibit lower tIoU values, underscoring their limitations in effectively handling extended input contexts. Furthermore, Fig. 5 demonstrates that higher tIoU scores are directly correlated with improved overall accuracy, particularly in the context of understanding long video sequences as opposed to single-step retrieval. This improvement arises from the iterative retrieval mechanism, which allows the system to gather additional relevant information and refine its retrieval strategy when earlier attempts are suboptimal. A specific example of this iterative refinement is detailed in Sec. E.2, where the model corrects an initial retrieval error to produce a more accurate and contextually grounded response. To evaluate the computational efficiency of the proposed framework, we measure the end-to-end latency of WorldMM on 100 randomly sampled queries from the EgoLifeQA dataset. As shown in Fig. 6, WorldMM achieves a superior balance between latency and accuracy compared to baseline methods. While long-video LLMs incur significantly higher inference latency and deliver relatively lower performance, RAG-based approaches, although faster, require substantial preprocessing and exhibit a notable performance gap. In contrast, WorldMM adaptively terminates its iterative retrieval process and selectively retrieves only the most relevant segments, achieving both low latency and high accuracy. To facilitate effective reasoning over extended video content, WorldMM employs distinct strategies tailored to each memory type.
