Therefore, OptPO-SFT employs the same update mechanism as TTSFT but determines the number of iterations through a Bayesian inference approach, thereby enhancing both efficiency and reliability. Both the reinforcement learning-based and supervised fine-tuning-based variants of OptPO adhere to a unified three-stage process: initiating the procedure, computing a label via a specified methodology, and subsequently updating the parameters. The distinction lies in their update mechanisms: one employs policy-gradient optimization, while the other utilizes cross-entropy fine-tuning; however, their overall operational principles remain analogous. For evaluation, we adopt challenging mathematical and scientific benchmarks, including MATH-500, comprising 500 high school-level problems; AIME 2024, featuring 30 pre-Olympiad-level problems; AMC, derived from the American Mathematics Competitions; and GPQA, which includes complex graduate-level science questions. To assess the performance of OptPO, we conduct experiments on widely used open-source models, such as Qwen2.5-Math1.5B, Qwen2.5-7B, and Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct, to evaluate its efficacy across varying model scales and architectures. When verifying the effectiveness of TTSFT and OptPO-SFT, we employed Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B and Llama3.1-8B-Instruct as our primary models. OptPO is a versatile framework that can be seamlessly integrated into various existing reinforcement learning (RL) systems to enhance test-time training efficiency. Moreover, we incorporated it into PPO, GRPO, and Reinforce++ to assess its performance comprehensively. We conducted a comparative analysis with TTRL, the predominant method for policy optimization during test-time. Furthermore, we assessed the efficacy of OptPO-SFT under analogous conditions. The critic's learning rate is configured at 1e-5. In the context of SFT-based training methodologies, encompassing both TTSFT and OptPO-SFT, gradient descent is employed on the pseudo-labeled batch utilizing the AdamW optimizer, with a learning rate set at 2e-5.
