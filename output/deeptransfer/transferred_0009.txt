(1) Synchronization. We evaluate the temporal correspondence between the source and edited videos using four distinct metrics: speech, gaze, blink, and pose. Each metric captures a specific aspect of motion dynamics: mouth movements for speech, iris trajectories for gaze, blink frequency for blinking, and upper-body joint angles for pose. While these metrics are identical to those used for dataset filtering (subsection 3.2), they are solely employed for data curation and final evaluation. Importantly, they are not utilized as loss functions or integrated into the model's training process. (2) Edit Fidelity. To assess whether the intended edits are accurately applied and remain consistent throughout the video, we employ two complementary CLIP-based metrics. Directional CLIP Score. Originally designed for image editing, we extend this metric to the video domain to evaluate how consistently the per-frame visual transformations align with the overall edit direction in CLIP's embedding space. This score is further divided into two variants: an image-based version, which measures alignment with the visual changes implied by the transition between the unedited and edited first frames, and a text-dual version, which evaluates alignment with the semantic edits described by the textual prompt. The text-dual variant ensures fairness for methods such as LucyEdit, which rely exclusively on textual guidance and do not utilize the edited first frame. Put simply, the image-based variant asks whether the visual changes across frames resemble those between the unedited and edited first frames, while the text-dual variant examines whether the changes align with the modifications suggested by the textual prompt. CLIP-Text Alignment Score. To further evaluate semantic fidelity, we measure how well each generated frame adheres to the textual description in CLIP space. This score ensures that the video remains faithful to the prompt semantics over time, complementing the Directional CLIP Score by capturing the consistency of the intended semantic content throughout the video.
