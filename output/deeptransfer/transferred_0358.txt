DeepSeek-V3.2 achieves similar performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across multiple reasoning benchmarks. Moreover, it significantly advances the agentic capabilities of open models, demonstrating exceptional proficiency in the long-tail agent tasks introduced in EvalSys (2025) and other contemporary studies. As a highly cost-efficient alternative in agent scenarios, DeepSeek-V3.2 effectively narrows the performance gap between open and leading proprietary models while maintaining substantially lower costs. To push the boundaries of open models in the reasoning domain, we developed DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale by extending the permissible context length. Consequently, DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale achieves performance parity with the leading closed-source system, Gemini-3.0-Pro, and demonstrates gold-medal performance in competitive benchmarks like the IOI, ICPC World Final, IMO, and CMO in 2025. Notably, DeepSeek-V3.2 uses the same architecture as DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, ensuring consistency across implementations. The only architectural modification from the previous iteration, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, is the introduction of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) through continued training. The DSA framework primarily consists of two components: a lightning indexer and a fine-grained token selection mechanism. Starting from the base checkpoint of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, which features an extended context length of 128K, we engaged in continued pre-training followed by post-training to develop DeepSeek-V3.2. The continued pre-training of DeepSeek-V3.2 consists of two stages, with training data fully aligned with the extended context data used for DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. In September 2025, we evaluated DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp on a comprehensive suite of benchmarks, comparing it with DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus and showing similar performance. While DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp significantly improves computational efficiency on long sequences, it does not exhibit substantial performance degradation compared to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus on both short- and long-context tasks.
