Procedure. The experiment lasted approximately 20 to 35 minutes. Initially, volunteers were asked to select a picture of their interest for a writing task, ensuring they fully understood its content. Subsequently, participants were introduced to the functionalities and operational features of TaleFrame. They were then instructed to compose a narrative based on their selected picture. Throughout the experiment, their actions and verbal responses were systematically observed and recorded. Upon completion of the task, participants were requested to provide feedback through a series of questions. Each participant received a remuneration of $5 for their involvement in the study. User Feedback. Participants evaluated their experience with TaleFrame using a 5-point Likert scale. ① Framework Construction (Q1-Q3): TaleFrame facilitates the construction of a comprehensive narrative framework through an intuitive interface and interactive options for defining key story components. This allows users to systematically organize narrative elements and visually represent their interconnections, thereby enhancing the comprehensibility of the story structure. ② Narrative Visualization (Q4-Q6): TaleFrame supports users in intuitively constructing and arranging entities, events, and relationships through interactive methods. It clearly visualizes the connections between these elements, making the story structure easy to understand. Moreover, flexible tools are provided to help users refine and optimize the narrative framework throughout the creation process. ③ Narrative Timing and Coherence (Q7-Q9): TaleFrame ensures the consistency of the narrative timeline and provides specific recommendations for enhancing emotional expression and thematic depth. It evaluates the sequence of events, maintains consistency in the progression of entity behaviors over time, and identifies potential temporal flaws in the story structure. Additionally, it offers support for creating plot events or entity relationships that better align with the timeline. ④ Evaluation and Improvement Suggestions (Q10-Q12): TaleFrame evaluates the quality of the story framework and offers actionable suggestions for optimization, thereby facilitating improvements in the overall narrative quality.
