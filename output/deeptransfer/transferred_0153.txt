We adopted a Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) paradigm with the Qwen-1.5 model as the backbone architecture. This approach effectively transferred general language understanding capabilities to domain-specific e-commerce scenarios. To enhance the model's domain adaptation, our methodology integrated knowledge graphs with In-Context Learning techniques, wherein domain-specific knowledge was systematically infused through carefully designed contextual prompts. Consequently, the model was able to assimilate e-commerce-specific semantic patterns and entity relationships through upper-context learning mechanisms. For evaluation, we employed standardized benchmark protocols alongside relevance-based metrics to ensure comprehensive assessment of the model's performance across diverse query understanding tasks, thereby guaranteeing both accuracy and robustness. Following training, the model was deployed as a high-precision offline judge for label annotation tasks. This deployment not only expedited annotation processes but also established an effective mechanism for bad case detection, significantly improving data quality and operational efficiency in downstream workflows. Building on these foundational capabilities, the model was further tasked with addressing more complex challenges, such as recognizing intricate query semantics (including existence, implicit meanings, and negations) and performing deep semantic reasoning for path construction. However, simple instruction-following proved inadequate for these multi-step reasoning tasks, which required enhanced logical inference capabilities. To overcome this limitation, we introduced the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) methodology. By enabling the model to decompose hierarchical relationships, identify complex entities and semantics, and systematically perform path comparisons, this approach significantly improved the model's accuracy in handling complex query discrimination. Furthermore, we developed a data synthesis framework to identify challenging cases and iteratively optimize the model through feedback mechanisms. Finally, we synthesized CoT data for attribute extraction and matching tasks, utilizing the SFT paradigm with Qwen-2 as the backbone architecture. This methodology facilitated the generation of intermediate reasoning steps while ensuring task-specific optimization through targeted fine-tuning.
