The model benefits most from learning through its own mistakes; however, the inclusion of highly off-policy negative samples can be counterproductive, potentially misleading or destabilizing the optimization process. Empirical observations reveal that Off-Policy Sequence Masking enhances stability in training scenarios that might otherwise exhibit instability. Keep Routing Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models improve computational efficiency by selectively activating a subset of expert modules during inference. Nonetheless, discrepancies between inference and training frameworks, compounded by policy updates, can lead to inconsistent expert routing even for identical inputs. This inconsistency causes abrupt shifts in the active parameter subspace, destabilizing optimization and intensifying off-policy challenges. To address this, we preserve the expert routing paths established during inference and enforce their use during training, ensuring consistent optimization of identical expert parameters. This Keep Routing operation has proven essential for maintaining RL training stability in MoE models and has been integrated into our RL training pipeline since DeepSeek-V3-0324. DeepSeek-R1 demonstrated that incorporating a cognitive process significantly enhances a model’s ability to solve complex problems. Building on this insight, we aim to integrate cognitive capabilities into tool-calling scenarios. However, replicating DeepSeek-R1’s strategy—discarding reasoning content upon the arrival of a second round of messages—results in considerable token inefficiency, as it forces the model to redundantly reprocess the entire problem for each subsequent tool invocation. To resolve this, we developed a context management system tailored for tool-calling scenarios, as illustrated in Fig 4: Historical reasoning content is discarded only when a new user message is introduced into the conversation, while reasoning content is retained if only tool-related messages, such as tool outputs, are appended. Furthermore, when reasoning traces are removed, the historical record of tool calls and their outcomes remains preserved within the context.
