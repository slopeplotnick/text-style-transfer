Online shoppers often articulate requests encompassing multiple attributes simultaneously, such as brand, price range, functionality, or target audience. Therefore, it is imperative for the system to process these attributes collectively rather than in isolation, ensuring their proper integration and organization. This approach enables the system to effectively determine the most relevant items to display and align user requests with appropriate product offerings. However, it is crucial not to determine the extent of reliance on various data types, such as textual or visual information, solely based on the user's inquiry. Instead, the system should analyze the specific details present within the inquiry and align them with the attributes of the items. Consequently, it should consider this information in the decision-making process regarding the presentation of results to the user. Fundamentally, the system operates by establishing a pathway from the user's inquiry to the identified items. Initially, the system deconstructs the question into specific components and utilizes these components to filter and select relevant items. This procedure comprises two distinct phases: firstly, ensuring the system comprehends the question and aligns it with the appropriate item details, and secondly, ensuring the accurate selection of items based on these details. The complexity of these steps often presents challenges and can lead to ambiguities. For instance, when a user queries "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set," the system must ascertain the user's intent and align it with the appropriate product attributes. The initial phase involves correlating the attributes specified in the query with those of the items. The challenge lies in ensuring the system accurately aligns all relevant attributes.
