In online shopping scenarios, user queries often encapsulate multiple dimensions of requirements simultaneously, such as brand, price range, functionality, and target user group. Therefore, the system must not only identify these dimensions individually but also extract and structure them collectively from a multi-dimensional perspective. This comprehensive understanding enables the system to effectively align user requests with appropriate items and optimize the presentation of results. However, it is crucial not to determine the extent of reliance on various data types, such as textual or visual data, solely based on the user's inquiry. Instead, the system should carefully analyze the specific details within the inquiry and align them with the attributes of the items. Subsequently, this information must be strategically utilized in the decision-making process regarding the presentation of information to the user. Fundamentally, the system operates by establishing a pathway from the user's inquiry to the relevant items identified. Initially, the system deconstructs the query into specific components, which are then used to filter and select relevant items. This procedure encompasses two distinct phases. First, the system must comprehend the query and map it to the appropriate item attributes. Second, it must ensure that the selection of items is accurately based on these attributes. Nevertheless, the complexity of these steps can introduce challenges in clarity and execution. For instance, when a query such as "Summer Mint Mambo Slimming Set" is submitted, the system must determine the user's intent and map it to the corresponding product attributes. The initial phase involves correlating the attributes specified in the query with those of the available items. The challenge, however, lies in ensuring that the system accurately aligns all relevant attributes.
