System & Strategy Update: We fully leverage advanced AI tools to upgrade both online strategies and system infrastructure. From a strategic perspective, we integrate the more reliable query-item relevance scores generated by the enhanced model into the downstream ranking module, achieving an optimal trade-off through Pareto optimization. From an infrastructure perspective, supported by the model's robust performance guarantees, we systematically retire or replace heuristic rules previously utilized in the pipeline. The combined implementation of these updates has demonstrated substantial improvements. For example, studies such as DeepSeek-R1 have observed the emergence of long chains-of-thought and the model's "Aha Moment," where abrupt problem-solving breakthroughs occur. Furthermore, we conducted an analysis of the dynamic changes in response length throughout the training process, with results illustrated in Figure 17. The findings indicate that as the model's capabilities improved, its responses became increasingly concise and eventually stabilized. This suggests that extended reasoning steps are not essential for enhancing model capabilities but rather emerge as a byproduct during training. Moreover, further analysis reveals that reasoning chain length requirements vary significantly across tasks. Specifically, tasks such as mathematical computation and code generation demand longer, step-by-step reasoning processes. In contrast, for relevance tasks, which involve accurately identifying query intent and item attributes, a straightforward and clear reasoning process is most effective, as additional reasoning steps do not contribute to performance gains. For tasks involving visual data, it is more efficient to directly utilize models with inherent image comprehension capabilities, such as Vision-Language Models (VLMs), rather than introducing supplementary processes like caption generation. To explore this approach, we employed the teacher model Qwen2.5-VL-72B to execute a two-step process: first generating knowledge and reasoning chains of thought, followed by applying rule-based reasoning.
