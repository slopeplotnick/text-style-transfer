The safety assessment for image-only harmfulness evaluation classified all samples as safe, demonstrating the narrow focus of existing guardrail systems. In the audio domain, OMNIGUARD-7B and OMNIGUARD-3B achieved superior performance across audio safety benchmarks. Since audio guardrails remain largely unexplored, our model provides a strong solution to the field and demonstrates that reasoning-based safety training can effectively generalize to the audio modality. We further evaluated OMNIGUARD in cross-modal safety scenarios to assess its capability to reason across modalities. As illustrated in various benchmarks, including MM-SafetyBench, Video-SafetyBench, and AIAH, the OMNIGUARD models exhibited commendable performance, affirming their generalizable safety reasoning capabilities across diverse media modalities. Compared to the Qwen2.5-Omni-7B model, OMNIGUARD-7B consistently achieved significant improvements in accuracy across all evaluations, highlighting its proficiency in managing heterogeneous media types concurrently. Moreover, the more compact OMNIGUARD-3B performed comparably to larger-scale models such as Qwen3-VL-235B in certain assessments, despite its reduced parameter count. These findings confirm that OMNIGUARD effectively generalizes safety reasoning across modalities, offering a scalable and parameter-efficient solution for cross-modal alignment. To examine whether reasoning-based safety training enhances the performance of omni-modal guardrails and addresses the additional complexity introduced by omni-modal safety reasoning, we conducted further studies on the 7B model across four modalities. We compared our OMNIGUARD-7B with the original Qwen2.5-Omni-7B model and its Label-only SFT variant, which is fine-tuned solely on safety classification labels. From these results, we draw several observations: (1) Both supervised fine-tuning methods improved performance over the base model across all unimodal benchmarks (text, image, video, and audio), showing that simple safety fine-tuning can enhance multimodal moderation capabilities. (2) Compared to the Label-only SFT variant, our model achieved superior performance, highlighting the efficacy of incorporating curated reasoning traces in the fine-tuning process.
