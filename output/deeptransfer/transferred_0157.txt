In the audio domain, OMNIGUARD-7B and OMNIGUARD-3B demonstrated superior performance across audio safety benchmarks. Since audio safety remains largely unexplored, our model provides a robust solution and demonstrates that reasoning-based safety training can effectively generalize to the audio modality. Furthermore, we evaluated OMNIGUARD in cross-modality scenarios to assess its capability to integrate across diverse media types. Our OMNIGUARD models consistently exhibited superior performance across various safety benchmarks, including MM-SafetyBench, Video-SafetyBench, and AIAH, underscoring their efficacy in ensuring safety across multiple media modalities. Compared to the Qwen2.5-Omni-7B model, OMNIGUARD-7B demonstrated superior accuracy across all evaluations, highlighting its capability to effectively manage heterogeneous media types. Notably, the more compact OMNIGUARD-3B achieved performance levels comparable to larger models such as Qwen3-VL-235B in certain assessments, despite its reduced complexity. These findings affirm OMNIGUARD's proficiency in omni-modal safety reasoning and its viability as a robust solution for integrated media management. To evaluate the impact of reasoning-based safety training on the performance of omni-modal guardrails and address the additional complexity introduced by multi-modal safety reasoning, we conducted further investigations using the OMNIGUARD-7B model across four distinct modalities. Specifically, we compared the performance of OMNIGUARD-7B against the baseline Qwen2.5-Omni-7B model and a variant fine-tuned exclusively on safety labels. Several observations can be drawn from the analysis: (1) Both supervised fine-tuning methodologies demonstrate enhanced performance relative to the original base model across all unimodal benchmarks, encompassing text, image, video, and audio domains. This indicates that straightforward safety fine-tuning can augment multimodal moderation capabilities. (2) In comparison to the variant fine-tuned exclusively on safety classification labels, our model exhibits superior performance.
