In this section, we establish the experimental framework employed in this study. We define the datasets, solvers, and verifiers, introduce the metrics used to evaluate solver and verifier performance, and specify the verification configurations adopted in our analysis. Notably, verifier gain is an asymptotic metric, reflecting the limit of infinite verification processes and serving as a bound on the potential improvement achievable through verifier-based rejection sampling. We use verifier gain throughout this work to assess the performance of various verifiers. Models are grouped into families, such as Llama3 and Qwen2.5, where each family comprises related models of varying sizes. Given the substantial behavioral differences between foundational and enhanced models, we classify them into separate categories. For instance, the foundational model meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-70B is classified under the Llama3-Base family, while the enhanced model meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct belongs to the Llama3 family. Each solver-verifier pair from our ensemble of models is categorized into one of three verification configurations. Specifically, each solver-verifier pair is considered an instance of: 1. Self-Verification, where the solver and verifier are identical, allowing the model to verify its own outputs. For example, when a 70B Llama3 model functions as both solver and verifier, metrics such as accuracy are assessed within this singular configuration. 2. Intra-Family Verification, where the verifier evaluates outputs generated by models within the same family. For instance, a 70B Llama3 verifier may assess solutions produced by 8B or 13B Llama3 solvers, with the metric averaged across all within-family solvers, excluding self-verification instances. 3. Cross-Family Verification, where the verifier evaluates solutions generated by models from distinct groups. For example, a base Llama3 verifier may evaluate outputs from Qwen3 or an enhanced Llama3 model, with the average metric computed across all these cross-group solvers.
