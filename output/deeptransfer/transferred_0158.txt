Llama-4 Maverick exhibits superior performance compared to the Bayesian reliability-weighted combiner, a normative framework frequently utilized in biological studies. This approach, which humans also employ, integrates information based on the relative reliability of cues. The enhanced performance of Llama-4 Maverick suggests the potential use of additional computational mechanisms that extend beyond those typically associated with this established method. Figure 7 presents a comparative analysis of Llama-4 Maverick's responses in multimodal sensory integration versus unimodal sensory processing. The findings indicate that a non-linear methodology surpasses linear approaches in efficacy. In noisy scenarios, Bayesian strategies emerge as optimal, serving as a benchmark for enhancing the performance of language models that currently exhibit suboptimal capabilities. Moreover, Figure 8 illustrates that models with higher accuracy tend to align more closely with Bayesian methodologies. Although Gemma 3 4B and Phi 4 Multimodal do not achieve the same level of accuracy, they nonetheless demonstrate satisfactory performance. An analysis of performance across various tasks and models is detailed in Appendix A.9. Our findings indicate that language models exhibit diverse behavioral patterns when evaluated on tasks inspired by human perceptual processes. High-performing models, such as GPT-5 Mini, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, and Llama-4 Maverick, demonstrate stronger evidence of employing Bayesian strategies, particularly in multimodal contexts involving visual stimuli (refer to Appendix A.11 for further details). These models exhibit adaptive responses consistent with Bayesian principles when confronted with variations such as noise or additional contextual information, effectively leveraging prior knowledge despite the absence of explicit training for such tasks. Analogous to human cognition, Bayesian models elucidate a substantial portion of perceptual phenomena (Petzschner et al., 2015; Knill & Pouget, 2004) without explicit training. It is pertinent to note that our current analysis is confined to behavioral aspects, and a comprehensive mechanistic or causal elucidation of these behaviors necessitates further investigation.
