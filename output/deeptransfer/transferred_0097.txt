Node 6: Retry with Feedback Integration This node manages retry logic with a maximum of three attempts per task, utilizing enhanced prompts that merge the original context with targeted feedback to refine reasoning during re-attempts. The phase-adaptive strategies are implemented through advanced LLM integration, which translates strategic weights into contextual reasoning guidance. To ensure alignment with project priorities, the prompt engineering system dynamically adjusts instructions based on the developmental phase. For example, in the early stages, prompts focus on maximizing success rates with directives such as "prioritize robots with the highest success rates" and "accept moderate workload imbalances." Conversely, in the later stages, the emphasis shifts to workload equity, with instructions like "favor workload fairness as the primary objective." This adaptive prompt mechanism enables the LLM to naturally apply the strategic frameworks defined for each phase. The authors operationalize this integration through three mechanisms: (1) probabilistic modeling of robot capabilities to compute task-specific success rates, (2) structured prompt generation embedding quantitative priors and workload context, and (3) standardized response formatting to ensure reliable parsing and validation. The robotic system includes three categories—Light, Medium, and Heavy—each with distinct operational strengths and probabilistic success profiles. These profiles are derived by evaluating the alignment between a robot's functional capabilities and the specific requirements of a task. Following the formulation of Faruq et al. (2018), the overall success rate for each robot type is calculated by aggregating feature-conditioned success probabilities. Thus, a robot's ability to complete a task depends on the overlap between the task's required features and the robot's capability distribution. Tasks are processed sequentially from a queue initialized with all 36 tasks from the TEACH dataset (Padmakumar et al. 2022). Each task undergoes the full LangGraph workflow, including phase determination, prompt generation, reasoning, validation, and assignment, before moving to the next task. Upon completing each task, the system updates progress metrics and decrements the queue length, iterating until all tasks are successfully assigned.
