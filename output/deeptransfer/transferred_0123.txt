Mathematical Reasoning. We use five benchmarks to evaluate the ability of models to solve mathematical problems: AMC, Minerva (Lewkowycz et al., 2022), MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021), and Olympiad-Bench (He et al., 2024). For evaluation, we follow prior methodologies (e.g., Zhao et al., 2025c; Ma et al., 2025) and employ GPT-4o as a programmatic judge to semantically compare the model-generated solutions with the ground truth. General Domain Reasoning. To assess general reasoning capabilities, we evaluate model performance across diverse disciplines, such as physics, biology, business, economics, and law, using benchmarks including MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024), SuperGPQA (Du et al., 2025), GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al.), and BBEH (Shoaa Kazemi et al., 2025). These benchmarks are designed to test complex reasoning skills and present higher levels of difficulty, thereby offering a more rigorous evaluation of general reasoning. For evaluation, we report Exact Match (EM) accuracy obtained via greedy decoding, following the methodology outlined by Ma et al. (2025). To analyze the contribution of key components, we conduct a detailed ablation study on the Qwen3-8B-Base model, systematically disabling individual modules and measuring their impact on both mathematical and general reasoning tasks. The results, summarized in Table 2, reveal that disabling challenger training leads to the most significant performance drop, reducing Math and General averages by 1.9 and 1.0 points, respectively. Similarly, removing challenger warm-up and curriculum learning results in noticeable declines, highlighting the importance of these components for robust performance. Notably, mathematical tasks exhibit greater sensitivity to these changes than general reasoning tasks. This trend aligns with observations in Table 1, where math benchmarks show higher performance variability across different methods. Overall, the findings suggest that while heterogeneous human data generally improves performance, domain-specific data sampling is particularly effective in enhancing the targeted domain. This insight is practically significant, as it allows developers to strategically guide model improvements by prioritizing specific data types, thereby maintaining alignment with intended objectives and minimizing the risk of unintended concept drift.
