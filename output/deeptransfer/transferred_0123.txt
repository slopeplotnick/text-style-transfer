Mathematical Reasoning. We use five benchmarks to evaluate model performance on mathematical problems: AMC, Minerva (Lewkowycz et al., 2022), MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021), and Olympiad-Bench (He et al., 2024). For evaluation, we follow prior methodologies (e.g., Zhao et al., 2025c; Ma et al., 2025) and employ GPT-4o as a programmatic judge to semantically verify the correctness of final answers against the ground truth. General Domain Reasoning. To assess general reasoning capabilities across diverse disciplines, including physics, biology, business, economics, and law, we evaluate model performance on benchmarks such as MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024), SuperGPQA (Du et al., 2025), GPQA-Diamond (Rein et al.), and BBEH (Shoaa Kazemi et al., 2025). These benchmarks present challenging tasks that rigorously test complex reasoning abilities. For evaluation, we adopt the methodology of Ma et al. (2025) and report Exact Match (EM) accuracy using greedy decoding. To better understand the contribution of key components, we conduct a comprehensive ablation study on the Qwen3-8B-Base model, systematically disabling specific modules and measuring their impact on both mathematical and general reasoning tasks. The results, summarized in Table 2, reveal that disabling challenger training leads to the most significant degradation, reducing Math and General averages by 1.9 and 1.0 points, respectively. Similarly, removing challenger warm-up and curriculum learning results in performance declines, emphasizing the importance of these components. Notably, mathematical tasks exhibit greater sensitivity to these changes compared to general reasoning tasks. This trend aligns with the observations in Table 1, where mathematical benchmarks show higher performance variance across different methodologies. Overall, the findings highlight that while heterogeneous human data improves general performance, domain-specific data sampling is particularly effective in enhancing performance within targeted domains. This insight underscores the potential for developers to strategically guide model optimization by curating domain-specific datasets, thereby ensuring focused learning, reducing unintended concept drift, and maintaining alignment between training inputs and emergent skills.
