To effectively leverage multilingual models for diverse tasks, we exploit the inherent structural relationships among languages, which naturally facilitate efficient cross-lingual information sharing. In multilingual transformer models such as XLM-RoBERTa (XLM-R), languages that exhibit semantic or syntactic similarity, or share orthographic and script-based features, are naturally embedded in close proximity within the representational space. The XLM-R model, pre-trained on 100 languages encompassing both high-resource and low-resource languages, provides a comprehensive foundation for multilingual generalization. Instead of requiring data for every individual language, we strategically select a subset of widely spoken languages for training purposes. This approach enables the extrapolation of learned knowledge to a broader linguistic spectrum, thereby facilitating cross-lingual knowledge transfer without the necessity of training on less common languages. Building on this strategy, we translated the MultiJail dataset into all 100 target languages using state-of-the-art translation systems tailored to each specific language. For widely spoken languages, we employ advanced translation models such as GPT-4o, M2MBart-50 (Tang et al. 2020), and Helsinki-NLP OpusMT (Tiedemann and Thottingal 2020), which have demonstrated robust performance in high-resource settings. However, these models exhibit limited efficacy when applied to low-resource languages. To address this limitation, we utilize SarvamTranslate (AI 2025), a cutting-edge translation model specifically optimized for Indic languages, achieving high translation accuracy even in low-resource scenarios. For other low-resource languages, we leverage GPT-4.1 (OpenAI et al. 2024) to ensure reliable translation outputs. Additionally, to validate translation quality, particularly for low-resource languages, we conduct supplementary evaluations using Google Translate. Through the integration of these models, we achieve translations that are fluent, high-quality, and precise.
