To effectively adapt multilingual models for various tasks, we exploit the inherent structural relationships among languages, which naturally facilitate seamless cross-lingual information exchange. When encoded in the representational space of multilingual transformer models such as XLM-RoBERTa (XLM-R), languages that are semantically or syntactically similar, or that share script and orthographic patterns, tend to be embedded in close proximity. We begin with the 100-language configuration of the XLM-R model, which encompasses a diverse range of both high- and low-resource languages. Rather than requiring extensive supervised data for each individual language, we adopt a strategic approach by selecting a subset of widely spoken languages for training. This enables the transfer of learned representations across languages, thereby facilitating cross-lingual generalization by leveraging shared linguistic structures and eliminating the need for direct training on low-resource languages. Building on this strategy, we translated the MultiJail (2024) dataset into all 100 target languages using a combination of state-of-the-art machine translation systems tailored to specific languages. For widely spoken languages, we employ advanced translation models such as GPT-4o (OpenAI et al. 2024), M2MBart-50 (Tang et al. 2020), and Helsinki-NLP OpusMT (Tiedemann and Thottingal 2020). However, these models demonstrate limited efficacy when applied to low-resource languages. To address this challenge, we utilize SarvamTranslate (AI 2025), a state-of-the-art translation model specifically designed for Indic languages, which achieves robust performance even for underrepresented languages in this group. For other low-resource languages, we rely on GPT-4.1 (OpenAI et al. 2024) to ensure translation quality. Moreover, to validate the accuracy of translations, particularly for low-resource languages, we conduct cross-verification using Google Translate. By integrating these models, we produce translations that are fluent, high-quality, and precise.
