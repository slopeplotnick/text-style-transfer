Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods address the growing size of Large Language Models (LLMs). However, many newly introduced PEFT methods are difficult to replicate, deploy, or compare effectively. To tackle these challenges, we present PEFT-FACTORY, a unified framework for fine-tuning LLMs using both off-the-shelf and custom PEFT methods. While its modular design ensures flexibility and extensibility, it also includes a representative set of 19 PEFT methods, 27 datasets for classification and text generation across 12 tasks, and evaluation tools for both standard and PEFT-specific metrics. Consequently, PEFT-FACTORY offers a reliable, controlled, and user-friendly environment that enhances replicability and facilitates the benchmarking of PEFT methods. Originating from the widely used LLaMAFactory framework, PEFT-FACTORY is publicly accessible at https://github.com/kinit-sk/PEFT-Factory. Large Language Models (LLMs) (Minaee et al., 2024; Radford et al., 2019; Dubey et al., 2024; Raffel et al., 2020) have achieved remarkable performance across a wide range of Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks, particularly after the introduction of the transformer architecture (Vaswani, 2017). Nevertheless, their exceptional scaling capabilities (Kaplan et al., 2020) have led to a continuous increase in the number of trainable parameters. This rapid growth has made fine-tuning computationally expensive, data-intensive, and less accessible to many researchers and practitioners. Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) methods (Xu et al., 2023; Ding et al., 2023; Lialin et al., 2023; Han et al., 2024) aim to address these challenges by training only a small subset of the model's parameters while maintaining performance comparable to full model fine-tuning. This reduction in trainable parameters can be achieved by introducing new parameters (Houlsby et al., 2019), selectively training specific parameters (Ben Zaken et al., 2022), or reparameterizing the model to use fewer parameters (Hu et al., 2022).
