This section explores joint generative translation models that integrate both speech and text modalities to enable seamless and high-quality translation across languages. It provides a detailed examination of state-of-the-art voice-interfaced language models (Reid et al., 2024; Chu et al., 2023; Radford et al., 2023) and their capabilities in processing and understanding speech and conversational signals with exceptional precision. Over the past decade, significant strides have been made in the development and deployment of large-scale voice-powered AI virtual assistants, beginning with Siri (SRI/Apple) and followed by Cortana (Microsoft), Google Assistant, Alexa (Amazon), and Viv/Bixby (Samsung). This tutorial will delve into the technological advancements that have shaped this era of AI virtual assistants, including intent detection and slot filling with recurrent neural networks (Mesnil et al., 2014), dialogue management through reinforcement learning (Shah et al., 2016), leveraging web-scale query-click data (Hakkani-Tür et al., 2011; Tur et al., 2011), exploiting the semantic web (Heck and Hakkani-Tür, 2012), and incorporating knowledge graphs for enhanced spoken language comprehension (Huang et al., 2015). Building on these foundations, current efforts in AI virtual assistants have shifted toward leveraging large language models to significantly enhance the coverage and robustness of spoken language understanding and dialogue state tracking, alongside notable advancements in spoken language generation. Moreover, this tutorial will provide an overview of contemporary language models and their adaptation to downstream tasks (Ni et al., 2021; Qin et al., 2023; Yi et al., 2024), highlighting recent progress in multi-turn dialogue systems, including both open-domain dialogue (ODD) and task-oriented dialogue (TOD) applications. Furthermore, it will examine relevant datasets and evaluation methodologies while addressing emerging research challenges in the development of large language models, particularly in response to the growing demand for systems capable of managing simultaneous conversational interactions.
