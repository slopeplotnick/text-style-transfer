The parsing system extracts structured allocation decisions from LLM markdown responses using regex pattern matching. This text parsing technique systematically identifies specific formatted sections within the LLM's markdown output, such as allocation decisions, success percentages, and reasoning explanations, and converts them into structured data for validation. To ensure the integrity of the extracted data, a comprehensive validation system applies eight rules, including the elimination of invalid allocations, verification of trade-off analyses, assessment of workload awareness, and evaluation of logical consistency. However, the limitations of traditional rule-based validation systems, which often fail to account for the nuanced reasoning and contextual sensitivity required for LLM-driven task allocation, necessitated the transition to a weighted validation framework. Recent studies on LLM evaluation (Guo et al. 2023, Evaluation and Benchmarking of LLM Agents, 2025) underscore that multi-aspect, criterion-based assessment provides a more robust and reliable measure of model reasoning performance compared to binary correctness checks. Building on these findings, the proposed validation system incorporates a weighted scoring approach that evaluates reasoning outputs across multiple dimensions, including explanation clarity, success-rate consistency, workload awareness, trade-off justification, phase compliance, and confidence alignment. This multidimensional evaluation framework ensures a balanced assessment of both the technical precision and interpretive quality of the model's decision-making processes. Each dimension contributes proportionally to a composite quality score, with greater weight assigned to factors that directly influence allocation outcomes, such as reasoning quality and success-rate accuracy, while secondary aspects like confidence justification are assigned lower weights. Consequently, this configuration ensures that the validation process remains both performance-sensitive and context-aware, effectively balancing interpretability and operational rigor in evaluating the model's reasoning behavior. The scoring dimensions are as follows: 1. Explanation Quality (Weight: 0.20): Reasoning depth and clarity. 2. Success Rate Accuracy (Weight: 0.20): Alignment between predicted and actual success probabilities. 3. Trade-off Analysis (Weight: 0.15): Explicit consideration of competing objectives. 4. Workload Awareness (Weight: 0.15): Consideration of current task distribution.
