Cross-LLM Agreement We establish pairs of LLM judges, such as GPT-4o and Gemini-2.5-pro, to assess the degree of concordance in their belief evaluations. Given the extensive dataset available from GPT-4o, albeit limited from other models, GPT-4o is designated as the primary judge for all comparative analyses. Human-LLM Agreement To evaluate the concordance between AI judges and human assessments, we engage human evaluators to perform belief evaluations analogous to those conducted by the AI systems. We then construct pairs of human-LLM judges, exemplified by pairings such as human evaluator 1 versus GPT-4o. Comprehensive results are presented in Table 2. Considering the findings from these comparisons, alongside the outcomes of cross-judge consistency assessments and human-evaluation validations, it appears improbable that the observed belief entrenchment results are attributable to judge bias. In this study, we propose the Martingale Score as a rigorous and unsupervised metric for quantifying belief entrenchment in the reasoning processes of large language models (LLMs). We demonstrate its efficacy by establishing its predictive capacity for ground-truth accuracy and subsequently apply it across diverse models, prompts, and problem domains. Furthermore, we investigate the underlying factors that exacerbate or mitigate the extent of belief entrenchment. Limitations A notable limitation of this study is the absence of evidence demonstrating a correlation between the Martingale Score, which reflects belief entrenchment, and the Brier Score, which quantifies predictive accuracy, within the OpenReview domain. This may be attributed to the nature of ground-truth labeling in OpenReview, where paper acceptance decisions are determined through community voting, a process susceptible to noise and inconsistency. Nevertheless, as illustrated in Table 1, belief entrenchment appears to manifest more prominently in domains reliant on human judgment, such as r/ChangeMyView and OpenReview. This raises concerns over adopting such LLMs in those domains.
