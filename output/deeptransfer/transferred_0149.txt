By introducing PPTArena and PPTPilot, we aim to establish a measurable framework for advancing intelligent systems in PowerPoint slide editing. PPTArena employs authentic slide decks, human-generated editing plans, and prescriptive style guidelines to establish a benchmark that evaluates adherence to instructions, structural organization, and layout quality beyond mere content creation. The findings from PPTPilot demonstrate that a plan-edit-check cycle enhances reliability, outperforming robust proprietary systems and emerging Vision-Language Models (VLMs). However, systems continue to encounter challenges with complex, lengthy, and heterogeneous tasks, indicating that proficient PowerPoint editing remains an unresolved issue. We anticipate that PPTArena and PPTPilot will serve as foundational tools for subsequent research on more advanced PowerPoint editing systems, which can operate safely and reliably on the presentations commonly utilized by individuals.
