Udio can transform natural-language prompts into polished music tracks across various genres, including lyrics in multiple languages. Regarding the perception of AI-generated music, research in computational creativity and applied machine learning has examined the creative value of these models through subjective listening tests. In these tests, listeners rate samples from a proposed music generation model against different baseline models or human-composed music. Common evaluation metrics include overall quality, creativity, and novelty, as well as music-specific metrics assessing melody, harmony, and rhythm. Another crucial metric is the assessment of naturalness or humanness, which is either directly evaluated via rating scales or examined using a musical Turing Test. In this test, listeners judge whether a piece was created by a human or an AI system, under conditions where they may be informed, uninformed, or intentionally misled about the source. In specific tasks, such as generating Bach-style chorales, even early models like DeepBach produced results that were difficult to distinguish from human compositions. To evaluate the emotional aspects of AI-generated music, listeners are typically asked to identify the intended emotions, either by categorizing them into discrete labels like "happy" or "sad" or by using dimensional models of valence and arousal. As AI-generated music becomes increasingly sophisticated, studies have explored whether listeners' evaluations are influenced by their perceptions or beliefs regarding the music's origin. However, the evidence remains inconclusive. Early studies that manipulated participants' awareness of the composer found no significant bias when evaluating live recordings of string-quartet compositions produced by both human and computer composers. Similarly, Zlatkov et al. observed no systematic bias against AI-generated music using contemporary pop music samples. Moreover, consumer marketing research by Moura and Maw identified no definitive preference for musical compositions based on authorship, regardless of whether the genre was classical or pop.
