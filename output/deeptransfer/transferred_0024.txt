The integration of diverse CoT data enabled the training of the Qwen2.5-VL-7B model through a two-stage process consisting of supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL). This model, notably, has a parameter size comparable to the LLM base model employed in this study. As shown in Tables 13 and 14, a comparative analysis of performance between VLM-based and LLM-based models revealed that the LLM-based approach consistently outperformed its VLM counterpart across both general and complex datasets, particularly demonstrating significant improvements in macro-F1 scores. These results highlight that, with equivalent CoT capability injection, LLMs exhibit superior reasoning and knowledge representation capabilities compared to VLMs. However, VLMs excel in processing visual details, as they inherently bypass the need for image-to-text conversion, thereby avoiding potential information loss during this process. Over three iterative phases, as illustrated in Fig. 18, the LORE model has undergone systematic advancements across five critical dimensions: (1) objective formulation; (2) model paradigm and backbone; (3) data and prompt engineering; (4) evaluation methodologies; and (5) application scenarios. Together, these phases chart a trajectory of innovation that integrates increasingly sophisticated architectural designs, high-quality data curation pipelines, and practical deployment strategies to optimize search relevance in e-commerce scenarios. In the initial phase, the primary challenge centered on query understanding within the e-commerce domain, particularly the accurate identification of product entities expressed through non-standardized terminology, such as unregistered terms or colloquialisms. To address this issue, we constructed extensive, high-quality e-commerce-specific training corpora and leveraged In-Context Learning (ICL) methodologies to systematically incorporate domain-specific knowledge into the model, thereby significantly enhancing its semantic understanding capabilities in the e-commerce context.
