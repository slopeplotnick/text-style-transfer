This plugin-style architecture promotes code reusability and enables fast prototyping of novel PEFT methods. Researchers can develop and test new methods separately, with the framework automatically integrating them at runtime. The dynamic loading approach has proven particularly valuable for comparative studies, allowing researchers to evaluate multiple PEFT variants under identical experimental conditions without code duplication or version control conflicts. From the SuperGLUE benchmark, we include seven datasets separated into four tasks: natural language inference (NLI) - CB; question answering - MultiRC, ReCoRD, BoolQ, COPA; word sense disambiguation - WiC; and coreference resolution - WSC. Moreover, we have integrated text generation datasets that are commonly used to benchmark generative LLMs. Specifically, we cover six datasets for reasoning and natural language understanding, organized into three tasks: question answering – MMLU, PIQA, SIQA, OBQA; natural language inference (NLI) – Hellaswag; and commonsense reasoning – WinoGrande. Additionally, the corpus encompasses three datasets dedicated to mathematical problem-solving, categorized into three distinct tasks: question answering – MathQA; mathematical word problems – GSM8K; and elementary mathematical problems – SVAMP. Furthermore, the corpus includes three datasets pertinent to code generation, namely Conala, CodeAlpacaPy, and APPS. It is noteworthy that some datasets may require further (mostly minor) format changes to be compatible with the input formatting of PEFT-FACTORY. Further details on this preprocessing are provided in Appendix B.2.
