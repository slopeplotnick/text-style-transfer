The left part of Figure 2 illustrates the prompt configuration along with an example of the extracted triples for a single training image, showcasing the semantic organization of various attributes. The output consists of multiple ontology-aligned triples that adhere to the Fashionpedia framework, covering aspects such as clothing category (depicts), silhouette (has_silhouette), material composition (has_material_type), pattern (has_pattern), and other relationships defined within the Fashionpedia ontology. Each triple follows the format (image_id, relation, attribute), offering an interpretable representation of the image. This structured representation enables the construction and comparison of knowledge graphs at the conceptual level. To assess influence, we compared the knowledge graph of each generated image with those of all training images, calculating node-level overlap to identify shared conceptual elements. For each prompt, we retained the training images with the highest degree of node overlap with the graph of the generated image. The prompt used was: "Generate triples for the fashion item depicted in the image based on the relationships defined by the Fashionpedia Ontology, available at: \\({ONTOLOGY_PATH} = \). Focus exclusively on the fashion item within the image identified by image_id: \\)([IMAGE_ID=)} and provide the triples that describe the image (image_id, relationship, attribute_value)." For instance, for image 58810.jpg, the extracted triples included: 58810.jpg, depicts, Shirt; 58810.jpg, belongs_to_category, Upperbody; 58810.jpg, has_nickname, None; 58810.jpg, has_silhouette, Slim-fit; 58810.jpg, has_waistline, Straight; 58810.jpg, has_length, Long-sleeved; 58810.jpg, has_collar_type, Classic; 58810.jpg, has_lapel_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_neckline, Crew-neck; 58810.jpg, has_sleeve_type, Long Sleeves with Cuffs; 58810.jpg, has_pocket_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_opening_type, Button-front Closure; 58810.jpg, has_material_type, Cotton-blend Fabric; 58810.jpg, has_leather_type, None; 58810.jpg, has_fabric_treatment, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_pattern, Plaid; 58810.jpg, has_animal_pattern, None. The retrieved knowledge graphs, which exhibited the highest node overlap with the graph of the generated image, were subsequently used in the unlearning phase. Beyond retrieval, the Neo4j framework facilitated a comprehensive analysis of the graph, enabling the identification of frequently occurring attributes and the visualization of concept distributions across the dataset. In the unlearning process, the generative model was retrained after removing specific training samples, allowing us to evaluate the influence of these samples on the model's output. We conducted ten unlearning experiments using both the baseline approach and our proposed method. In each experiment, images were generated for 15 prompts both before and after the unlearning process. To ensure consistency with the baseline configuration, a comparable proportion of training images, identified as the most influential for each prompt, were systematically removed.
