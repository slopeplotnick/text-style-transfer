This platform has been operational for approximately 5-6 years. According to the criteria established by the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK's proprietary platform is classified as a "Level 3: Software Platform," characterized by a standardized reference architecture common to all products. The current architecture includes a collection of shared assets with explicitly defined variation points to support the development of new variants. The primary objective of the migration is to achieve an architecture where domain commonalities and variabilities are clearly identified, enabling the specification of a reference architecture for the entire software product line. This involves the systematic reuse of components from a predefined asset repository, with explicit variability in the assets and a standardized configuration that governs permissible modifications in application architectures. Moreover, this approach incorporates numerous quality solutions within the software product line architecture. Variability management is systematically addressed, defining allowed configurations for application architectures and specifying variation points and constraints for these variations, including rules that application-specific variants must follow. Ultimately, the goal is to establish a software configuration that enables the automated synthesis of variants. Furthermore, in accordance with the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK operates at the organizational level at the fourth tier, which entails the synchronization and coordination of domain engineering and application engineering processes. The development of the project is conducted through strong collaboration between agronomists and software developers. The decision to migrate and collaborate with our research team was made through a collegiate discussion involving project leaders and stakeholders. However, prior to designing the interviews, a well-defined research methodology for the remainder of the project had not yet been established. Initially, the objective was to evaluate the perceptions of the organization's stakeholders regarding the transition to a Software Product Line (SPL). Depending on the results of the interviews, a structured methodology for the migration project will subsequently be developed.
