This platform has been operational for approximately 5-6 years. According to the criteria established by the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK's proprietary platform is classified as a "Level 3: Software Platform," characterized by a reference architecture that is uniformly applied across all products. The current architecture comprises a set of shared assets and explicitly defined variation points to accommodate the development of new variants. The objective is to achieve a comprehensive understanding of the commonalities and variabilities within the domain, thereby establishing a standardized configuration for the entire software product line. This entails the strategic reuse of components from a predefined parts library, with explicit delineation of component variations, and a standardized configuration that governs permissible modifications in application setups. Furthermore, the integration of numerous quality enhancements within the software product line configuration is imperative. Variability management is systematically addressed within the software product line framework, delineating permissible application configurations and defining the parameters for modifications, including the stipulation of compliance rules for specific versions. The primary objective is to establish a software configuration enabling the automated synthesis of variants. In alignment with the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK operates at the fourth organizational level, signifying the integration and coordination of domain engineering and application engineering activities. The development of the project is executed through robust collaboration between agronomists and software developers. The decision to undertake migration and engage with our research team was reached through a collegiate discussion involving project leaders and stakeholders. Prior to initiating the design of interviews, a well-defined research methodology for the remainder of the project had not yet been established. Our initial objective was to comprehend the perceptions of the company's stakeholders regarding the transition to Software Product Line (SPL). Based on the insights gleaned from the interviews, we will proceed to develop a comprehensive methodology for the migration project.
