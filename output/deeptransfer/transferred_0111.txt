Implementation. For both solvers and verifiers, we employed a generation configuration with a temperature of 0.7, a top-p value of 0.9, and a maximum output length of 8192 tokens. We discarded outputs lacking a boxed answer. All inference experiments were conducted using vLLM on H200 GPUs. For further details regarding the prompts and the output filtering process, please refer to Appendix F. Solver performance. We evaluated all 37 models across 9 datasets, computing the average performance across tasks (as illustrated in Figure 1) and providing task-specific results in Appendix G. Overall, solver accuracy increased with model capacity. Models within the Qwen3 and DeepSeek families performed particularly well, whereas Llama3-Base exhibited suboptimal performance due to its unfamiliarity with the question-answering instruction format. Within each family, Qwen2.5-Base and DeepSeek demonstrated distinct performance enhancements with increased scaling, and the other families exhibited similar trends of improvement with scaling. Correlating verifier and solver performance. After establishing solver accuracy, we examined the correlation between solver performance and verifier performance (refer to Figure 2). For all 21 post-trained models and each dataset, we evaluated verifier performance on the same set of solver models, measuring verifier accuracy, F1-score, precision, false positive rate (FPR), false negative rate (FNR), and gain for each solver-verifier pair. For each verifier, the metrics were categorized into three distinct verification settings, and the results were averaged across solvers and datasets within each setting. Verifier accuracy generally exhibited an upward trend with increasing solver accuracy; however, other metrics demonstrated more intricate patterns. The FPR increased during self-verification and intra-family verification but decreased slightly during cross-family verification. This observation suggests that verifiers with higher solver proficiency are more prone to erroneously classify their own outputs or outputs from models within the same family as correct. Additional visualizations for F1-score and precision are provided in Appendix H.
