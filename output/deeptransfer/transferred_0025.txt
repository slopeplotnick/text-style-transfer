The project focuses on integrating new features and updates into the existing framework, addressing advancements in both technical IT domains and agricultural methodologies. The central objective is to facilitate rapid development processes, enabling the creation of applications at an accelerated pace compared to previous iterations. This approach embodies the Software Engineering spirit by combining principles such as factorization, reuse, innovation, and rapidity. Initially conceived as a reusable code base, the project aligns closely with SPLE objectives, thereby promoting the migration project. We identified seven current good practices, with Factorization and Reuse emerging as the most frequently mentioned ideas, occurring nine and eight times respectively, as illustrated in Figure 1. Understanding the perceived benefits is crucial as they serve to motivate the entire team. Customization, a fundamental characteristic of an SPL, was the most frequently mentioned concept by ITs, UXs, and AGROs. An SPL is perceived as a platform capable of generating mass-customized software from a collection of existing solutions. Rapidity, the second most frequently mentioned concept by ITs, POs, and UXs, pertains to the ability to quickly derive new software. Moreover, automation, which refers to the autonomous generation of software applications, was highlighted. ITs and POs associate automation with enhanced efficiency and speed. Additionally, industrialization, as referenced by IT and UX professionals, pertains to the on-demand and cost-effective production of applications. Notably, the improvement of code quality, characterized by consistency, testability, and reliability, was exclusively highlighted by IT professionals. Furthermore, the advantages related to sales, articulated by IT and PO professionals, signify an increase in software sales or a swift response to emerging opportunities.
