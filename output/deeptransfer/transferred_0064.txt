4.1.2 Graph-Based Statistical Analysis of Retrieved Training Images. Our investigation assessed the impact of selected training images by evaluating the similarity of generated images before and after retraining. However, this comparative analysis alone does not sufficiently elucidate why certain images exert varying influences. Therefore, we employed a graph-based analytical approach to examine the structural characteristics and shared attributes of the training images. Specifically, the objective was to ascertain whether the selected images constituted distinct and meaningful clusters. Table 3 presents statistical data derived from ten experimental trials, each comprising fifteen prompts, to evaluate the consistency and clustering of the images. These findings substantiate the efficacy of our image selection methodology and enhance our understanding of the significance of these images within the analytical framework. The average number of distinct attribute values per relationship ranged from 3.9 to 5.1, indicating a moderate degree of variation. This level of diversity aligns with the structured nature of the fashion domain, wherein the range of styles and patterns is inherently constrained by predefined ontological categories. Moreover, the retrieved images exhibited sufficient similarity to facilitate the formation of distinct, coherent clusters, while maintaining enough variation to prevent redundancy. This indicates that the observed influence stems from shared conceptual frameworks rather than mere replication of identical elements. Additionally, an analysis of attribute overlap among the images revealed an average shared attribute range of 5 to 7.1, with certain clusters exhibiting as many as 13 shared attributes. The images exhibited not only intricate details but also significant congruence in meaningful attributes. Prompts characterized by greater specificity facilitated a higher incidence of shared attributes, whereas prompts of a more general or creative nature engendered increased variability. Thus, this demonstrates the system's capacity to adapt to varying prompt types while consistently retrieving semantically aligned examples.
