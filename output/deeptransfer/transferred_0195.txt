LLaMA-Factory primarily focuses on text-generation problems and does not incorporate the possibility of casting these problems as classification tasks. Our framework, PEFT-FACTORY, addresses both the limited number of available PEFT methods and the potential for fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) for classification problems. While alternative frameworks for training LLMs exist, they are often less user-friendly compared to LLaMA-Factory, though they offer specific benefits. For instance, FastChat is a specialized framework designed for training LLMs specifically for chat-completion tasks. Moreover, LitGPT and LMFlow are extensible and convenient general training frameworks that support various generative models and training methods. Axolotl is a terminal-based tool that enables efficient post-training of LLMs without sacrificing functionality or scalability. Open-Instruct focuses on instruction fine-tuning for LLMs, providing multiple models and recipes for this purpose. H2O LLM Studio is a comprehensive, enterprise-oriented tool that also offers a graphical interface for developing and deploying LLM models. Additionally, GPT4All creates a user-friendly interface around the LLaMA model for efficient training. ColossalAI provides a robust framework for fine-tuning across multiple nodes, facilitating distributed computing. In contrast, LLaMA-Adapter and LLaMA-Accessory are more lightweight frameworks; LLaMA-Adapter introduces trainable adapters to LLaMA models, while LLaMA-Accessory offers a comprehensive toolkit for LLM development. Notably, LLaMA-Adapter is often implemented in previously mentioned frameworks, such as LitGPT. Based on our analysis of related frameworks and to the best of our knowledge, we have identified three key features that are currently missing or limited, which our work seeks to address: enabling the training of LLMs with methods beyond reparameterization-based PEFT techniques, allowing for the modular and dynamic addition of new PEFT methods, and supporting the training and evaluation of LLMs for classification tasks.
