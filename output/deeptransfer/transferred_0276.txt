Case 3: Suicidal Risk Level Detection. This case highlights a misclassification where a Reddit post, intended to be supportive, was erroneously predicted as behavior by the Menta model. The primary cause of this error was the excessive input length, which included a lengthy multi-speaker thread with formatting noise, such as unusual symbols, HTML artifacts, and placeholder tokens like "Hyperactive behavior." These elements acted as misleading cues, biasing the model towards a behavioral interpretation. The post in question involved an individual encouraging another to persevere for their younger siblings and to continue efforts to prevent harm from their father. Furthermore, the individual advised ignoring a disparaging comment from a troll, emphasizing that they did not perceive the person as worthless or deserving of self-harm. The misclassification was attributed to the individual's experience of being overwhelmed by academic and social challenges. However, they reported that learning to establish boundaries and decline excessive commitments contributed to improved well-being and the development of more meaningful social connections.
