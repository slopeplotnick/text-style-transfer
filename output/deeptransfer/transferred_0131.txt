To address these questions, we evaluate two widely used, autonomous programming agents: SWE-agent (SA) and OpenHands (OH). Both agents are tested under their respective default configurations: SWE-agent operates with a per-instance cost cap of $2, while OpenHands runs for a maximum of 100 trajectory iterations. Both agents utilize their default file-viewing and code-editing tools. Additionally, we pair these agents with four large language models (LLMs): DeepSeek-V3 671B MoE (DSK-V3) [14], DeepSeek-R1 (DSK-R1) [18], Devstral-small-2505 24B coding-specialized open weights (Dev) [7], and Claude Sonnet 4 (CLD-4) [9]. This selection encompasses both general-purpose and reasoning-oriented models, as well as advanced and open-source alternatives, resulting in eight distinct ⟨agent, model⟩ combinations for analysis.
