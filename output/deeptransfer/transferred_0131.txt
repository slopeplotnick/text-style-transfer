To address these research questions, we evaluated two widely adopted programming agents: SWE-agent (SA) and OpenHands (OH). The experiments adhered to each framework's default configurations: SWE-agent operated under a per-instance cost cap of $2, while OpenHands executed up to 100 trajectory iterations. Both agents utilized their respective default tools for file navigation and code editing. Additionally, the agents were paired with four distinct large language models (LLMs): DeepSeek-V3 671B MoE (DSK-V3) [14], DeepSeek-R1 (DSK-R1) [18], Devstral-small-2505 24B coding-specialized open weights (Dev) [7], and Claude Sonnet 4 (CLD-4) [9]. This selection encompassed both general-purpose and reasoning-oriented models, including advanced proprietary systems and open-source alternatives, resulting in eight ⟨agent, model⟩ combinations for evaluation. For each combination, process-centric metrics were computed, as detailed in Table 1 (§2.2).
