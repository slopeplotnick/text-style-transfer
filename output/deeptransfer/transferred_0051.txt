The text explores methodologies for evaluating the logical and ethical coherence of AI model responses. It introduces the Moral Consistency Pipeline (MoCoP), an innovative framework for ethical assessment that operates independently of predefined datasets. MoCoP autonomously generates, evaluates, and refines moral reasoning scenarios, thereby eliminating the need for external intervention. Moreover, the proposed framework evaluates the extent to which AI models exhibit temporal variability in their moral reasoning and facilitates comparative analyses to assess consistency across different systems. By integrating mechanisms for lexical, semantic, and reasoning-level assessments, the framework enables longitudinal monitoring of ethical decision-making processes in AI models. The remainder of this paper is organized as follows: Section 2 reviews related work on evaluating ethical reasoning in AI systems; Section 3 provides a detailed description of the MoCoP methodology; Section 5 presents experimental results; Section 6 offers an analysis of the findings; Section 7 examines potential methodological limitations; and Section 8 discusses the constraints of the current approach alongside directions for future research. Previous research predominantly concentrated on identifying and rectifying biases or harmful content within AI systems. For instance, the OpenEthics initiative endeavored to enhance the transparency and verifiability of AI ethics. Concurrently, another study focused on fairness, aiming to mitigate bias against specific demographic groups within AI systems. Although these studies contributed to enhancing the transparency of AI systems, they relied on static datasets and prompts, thus limiting their adaptability to evolving contexts. While these approaches were effective in capturing the ethical behavior of AI models at a specific temporal juncture, they were inadequate for monitoring the evolution of moral reasoning or assessing stability across diverse scenarios. More recent investigations have extended beyond the realm of fairness, focusing on the mechanisms through which AI models engage in moral and ethical decision-making processes.
