The primary objective is to maximize code reuse and expedite the delivery of new applications. Therefore, the goal is to transition the existing code base to a higher-level Software Product Line (SPL), such as Level 4 or 5. To facilitate this transition, we have decided to first conduct interviews with company collaborators to evaluate their perceptions of SPL engineering. These interviews are structured to address the research questions outlined in the subsequent section. Moreover, a follow-up series of interviews is scheduled for the conclusion of the project to refine the guidelines for an effective migration strategy. The forthcoming phases of the migration process will focus on variability analysis, beginning with the establishment of a systematic approach for feature localization to facilitate the synthesis of feature models. Subsequently, the investigation will focus on the interactions and dependencies among these features to achieve a comprehensive system description. We conducted the interviews to ascertain the perceptions of current practices, benefits, risks, and concerns prior to transitioning to an SPL. Research Question 1 (RQ1) seeks to understand the company's actors' perception of current development best practices. Our objective is to ascertain their perspectives on the efficacy of current practices. Research Question 2 explores their perceptions regarding the anticipated advantages of the prospective SPL, aiming to engage in a discourse concerning the added value that the forthcoming SPL is expected to contribute to the application development process. Once the benefits are discussed, RQ3 seeks to elucidate the perceptions of company stakeholders regarding the risks associated with the forthcoming SPL. Finally, RQ4 aims to explore the apprehensions that arise as a consequence of the previously identified risks. This question facilitates the assessment of the apprehensions experienced by company personnel concerning the implications of the migration project. These apprehensions pertain to potential adversities, challenges anticipated during the transition, and the necessity for meticulous planning and strategic risk management.
