PEFT-FACTORY also implements the calculation of classification and efficiency metrics during the prediction phase, in addition to existing token accuracy and semantic similarity metrics such as Rouge (Lin, 2004) and Bleu (Papineni et al., 2002). Among the classification metrics, PEFT-FACTORY employs standard Accuracy and F1 metrics. To measure efficiency during the evaluation of PEFT methods, it utilizes the PSCP metric (Balanec et al., 2025), a highly adaptable metric that considers various efficiency parameters, including the number of parameters, memory usage, and inference time. These metrics are implemented within the train/sft/metric.py file for supervised fine-tuning, following the framework established by LLaMA-Factory and using distinct data classes for each metric, specifically named ComputeAccuracy, ComputeF1, and ComputePSCP. For classification, a binary flag attribute within the training arguments, called compute_classification.metrics, enables or disables the computation of classification metrics. Similarly, for efficiency metrics, a binary flag, compute_pscp, is included. Further details on the usage of efficiency metrics are provided in Appendix B.3. The extensibility of PEFT methods and datasets, combined with a ready-to-use, controlled, and stable environment, is a key feature of PEFT-FACTORY that aims to advance research on PEFT methods. To demonstrate its utility in enhancing reproducibility and facilitating benchmarking, we present two specific use cases. The modular design of PEFT-FACTORY significantly promotes reproducibility and transparency of newly developed PEFT methods, especially when fellow researchers and practitioners develop new PEFT methods. Currently, when a new PEFT method is developed, the published source code often lacks full functionality or is difficult to reproduce. Moreover, developers frequently need to implement the code for training and evaluation from scratch, which is often repetitive, increases the likelihood of errors, and may lead to inconsistencies in the final results.
