The methodology employed involved deconstructing narratives into constituent components such as events, entities, and settings, subsequently converting these elements into a structured JSON format. Based on this structured data, GPT-4 was tasked with generating novel narratives that preserved the core themes and structure while incorporating innovative elements. To assess the quality of the generated narratives, specific evaluative criteria were established, focusing on aspects such as functionality, creativity, readability, and the extent to which they conveyed authenticity and thoughtfulness. Moreover, alternative artificial intelligence tools, such as GPT-4o, were employed to evaluate both the original and newly generated narratives, categorizing them as either "Chosen" or "Rejected" based on their quality. Furthermore, the narrative data, along with these evaluative labels, were systematically archived in JSON format to construct a comprehensive dataset, encapsulating emotional patterns and additional pertinent details, as illustrated in Figure 4. Initially, the dataset was bifurcated into two subsets: one designated for training purposes and the other reserved for testing, with the majority allocated to the training subset. Specifically, the training dataset comprised 8,866 preference pairs utilized for model enhancement, while the test dataset consisted of 985 previously unseen pairs. Subsequently, the Llama-3-8B model was refined employing the AdamW optimizer in conjunction with the LoRA fine-tuning methodology. This process was executed with predetermined parameters for the learning rate, batch size, and number of training epochs, culminating in an evaluation loss of 0.038 on the test dataset. The selection of these experimental settings was informed by established guidelines, empirical testing, and the computational resources available. Additionally, an ablation study was conducted to evaluate the significance of various compositional elements in the generation of high-quality narratives. Initially, a baseline model utilizing the Llama-3-8B architecture, which incorporated all compositional elements, was developed. In the ablation study, individual components, such as the "event" unit, were systematically removed, and the model was retrained and fine-tuned to accommodate the revised configuration. All models were trained under uniform conditions to ensure the fairness of the evaluations. The performance of each modified model was assessed using an independent test set. Furthermore, the stories were evaluated using consistent criteria across seven dimensions, including creativity and clarity. The outcomes of these tests will be elucidated in Section 6.
