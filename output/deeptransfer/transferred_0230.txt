During the parsing process, narratives were deconstructed into elements such as events, characters, and settings, which were then encoded into a JSON format. Based on this structured data, GPT-4 generated new stories that retained core themes and structures while allowing for innovation. We established specific evaluative criteria to assess the quality of these narratives, focusing on seven critical dimensions: functionality, creativity, readability, thoughtfulness, emotional authenticity, and clarity of perspective. Consequently, third-party LLMs, such as GPT-4o, evaluated both the original and generated narratives, assigning them "Chosen" or "Rejected" labels. Furthermore, the structured information of the narratives was recorded in JSON format, and together with the labeled stories, formed a comprehensive preference dataset. The emotional distributions and other narrative attributes in this dataset are illustrated in Figure 4. Initially, the dataset was divided into a training set and a test set at a ratio of 9:1. The training set contained 8,866 preference pairs utilized for model enhancement, while the test set comprised 985 previously unseen pairs. Subsequently, we fine-tuned the Llama-3-8B model using the AdamW optimizer. We employed a LoRA-based fine-tuning method with a learning rate of 5e-6, a batch size of 10, and 10 training epochs, resulting in an evaluation loss of 0.038 on the test set. The selection of these parameters was informed by expert recommendations, empirical testing, and the computational resources available. Moreover, an ablation study was conducted to evaluate the significance of various compositional elements in enhancing story quality. A baseline model, Llama-3-8B, was initially developed incorporating all constituent elements. In the ablation study, specific components, such as the "event" unit, were systematically removed, followed by retraining and fine-tuning the model to accommodate the revised structural framework. All models were trained under consistent conditions to ensure the fairness and validity of the experimental comparisons. The performance of each modified model was assessed using an independent test set. Additionally, the generated stories were evaluated based on seven predefined dimensions, including creativity and clarity, employing consistent criteria established in prior evaluations.
