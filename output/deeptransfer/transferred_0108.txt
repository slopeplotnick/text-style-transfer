We conducted the interviews in the following manner. Our aim was to ensure that the interviews were fluid, resembling a conversation rather than a rigid Q&A session. At the outset, we provided a brief reminder of the principles of SPLE and explained the purpose of the questionnaire. The initial questions focused on the interviewee's role within the organization, aiming to establish their profile and contextualize their perspective. We then moved on to broader questions regarding the company's development processes to gain a general understanding of their operational approach. Following this, we asked more specific questions about the methodologies used in developing new products. This part aimed to understand perceptions of current strengths, commendable practices, and the overall status of the development process. Discussing these practices served as a perfect transition to introduce a novel approach that could potentially address identified shortcomings. In the concluding segment, we discussed the transition to the new approach, encouraging participants to express any apprehensions, perceived risks, and potential advantages. The final questions aimed at improving the interviews and provided a conclusion to the discussion. All interviews were conducted by the same interviewer, the first author of this study, and each session lasted between 30 minutes and one hour. Participants appreciated the interviews and reported feeling actively involved in the migration project. Each interview concluded with the question, "Was there a question you anticipated that was not asked?" This encouraged participants to reflect on the entire discourse and contribute additional insights, thereby enhancing the fluidity of subsequent interviews. The interview questions remained uniform across all sessions to ensure consistency in data analysis. Our objective was to encourage participants to reflect comprehensively on the topics discussed without altering the structure of the questions, while simultaneously enhancing the overall quality of the interviews. Handwritten notes were taken during the sessions to complement the recordings. In accordance with established guidelines, the interviewer made deliberate efforts to ensure that the phrasing of the questions remained neutral and free from bias, thereby minimizing the risk of influencing participants' responses. Additionally, he ensured that a sufficient number of questions were posed without directing the interviewees towards specific responses. A preliminary interview was conducted with our primary collaborator at the company, who is actively engaged in this project alongside us. This preliminary interview facilitated the refinement of the questionnaire and contributed to the enhancement of the overall process. The data obtained from the initial interview was excluded from the subsequent analysis.
