We planned the interviews to foster a conversational dynamic rather than strictly adhering to a question-and-answer format. Initially, a brief reminder of the principles underlying SPLE and the purpose of the questionnaire was provided. The first questions focused on understanding the profile of the interviewee and their specific role within the organization. This approach aimed to ascertain a comprehensive understanding of the individual's position and responsibilities. We then proceeded with general inquiries concerning the company's development processes. Following this, more specific questions were posed about the existing methodologies employed in the development of new products. Our objective was to ascertain perceptions regarding the efficacy of current practices, identify commendable practices, and assess the overall status of the process. Discussing the current state of practice facilitated the introduction of a novel approach, which holds potential to address some prevailing challenges. The final part of the interview was dedicated to discussing the migration, where participants were encouraged to articulate any apprehensions, perceived risks, and potential advantages associated with the transition. The last two questions aimed at soliciting feedback for enhancing the interview process and effectively concluding the discussion. All interviews were conducted by the first author of this study, ensuring consistency in approach and execution. Each interview lasted between 30 minutes and one hour. Participants expressed appreciation for the interviews and reported feeling actively involved in the migration project. At the conclusion of each interview, participants were asked, "Was there a question you anticipated that was not asked?" This question facilitated participants in reflecting comprehensively on the discussion and provided an opportunity to supplement any information they deemed absent. Additionally, it contributed to enhancing the fluidity of subsequent interviews. The questions remained consistent across all interviews to ensure uniformity in data analysis. The objective was to facilitate participants' contemplation on the subject matter without altering the questions, while simultaneously enhancing the quality of the interviews. Detailed notes were meticulously recorded throughout the interviews. In accordance with received guidance, the interviewer endeavored to ensure that the questions remained neutral and non-leading. A preliminary interview was conducted with the primary collaborator from the company involved in this research project, serving as a pilot to refine the questionnaire and enhance the overall fluidity of the process. The data obtained from this initial interview were excluded from the subsequent analysis.
