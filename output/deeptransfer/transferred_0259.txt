We find that high task accuracy does not always imply optimal integration of multiple information modalities (see the middle panel of Figure 8). For instance, GPT-5 Mini achieves a notably low NRMSE, yet it does not combine modalities efficiently compared to other models. This shortcoming is most apparent when there is a significant imbalance in unimodal performance; optimal behavior would require the model to markedly down-weight the weaker modality, which some LLMs fail to do. This has direct practical implications, as it exposes the risk that benchmarks focusing solely on accuracy may favor models with less robustness against noise. Conversely, Llama-4 Maverick surpasses the conventional Bayesian reliability-weighted linear fusion method, indicating the use of more sophisticated non-linear integration strategies. This is consistent with the fact that a non-linear random forest model more accurately captures the operational dynamics compared to simpler linear methodologies. Comparing unimodal and bimodal performance reveals that, while models such as Llama-4 Maverick, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, and GPT-4o effectively leverage the additional modality to achieve lower error rates when both text and image inputs are present for the majority of multimodal tasks, this is not a universal trend. The variability in gains indicates potential headroom for advancing multimodal models. See Appendix A.11 for further details on specific models. To capture behavioral features beyond static task metrics, we devised the Bayesian Consistency Score (BCS), which captures principled behavioral shifts. This allows us to evaluate model behavior more holistically, even when accuracy saturates. Measuring behavior changes under controlled ablations enables us to compare models that may have different baseline performance levels and can offer additional insights into their implicit computational strategies. While more complex heuristics, such as salience-based weighting or rule-switching strategies, may fit static response patterns better, it is more revealing to study Bayes-consistent behaviors under a Bayesian observer model when conditions change.
