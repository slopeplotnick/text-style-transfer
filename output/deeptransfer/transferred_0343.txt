Guardrails are mechanisms designed to ensure safety during interactions between humans and AI systems. Initially, these systems employed basic rule-based filtering techniques. While effective in constrained settings, such rudimentary approaches struggled to adapt to evolving safety policies and emerging risks, often suffering from limited coverage and low accuracy. However, recent advancements in guardrail systems have incorporated sophisticated AI-driven models, enhancing their adaptability and generalization capabilities. For instance, Llama Guard, developed using specialized safety datasets from Meta AI, exemplifies a model designed to ensure safe interactions between users and AI systems. Moreover, models such as Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard extend these capabilities to address safety concerns in visual modalities. Despite these advancements, several challenges persist. Most prior research predominantly focused on textual and image-based modalities, whereas video and audio modalities have been addressed in a rudimentary manner, often employing simplistic techniques that fail to capture contextual nuances. Furthermore, existing systems typically operate within single-modality frameworks or specific scenarios, lacking the capability to integrate and reason across multiple media types. To address these issues, OMNIGUARD was developed as the inaugural system capable of managing diverse media types while employing deliberate reasoning to ensure safety. By incorporating omni-modal understanding and explicit reasoning, OMNIGUARD delivers comprehensive and interpretable safety assurance across all media modalities.
