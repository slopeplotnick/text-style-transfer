This method promotes fairness across tasks and groups by directly optimizing a smooth surrogate of balanced accuracy. Unlike conventional cross-entropy (CE) loss, it specifically addresses performance disparities between majority and minority groups, thereby enhancing consistency in multi-task fine-tuning. An empirical study was conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of various training strategies. Specifically, we investigated the influence of single-task versus multi-task fine-tuning methodologies on mental health prediction outcomes. We developed an on-device mental health evaluation application for iOS using llama.cpp as the inference backend, leveraging GGUF V3-quantized models optimized for mobile hardware. These models were converted from PyTorch to the GGUF format via a specialized conversion pipeline, facilitating efficient execution on both CPU and GPU architectures. The application is developed utilizing Swift/SwiftUI and establishes connectivity with llama.cpp via a C++ bridge layer, allowing direct execution within the iOS environment without reliance on external servers. During execution, matrix operations are accelerated through Apple Metal, with an automatic fallback to CPU processing when GPU resources are fully utilized. Moreover, thread-level parallelism is achieved through the employment of up to 8 threads, optimizing performance across Apple's efficiency and performance cores. Model weights are memory-mapped to minimize RAM usage and facilitate the lazy loading of tensors. To ensure stability, the system employs a 4,096-token context window with efficient KV-cache management and batched inference, thereby mitigating fragmentation during the processing of extended inputs. Long social media posts are truncated to 6,000 characters. We conducted an evaluation of three models on an iPhone 15 Pro Max, namely Menta (2.33 GB), Phi-4 Mini (2.40 GB), and Qwen-3 (2.30 GB), utilizing open-sourced code. The deployment framework is compatible with various quantization families, facilitating a balance between latency and accuracy. For the fine-tuning of our Menta model, we employed four high-quality corpora specifically curated for the detection of depression, stress, and suicidal ideation sourced from the social media platform Reddit.
