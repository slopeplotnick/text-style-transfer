This method aims to promote fairness across tasks and groups by directly optimizing balanced accuracy. Unlike the conventional CE loss, it explicitly addresses performance disparities between majority and minority groups, thereby improving the consistency of multi-task fine-tuning. To evaluate the effectiveness of different training strategies, an ablation study was conducted comparing single-task and multi-task fine-tuning approaches for mental health prediction. Furthermore, we developed an on-device mental health assessment application for iOS using llama.cpp<sup>1</sup> as the inference backend, leveraging GGUF V3-quantized models optimized for mobile hardware. The models were converted from PyTorch to the GGUF format via a specialized conversion pipeline, enabling efficient execution on both CPU and GPU architectures. The app, implemented in Swift/SwiftUI, integrates llama.cpp through a C++ bridge layer, facilitating direct inference within the iOS runtime without reliance on external servers. At runtime, matrix operations are accelerated using Apple Metal, with automatic fallback to CPU processing when GPU resources are saturated. Moreover, thread-level parallelism, utilizing up to eight threads, is managed through llama.cpp's native thread scheduling API to ensure optimal utilization of Apple's performance and efficiency cores. Model weights are memory-mapped to minimize RAM usage and enable lazy loading of tensors. To ensure stability, the system employs a 4,096-token context window with efficient key-value cache management and batched inference, mitigating fragmentation during the processing of extended inputs. Social media posts exceeding 6,000 characters are truncated accordingly. We evaluated three models locally on an iPhone 15 Pro Max: Menta (2.33 GB), Phi-4 Mini (2.40 GB), and Qwen-3 (2.30 GB), utilizing open-source code<sup>2</sup>. The deployment framework supports various quantization techniques, enabling trade-offs between latency and accuracy. For the fine-tuned Menta model, we curated four high-quality corpora specifically designed for detecting depression, stress, and suicidal ideation from Reddit posts.
