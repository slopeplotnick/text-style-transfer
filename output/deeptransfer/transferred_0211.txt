Stage 3: Instruction-specific fine-tuning. In this stage, we focus on enhancing specialized instruction-following capabilities across the diverse task categories within Infinity-MM. The training process utilizes a comprehensive dataset that spans document understanding, chart reasoning, OCR-centric tasks, and multi-turn conversations. In accordance with the Infinity-MM methodology, we apply task-specific mixture weighting, allocating 35% to general visual question answering, 25% to document understanding, 20% to chart reasoning, 15% to OCR tasks, and 5% to multi-turn conversations and specialized domains. The learning rate is set to 1e-5, and training is conducted for one epoch with an effective batch size of 512. The objective follows standard autoregressive language modeling with next-token prediction. To assess performance, we compare our NPU-native AutoNeural model against state-of-the-art vision-language models across five multimodal benchmarks. These comparisons include InternVL2 (1B and 2B variants), which represent ViT-Transformer architectures optimized for GPU deployment, and Qwen2-VL (2B and 3.75B variants), which exhibit robust general-purpose multimodal capabilities. Notably, our AutoNeural model, trained to Stage 3 of the Infinity-MM curriculum, achieves an optimal balance between predictive accuracy and computational efficiency. Benchmark evaluations are conducted across five distinct datasets, each targeting specific multimodal skill domains. MMStar evaluates vision-language reasoning through multi-step inference and commonsense knowledge in visual questions. HallusionBench tests the model’s ability to handle linguistic hallucinations and visual illusions. MathVista_MINI examines mathematical reasoning over diagrams and charts, focusing on extracting quantitative information and performing numerical computations. AI2D_TEST assesses diagram comprehension and scientific reasoning using annotated illustrations from textbooks and educational materials. OCRBench evaluates scene text recognition and document understanding across diverse text-in-image scenarios, including natural scenes, documents, and signage. We report accuracy for each benchmark and calculate the average score to determine the model’s overall proficiency in managing diverse multimodal tasks.
