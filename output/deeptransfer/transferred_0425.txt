In this section, we discuss the limitations of TaleFrame regarding interaction modes and advanced narrative capabilities, as well as directions for future research. - Interaction and visualization. Currently, TaleFrame operates on desktop and laptop environments, utilizing mouse and keyboard inputs for precise control, which is crucial for integrating and comprehending event linkages. User studies indicate a preference for drag-and-drop interactions; however, this mode may be less effective on mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, due to their smaller screen sizes. Although touch pens and fingers can serve as alternatives to a mouse, the complexity of extended interaction processes and the input of relational information can lead to errors, thereby impairing the user experience on mobile devices. To reduce cognitive load, we have optimized information visualization through color coding, icons, and connecting lines, and we have implemented adaptive layout resizing to accommodate different screen sizes. In future work, we plan to explore and define a broader range of interaction styles, including touchscreens, keyboard input, and voice commands, to create a flexible framework that allows for diverse user engagement across various devices. - Challenges in handling advanced narrative structures in story generation. TaleFrame encounters difficulties in managing complex event relationships beyond basic chronological sequencing, particularly with causal relationships, flashbacks, and other advanced storytelling techniques. Moreover, the relationships between entities complicate the connections between events, impacting the coherence and accuracy of the generation process. While we have attempted to improve the modeling of event dependencies, the results have been less than ideal when handling multi-layered event interweaving, temporal reversals, and interactions between different entities. The relationships between entities, especially in events involving multiple entities, often lead to difficulties in generating the correct event sequence or capturing causal chains, which can result in incoherent or logically unclear storylines. As the complexity of the story plot and the relationships between entities increase, the model faces greater challenges in understanding and generating these event relationships. In future endeavors, we aim to enhance our modeling of entity relationships and develop optimized strategies for generating non-linear narrative structures to improve the model's performance in complex story generation, ultimately providing users with a richer and more coherent narrative experience.
