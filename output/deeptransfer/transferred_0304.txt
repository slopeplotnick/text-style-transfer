Datasets. Following previous studies, we compile a collection of 5,500 Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset (Hendrycks et al., 2021). Evaluation Benchmarks. We utilize a range of benchmarks for evaluation, including AIME 2024 (MAA, 2024), AMC, MATH500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math. For out-of-distribution tasks, we incorporate ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond. Inference is conducted using a greedy decoding approach with a constraint of 3,000 tokens. Baselines. CAPO is evaluated across four principal reinforcement learning methodologies: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. For each methodology, we also incorporate its CAPO-enhanced variant, thereby facilitating a direct evaluation of CAPO's efficacy as a general augmentation. This approach enables us to assess both the independent performance of the baseline methods and the enhancements achieved through the integration of CAPO. Implementation. To ensure fairness across all baseline algorithms and CAPO variants, we standardize the description of all implementation details. Given that each method necessitates distinct parameterizations, the comprehensive training configurations are provided in Appendix B. Datasets. We utilize the GUI-R1-3K dataset, derived from OS-Atlas, comprising 3,000 vision-language-action instances across web and mobile platforms. This dataset aligns visual states with natural language instructions and action sequences, offering a robust testbed for evaluating multimodal reasoning and planning capabilities. Evaluation Benchmarks. In our planning evaluation, we consider GUI-Act-Web, OmniAct-Web, and AndroidControl-Low/High. For the perception evaluation, we employ Screenshot Pro. Baselines. We conduct a comparative analysis of GRPO and its CAPO variant utilizing the primary graphical user interface benchmark configuration. This constitutes an additional assessment to illustrate the efficacy of CAPO in diverse reasoning contexts, extending beyond its application in mathematical reasoning scenarios.
