Datasets. Following prior studies, we compile a collection of 5,500 Level 3-5 problems from the MATH dataset (Hendrycks et al., 2021). Evaluation Benchmarks. We utilize a diverse set of benchmarks, including AIME 2024 (MAA, 2024), AMC, MATH500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021), GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math. Additionally, to assess performance on more unconventional tasks, we incorporate ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond. Inference employs greedy decoding with a token limit of 3,000. Baselines. CAPO is evaluated across four principal reinforcement learning algorithms: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. For each algorithm, a CAPO-augmented variant is also tested, allowing us to assess CAPO's efficacy as a general enhancement. This approach enables us to evaluate both the independent efficacy of the baseline methods and the enhancements realized through the integration of CAPO. Implementation. To maintain fairness across the diverse baseline algorithms and CAPO variants, we standardize the description of all implementation details. Given that each method necessitates distinct configurations, comprehensive training details are provided in Appendix B. Datasets. We employ the GUI-R1-3K dataset, sourced from OS-Atlas, which comprises 3,000 instances of vision-language-action interactions across web and mobile platforms. This dataset aligns visual states with natural language instructions and action sequences, providing a robust framework for assessing the efficacy of multimodal reasoning and planning strategies. Evaluation Benchmarks. In our analysis, we focus on the GUI-Act-Web, OmniAct-Web, and AndroidControl-Low/High paradigms for planning. For the perception module, we employ Screenshot Pro. Baselines. We conduct a comparative analysis between GRPO and its CAPO variant utilizing the primary graphical user interface (GUI) benchmark configuration. This serves as an ancillary evaluation to illustrate the efficacy of CAPO across diverse reasoning contexts, extending beyond its application in mathematical reasoning scenarios.
