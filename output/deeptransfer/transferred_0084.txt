Our system was designed to identify clear and readily observable errors, rather than engaging in deeper scientific reasoning or analyzing kinematic behavior. By contrast, our dataset emphasizes complex physical phenomena and scientific principles. Therefore, explicit physics decomposition through domain-specific checklists provides substantially more reliable outcomes than general-purpose rationales, highlighting the need for specialized evaluation frameworks to assess nuanced physical reasoning in video generation. Consequently, VideoScore2 is not well suited for our benchmark, as it requires rigorous scientific validation, leading to weaker alignment with human judgments on this dataset.
