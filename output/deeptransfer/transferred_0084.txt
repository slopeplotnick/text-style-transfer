The system we developed is designed to identify overt errors that are readily apparent, rather than engaging in profound scientific analysis or assessing kinematic behavior. In contrast, our dataset prioritizes intricate physical phenomena and scientific principles. Therefore, the explicit decomposition of physics using domain-specific checklists yields more reliable outcomes than general-purpose rationales, underscoring the necessity for specialized evaluative frameworks when appraising nuanced physical reasoning in video production. Consequently, VideoScore2 is not well suited for our benchmark, as it necessitates rigorous, scientifically grounded evaluations that do not align closely with human judgments on this dataset.
