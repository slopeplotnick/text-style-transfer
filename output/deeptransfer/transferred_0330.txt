An overview of participants' characteristics is provided in Table B.3 in the Appendix, where no significant differences were observed across the experimental groups. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups: (1) In the first group, participants were informed that the music excerpts they would hear were composed by AI; (2) in the second group, participants were told that the music excerpts were part of movie soundtracks; (3) in the third group, participants were instructed that they would hear music composed collaboratively by humans and AI and were tasked with identifying the composer for each piece. To ensure clarity, participants in groups 1 and 3 were provided with definitions of AI and AI music tools prior to the music rating task. Following these group-specific instructions, all participants were asked to rate 18 music excerpts, which were randomly selected to ensure a balanced representation across subgenres and AI tools. Before beginning the task, participants were instructed to adjust the audio volume to a comfortable level and review all emotion terms. While the order of terms varied randomly between participants, it remained consistent across all excerpts for each individual. Participants were further instructed to rate the emotions they personally experienced while listening, rather than the emotions they perceived the music intended to convey. Each music excerpt was presented twice. During the first presentation, participants were asked to listen attentively without responding. Subsequently, the rating questions were displayed, and the excerpt was replayed to reduce potential biases related to memory recall. Upon completing the music ratings, participants proceeded to a questionnaire section, where they provided information about their personality traits, music-related behaviors, and socio-demographic background. Additionally, participants' attitudes toward AI were assessed. After completing the questionnaires, participants were presented with a debriefing screen that explained the study’s purpose and how their data would be used. They were also invited to share their reflections on AI-generated music and their overall experience of the study through an open-ended question. The survey concluded with the presentation of individualized feedback for each participant.
