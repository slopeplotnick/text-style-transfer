An overview of participants' characteristics is provided in Table B.3 in the Appendix, where no significant differences were found across experimental groups. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups: (1) In the first group, participants were informed that the music excerpts they would hear were composed by AI; (2) in the second group, participants were told that the music excerpts originated from movie soundtracks; (3) in the third group, participants were instructed that the music was collaboratively composed by humans and AI, and they were tasked with identifying the composer for each piece. Notably, participants in groups 1 and 3 were given definitions of AI and AI music tools prior to beginning the music rating task. After receiving their respective instructions, all participants were required to rate 18 music excerpts, which were randomly selected to ensure a balanced representation across subgenres and AI tools. Before starting the task, participants were asked to adjust the audio volume to a comfortable level and familiarize themselves with the emotion terms provided. While the sequence of terms varied randomly between participants, it remained consistent across all excerpts for each individual. Importantly, participants were instructed to rate the emotions they personally experienced, rather than those they perceived the music to express. Each music excerpt was presented twice: during the first presentation, participants were asked to listen attentively without responding, and during the second presentation, the rating questions were displayed to reduce potential memory-related biases. Upon completing the music ratings, participants moved on to a questionnaire section, where they provided information about their personality traits, music-related behaviors, and socio-demographic background. Additionally, their attitudes toward AI were assessed. After finishing the questionnaires, participants were presented with a debriefing screen that explained the study and outlined how their data would be used. They were also invited to share their thoughts on AI-generated music and reflect on their overall experience of the study through an open-ended question. Finally, the survey concluded with individualized feedback for each participant.
