Data sampling. Based on Taobao search logs, we first categorize the data into distinct industries such as consumer electronics and apparel, and conduct stratified sampling according to the proportion of each industry. This ensures that the training data encompass attributes and features across all industries. Next, we divide the data within each industry into high, medium, and low frequency categories based on query frequency, conducting sampling according to their respective proportions. This approach guarantees the inclusion of both common high-frequency queries and long-tail difficult samples. Subsequently, the data is assigned to specialized annotators for labeling. Data cleaning. The errors in manual annotation primarily arise from two sources: first, annotators may overlook intricate details in lengthy multi-attribute queries, and second, the annotation guidelines themselves may contain ambiguities, failing to comprehensively cover all scenarios. To address these issues, we leverage robust language models for data filtering. Specifically, the attribute matching model employs a stringent matching strategy, demonstrating superior fine-grained feature extraction capabilities compared to manual annotation in complex multi-attribute scenarios. Moreover, by incorporating comprehensive annotation rules into its prompts, the second model is guided to perform reasoning and judgment according to the instructions, effectively identifying and filtering out corner cases beyond the scope of existing rule coverage. The rationale for employing a dual-model framework lies in leveraging the powerful long-context comprehension capability of the text model alongside the multimodal understanding capability. To enhance the reasoning capabilities of the framework, we propose a systematic, multi-stage methodology encompassing three sequential phases: Knowledge Injection & Reasoning, Multimodal Comprehension, and Rule Awareness. In the initial phase, user queries are deconstructed into discrete attributes, facilitating comparative analysis against relevant items. The subsequent phases involve evaluating the extent to which items fulfill search requirements, utilizing item specifications and predefined criteria.
