TokenPowerBench is a sophisticated, extensible, and reproducible framework designed to benchmark the power and energy consumption of LLM inference across diverse hardware platforms, model architectures, and task types. To enable meaningful comparisons between inference systems, the framework introduces energy-centric metrics such as joules per token, joules per response, and real-time power usage. These metrics are closely aligned with the operational phases of transformer-based LLMs, including the prefill and decode stages, allowing for fine-grained attribution of energy costs. Unlike traditional performance metrics such as latency or throughput, which focus on speed and efficiency, these energy metrics emphasize sustainability and cost-effectiveness—crucial considerations in modern AI systems. Power measurement in LLM inference, however, is highly sensitive to hardware configurations and instrumentation fidelity. To address this, TokenPowerBench employs a three-level measurement model, ranging from GPU-specific telemetry to full-system monitoring using advanced tools like IPMI and rack-level PDUs. This approach ensures adaptability across environments, from single-user workstations to institutional-scale testbeds, while a declarative configuration harness guarantees reproducibility across experiments. Furthermore, TokenPowerBench systematically examines key configuration dimensions—such as batch size, context length, and system architecture—to reveal their impact on energy consumption. Its scalable design supports a wide range of scenarios, from single-GPU setups to multi-node distributed systems, providing insights into both component-level energy usage and cluster-wide patterns. As LLM technology evolves, TokenPowerBench is designed to evolve alongside it. Future updates will extend its applicability to emerging GPU architectures, including next-generation NVIDIA GPUs, AMD accelerators, and novel AI chips and DPUs. Additionally, the framework will enhance its functionality to evaluate the trade-off between energy efficiency and inference accuracy, offering users actionable insights into optimizing this critical balance.
