Reducing risks is essential to minimizing resistance to change. In this section, we outline strategies to mitigate these concerns. It is imperative to address all apprehensions, irrespective of their direct association with these risks, as they can impede successful adoption. Certain risks and challenges are recurrent in the adoption of Software Product Lines (SPL). However, these challenges are not insurmountable if we navigate the transition with due consideration. The perception of complexity arises from a misconception of SPLE. Nonetheless, an SPL may also present intricate challenges for individuals familiar with SPLE. The inherent complexity of an SPL should not surpass that of the existing codebase with which the IT team is already familiar and proficient. Within the SPL, certain assumptions regarding variability are explicitly articulated through models, thereby enhancing comprehensibility and facilitating the management of complexity. We contend that the necessity for training and demonstration is intrinsically tied to this perception. Thus, we elucidated that a Software Product Line (SPL) facilitates the conservation of time, which can be allocated to the implementation of novel features and the pursuit of more innovative development endeavors. By articulating this, we mitigate the risk of losing valuable time for creativity, as articulated by user experience specialists, and the potential loss of time for innovative development. Furthermore, side effects are preventable through more systematic and comprehensive non-regression testing. Trust can be restored by providing transparent access to the Software Product Line (SPL), accompanied by comprehensive explanations and extensive documentation, and by ensuring the inclusion of all stakeholders in the project.
