Automotive AI systems face stringent operational constraints, requiring responses within milliseconds to maintain driver focus and safety. Additionally, these systems must operate within the vehicle's electrical power limitations. Furthermore, they must demonstrate robust performance under challenging environmental conditions, such as extreme brightness during daylight or complete darkness at night, and withstand temperature extremes from freezing cold to intense heat. They must also function effectively amidst vehicular vibrations and accommodate diverse user interactions. Therefore, these constraints necessitate specialized hardware designed to operate with high speed and precision, even when processing simplified data inputs. To address these challenges, we utilize the Infinity-MM dataset, a large-scale, high-quality multimodal instruction dataset designed for comprehensive vision-language training. Infinity-MM comprises approximately 44.8 million meticulously curated examples, designed to train AI systems in handling a diverse array of vision-and-language tasks. These tasks include general visual question answering (VQA) involving everyday visual reasoning, object recognition, and scene understanding; document comprehension, including forms, receipts, scientific papers, and business documents; reasoning over charts and diagrams; OCR-focused tasks; multi-turn conversational interactions; and domain-specific applications such as medical imaging, autonomous driving scenarios, and industrial inspection. Each instance within the dataset consists of an image, a structured instruction sequence, and corresponding reference responses. The dataset emphasizes high-quality, detailed responses that require multi-step reasoning rather than simple label recognition, making it particularly suitable for training models that need to provide thorough explanations—critical for automotive safety applications where users must understand the model's reasoning process. Our training methodology follows a structured four-step framework tailored specifically for the Infinity-MM dataset. During the second phase, we engage in general visual understanding tasks, encompassing object recognition, scene comprehension, and fundamental visual question answering. At this juncture, all components of the artificial intelligence system, including the vision encoder and language model, are adjusted. Training is conducted with a predetermined learning rate, initially utilizing small data batches to mitigate potential issues, before transitioning to larger batches as the process progresses.
