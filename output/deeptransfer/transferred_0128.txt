Unlike general-purpose VLMs, automotive AI systems are subject to stringent operational constraints. Latency requirements demand responses within milliseconds to ensure driver focus and safety, while strict power budgets are dictated by the vehicle's electrical architecture. Moreover, these systems must maintain robustness across challenging environmental conditions, including extreme variations in lighting—from intense daylight to complete darkness—temperature fluctuations ranging from severe cold to extreme heat, and the physical vibrations inherent to vehicular motion. Furthermore, they must accommodate diverse user interactions and usage patterns. To address these demands, the AI relies on specialized hardware designed to ensure high-speed and precise performance, even when processing simplified input data. We utilize the Infinity-MM dataset, a large-scale, high-quality multimodal instruction dataset designed to support these requirements. The dataset comprises approximately 44.8 million instances, carefully curated to balance multiple task categories including general visual question answering (VQA) related to everyday visual reasoning, object recognition, and scene understanding; document comprehension involving forms, receipts, and scientific and business documents; interpretation of charts and diagrams; optical character recognition (OCR)-focused tasks; multi-turn dialogues; and specialized fields such as medical imaging, autonomous vehicle scenarios, and industrial inspection. Each instance consists of an image, a sequence of instructions, and corresponding reference responses. The emphasis is placed on generating comprehensive responses that necessitate multi-step reasoning rather than merely selecting a straightforward label, which is crucial for automotive AI systems as they must articulate their decision-making processes clearly to engender user trust. Our training follows the four-stage curriculum prescribed by the Infinity-MM training protocol. During the second stage, we engage in general visual task training, encompassing object recognition, scene comprehension, and foundational visual question answering. At this juncture, all parameters, including those of the vision encoder and language model, are fine-tuned. The training process employs a carefully calibrated learning rate, initially utilizing small data batches to mitigate potential issues, before transitioning to larger batches as training progresses.
