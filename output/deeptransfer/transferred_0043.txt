Radiology reporting is a fundamental yet labor-intensive task for radiologists, especially when dealing with volumetric medical images such as CT and MRI. Ensuring rigorous quality control is equally critical, albeit tedious, to guarantee that the final report meets clinical standards. However, existing automated approaches predominantly focus on the report generation phase, neglecting the crucial quality control procedures, which limits their ability to provide comprehensive support to radiologists. To address this, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an advanced AI assistant equipped with orchestrated tools for automated radiology reporting and quality assurance. By leveraging large language models as the reasoning backbone, the system autonomously selects tools, plans, and executes actions, emulating the behavior of radiologists throughout the holistic radiology reporting process. The integrated toolset includes functionalities for region localization, analysis planning through the Think with Image paradigm, strategic report template selection, and iterative quality enhancement via feedback-driven refinement. Consequently, Radiologist Copilot facilitates the generation of accurate, complete, and efficient radiology reports, thereby assisting radiologists and enhancing clinical efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot significantly surpasses existing state-of-the-art methodologies in report generation. The source code will be released upon acceptance. Radiology reporting necessitates meticulous examination of medical images by radiologists, who must then compose detailed reports highlighting the primary findings. This process is often protracted and prone to errors, particularly with three-dimensional medical imaging. Therefore, the automation of report generation with integrated quality assurance is of paramount importance for supporting radiologists and improving operational efficiency.
