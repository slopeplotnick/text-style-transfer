Radiology reporting is a critical yet time-intensive and error-prone task for radiologists, particularly in the evaluation of volumetric medical images such as CT and MRI. Moreover, rigorous quality control is indispensable to ensure that the final report adheres to clinical standards, but this process is often perceived as tedious and laborious. Existing automated approaches, including radiology report generation methods and medical vision-language models, predominantly focus on the generation phase while neglecting the essential quality control procedures, thereby limiting their ability to provide comprehensive support to radiologists. To address these challenges, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an agentic AI assistant equipped with orchestrated tools for automated radiology reporting and integrated quality control. By leveraging large language models as its reasoning backbone, the system autonomously selects tools, plans tasks, and executes actions, effectively emulating the workflow of radiologists throughout the reporting process. Its orchestrated toolset includes functionalities for region localization, region analysis planning guided by the think-with-image paradigm, strategic template selection for report generation, and feedback-driven adaptive refinement for quality control. Consequently, Radiologist Copilot enables the efficient production of accurate and comprehensive radiology reports, thereby enhancing radiologist productivity and improving clinical workflows. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that Radiologist Copilot significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in radiology reporting. The source code will be released upon acceptance.
