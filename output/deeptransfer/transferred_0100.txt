Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) have been predominantly driven by scaling computational resources during the inference phase. For instance, models like OpenAI's o1 (OpenAI, 2024) and DeepSeek-R1 (Guo et al., 2025) generate extended "thinking" segments that include intermediate hypotheses, derivations, and self-corrections before producing a final response (Chen et al., 2025b; Yang et al., 2025c). This sequential scaling of inference-time computation has established a new paradigm: longer reasoning traces often improve accuracy and problem-solving capabilities. However, simply extending the reasoning process has diminishing returns and can even introduce inefficiencies, such as overthinking (Chen et al., 2024; Cuadrón et al., 2025). Empirical studies indicate that correct answers frequently emerge from shorter reasoning sequences (Zeng et al., 2025). A complementary approach is parallel scaling, wherein multiple reasoning traces are generated and their evidence is combined, typically through majority voting for tasks with definitive solutions (Wang et al., 2022; Aggarwal et al., 2023; Brown et al., 2024; Knappe et al., 2024). Nevertheless, many real-world tasks are inherently open-ended. For example, coding assistants must produce executable programs (Jimenez et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2025b), while autonomous research tools often require multi-step plans, tool integration, or detailed explanations (OpenAI, 2021; Anthropic, 2025; Alibaba-NLP, 2025). In such cases, majority voting is not applicable, as there is no single canonical answer, despite its success in structured tasks like mathematics and QA (MAA, 2025; Cobbe et al., 2021; Rein et al., 2024). This limitation raises a critical question: Can the advantages of parallel reasoning at inference-time be extended to open-ended tasks without relying on majority voting over complete outputs? To address this, we introduce THINKMERGE, a novel framework that synthesizes outputs from multiple reasoning paths by averaging logits to construct a cohesive and high-quality solution.
