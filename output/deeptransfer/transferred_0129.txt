Individuals with a stronger focus on business or less technical expertise are likely to be the most hesitant to embrace change. While they may acknowledge the potential benefits, their concerns about the rapid pace of development could outweigh their willingness to adapt. However, given that our team is relatively small, accustomed to working with new technologies, and that the adoption of a Software Product Line (SPL) was considered from the project's outset, we anticipated minimal resistance to this transition. In this context, and based on the results of our interviews, we have identified strategies to consolidate effective practices, maximize benefits, and address any concerns or risks. Highlighting and reinforcing existing effective practices during the implementation and maintenance of SPL is essential to providing reassurance. Although the team’s processes will undergo significant changes, maintaining these familiar practices will help foster motivation and facilitate acceptance of new methodologies. SPL is built on principles such as reuse, modularization, and accelerated development, which are already part of our team's practices. Therefore, these existing strengths can be leveraged to emphasize the advantages of the transition. Migrating to an SPL framework ensures sustainable reuse and decomposition. Additionally, SPL introduces other benefits, including enhanced customization, automation, and industrialization, which contribute to a more systematic and efficient development process. Furthermore, SPL adoption improves code quality by addressing technical debt, resulting in a more consistent and homogeneous code base with fewer defects and reduced duplication. The correction of common bugs further enhances code quality. Interestingly, the marketability benefits of SPL are recognized not only by product owners but are even more frequently highlighted by IT professionals. For instance, adopting this engineering approach can strengthen and enhance the company’s reputation in research and development (R&D). Moreover, SPL facilitates the faster production of new software, further underscoring its value.
