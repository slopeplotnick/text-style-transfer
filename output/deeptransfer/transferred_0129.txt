Individuals with limited technical expertise or those primarily focused on business aspects may exhibit the greatest reluctance to embrace change, as they might harbor concerns regarding the rapid pace of implementation, despite acknowledging its advantages. However, given that our team is relatively small and accustomed to engaging with emerging technologies, and considering that the adoption of a Software Product Line (SPL) was contemplated from the project's inception, we anticipated minimal resistance to this transition. Therefore, drawing upon the insights gained from interviews, we have identified strategies to consolidate existing effective practices, enhance benefits, and address any apprehensions. Moreover, it is crucial to continue emphasizing and reinforcing the existing effective practices associated with SPL implementation to ensure a sense of comfort and familiarity among team members. Although the operational methodologies will undergo substantial transformations, preserving established beneficial practices will facilitate the team's adaptation to new paradigms. SPL emphasizes efficiency, component reuse, and modularization—principles already integrated into our current practices—thereby allowing us to concentrate on leveraging the associated advantages. Transitioning to an SPL facilitates the sustainability of reuse and modularization. Additionally, the advantages of SPL implementation encompass customization, automation, and the establishment of a systematic, factory-like process. Furthermore, the enhancement of code quality is a significant benefit, contributing to the mitigation of technical debt. This indicates that the code base achieves greater consistency, exhibits fewer defects, and mitigates the risks associated with clone-and-own practices. Addressing common defects further contributes to an overall improvement in code quality. Notably, the perceived advantages are not exclusively recognized by product owners; rather, they are more frequently emphasized by IT professionals. For example, employing this type of engineering may enhance and reinforce the company's research and development (R&D) reputation. Additionally, the adoption of SPL methodologies facilitates the accelerated production of new software.
