The advent of AI agents has enabled autonomous reasoning, planning, and tool utilization. Recently, medical agents have been developed to tackle complex medical tasks. For instance, MMedAgent [12] serves as a multi-modal medical agent that employs various tools to manage tasks across different modalities, such as generating radiology reports for chest X-rays (CXR). Similarly, MedRAX [6] offers a specialized framework for chest X-ray interpretation by integrating state-of-the-art CXR analysis tools to improve diagnostic accuracy. Additionally, CT-Agent [15] is an anatomy-aware and token-efficient agent designed for 3D CT visual question answering (VQA), facilitating effective reasoning. However, in the context of radiology report generation (RRG), these agentic approaches predominantly rely on a single tool and demonstrate limited inter-tool collaboration. In contrast, our Radiologist Copilot introduces a diverse and comprehensive toolset, enabling robust inter-tool collaboration and supporting significantly more complex planning and execution of tasks. Radiology reporting is a critical yet labor-intensive and error-prone process, particularly for 3D imaging modalities. Moreover, rigorous quality assurance is essential to ensure clinical validity, yet this step is often overlooked in prior research. To address these limitations, we propose Radiologist Copilot, an AI agentic assistant equipped with an orchestrated suite of tools for automated radiology reporting and quality assurance, aimed at supporting radiologists and enhancing clinical efficiency. As illustrated in Fig. 1, Radiologist Copilot is designed to assist radiologists throughout the entire radiology reporting workflow. The agentic assistant begins by initializing the tool library and subsequently employs the action planner and executor to iteratively plan and execute tasks. A memory module systematically records executed actions and their outcomes, enabling task completion verification. The action executor is responsible for generating and executing commands based on the next action determined by the action planner. For each action, it formulates the appropriate command for the selected tool, executes the command, and retrieves the resulting outcome.
