The advent of AI agents facilitates autonomous reasoning, planning, and tool usage. Recently, medical agents have been introduced to undertake complex medical tasks. For instance, MMedAgent employs a variety of tools to perform medical tasks, such as generating radiology reports for chest X-rays. MedRAX constitutes a specialized agent framework for the interpretation of chest X-rays, integrating state-of-the-art CXR analysis tools. Similarly, CT-Agent is an anatomy-aware and token-efficient agent designed for 3D CT scan visual question answering, facilitating effective reasoning. However, in the context of radiology report generation, these agentic methodologies typically rely on a singular tool and exhibit a lack of cooperative interaction among various tools. In contrast, the Radiologist Copilot distinguishes itself by integrating a diverse array of tools that facilitate seamless inter-tool collaboration and enable the planning and execution of highly complex tasks. Radiology reporting is essential but time-consuming and error-prone, particularly for 3D medical imaging. Moreover, rigorous quality assurance of radiology reports, while essential to ensure clinical reliability, remains a laborious and often overlooked component in prior studies. To address these challenges, we developed Radiologist Copilot, an AI-driven assistant equipped with an integrated suite of tools designed to facilitate automated radiology reporting with quality assurance, thereby supporting radiologists and enhancing clinical workflow efficiency. As illustrated in Fig. 1, Radiologist Copilot is engineered to assist radiologists throughout the comprehensive radiology reporting process. The system initializes by configuring the tool library and subsequently employs the action planner and action executor to systematically plan and execute tasks. The memory module systematically records executed actions and their corresponding outcomes, facilitating the verification of task completion. The action executor is tasked with generating and implementing commands based on the subsequent action determined by the action planner. For each action, it formulates the appropriate command for the designated tool, executes the command, and derives the resultant outcome.
