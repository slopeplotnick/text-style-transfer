What streamers say is super important for people deciding what to buy during live online shopping, especially when they're also teaching stuff and giving information while showing products. Other research has already found that what streamers say can affect how much stuff they sell, but not many studies have really dug into the exact meanings in what they say from different angles and then measured how much that helps guess future sales. The main thing we did in our study was create a system to predict how much stuff will sell during those live shopping videos where they teach you things. This system combines ideas about social support with specially trained AI language programs. It uses them to carefully pull out all sorts of hidden meanings from what streamers say, which then helps predict sales. For our tests, we gathered information from 80 live videos on 35 different Douyin channels over two months. When we tested how well our system could figure out social support messages, our special Ernie-SFT AI did much better than the usual best AI. It was more accurate by 11.12%, better on F1-score by 11.87%, and also better on macro F1-score by 7.83%. For the sales guessing tests, we checked our system using four popular ways to sort data, and it consistently worked better. The best one of those sorting methods was more accurate by 12.53%, better on F1-score by 10.83%, and better on macro F1-score by 4.24%. What we found really shows how much those social support messages from streamers can help predict things. This gives streamers good tips and helps platforms use data to make their services even better.
