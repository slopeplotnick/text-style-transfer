The process of detecting varying levels of depression and stress in user-generated content is exemplified through two distinct cases, highlighting the comparative performance of the Menta and Qwen-3 models. In the first case, concerning depression level detection, a Reddit post categorized as 'Minimum' involves a user expressing concerns about a persistent physical sensation involving their tongue, as illustrated in Figure 10. The user articulates their worries in a calm manner while seeking assistance from the community, demonstrating a willingness to engage despite evident discomfort. The Menta model accurately classifies the post as 'Minimum' and provides an explanation for its decision, noting the absence of intense emotional language and recognizing the user’s composed tone, which collectively indicate low mental distress. This observation underscores the distinction between general discomfort and depression. Conversely, the Qwen-3 model erroneously categorizes the post as 'Severe,' misinterpreting the physical sensations and discomfort as indicative of profound mental distress. This misclassification stems from an overemphasis on overt signs of distress without adequately differentiating somatic complaints from depression-specific symptoms. The Menta model, through extensive training, has refined its ability to identify depression-related markers and align predictions with the criteria established within the dataset, thereby demonstrating enhanced accuracy in delineating ordinary distress from clinical depression. In the second case, which pertains to stress level detection, a Reddit post labeled 'No stress' depicts a user deliberating a housing decision in collaboration with a friend. The post is characterized by a logical presentation of choices and a calm demeanor, devoid of panic or excessive pressure. The Menta model appropriately identifies the post as 'No stress' by highlighting the user’s rational tone and maturity in navigating a challenging decision, accurately interpreting the absence of impulsive or anxiety-driven language as indicative of low stress levels. In contrast, the Qwen-3 model incorrectly classifies the post as 'Stressed,' disproportionately focusing on expressions such as 'cold feet' and '99% sure,' and erroneously associating careful deliberation with internal emotional turmoil.
