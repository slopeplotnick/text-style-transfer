PEFT-FACTORY is designed to calculate classification and efficiency metrics during prediction tasks, incorporating token accuracy and semantic similarity metrics such as Rouge and Bleu. The classification process employs standard metrics of Accuracy and F1, while efficiency is evaluated using the PSCP metric, which is characterized by its flexibility and considers factors including the number of parameters, memory usage, and prediction time. These metrics are systematically set up in the train/sft/metric.py file for supervised fine-tuning, thereby adhering to the configuration established by LLaMA-Factory, with distinct data classes for each metric named ComputeAccuracy, ComputeF1, and ComputePSCP. Within the classification framework, a configurable setting exists in the training options, termed compute_classification.metrics, which can be activated or deactivated to encompass classification metrics. Furthermore, efficiency metrics are managed through a separate setting known as compute_pscp, with additional elaboration provided in Appendix B.3. The design of PEFT-FACTORY prioritizes flexibility and user-friendliness, rendering it a valuable tool for conducting research on PEFT methods. Two illustrative examples are provided to demonstrate its contribution to reproducibility and benchmarking. The modular architecture of PEFT-FACTORY facilitates researchers and developers in the creation and testing of novel PEFT methods. Presently, the development of new PEFT methods is often hampered by shared code that may not function optimally or be readily reproducible. Moreover, developers frequently find themselves compelled to independently write training and evaluation code, a process that is prone to repetition, errors, and results inconsistency.
