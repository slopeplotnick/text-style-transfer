Cov_tgsp and CSP typically perform effectively, as evidenced by the results presented in Table 2 and Figure 3; however, certain variations can be observed contingent upon the dataset utilized. Specifically, CSP and cov_tgsp generally rank highly across datasets, yet their performance on the PhysionetMI dataset is comparatively inferior to that observed with Cho2017 and Zhou2016. This discrepancy may be attributed to differing experimental methodologies, including sampling frequency, the number of electrodes employed, and participant count. It has been demonstrated that the efficacy of these methods in extracting motor imagery from EEG signals is considerably dependent on the dataset in question. On Zhou2016, the combination of CSP with StandardScaler and logistic regression achieved the highest accuracy of 0.87, closely followed by cov_tgsp with RobustScaler and logistic regression at 0.84. Conversely, on Cho2017, cov_tgsp surpassed CSP, attaining a top score of 0.74 as opposed to 0.68. Methods incorporating instantaneous functional connectivity and non-linear features were found to be less effective across all datasets. For instance, svd_entropy with RobustScaler and logistic regression produced scores of 0.68 on Zhou2016, 0.59 on Cho2017, and 0.54 on PhysionetMI. The PhysionetMI dataset exhibited generally lower scores, with the optimal configuration (cov_tgsp) reaching only 0.64, whereas CSP averaged 0.62 across various frequency bands. This indicates that despite the capability of non-linear and connectivity-based features to capture intricate EEG signal patterns, they may not be optimal for motor imagery classification in this context. Furthermore, when individual subjects were analyzed, CSP and cov_tgsp consistently demonstrated superior performance across datasets, as illustrated in Figures 5 and 6 for Cho2017 and Zhou2016; nevertheless, this trend was less pronounced within the PhysionetMI dataset (Figure 7), where these methods do not consistently outperform alternatives. In some instances, other approaches achieved superior scores: for example, Hjorth parameters outperformed CSP and cov_tgsp for subjects #57 and #59, while the instantaneous connectivity method with TGSP was most effective for subjects #17 and #73.
