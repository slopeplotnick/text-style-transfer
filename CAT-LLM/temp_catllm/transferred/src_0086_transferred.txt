In the present study, seven distinct methodologies for model combination were evaluated, namely Linear, Karcher Mean, SLERP, NuSLERP, TIES, DELLA, and Nearswap. These techniques were employed to amalgamate a bias-inverted model with a pre-trained large language model. Linear, as proposed by Wortsman et al. in 2022, represents the most straightforward approach by merely aggregating and averaging the weights of fine-tuned models, thus offering a cost-effective and efficacious solution. Conversely, Karcher Mean, introduced by Goddard et al. in 2024, adopts an alternative strategy by accounting for the curved space of model parameters, consequently enhancing performance in non-flat spaces. SLERP, also delineated by Goddard in 2024, facilitates the mixing of model weights along a curved path on a spherical surface, thereby preserving the inherent shape of the space. Furthermore, NuSLERP, another innovation from Goddard in 2024, modifies SLERP by permitting disparate sections of the model to blend differently, concentrating more on salient components to achieve improved balance and performance. TIES, formulated by Yadav in 2023, amalgamates models by scrutinizing parameter differences pertinent to specific tasks, thus diminishing interference and preserving the intrinsic strengths of each model. DELLA, as developed by Deep in 2024, mitigates interference by excising less significant updates, employing an astute technique to retain crucial elements, which consequently aids in maintaining the performance of the original model.
