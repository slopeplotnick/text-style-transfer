The new standard, termed HEALTHCONTRADICT, has been developed with the objective of evaluating the proficiency of language models in processing extensive and conflicting medical information. This standard demonstrates superiority over current medical question-answer benchmarks in assessing model performance, specifically in their ability to utilize accurate information, disregard erroneous data, and resolve conflicting content. It is noteworthy that the TREC Health Misinformation tracks represent the sole public resource comprising expert-generated health questions accompanied by definitive answers and scientific validation, alongside document pools containing both corroborative and contradictory evidence assessed by experts. These components are crucial for formulating health questions with definitive answers, using pairs of conflicting documents to assess the capacity of language models to manage medical knowledge conflicts. Within HEALTHCONTRADICT, the focus is placed upon significant and credible documents that effectively substantiate the response to a query. Consistency in labeling was achieved by modifying the 2019 tags "effective"/"ineffective" and the 2021 tags "supportive"/"dissuades" to "yes"/"no," aligning them with the 2022 labels. Furthermore, if two documents pertaining to the same question express opposing perspectives—one affirmative, one negative—they are classified as a contradictory pair. Additionally, a document is deemed accurate if its perspective aligns with scientific evidence, and inaccurate if it contradicts such evidence. The original datasets feature 130 expert-validated questions (50 in 2019, 35 in 2021, and 45 in 2022), encompassing questions with yes/no perspectives and those supported by both corroborative and contradictory documents. Application of this criterion reduces the dataset to 110 questions. Supporting and opposing documents are matched into contradiction pairs, ensuring each document is utilized singularly, given that some documents may pertain to multiple questions. Questions unable to form at least one contradiction pair due to insufficient unique documents are excluded. Consequently, the final dataset comprises 81 questions and 920 pairs of conflicting documents.
