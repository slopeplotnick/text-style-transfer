The MODOMA system has been established for the purpose of conducting language learning experiments, allowing for the adjustment of experimental parameters through the selection of specific settings. It encompasses various aspects of language acquisition, including adult language processes, the daughter agent's interaction, and the underlying mechanisms of language learning, with configurability available for all these components. MODOMA is capable of executing multiple language learning processes, such as the one discussed by Shakouri et al. in 2025. The initial setting in Table 1 pertains to the activation status of functional category learning, which is consistently enabled across all referenced experiments. Subsequent settings determine critical elements of the experimental design. The setting associated with the mother agent governs the structural composition of Delilah's linguistic output, influencing whether it is organized into complete sentences or noun phrases. Variations in this setting are implemented across the training and testing experiments presented in this study. Furthermore, the minimum dataset required by the daughter agent for processing prior to commencing the learning of functional and content categories is subject to modification throughout the experiments. These two settings constitute the foundational framework of the experimental design. In the training experiments, the initiation of learning functional and content categories is contingent upon the processing of 1,000 noun phrases and 1,000 sentences, followed by 10,000 noun phrases and 10,000 sentences. This methodology facilitates the determination of the requisite number of examples from Delilah needed to discern the underlying pattern. Moreover, the setting for functional category learning dictates the ratio employed to transform distinctions between function and content words into explicit linguistic knowledge. The training sessions reported in this paper aim to identify the optimal values for these settings, while the test experiments serve to validate these configurations using novel data from Delilah.
