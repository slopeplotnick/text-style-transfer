DeepSeek-V3.2 demonstrates comparable performance to Kimi-k2-thinking and GPT-5 across various reasoning assessments, while achieving notable advancements in the handling of tasks by open models, particularly excelling in specific agent-based tasks derived from EvalSys and other studies conducted in 2025. Furthermore, DeepSeek-V3.2 represents a cost-effective alternative for agent tasks, narrowing the disparity between open-source and leading proprietary models while maintaining a significantly lower cost. In order to enhance the reasoning capabilities of open models further, the development of DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale was undertaken by relaxing constraints on sequence length, resulting in performance parity with the top closed-source system Gemini-3.0-Pro and demonstrating remarkable success in competitive events such as the IOI, ICPC World Final, IMO, and CMO held in 2025. The architectural foundation of DeepSeek-V3.2 remains consistent with DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, with the primary modification from the preceding iteration, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, involving the integration of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) accompanied by extended training procedures. The DSA mechanism comprises two critical components: a rapid indexing system and an intricate token selection framework. Initially, DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, characterized by its extended context length of 128K, served as the basis for the development of DeepSeek-V3.2, which underwent additional training conducted in two distinct stages utilizing data analogous to the extended context data applied in DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus. Empirical evaluations conducted in September 2025 assessed the performance of DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp across a variety of benchmarks in comparison to DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus, revealing comparable outcomes. It is observed that DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp exhibits a substantially improved capability for efficiently processing long sequences while maintaining performance levels comparable to those of DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus across tasks involving both short and extended sequences.
