Concerns have been expressed regarding migration and its potential negative impacts. These apprehensions primarily focus on possible adverse outcomes. During discussions about the benefits, participants simultaneously articulated their anxieties concerning perceived risks. Two pivotal inquiries facilitated the exploration of these apprehensions: "Do you think this approach is useful for the company's current processes?" and "What do you think about a platform to quickly develop similar software?" Seven distinct fears were identified, as depicted in Figure 4. The predominant concerns among IT professionals include the potential ineffectiveness of the approach, excessive complexity, and resistance to change, as outlined in Table 7. Ultimately, regardless of their role, stakeholders expressed a desire for training and demonstrations to comprehend the utility of an SPL for their work and the methodology for integrating this new approach. Based on insights into best practices, benefits, risks, and fears, guidelines have been formulated to facilitate the transition to an SPL. These guidelines were derived from discussions and decisions within the company. This section delineates anticipated outcomes (Sect. 5.1), implementation of best practices (Sect. 5.2), promotion of future benefits (Sect. 5.3), mitigation of risks (Sect. 5.4), alleviation of fears (Sect. 5.5), specific actions to be undertaken (Sect. 5.6), and a comprehensive discussion (Sect. 5.7). Prior to the interviews, expectations regarding potential outcomes were considered. It was hypothesized that individuals familiar with or appreciative of reusability would exhibit the most positive attitudes and engagement, thereby serving as primary motivators and supporters of the project. In this particular context, more experienced developers emphasized mindset considerations, whereas those less experienced concentrated on technical aspects. However, this disparity in experience did not significantly affect the quantity or quality of responses obtained.
