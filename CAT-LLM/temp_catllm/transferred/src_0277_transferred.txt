Advanced AI tools are being utilized to enhance online strategies and system configurations. Specifically, improved relevance scores derived from the upgraded model are employed to refine ranking processes, with optimization achieved through the method known as Pareto optimization. In terms of system enhancements, the superior performance of the model has led to the elimination or substitution of outdated rule-based methodologies. These combined modifications have demonstrated significant improvements. For instance, studies such as DeepSeek-R1 have identified the model's capacity to manage extended reasoning steps, occasionally exhibiting sudden insights. Furthermore, an examination of the evolution in the length of the model's responses during training, as depicted in Figure 17, indicates that as the model's proficiency increased, its responses became more concise and eventually stabilized. This implies that prolonged reasoning steps are not invariably requisite for improvement and occur predominantly during the training phase. Additionally, it was observed that task-specific reasoning length varies; for instance, mathematical and coding tasks necessitate detailed step-by-step reasoning whereas tasks involving user intent identification and item matching benefit from succinct and clear reasoning processes. In these latter cases, additional reasoning does not contribute to enhancement. Regarding image-related tasks, efficiency is achieved by employing models with inherent image comprehension capabilities, such as Visual Language Models (VLMs), rather than incorporating supplementary steps like caption creation. This concept was evaluated by employing a teacher model, Qwen2.5-VL-72B, to facilitate two distinct phases: knowledge generation and reasoning followed by the application of rules.
