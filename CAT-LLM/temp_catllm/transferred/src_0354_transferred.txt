Creating virtual characters that exhibit lifelike movements is essential for enhancing the realism of video games and virtual reality experiences. Furthermore, the development of naturalistic motion in robotics is significant, as it contributes to improved safety and energy efficiency. The study of human movement serves as a valuable foundation for enabling robots to acquire versatile skills applicable across diverse scenarios. To achieve realistic movements, data-driven methodologies, which leverage real human motion data, are frequently employed. Notably, approaches such as autoregressive models, variational autoencoders, and diffusion models have been utilized to generate convincing animations. However, these techniques often fail to adhere to the principles of physics, resulting in movements that may appear unnatural when applied to novel situations. In contrast, physics-based methods aim to produce motions that conform to real-world physical laws by employing control policies and dynamic equations, utilizing techniques such as trajectory optimization and reinforcement learning. Traditional approaches that did not integrate data often succeeded in enabling robotic motion; nonetheless, the resulting movements were typically perceived as unnatural. Consequently, replicating human-like motion solely through heuristic methods has proven to be challenging. Recent advancements have emphasized data-driven techniques, including model predictive control and trajectory optimization, which have demonstrated improved fidelity in replicating human movements. Moreover, reinforcement learning methods have facilitated the generation of more realistic and agile motions by leveraging real movement clips. Nevertheless, these approaches are frequently constrained by the limitations of the source clips, restricting the robot’s ability to perform tasks beyond those demonstrated in the reference data. To address this limitation, certain systems have integrated specialized planners or high-level policies designed to select optimal movements or skills tailored to the specific requirements of the task.
