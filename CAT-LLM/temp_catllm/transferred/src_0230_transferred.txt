A method was employed to decompose stories into constituent elements, including events, characters, and settings, which were subsequently converted into a structured JSON format. This data was utilized by GPT-4 to generate novel stories that retained the core thematic components while incorporating additional creative elements. Specific evaluation criteria were established to assess the quality of the generated stories, focusing on attributes such as functional coherence, creativity, readability, and the extent to which they appeared authentic and reflective. Additional artificial intelligence tools, such as GPT-4o, were implemented to evaluate both the original and generated stories, categorizing them as either "Chosen" or "Rejected." The processed information was stored in JSON format alongside the associated labels, thereby facilitating the creation of a comprehensive dataset. This dataset revealed emotional patterns and other salient features, as illustrated in Figure 4. The dataset was partitioned into two subsets: one allocated for training purposes and the other reserved for testing, with the majority designated for training. Specifically, the training subset encompassed 8,866 pairs of preference data to enhance the model, while the testing subset comprised 985 pairs of unseen data. The Llama-3-8B model was optimized using the AdamW optimizer and fine-tuned through the application of LoRA methodology, employing predefined parameters for learning rate, batch size, and training iterations, which collectively resulted in a test loss of 0.038. These parameters were selected based on a combination of prior recommendations, empirical testing, and computational resource constraints. Subsequent experiments were conducted to investigate the relative significance of individual story components in the generation of high-quality narratives. Beginning with a baseline version of the Llama-3-8B model that incorporated all components, elements such as "events" were systematically excluded, followed by retraining and model adjustment to accommodate the modified configuration. Uniform training protocols were applied across all models to ensure the comparability of experimental results. The performance of each modified model was evaluated using a separate test dataset, while the generated stories were assessed according to the previously established criteria, encompassing seven dimensions including creativity and clarity. The findings from these evaluations are detailed in Section 6.
