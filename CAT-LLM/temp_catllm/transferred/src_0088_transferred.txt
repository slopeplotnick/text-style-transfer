The operational mechanism of the fine-tuned model is depicted in Figure 1. Initially, significant details are extracted from both the query and item descriptions, including product names, brands, model specifics, and target demographics. Subsequently, an assessment is conducted to evaluate the congruence of these details between the query and the item to ascertain their relevance. The CoT tuning facilitates the model in accurately predicting relevance labels while also providing explanatory insights. Upon analyzing the outputs of the fine-tuned model, it is observed that its rigor in relevance assessment often results in the categorization of products as "Irrelevant" when certain query details are absent from the product description, despite their negligible impact on purchasing decisions. This stringent approach leads to an increased incidence of false negatives, thereby diminishing the model's recall rate and adversely affecting platform transactions and revenue. In order to address this issue, alignment of the model's predictions with actual user behavior and feedback is prioritized. Historical data of purchased query-item pairs that the fine-tuned model identified as "Irrelevant" is utilized to construct a training set. These predictions, considered overly strict, are treated as suboptimal cases, while GPT-40 is employed to generate "Relevant" reasoning for the original query-item pairs, which are regarded as optimal cases. The KTO algorithm is subsequently employed to align the model with these preferences. The KTO algorithm distinguishes itself from other alignment methods by utilizing straightforward binary labeled data, thereby simplifying and expediting the alignment process.
