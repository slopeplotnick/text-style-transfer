The comprehensive analysis of interview summaries was conducted to enhance the understanding of the results. During the interviews, participants' statements were recorded, and notes were meticulously documented by hand, followed by a thorough summation of the recorded content. This methodology ensured that no significant information was overlooked, aligning with common research practices in interview data management. The research team, including the individual responsible for conducting the interviews, systematically analyzed the information. Following this, quantitative methodologies were developed to facilitate a numerical analysis. Specifically, instances of good practices, benefits, risks, and fears mentioned by participants were quantified. Similar responses were identified and articulated using simplified terminology to encapsulate the core concepts. For instance, when a participant expressed, "The genericity pushes to factorize more and more," the terms "genericity" and "factorisation" were noted. Additionally, the frequency of particular words and their attribution to specific individuals were examined. To identify key terms, several steps were undertaken: non-essential words such as "the" and "and" were eliminated, followed by sorting the remaining words based on their frequency of occurrence. The most pertinent terms were selected and verified within the context of the documents to ensure coherence. Occasionally, supplementary words were added to clarify meanings, such as replacing "complex" with "too complex." Furthermore, a comparative analysis was performed between the interview-derived terminology and that prevalent in academic literature. In instances where discrepancies arose, adjustments were made, for example, substituting "unusable" with "useless," whereas terms that aligned well with the literature were retained. This approach facilitated the integration of insights from existing literature, thereby enhancing the relevance and linguistic congruence of the analysis. Ultimately, this process enabled the identification of salient terms and bolstered the robustness of the analytical outcomes.
