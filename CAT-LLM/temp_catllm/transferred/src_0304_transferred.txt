In this study, a total of 5,500 Level 3-5 problems were compiled from the MATH dataset, drawing upon previous research. The performance of these problems was evaluated using various benchmarks including AIME 2024, AMC, MATH500, GSM8K, Minerva, OlympiadBench, and College Math. Additionally, ARC-C and GPQA-Diamond were considered for their relevance outside the conventional scope. The methodology employed involved greedy decoding with a token limit of 3,000. The CAPO technique was tested alongside four principal reinforcement learning methods: GRPO, PPO, RLOO, and Reinforce++. Each method was further analyzed with and without the integration of CAPO to assess its impact. This approach enabled an evaluation of both the standalone effectiveness of the original methods and the enhancements achieved through CAPO. Consistency in experimental conditions was ensured by maintaining uniform setup details across all methods. Given the distinct requirements of each method, comprehensive training details are documented in Appendix B. The study utilized the GUI-R1-3K dataset from OS-Atlas, comprising 3,000 examples of vision-language-action across web and mobile platforms. This dataset aligns visual states with natural language instructions and action sequences, thus serving as an effective testbed for evaluating reasoning and planning capabilities. For planning, the datasets GUI-Act-Web, OmniAct-Web, and AndroidControl-Low/High were employed, while Screenshot Pro was used for perception assessment. The efficacy of GRPO and its CAPO-enhanced version was compared using the primary GUI benchmark setup. This additional testing was conducted to demonstrate the versatility of CAPO in various reasoning scenarios beyond mathematical contexts.
