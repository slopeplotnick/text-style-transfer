The implementation of the KARMA system is aimed at enhancing the functionality and safety of large language models in specific domains. KARMA is comprised of three primary components: the first component is designed to facilitate the acquisition of precise information from a variety of sources, the second component governs the utilization of the acquired information, and the third component ensures the safety and reliability of the generated responses. Methods to enhance KARMA have been proposed by several researchers. Specifically, one method involves the integration of temporal and spatial data to improve answer quality. Furthermore, a system that ranks and recalls information has been proposed to address temporal changes and enhance the accuracy of results. There is also a method that extracts significant words from sentences to improve the system’s ability to comprehend relationships. It has been demonstrated by researchers that the implementation of specific tools within a testing setup yielded positive results for medical queries, suggesting that the incorporation of these tools into KARMA could enhance its performance in healthcare applications. Moreover, there is a recommendation to adjust safety parameters dynamically during system operation to increase safety for sensitive inquiries. This is complemented by a method that amalgamates diverse data types and modulates the focus on each type, potentially enabling KARMA to better manage complex or heterogeneous data. Consequently, these proposed advancements are intended to render KARMA more intelligent, secure, and dependable in practical applications.
