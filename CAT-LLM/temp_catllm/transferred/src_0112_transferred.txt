The present study undertakes a comparative analysis of our samples against EMOPIA MIDI files, whereby the major key ratio is indicative of the music's perceived positivity, while average note length and note velocity serve as measures of energetic expression. Specifically, our samples predominantly align with EMOPIA across three quadrants, with the exception of Q2, where a disproportionately higher number of major-key tracks are generated by our model compared to EMOPIA. This discrepancy is likely attributable to the dataset composition, which is characterized by a preponderance of major-key tracks relative to minor-key ones, coupled with the limited size of the fine-tuning set. In terms of energy, the fine-tuned model accurately replicates the note-length pattern, with shorter notes prevalent in high-energy quadrants (Q1 and Q2) and longer notes observed in low-energy quadrants (Q3 and Q4), mirroring the EMOPIA dataset. However, the differentiation in note velocity across quadrants is less pronounced, potentially due to REMI's simplification of MIDI velocities into fewer categories, thereby constraining the range and impeding discernible differences between quadrants. Listener feedback partially corroborates these findings, indicating that the model succeeds in capturing certain musical attributes related to energy and positivity, although a stronger correlation is evident in the domain of energy as opposed to positivity, wherein results appear quasi-random. It is hypothesized that the excessive generation of major-key tracks may contribute to this phenomenon. Consequently, the inclusion of additional metrics is proposed to elucidate areas where positivity generation may be deficient. Furthermore, this project introduces Story2MIDI, a transformer-based model designed to generate symbolic music from emotional text input. To achieve this, a text-music dataset was constructed, alongside the development of an encoder-decoder system to facilitate the linkage of textual emotions with musical output. The encoder underwent fine-tuning to produce distinct emotion representations across varying energy and positivity levels, whereas the decoder was initially trained on a large collection of symbolic music and subsequently fine-tuned on a smaller, emotion-labeled set to enable emotion-based music generation. Empirical evaluation of Story2MIDI, encompassing listener feedback and music metrics, affirms the model's efficacy while simultaneously identifying areas necessitating further refinement.
