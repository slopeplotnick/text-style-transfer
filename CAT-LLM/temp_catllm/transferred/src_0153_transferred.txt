A method known as Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) was employed utilizing the Qwen-1.5 model as the principal framework. This approach facilitated the acquisition of general language skills by the model, thereby enabling its application to e-commerce tasks. Specifically, knowledge graphs were utilized as data sources, and domain-specific information was incorporated through meticulously designed prompts to assist the model in comprehending e-commerce-related patterns and relationships. The model's performance was evaluated using standard tests and relevance metrics to ensure accuracy and versatility in handling diverse queries. Following training, the model was deployed to assist with labeling tasks, thereby expediting the process and enhancing data quality by detecting errors. Furthermore, the model was engaged in more complex tasks requiring the understanding of intricate queries, implicit meanings, and logical reasoning for constructing pathways. Simple instructions proved inadequate for these tasks; hence, a method termed Chain-of-Thought (CoT) was applied, which enabled the model to deconstruct problems, discern relationships, and systematically compare pathways, thereby significantly enhancing its capability to manage challenging queries. Additionally, a system was devised to identify difficult cases and refine the model through feedback. Subsequently, CoT data were utilized for tasks such as attribute extraction and matching, and the model was retrained using the SFT method with Qwen-2, thereby enhancing its ability to manage reasoning steps while maintaining focus on specific tasks.
