Guardrails, as systems designed to ensure safety in interactions between humans and artificial intelligence (AI), initially employed rudimentary rules aimed at filtering content, which proved ineffective as the landscape evolved and new challenges emerged. These early systems demonstrated limitations in both comprehensiveness and accuracy. In recent developments, advanced AI models have been utilized, enhancing flexibility and generalization capabilities. Notably, Llama Guard serves as a model facilitating safe exchanges between users and AI, drawing upon specialized safety data procured from Meta AI. Additional models, such as Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard, have been introduced to address visual safety concerns. However, despite these advancements, certain challenges persist. Prior research predominantly concentrated on textual and image-based content, whereas video and audio formats were subjected to simplistic approaches lacking contextual understanding. Furthermore, existing systems are predominantly tailored to single media types or specific scenarios, thereby exhibiting limitations in handling multiple media formats or facilitating cross-media reasoning. Consequently, OMNIGUARD has been developed as an innovative system capable of integrating diverse media types and executing comprehensive reasoning processes to ensure safety. It strives to deliver transparent and exhaustive safety evaluations across all media formats, thereby addressing previously identified deficiencies.
