In the third stage, the focus is placed on the training of the model to adhere to specific instructions pertinent to diverse tasks within Infinity-MM. A comprehensive dataset is utilized, encompassing document understanding, reasoning with charts, tasks centered around optical character recognition (OCR), and bidirectional conversations. Specifically, the Infinity-MM method is employed, assigning varied weights to tasks: 35% to general visual question answering, 25% to document understanding, 20% to chart reasoning, 15% to OCR tasks, and 5% to conversations and specialized areas. Furthermore, the learning rate is set at 1e-5, and training is conducted for one round with a batch size of 512. The primary objective involves predicting the subsequent word using standard language modeling techniques. Subsequently, the model, constructed for the Neural Processing Unit (NPU), is evaluated against contemporary vision-language models employing five distinct tests. It is compared with recent models such as InternVL2 and Qwen2-VL, which are tailored for disparate systems and exhibit various sizes. The AutoNeural model, trained to this third stage, demonstrates an optimal combination of accuracy and training speed. Five benchmarks are employed to assess different skill sets: MMStar evaluates the model's proficiency in answering visual questions necessitating multiple steps and common sense; HallusionBench examines the model's capability in navigating language and visual intricacies; MathVista_MINI tests the model's aptitude in addressing mathematical problems with the aid of diagrams and charts; AI2D_TEST assesses the model's comprehension of diagrams and scientific concepts derived from textbooks; OCRBench scrutinizes the model's ability to decipher text within images from varied scenes, documents, and signs. The accuracy of the model is measured across each test, and an average score is calculated to evaluate its overall proficiency in managing diverse task types.
