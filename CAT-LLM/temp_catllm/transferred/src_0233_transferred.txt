The tracking of the main character's speed and directional changes is conducted in their own space at the final point in a movement sequence. The mechanics of the character's joint movements are examined. The positioning of the character's hands and feet in a three-dimensional space is depicted within their own domain. To evaluate the efficacy of the Scalable Motion Policy (SMP) framework, it is utilized for training in challenging movement control tasks. In Section 8.1, the flexibility of SMP is demonstrated through the application of a pre-trained model to generate novel control rules without the necessity of original movement data. By employing a single style-based model trained on a comprehensive dataset, it is possible to adapt the model for tasks in various styles by merely configuring it to the desired style. Furthermore, new styles can be synthesized by combining the trained model with differing styles. Section 8.2 illustrates that a singular movement model is applicable to diverse tasks such as steering, target acquisition, and dodgeball gameplay. In addition to modulating the character's behavior, Section 8.3 reveals that SMP is capable of managing interactions between individuals and objects. Through training the model on data pertaining to human-object interactions, SMP acquires the ability to regulate both object and character movements, enabling characters to emulate human-like behaviors when interacting with objects. SMP further assists characters in mastering natural recovery techniques, as elucidated in Section 8.4. Analogous to other methodologies that align with data distributions, the rules derived from SMP training can engender novel skills absent from the original dataset. In Section 8.5, when trained on a concise walk-jog-run dataset, SMP intrinsically devises distinct locomotion patterns that facilitate characters in matching various speeds and transitioning seamlessly between movements. Lastly, to assess the proficiency of SMP in replicating movements, it is evaluated on tasks involving the imitation of individual clips, as detailed in Section 8.6. SMP demonstrates the capability to accurately replicate a broad spectrum of dynamic and acrobatic maneuvers, achieving a level of imitation quality comparable to leading methods in the field of imitation learning.
