The large language model (LLM) was adjusted utilizing the Knowledge Transfer Optimization (KTO) method and subsequently employed to label specific online items. When the online BERT model incorrectly predicts difficult examples, these are incorporated into the training set to enhance the model's performance. The LLM developed within the Relevance Detection (RD) module assists in identifying the challenging examples where errors occur in the online model's predictions. However, this approach addresses only isolated mistakes rather than resolving a broader range of issues. When there are slight changes in search terms or product descriptions, inaccuracies may persist in the online model's predictions. Therefore, an LLM was developed to generate data that the online model frequently misclassifies, thereby improving its functionality and accuracy. For any search-product pair, the model is capable of altering the details of the search or product based on instructions, thereby creating new examples categorized as either Relevant or Irrelevant. Training examples are obtained using the GPT-4o model with a limited number of instances. To ensure the model comprehends the online model's errors, results where incorrect predictions are made are consistently selected for training the LLM. Following the development of the LLM that recognizes error types, data from online searches are used to create challenging examples that may confuse the online model. To mitigate the impact of erroneous data during training, the relevance model from the RD module is employed to filter the results.
