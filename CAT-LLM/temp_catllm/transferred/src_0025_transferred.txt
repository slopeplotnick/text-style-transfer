The project focuses on the integration of new features and updates into the existing framework, encompassing technological advancements alongside improvements in agricultural processes. The primary objective is to expedite the development cycle, exemplified by achieving faster application creation compared to previous iterations. This initiative incorporates concepts such as code reuse, innovation, and rapid execution, originating from a code base designed for reusability, which proves instrumental in facilitating the migration project. A total of seven beneficial practices were identified, with factorization and reuse being the most prominent, appearing nine and eight times respectively, as illustrated in Figure 1. Understanding these benefits is deemed essential as it serves to motivate the project team. Customization emerges as a pivotal aspect of a Software Product Line (SPL), frequently highlighted by professionals in IT, User Experience (UX), and the agricultural domain. An SPL is conceptualized as a platform capable of generating customized software solutions derived from pre-existing ones. Speed constitutes the second most referenced concept, particularly emphasized by IT, Product Owners (PO), and UX personnel, referring to the accelerated development of new software. Automation, defined as the automatic creation of software, was similarly noted, with IT and PO stakeholders perceiving automation and speed as interconnected. Industrialization, discussed by IT and UX contributors, refers to the on-demand production of applications at reduced costs. The enhancement of code quality, exclusively mentioned by IT professionals, pertains to achieving consistency, testability, and reliability in the codebase. Additionally, the notion of selling advantages, referenced by IT and PO teams, encompasses the ability to increase software sales or respond swiftly to market opportunities.
