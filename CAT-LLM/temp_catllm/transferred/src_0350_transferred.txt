Significant progress has been made in the domain of sound and music creation utilizing computational systems; however, a standardized methodology for evaluating these systems has not yet been established. Researchers have proposed objective evaluation techniques that extend beyond mere auditory assessments to examine the fidelity of audio reproduction and the efficacy of music categorization within established frameworks. In the present study, both subjective listening tests and objective metrics derived from preceding research were employed to assess the performance of symbolic music generation. A total of 100 MIDI samples were generated for each component of our test set, GoEmotions, and subsequently analyzed. The initial listening test aimed to evaluate the congruence between the model-generated music and the emotional content of the text, requiring participants to engage in a subjective assessment process. Participants were tasked with determining the emotional category of the given text prior to evaluating the suitability of the accompanying music. The procedure involved presenting each participant with a textual excerpt to categorize into one of four emotion categories, followed by the selection of the music option that best aligned with the perceived emotion of the narrative. This bifurcated approach facilitated the examination of consensus among participants regarding textual emotion and the corresponding musical alignment. Given the variability in music selection concerning exact emotional categories, an analysis was conducted based on broader dimensions of positive/negative valence and high/low energy levels. Each emotion category was further delineated into positive or negative valence and high or low energy, and the congruence between musical emotion and narrative emotion was assessed accordingly. This methodological framework enabled a flexible assessment of emotional alignment, offering insights into the degree of agreement between the music's emotional expression and the narrative's emotional content.
