The Menta interface has been developed to manage various tasks, including the assessment of stress and depression, both in terms of presence and severity, as well as evaluating suicide risk, with categories encompassing both binary and detailed levels. Upon activation of the "Start Evaluation" button, social media posts are processed through the selected model, thereby presenting the results, which encompass predicted categories, their correctness, and additional evaluative details. The efficacy of the model was tested across six tasks, utilizing datasets ranging from 10 to 3,000 posts, contingent upon the complexity of the task and the availability of data. The analysis has revealed a balance between the speed and accuracy of the model in comparison to other analogous systems. Menta has been engineered to be precise, swift, and responsive, rendering it a suitable option for application in real-world scenarios where performance and rapid responses are critical. Figure 9 illustrates three metrics: the speed of task processing (ITPS), the time taken to deliver the initial result (TTFT), and the accuracy. Each circle on the chart represents a model, with its size indicating the memory consumption. Falcon prioritizes speed, thus exhibiting rapid processing and initiation of tasks, albeit with reduced accuracy. StableLM demonstrates higher accuracy but requires more time to respond and consumes greater resources, resulting in increased expense and slower operation for users. Menta, conversely, achieves high accuracy, except in Task 5 where StableLM and Phi-4 Mini slightly outperform, while maintaining superior speed and efficiency compared to StableLM. This indicates that Menta delivers prompt and reliable results without excessive memory usage, which is crucial for systems widely utilized by the public. In summary, although each model possesses distinct advantages and disadvantages, Menta is optimally positioned for deployment in practical settings. It offers robust performance without substantial resource demands, thereby minimizing costs and enhancing user experience.
