The primary focus of LLaMA-Factory lies in generating text, as opposed to converting text creation challenges into sorting tasks. The proposed setup, known as PEFT-FACTORY, addresses the limited number of PEFT methods and facilitates the adaptation of large language models specifically for sorting tasks. Although alternative setups for training large language models exist, they do not offer the same ease of use as LLaMA-Factory, yet possess distinct advantages. FastChat is particularly effective for training large models oriented towards chat functionalities. Meanwhile, LitGPT and LMFlow provide flexible and user-friendly configurations that are compatible with various generative models and training methodologies. Axolotl, an efficient tool for post-training large models in terminal environments, ensures functionality and scalability are maintained. Open-Instruct focuses on instruction refinement for large models and supplies diverse models and methodologies for such purposes. H2O LLM Studio caters to business applications through a graphical interface designed for the creation and deployment of large models. GPT4All enhances user interaction with LLaMA, facilitating efficient training. The framework provided by ColossalAI emphasizes fine-tuning across multiple nodes to enable distributed computing. LLaMA-Adapter and LLaMA-Accessory represent streamlined configurations, where LLaMA-Adapter integrates trainable adapters into LLaMA models, and LLaMA-Accessory supplies a comprehensive toolkit for large model development. It is noteworthy that LLaMA-Adapter is frequently employed in setups such as LitGPT. Upon examining related configurations, three primary features were identified as either absent or limited, which constitute novel contributions in our work: the training of large models utilizing methods beyond reprametrization-based PEFT techniques, the modular and dynamic incorporation of new PEFT methods, and the provision of support for training and evaluating large models in sorting tasks.
