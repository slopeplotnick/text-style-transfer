In the left section of Figure 2, an illustrative example is presented, demonstrating the configuration of the prompt and the extraction of triples for a single training image. This example elucidates the manner in which various image details are systematically organized. The generated output comprises multiple triples aligned with the Fashionpedia ontology, encompassing attributes such as clothing type (depicts), silhouette (has_silhouette), material composition (has_material_type), pattern (has_pattern), and additional relationships specified within Fashionpedia. Each triple adheres to the standardized format (image_id, relation, attribute), thereby facilitating an enhanced understanding of the image's representational attributes. This structured approach enables the construction and comparative analysis of conceptual graphs. To assess influence, the graph corresponding to each generated image was systematically compared with the graphs associated with all training images by evaluating the extent of shared nodes. For each prompt, training images exhibiting the highest overlap with the generated image’s graph were retained. The prompt utilized was formulated as follows: "Create triples for the fashion item in the image based on the relationships of Fashionpedia Ontology, provided at: \\({ONTOLOGY_PATH} = \). Focus exclusively on the fashion item of the image with image_id: \\)([IMAGE_ID=)} and give me the triples that describe the image (image_id, relationship, attribute_value). Please do not give more comments." For instance, in the case of image 58810.jpg, the resulting triples were 58810.jpg, depicts, Shirt; 58810.jpg, belongs_to_category, Upperbody; 58810.jpg, has_nickname, None; 58810.jpg, has_silhouette, Slim-fit; 58810.jpg, has_waistline, Straight; 58810.jpg, has_length, Long-sleeved; 58810.jpg, hascollar_type, Classic; 58810.jpg, has_lapel_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_neckline, Crew-neck; 58810.jpg, has_sleeve_type, Long Sleeves with Cuffs; 58810.jpg, has_pocket_type, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_opening_type, Button-front Closure; 58810.jpg, has_material_type, Cotton-blend Fabric; 58810.jpg, has_leather_type, None; 58810.jpg, has_fabric_treatment, Not Applicable; 58810.jpg, has_pattern, Plaid; 58810.jpg, has_animal_pattern, None. Subsequently, these graphs, characterized by the highest degree of shared nodes with the generated image’s graph, were employed in the subsequent unlearning phase. In addition to identifying similar images, the Neo4j framework was utilized to examine the entire graph, enabling the identification of prevalent attributes and the distribution of concepts across the dataset. For the unlearning process, the generative model was retrained after the exclusion of specific training samples, thereby facilitating an assessment of their influence on the generated output. A series of ten unlearning tests was conducted utilizing both the baseline method and the proposed approach. For each test, images corresponding to fifteen prompts were generated both prior to and following the unlearning process. To ensure consistency with the baseline configuration, a comparable number of training images deemed most influential for each prompt were excluded.
