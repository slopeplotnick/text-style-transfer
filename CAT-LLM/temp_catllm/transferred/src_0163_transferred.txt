The problem-solving capacity of OpenHands is potentially enhanced due to its system prompt containing more detailed instructions compared to the SWE-agent. Advanced language models such as Claude Sonnet 4, which typically excel across various tasks, exhibit a complex method of information organization, characterized by the assimilation of extensive context and the conduction of additional tests to ensure operational efficacy and stability. The Devstral model, despite its smaller open-source nature, also displays intricate information organization when integrated with OpenHands, likely attributable to its training with OpenHands in software-related tasks, thus necessitating more thorough searches and evaluations prior to implementing modifications. Furthermore, it is essential to investigate the correlation between process measurement methodologies and the agent's capability to resolve issues. Numerous early termination instances were observed, predominantly due to runtime errors when the model failed to respond accurately, a well-documented concern. The measurement of the process is significantly correlated with both the success rate of problem resolution and the perceived difficulty by human evaluators. Successful endeavors tend to be succinct and concentrated, whereas unsuccessful attempts are characterized by elongated pathways and repetitive actions. The complexity inherent in information organization is influenced by the design of the agents and the core language models employed, with stronger models fostering a more compact organization through extensive exploration. In addition to the binary outcome of agents in terms of issue resolution, an analysis of agent behavior using Phase Flow Analysis is conducted. Successful attempts typically adhere to a structured methodology encompassing identification, rectification, and validation, consistent with the agents' designated operational parameters. As task complexity escalates, successful trajectories extend in duration while maintaining clarity, in contrast to unsuccessful endeavors which exhibit repetitive or erratic phase transitions. Robust language models demonstrate superior and adaptable strategic approaches.
