In the context of planning a series of measurements, such as determining optimal drilling locations, artificial intelligence methodologies, including Monte Carlo tree search, can be employed to optimize drilling strategies (Mern and Caers, 2023). The priorities in such processes are dynamic and can be conceptualized as follows: initially, hypotheses are evaluated; subsequently, resource quantification is assessed; and finally, quality parameters are examined. Once all but one hypothesis have been eliminated, attention should be directed towards resource quantification, quality assessment, or an integrated quality-quantity analysis. Although scientific methodologies have the potential to render exploration more cost-effective and efficient, certain non-scientific challenges persist within the industry. These challenges encompass entrenched practices and organizational structures, the industry's fragmented nature, and the absence of comprehensive software solutions. Companies typically engage specialists to perform tasks aligned with their expertise, often emphasizing minutiae without ascertaining their impact on final decision-making. To harness AI effectively, large corporations must undergo organizational transformations aimed at resolving decision-making challenges rather than merely executing tasks. This necessitates not only structural adjustments but also an educational paradigm shift. While geologists are not required to become data experts, interdisciplinary understanding is imperative, a topic addressed further in the education section. Currently, practitioners often rely on singular models facilitated by software that permits biased and rigid interpretations. Over time, these models are perceived as factual representations. When personnel changes occur, the rationale behind interpretations may be forgotten, leading to the erroneous perception of constructed shapes as data rather than models. Within corporate environments, a lack of knowledge is frequently misconstrued as a failure, fostering the belief that increased effort and time investment would enhance knowledge and diminish uncertainty.
