In recent years, significant attention has been devoted to emerging language models capable of operating with voice, with particular interest in proprietary models such as GPT-4o and Gemini-1.5-pro, which demonstrate superior performance in tasks involving speech comprehension, translation, and spoken language processing, surpassing the capabilities of publicly accessible models. Despite these advancements, there remains a paucity of studies that thoroughly investigate the integration of speech with language models to enhance understanding. Furthermore, the interaction between speech models and language models, especially within autonomous learning systems, is an area that is only beginning to be explored. This tutorial aims to examine the historical utilization of language models in speech processing, offering insights into the development of interoperable systems. Advanced concepts, including the integration of heterogeneous data types and the foundational principles for aligning non-textual data with textual data, will be discussed. Additionally, practical examples will be provided to facilitate comprehension of these concepts. The tutorial will also explore innovative approaches for developing models that concurrently handle speech and text, employing methodologies that sequentially generate data incorporating both modalities. By analyzing both traditional and contemporary methodologies, this tutorial endeavors to elucidate current developments and unresolved challenges in the realm of speech-integrated language models. The session, spanning three hours, will address the evolution of speech and language models in conjunction with voice interfaces and intelligent systems. Each principal topic will be allocated 35 minutes, followed by a 10-minute question period and a 10-minute intermission. For each section, an introductory overview will precede an in-depth examination of pivotal studies currently shaping the field. Ultimately, the tutorial will highlight the challenges and emerging research opportunities within this dynamic domain of speech and language modeling integration.
