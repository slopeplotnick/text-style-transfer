Despite extensive cleaning procedures, CPV data retains residual noise attributable to challenges associated with item information quality. Furthermore, the inherent characteristics of item SKU features introduce additional complexities, including redundant information. To evaluate the efficacy of these features, the dataset was divided into training and test subsets, enabling systematic assessment. Initially, product titles were utilized as input features, with supplementary features incrementally incorporated to determine their contribution to model performance. As demonstrated in Table 2, the integration of CPV and SKU features yielded improvements in model effectiveness, indicating that stable and relevant input information can enhance outcomes even in the presence of noise and extraneous elements. Visual features were subsequently included through the addition of main item images, addressing informational gaps inherent to text-based features. SKU images were excluded from the analysis, as the primary image typically encapsulates the majority of visual information, while SKU images primarily highlight minor variations such as style or color, which are adequately captured by SKU textual features. Including SKU images was deemed suboptimal due to their potential to increase model complexity without providing significant added value. Ultimately, a comprehensive set of product information was compiled for subsequent training endeavors. To identify the optimal base model, two critical criteria were considered: the capacity for advanced reasoning and knowledge integration, as outlined in Section 2, and operational efficiency for real-world applications. Several high-quality open-source models, ranging from 7B to 14B parameters, were selected for evaluation, as their scale was sufficient for training and deployment on a single GPU. Annotated data was partitioned into training and validation subsets, and a basic relevance task prompt, as defined in Section 3.1, was employed for fine-tuning each model. Performance metrics were systematically analyzed, leading to the determination that the Qwen2.5-7B model exhibited superior outcomes across all evaluated models while maintaining operational efficiency. Consequently, this model was selected as the foundational framework for the proposed setup.
