The analysis utilizing numerical data is effective; however, it is not as efficacious as the application of textual data alone. The integration of both modalities results in outcomes that closely approximate the optimal results achievable with text in isolation, indicating the predominant importance of textual data, with numerical data serving to augment the information. Within diverse datasets and tasks, a hierarchy of significance emerges, specifically in the context of detecting fraudulent news, where text proves highly effective. Implementing a supplementary layer on text models nearly achieves peak performance on certain datasets and surpasses previous methodologies on others. The exclusive use of numerical data is adequate yet less robust; for instance, in predicting the potential virality of content, numerical data does not match the efficacy of text. Consequently, text is identified as the principal component, with numerical data providing supportive assistance. It is imperative to articulate these findings clearly. In scenarios where errors have substantial implications, adherence to standard methodologies that balance thoroughness and precision is advisable. Regarding model configurations, within one dataset, various models such as GRU, LSTM, RNN, CNN, and Transformer exhibit comparable performance, albeit with Transformer demonstrating slight superiority. This parity underscores the greater significance of data utilization and terminology over minor model adjustments, particularly under constraints. Recommended practices include conceptualizing the prediction of virality as a series of tasks with defined limitations and timelines, ensuring clarity in result presentation. Furthermore, initiating fake news detection with text-centric approaches and subsequently incorporating simple numerical data when available is suggested. For rapid evaluations, prioritizing comprehensive detection is paramount, while acknowledging potential omissions. The employment of straightforward, well-articulated frameworks is advised, emphasizing data integrity, error avoidance, and clear procedural explanations.
