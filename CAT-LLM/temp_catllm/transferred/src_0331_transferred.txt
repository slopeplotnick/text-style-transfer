A dataset, designated as HEALTHCONTRADICT, comprising 920 examples, has been constructed. Each example incorporates a health-related inquiry alongside two documents presenting contradictory perspectives, supplemented by a factual response substantiated by scientific evidence. This dataset was utilized to assess various language models, ranging from 1 billion to 8 billion parameters, with the aim of understanding their performance in addressing health-related queries amidst conflicting data. Specifically, the investigation focused on evaluating the responses of these models when provided with a biomedical context in conjunction with a language model framework. Two primary aspects were scrutinized: the proficiency with which language models resolve health inquiries under conditions of conflicting information, and the influence exerted by the biomedical context on their responses. The assessment involved the provision of correct, incorrect, or contradictory contextual information to the models, followed by an evaluation of their accuracy. The principal findings are as follows: a novel dataset, HEALTHCONTRADICT, was established to facilitate the testing of language models confronted with conflicting biomedical information; a comprehensive evaluation of these models was conducted using this dataset to ascertain their capability in processing extensive, conflicting biomedical texts, underpinned by explicit metrics; a comparative analysis was performed between general language models and their specialized biomedical counterparts, revealing that the latter exhibit superior aptitude in utilizing accurate information while disregarding erroneous data. Each document within the dataset was ensured to be distinct to maintain the integrity of the evaluation process. For instance, concerning the inquiry, "Can coffee help you lose weight?" two documents were provided with opposing viewpoints (one affirming and the other negating), while the factual answer, supported by scientific evidence, confirmed that caffeine may contribute to reductions in weight, BMI, and body fat.
