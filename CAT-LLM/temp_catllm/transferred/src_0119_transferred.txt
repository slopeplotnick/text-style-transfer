The functionality of OMNIGUARD is evaluated through a series of public safety tests encompassing both single and mixed data types. Single-type evaluations include text, images, videos, and audio, utilizing diverse benchmarks such as BeaverTails, ToxicChat, WildGuardMix, Aegis2.0, and OpenAI Moderation for text; UnsafeBench, VLGuard, and LlavaGuard for images; SafeSora and SafeWatch-Bench for videos; and MuTox English split and WildGuardMix-TTS, which converts WildGuardMix test samples into speech, for audio. Mixed-type assessments are conducted with image-text, video-text, and audio-text configurations, employing MM-SafetyBench, VideoSafetyBench, and AIAH. The accuracy and reliability of the system are analyzed using Accuracy and F1 scores as primary metrics, with accuracy reported exclusively for tests containing only unsafe samples. Furthermore, OMNIGUARD is systematically compared with alternative models, including both large-scale and smaller models, to evaluate its protective capabilities. Specialized models, such as those derived from LLM and VLM architectures, are also included in these comparative analyses, with detailed results presented in Table 7. For single-type evaluations, OMNIGUARD is benchmarked against leading models across text, image, video, and audio safety domains. The findings indicate that OMNI-GUARD-7B demonstrates superior overall performance, while OMNI-GUARD-3B achieves competitive results, often matching or surpassing larger models such as GPT-4o, Qwen3-235B, and Qwen3-VL-235B. These outcomes provide substantial evidence that the proposed safety framework is effective in ensuring robust protection.
