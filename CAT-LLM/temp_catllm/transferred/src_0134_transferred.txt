Artificial intelligence agents have demonstrated the ability to autonomously think, plan, and utilize tools. Recently, there has been a notable emergence of medical agents capable of performing complex medical tasks. Specifically, MMedAgent utilizes various tools to execute medical functions, such as generating reports for chest X-rays. MedRAX, a specialized agent, is dedicated to interpreting chest X-rays, employing the most advanced tools available for this purpose. Similarly, CT-Agent excels in comprehending 3D CT scans and assists with logical reasoning. However, the creation of radiology reports typically involves the use of a singular tool, resulting in limited collaboration among agents. Our innovative Radiologist Copilot is distinguished by its integration of multiple tools that synergize effectively, enabling the execution of more intricate tasks. Crafting radiology reports is a task of critical importance, yet it is time-consuming and prone to errors, particularly when dealing with 3D imaging. The verification of report quality is crucial, though it is often overlooked in previous research studies. Consequently, Radiologist Copilot was developed as an artificial intelligence assistant equipped with a comprehensive toolkit for both the generation and quality assessment of radiology reports, thereby enhancing radiologist efficiency. As illustrated, Radiologist Copilot supports radiologists throughout the entire report creation process. Initially, it establishes the tool library, followed by the utilization of an action planner and executor to strategize and perform tasks. A memory module is employed to monitor completed actions and their outcomes, facilitating task verification. Commands are generated and executed by the action executor based on the forthcoming planned action. Each action entails the formulation of an appropriate command for the selected tool, execution of the command, and acquisition of the action's result.
