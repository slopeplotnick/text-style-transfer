The prediction of stock prices presents significant challenges due to the inherent volatility and rapid responsiveness of the market to contemporaneous events. Large language models (LLMs) have facilitated novel methodologies for forecasting stock prices through textual analysis. While these models exhibit proficiency in text comprehension, their application to complex, real-world tasks necessitates specialized memory systems capable of consistent utilization of historical information. Considerable research has been directed towards the development of such memory systems; however, they encounter difficulties in processing the vast quantities of unstructured online news. This news rarely provides direct explanations for price fluctuations and is often laden with extraneous information. The primary challenge lies in the extraction of salient details and logic from this unstructured text that have a tangible impact on stock prices. To address this issue, this paper proposes a novel dual-layer memory system. Initially, it organizes events through two mechanisms: aggregating daily event details to construct a coherent overview and monitoring the evolution of events over time. This process assists in the establishment of a knowledge base that comprehends the dynamics of market expectations over temporal intervals. Subsequently, it examines the correlation between event patterns and stock price variations, creating an additional knowledge base dedicated to analyzing this relationship. Collectively, these approaches foster a system that autonomously learns and organizes knowledge. The implementation of this system involves two principal phases: identification and reasoning. Initially, it employs recent events to locate analogous historical scenarios within its memory, thereby facilitating enhanced decision-making in the present. Thereafter, it integrates recent events, novel data, and historical exemplars to predict stock price movements. Empirical validation indicates that this system surpasses previous models in predictive accuracy and offers increased interpretability by clearly delineating the connections between events and price variations, thereby elucidating causative factors.
