Artificial Intelligence (AI) has demonstrated rapid advancements, particularly in the domain of natural language processing, with narrative generation constituting a significant area of focus. The primary objective is to produce coherent and engaging narratives based on user input. However, challenges persist in maintaining narrative consistency, character development, and plot control. Ensuring that the narrative aligns with user expectations while preserving plot integrity, character depth, and structural coherence remains a formidable task. It is imperative to eliminate logical inconsistencies and align the narrative with user expectations to maintain believability and creativity. Large language models, such as GPT-4 and Llama-3, have demonstrated the capability to generate quality text; nevertheless, their training on generalized data results in difficulties in producing narratives that are sufficiently controlled and structured. This limitation is particularly pronounced when users require specific details and a well-defined narrative framework. Consequently, enhancing these models' ability to manage narrative generation in a manner that satisfies user expectations while fostering creativity is imperative. Fine-tuning these models alongside the utilization of structured prompts appears to be an effective strategy to enhance narrative generation capabilities. This approach not only improves the quality of the narratives but also enhances the models' stability and reliability for complex tasks. The combination of fine-tuning and structured input contributes to improving the predictability and consistency of the narratives, thereby addressing issues of narrative control.
