Research indicates that the implementation of reasoning-based guardrails enhances the reliability and comprehensibility of moderation, marking a significant advancement towards ensuring AI safety. Consequently, OMNIGUARD, a suite of models capable of performing safety checks across text, images, videos, and audio through reasoning, has been developed. In addition, a training method has been established to facilitate the model's ability to elucidate its safety decisions more effectively. Empirical evaluations reveal that OMNIGUARD is notably accurate, exhibits robust generalization, and provides superior explanations of its decisions in comparison to preceding models. Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have prompted increased interest in omni-modal language models (OLLMs), which possess the capability to process diverse data types concurrently. Unlike earlier models that utilized discrete components, OLLMs are trained on heterogeneous data, enabling them to comprehend and generate text, audio, images, and videos collectively. These models operate by converting varied inputs into a unified format, thereby aiding reasoning across disparate data types. Models such as Qwen2.5-Omni and LLaMA-Omni are proficient in generating text and speech in real-time. NExT-OMNI extends this capability by facilitating the understanding and creation of any data type. However, these models encounter safety challenges due to parameter issues, which consequently may result in precarious situations.
