This study aims to identify patterns indicative of inefficiencies or regressions in task execution, utilizing the GRAPHECTORY tool to facilitate the detection of such patterns. Specifically, the analysis focuses on distinct indicators that may suggest suboptimal performance. Hypothesis H1 posits that repetitive engagement in ineffective actions necessitates the exploration of alternative strategies. Hypothesis H2 identifies erratic project navigation, characterized by inconsistent progression, as a potential sign of inefficiency. Hypothesis H3 emphasizes prolonged task engagement without measurable progress as indicative of stagnation. Hypothesis H4 suggests that operational failures may stem from improper utilization of available tools. The concept of RepeatedView is defined as recurrent examination of the same project component, such as a file, which often occurs following unsuccessful edits or unresolved issues; for instance, after two unsuccessful attempts to modify the code (step 9), the file client.py is reopened (step 10) to identify the underlying problem. Additionally, ZoomOut is characterized by the incongruence between the sequence of actions and the hierarchical structure of the project, often signifying a retracing of steps due to the realization of an initial error in navigation.
