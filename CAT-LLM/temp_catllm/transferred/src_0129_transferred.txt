Individuals who possess limited technical expertise or exhibit a stronger inclination towards business-oriented roles may demonstrate reluctance to embrace change, even when the advantages are apparent, primarily due to concerns regarding the rapid pace of technological advancements. The team under consideration is characterized by its small size and familiarity with emerging technologies; furthermore, the early conceptualization of utilizing a Software Product Line (SPL) suggested minimal resistance to its adoption. Insights derived from conducted interviews have facilitated the identification of strategies to sustain existing successful practices, enhance associated benefits, and address potential apprehensions. It remains essential to preserve effective methodologies currently implemented within the SPL framework to ensure a sense of comfort and continuity among team members. Although the operational dynamics are expected to undergo significant transformations, the retention of established practices is anticipated to mitigate concerns and foster smoother transitions during the adoption process. The SPL approach emphasizes expedited processes, reusable components, and modular design principles, all of which align with the current operational strategies, thereby enabling a focus on the realization of associated advantages. Transitioning to an SPL paradigm promotes the sustainability of reuse and modularization while introducing additional benefits such as customization, automation, and a systematic, factory-like production model. Moreover, SPL adoption contributes to enhancements in software quality, addressing technical debt through improved code consistency, reduced defects, and the minimization of repetitive coding practices. The resolution of prevalent issues further augments code reliability and robustness. Notably, the merits of SPL adoption are recognized not only by product owners but also more prominently by IT professionals, who frequently highlight its potential to elevate the organization’s research and development capabilities and overall reputation in technological innovation. Additionally, the implementation of SPL methodologies facilitates accelerated development cycles for new software solutions, underscoring its strategic value in contemporary software engineering practices.
