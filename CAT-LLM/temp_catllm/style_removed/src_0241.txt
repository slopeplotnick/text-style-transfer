People are really good at picking up new tasks quickly if they get clear instructions, like words or diagrams, without needing extra practice. Researchers have looked at how the brain and AI work in areas like sensing, thinking, and moving by breaking down their processes into three parts: how circuits are set up, how they handle inputs and outputs, and how they learn. They found that newer AI systems are starting to work more like the brain and learned some key things about how the brain works. The neocortex and cerebellum use past information to guess what’s going to happen next and try to make those guesses as accurate as possible. While doing this, they simplify and organize information about the world into models that are small but powerful. These models help with three main things: predicting what’s going to happen, understanding the world by using abstract ideas like features and cause-and-effect relationships (like recognizing objects or understanding text), and creating outputs like actions, plans, or language. This way of processing information is what lets the neocortex and cerebellum do so many different things even though their structures and functions are pretty similar. In short, this kind of information processing is a shared way of working that supports a lot of what the brain and AI can do. Learning more about it will help improve neuroscience and AI and give us better insight into how intelligence works. The human brain learns way faster than large language models and needs way fewer examples to do it, plus it’s much more efficient. Newer AI models have gotten better at learning and being efficient by copying how the brain works, like using Mixture-of-Experts methods, but they’re still not as good as the brain. As AI keeps improving by learning from the brain, neuroscience will also learn new things by studying AI.
