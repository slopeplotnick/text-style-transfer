This part talks about models that help translate speech and text together so that translations between languages are smooth and good quality. It explains how the latest voice-interfaced language models process and understand speech and conversation signals. Over the past ten years, there has been a lot of progress in making and using voice-powered AI assistants, starting with Siri from Apple, then Cortana from Microsoft, Google Assistant, Alexa from Amazon, and Samsung's Bixby. This part of the tutorial will go over the tech behind these AI helpers, like how they detect what you want and fill in details using certain types of neural networks, manage conversations with learning techniques, use data from web searches, take advantage of the semantic web, and use knowledge graphs to understand spoken language. Right now, AI assistants are improving by using language models to better understand and track conversations, and make spoken language better. This tutorial will give an overview of current language models and how they're adapted for specific tasks. It talks about recent improvements in systems that handle multiple conversations, both for general and specific tasks, and includes information on datasets and how they're evaluated. It also discusses new research challenges in developing language models and the increasing needs for systems that handle multiple conversations.
