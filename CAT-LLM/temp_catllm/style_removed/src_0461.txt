We're planning to do more work in the future because we didn't have enough resources to look into how beliefs get stuck in reinforced reasoning, even though this method is getting popular for LLM reasoning. We also showed that breaking ideal Bayesian rationality can happen when people don't think right about their own reasoning or outside evidence, but we only looked at the first part. Next studies could add a part where we check how well LLMs change their beliefs when they get new outside evidence, instead of just sticking to what they already think. The tricky part is making sure the evidence searching doesn't let LLMs directly see the real facts, if those facts are available. In areas where we know the truth, like forecasting, we showed how belief entrenchment affects accuracy. Future research could check if the Martingale Score works in open-ended areas by showing other results of belief entrenchment. To show Martingale Score is useful for evaluating reasoning, we could use it to check sycophancy, group conformity, and inverse scaling. Martingale Score could also be turned into a training goal to test how strong and useful it is. If using martingale training lowers both Martingale Score and brier score, it shows Martingale Score can evaluate reasoning and is useful beyond forecasting. We could also look into using Martingale Score to measure and reduce belief entrenchment or polarization in human-AI systems like recommender systems in the future.
