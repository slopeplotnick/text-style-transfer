Here’s how we handled the interviews. We wanted them to feel more like a conversation than a strict Q&A. At the start, we gave a quick reminder about SPLE and explained what the questionnaire was for. Then, we asked the first questions, which were about the person we were interviewing and their role in the company. This was to get a sense of who they were. After that, we asked general questions about how the company develops things. Then we moved on to more specific questions about how they currently develop new products. We wanted to know what they thought was working well, what good practices they had, and how things were going overall. Talking about the current process made it easier to bring up a new approach that might fix some of the issues they were facing. At the end, we talked about what it would take to switch to the new approach. We asked them to share any concerns, risks they saw, and what they thought could be good about it. The last two questions were about how we could make the interviews better and to wrap things up. All the interviews were done by the same person, who was the first author of this project. Each one lasted between 30 minutes and an hour. People said they liked the interviews and felt like they were part of the migration project. At the end of each interview, we asked, "Was there a question you thought I’d ask but didn’t?" This helped them think about everything we talked about and add anything they felt was missing. It also helped us make the next interviews smoother. The questions stayed the same for everyone so we could analyze the data consistently. The goal was to let people think about everything without changing the questions but still make the interviews better. Notes were taken during the interviews. Following some advice we got, the interviewer tried to make sure the questions weren’t biased or leading in any way. He also made sure to ask enough questions without pushing the interviewees in any direction. We did a test interview with our main collaborator at the company who’s working with us on this project. This helped us improve the questionnaire and make the process smoother. We didn’t include the data from that first interview in our analysis.
