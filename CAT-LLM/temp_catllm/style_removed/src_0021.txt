We worked on the Swivuriso dataset by training three speech recognition models: Whisper-large-v3-Turbo, Wav2Vec-BERT 2.0, and Massively Multilingual Speech (MMS). Table 6 shows the Word Error Rate (WER) for each model after 1,000 and 10,000 training steps. Training for longer made all the models better, with Whisper-large-v3-Turbo improving the most by 0.22, Wav2Vec-BERT by 0.27, and MMS by 0.15. We also looked at how the amount of training data affects results. Whisper-v3-turbo was trained for one round using 50 hours, 150 hours, and 250 hours of training data. The experiments showed that the Swivuriso dataset helps improve speech recognition across different models and languages. Training always lowered error rates, and the dataset worked well even in early stages of training. Adding more training data helped performance for most languages. These results show that Swivuriso is a good resource for future work on South African speech recognition and for improving multilingual speech models. Other researchers have done a lot of work on making good datasets for speech recognition, which needs high accuracy and no mistakes. For African languages and other low-resource languages, progress has been slower. One study by Abdulmumin and others looked at the FLORES dataset to make it better for evaluating four African languages: Hausa, Northern Sotho (Sepedi), Xitsonga, and isiZulu. Native speakers checked and fixed problems in the dataset that could affect tasks like machine translation. Their changes made the dataset more accurate and reliable, and they showed this with both reviews and data analysis. This study showed how important it is to involve native speakers to make sure datasets fit the language and culture. We also worked closely with native speakers to make our dataset better and more accurate, as suggested by that study. Another study helped us with sentiment analysis. It pointed out that there are many sentiment analysis datasets for big languages, but not for low-resource ones like Hindi. The study worked on sentiment analysis in Hindi, focusing on socially important topics, and created a balanced dataset with positive, negative, and neutral sentiment labels.
