To make things easier to understand and safer, we add feedback to each example in our data set using advanced models. We use gpt-oss-120b for text, Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct for images and videos, and Kimi-Audio-7B-Instruct for sound. For each example, the model gets the original content, its safety label, and any problem areas, then it explains why it made its safety decision. The full setup for creating this feedback is shown in Figure 5. Unlike basic models that just say if something is safe or not, OMNIGUARD takes its time to give detailed and clear safety checks. It gives a structured response with these parts: (1) Safety judgment: saying if the input is safe or not. (2) Violation categories: listing any specific safety issues if the content is unsafe. (3) Reasoning critique: explaining why the model made its decision based on the rules. This helps OMNIGUARD do more than just spot patterns, allowing for a clear analysis of possibly unsafe content in different formats. Here, we show detailed test results of OMNIGUARD.
