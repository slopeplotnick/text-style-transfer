Recently, there's been a lot of progress with large language models (LLMs) that are bringing together task-focused and open-ended chat systems. This part of the tutorial talks about this change in technology and shows some examples of where things might go in the future. Some examples are making new LLMs that are trained with chat data, training dialogues from start to finish, and having dialogues practice with themselves. Another big future idea is making chat systems that use content from different sources like web pages, lists, forms, tables, papers with figures and equations, and documents that are found online.
