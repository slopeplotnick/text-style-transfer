We made a model called Story2MIDI that can create music matching the emotions of a piece of text. To build it, we combined datasets that analyze emotions in text and music. The dataset pairs short pieces of text with music that bring out the same feelings. Even though our dataset is small and we didn’t have a lot of computer power, the model still learned how to pick up on emotional features in music and use them to make new music with different emotional vibes. We checked the results using music-related measurements and by asking people to listen, and it showed the model can match the emotions it’s supposed to. These days, there’s a huge demand for entertainment like music, audiobooks, and podcasts, especially with social media and streaming platforms growing so fast. A lot of this entertainment is built around stories, whether it’s in movies, games, podcasts, or documentaries. Stories are great at making people feel emotions as they follow the characters, the plot, and the themes. This emotional journey is a big part of why stories stick with people. Music works the same way—it can really affect how people feel and is known as a way to express emotions. Because of this, creators often add background music to stories, which helps people feel more connected and makes the experience more engaging. With AI advancing so quickly, new models are being made all the time that can create different types of content.
