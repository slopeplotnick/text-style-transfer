Here, we look at three main questions about using smaller models and our fine-tuned Menta model to predict mental health outcomes, while also discussing current limits and future research directions. Can smaller models predict mental health status well? Our findings show that smaller models can predict mental health conditions from social media text data. Even though they have fewer parameters, the well-trained Menta handled complex tasks well, showing their potential for accurate predictions in low-resource or limited settings.
