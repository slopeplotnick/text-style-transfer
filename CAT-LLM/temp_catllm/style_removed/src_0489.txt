The experiment took about 20 to 35 minutes. First, volunteers picked a picture they liked for a writing task. We made sure they understood the picture they chose. Then, we showed them how TaleFrame works. After that, they made up a story using their picture. We kept track of what they did and said during the experiment. When they finished, we asked them questions to get their feedback. Each volunteer got $5 for participating. For user feedback, volunteers rated TaleFrame on a scale from 1 to 5 based on their experience. TaleFrame helps users build a detailed story framework easily with its simple interface and options for defining parts of the story. It lets users arrange story elements interactively and shows how they're connected, making it easy to follow the story. Users can also tweak the story structure as they go. TaleFrame helps keep the story's timeline consistent and suggests ways to improve emotional and thematic parts of the story. It checks the story's quality and gives tips to make it better.
