Switching to more electric energy has made people focus on looking for important minerals. Even though there's a lot of demand, finding these minerals isn't making money. Even with more money being put into it, fewer new finds have happened over the last 20 years. In this paper, I suggest using AI to help make finding minerals more scientific, cut down on mistakes, and make it cheaper. Right now, the way we look for minerals using different sciences like geology and geophysics isn't working well to find hidden deposits. The current method doesn't measure uncertainty well, leading to bad decisions and wasted money on drilling that often doesn't pay off. I suggest a new way based on ideas from Bayesianism and falsification, where collecting data is mainly to challenge human ideas. Deciding what data to get next is measured with clear metrics and based on smart choices. There's a practical guide that can be used in any mineral search. But to make this guide work, different types of AI are needed. The most important ones are new unsupervised learning methods that work with experts to understand data better and come up with different geological ideas, and human-in-the-loop AI that can plan data collection in geology, geophysics, geochemistry, and drilling in a way that reduces uncertainty about geological ideas before worrying about the quality and amount of the minerals. This method will be shown in some current mineral search and resource evaluation cases.
