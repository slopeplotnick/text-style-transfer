What do people think about when they look at AI-generated stuff? Studies now use rating scales from similar areas, but these might not really show how people feel about AI-made music. We're trying to find out what people really think by asking open-ended questions. We used AI tools called Stable Audio and Suno to make the music samples. We based these samples on the Emotion-to-Music Atlas, which has lots of info on how music makes people feel. We used data from the Music4All-Onion database, which has music from five big genres, each with three subgenres: Electronic (like Trance, Downtempo, Dubstep), Jazz (like Bossa-Nova, Smooth-Jazz, Swing), Pop (like Europop, Indie-Pop, Pop-Punk), Rock (like Soft-Rock, RockBallad, Doom-Metal), and Soul/Funk (like Funk, Disco, Soul). For each subgenre, we made prompts based on the main emotions found in the music, using average values from the Emotion-to-Music Atlas and their GEMS-9 descriptions. This gave us 2-3 prompts per subgenre, making 38 prompts total. We changed these for Suno and Stable Audio, ending up with 76 samples. For Suno, we made the prompts in instrumental mode and picked one of two outputs, cutting it to 45-60 seconds to match past studies. For Stable Audio, we added 'instrumental' to each prompt and aimed for 60 seconds. More info on how we made and edited these samples is in the extra materials.
