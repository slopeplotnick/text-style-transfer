Our framework PEFT-FACTORY addresses both the limited number of available PEFT methods and the potential for fine-tuning LLMs for classification problems. There are also other LLM training frameworks that are less easy to run (compared to LLaMA-Factory) and have their specific benefits. FastChat (Zheng et al., 2023) is a specialized framework for training LLMs for chat-completion. LitGPT (AI, 2023) and LMFlow (Diao et al., 2023) are extensible and convenient general training frameworks that support various generative models and training methods. Axolotl (Axolotl maintainers and contributors, 2023) is a terminal-based tool for efficient post-training of LLMs without sacrificing functionality or scale. Open-Instruct (Wang et al., 2023a) focuses on instruction fine-tuning for LLMs and provides multiple models and recipes for this purpose. H2O LLM Studio<sup>1</sup> is a more enterprise-oriented, all-in-one tool that also provides a graphical interface for developing and deploying LLM models. GPT4All (Anand et al., 2023) creates a user-friendly interface around llama<sup>1</sup> for efficient training. ColossalAI (Li et al., 2023) focuses on delivering a framework for fine-tuning across multiple nodes for distributed computing. In addition, LLaMA-Adapter (Zhang et al., 2023) and LLaMA-Accesory (Gao et al., 2023) are more lightweight frameworks, where LLaMA-Adapter adds trainable adapters to (not only) LLaMA models and LLaMA-Accesory provides a full toolkit for LLM development. LLaMA-Adapter is often implemented in previously-mentioned frameworks, such as LitGPT. Based on our analysis of related frameworks and to the best of our knowledge, we have identified 3 key features that are currently missing or limited, and are novel in our work: 1) training of LLMs with other than reprametrization-based PEFT methods, 2) modular and dynamic addition of new PEFT methods, and 3) support for training and evaluation of LLMs for classification tasks.
