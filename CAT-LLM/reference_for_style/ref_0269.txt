This tutorial is designed for researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of conversational agents, and spoken information interactions. We assume attendees will have a foundational understanding of NLP and speech processing. While experience with system deployment is beneficial, we will provide a carefully paced introduction to core materials to accommodate a broader audience.
