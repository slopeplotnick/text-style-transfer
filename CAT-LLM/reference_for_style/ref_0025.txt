It involves the capacity to produce a new application faster than the previous one. Software Engineering spirit combines all the principles detailed above, i.e. factorization, reuse, innovation and rapidity. The project was initially thought and designed like a common reusable code base. The proximity to the SPLE objectives helps to promote the migration project. We identified 7 different current good practices. We noticed that Factorization and Reuse are the two most frequently mentioned ideas with 9 and 8 occurrences, respectively (Table 4). Figure 1 graphically highlights the distribution of the mentions. The perceived benefits are very important to know because their expression will be motivating for the whole team. Customization is one of the main characteristics of an SPL and was the most frequently mentioned idea by ITs, UXs and AGROs. An SPL is perceived as a platform that is able to generate a mass customized software from a collection of existing software. The rapidity, mentioned by ITs, POs and UXs, is the second most frequently mentioned idea. It corresponds to the capacity to quickly derive a new software. Automation has also been mentioned. It is the process of deriving new software automatically. ITs and POs linked automation and rapidity. Industrialization was mentioned by ITs and UXs, meaning here the on-demand and automated production of applications at low cost. Improving the code quality was mentioned only by ITs. Code quality here takes into consideration code homogeneity, testability and reliability. Selling (or rapid time-to-market) advantages were mentioned by ITs and POs and grouped all characteristics allowing to improve the business part. It can refer, for example, to selling more software or answering quickly to a call for tender. These 7 benefits are highlighted in Figure 2.
