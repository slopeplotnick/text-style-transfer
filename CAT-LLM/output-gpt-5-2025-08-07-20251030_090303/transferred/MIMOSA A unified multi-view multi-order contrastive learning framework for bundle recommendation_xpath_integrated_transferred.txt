Bundle recommendation is an important online service that delivers users personalized sets of items and relies on modeling complex relations, yet existing multi-view approaches inadequately exploit inter-entity links, particularly when distinguishing linked from unlinked pairs. To address this limitation, a novel method, termed multi-view multi-order contrastive learning (MIMOSA), is proposed to simultaneously model relationships across multiple views and heterogeneous entity types. MIMOSA employs a unified and effective graph-based contrastive learning framework that accounts for intra-type links and cross-type user–bundle interactions, while leveraging multi-order proximity in the user–bundle graph. Therefore, the approach specifies principled positive-sample selection and tailored contrastive loss computation to accommodate heterogeneous associations, encouraging representations of linked entities to converge and driving unlinked entities apart. To further enforce cross-view consistency and accelerate contrastive learning, a center-matching strategy is introduced that aligns multi-view entity representations during training. Empirical evaluation is conducted on two large-scale datasets, Youshu and NetEase, demonstrating that MIMOSA consistently outperforms competitive baselines. Relative to the best-performing baseline, MIMOSA achieves improvements of 2.87% in R@20 and 3.02% in N@20 on Youshu, and 5.19% in R@20 and 4.07% in N@20 on NetEase.
