Human Action Recognition (HAR) matters for things like video surveillance. There are many HAR methods, but they still don’t handle spatial and temporal features well. In this work, we build a HAR model that mixes Multiple Residual Shrinkage Building Units (MRSBU) for spatial feature extraction with a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) for modeling time. The model uses Inception v3 to pull rich spatial features from video frames, and MRSBU learns complex temporal features with an adaptive soft-thresholding step to cut noisy and redundant features. We then feed this into a GRU with temporal attention that gives different importance to each frame and does the activity classification. This model is small and effective and generalizes well across several tough HAR datasets. Our MRSBU with GRU gets 99.75% accuracy on UCF50, 99.55% on UCF101, and 98.95% on HMDB51, beating typical CNN-LSTM, Vision Transformer (ViT), and 3D-CNN models. These results show the model is effective and robust in different video scenarios, including real-world surveillance and anomaly detection.
