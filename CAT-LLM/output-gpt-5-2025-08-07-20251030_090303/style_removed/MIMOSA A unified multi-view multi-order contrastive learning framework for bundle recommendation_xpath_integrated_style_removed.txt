Bundle recommendation is an important online service that gives users personalized sets of items they may like. Existing methods try to use multi-view information, but they often don’t make good use of the links between entities (users and bundles), especially when telling apart linked and unlinked ones. To fix this, we propose a new bundle recommendation method called multi-view multi-order contrastive learning (MIMOSA). It models relationships across multiple views and between different entities at the same time. It uses one graph-based contrastive learning framework that handles links within the same type and across users and bundles, using different closeness levels in the user-bundle graph. MIMOSA sets special rules for choosing positive samples and for computing the contrastive loss, so it can handle different types of associations. This makes embeddings of linked entities close and pushes unlinked ones apart. We also add a center-matching strategy to keep multi-view entity representations consistent and speed up contrastive learning. Tests on two large datasets, Youshu and NetEase, show that MIMOSA outperforms the baselines. Compared to the best baseline, it improves by 2.87% (R@20) and 3.02% (N@20) on Youshu, and by 5.19% (R@20) and 4.07% (N@20) on NetEase.
