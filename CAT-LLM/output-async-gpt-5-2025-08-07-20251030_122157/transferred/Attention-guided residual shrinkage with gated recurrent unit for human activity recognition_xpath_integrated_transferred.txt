Human action recognition (HAR) supports video surveillance and related applications, yet many algorithms inadequately capture spatial and temporal dependencies in complex scenes. To address these limitations, an architecture was developed integrating Multiple Residual Shrinkage Building Units (MRSBU) for spatial features and a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) for temporal modeling. The pipeline employs Inception v3 to extract frame-level spatial representations, and MRSBU analyzes complex temporal relationships through adaptive soft-thresholding that suppresses noisy and redundant features. The sequence is processed by a GRU with temporal attention, which assigns importance to frames and classifies activities, yielding a lightweight, effective model that generalizes across challenging HAR datasets. On UCF50, UCF101, and HMDB51, accuracies of 99.75%, 99.55%, and 98.95% were achieved, surpassing CNN-LSTM, Vision Transformer (ViT), and 3D-CNN; therefore, results demonstrate robust performance across real-world surveillance and anomaly detection.
