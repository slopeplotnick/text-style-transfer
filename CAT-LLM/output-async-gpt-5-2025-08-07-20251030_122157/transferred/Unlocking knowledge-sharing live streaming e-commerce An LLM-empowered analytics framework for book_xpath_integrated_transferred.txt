It has been demonstrated by prior research that streamer discourse shapes purchasing in live shopping when instruction accompanies promotion, yet few studies quantify linguistic features’ predictive value. This study develops a robust prediction framework for knowledge-sharing streams that integrates social support theory with fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) to extract effective meaning cues from streamer discourse. A dataset comprising 80 live streams from 35 Douyin rooms over two months was collected to operationalize and test the approach. Specifically, in the social support classification test, fine-tuned Ernie-SFT surpassed the strongest baseline LLM, increasing accuracy by 11.12%, weighted F1 by 11.87%, and macro F1 by 7.83%. For sales prediction, the framework underwent testing with four common classifiers and yielded consistent gains; the best improved accuracy by 12.53%, weighted F1 by 10.83%, and macro F1 by 4.24%. Collectively, the findings indicate that social support features in streamer discourse provide strong signals for prediction, guide actionable practices for streamers, and enable data-driven optimization on platforms.
