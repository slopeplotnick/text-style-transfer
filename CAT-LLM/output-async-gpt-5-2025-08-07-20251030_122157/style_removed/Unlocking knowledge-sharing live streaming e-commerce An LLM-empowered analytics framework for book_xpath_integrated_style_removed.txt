What streamers say really affects what people buy in live shopping, especially when they teach while selling. Past studies show this, but few have broken down the language features from different angles and measured how much they help with sales prediction. We built a sales prediction setup for knowledge-sharing live streams. It combines social support theory with fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) to pull out different kinds of meaning cues from streamers’ talk for predicting sales. We gathered data from 80 live streams in 35 Douyin rooms over two months. In the social support classification test, our fine-tuned Ernie-SFT beat the best baseline LLM, raising accuracy by 11.12%, weighted F1 by 11.87%, and macro F1 by 7.83%. In the sales prediction tests, we checked the setup with four common classifiers and saw steady gains. The best classifier improved accuracy by 12.53%, weighted F1 by 10.83%, and macro F1 by 4.24%. This shows that social support features in what streamers say are strong signals for prediction, gives streamers practical takeaways, and helps platforms optimize with data.
