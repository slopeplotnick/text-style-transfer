Human Action Recognition (HAR) is important for things like video surveillance. Even though many HAR algorithms exist, they still don’t extract spatial and temporal features well. In this work, we built a HAR setup that uses Multiple Residual Shrinkage Building Units (MRSBU) for spatial features and a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) for the time part. It uses Inception v3 to get spatial features from video frames, and the MRSBU also learns complex temporal features with an adaptive soft-thresholding step that suppresses noisy and redundant features. Then the output goes into a GRU with temporal attention, which gives different importance to each frame and classifies the activities. This model is lightweight and effective and works well across several tough HAR datasets. The MRSBU with GRU model reached 99.75% accuracy on UCF50, 99.55% on UCF101, and 98.95% on HMDB51, beating CNN-LSTM, Vision Transformer (ViT), and 3D-CNN models. These results show the model is effective and robust across different video scenarios, including real-world surveillance and anomaly detection.
