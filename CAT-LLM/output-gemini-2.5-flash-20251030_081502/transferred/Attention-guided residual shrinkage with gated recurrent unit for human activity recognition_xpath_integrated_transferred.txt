Human Activity Recognition (HAR) is a critical component in various applications, particularly video surveillance.Although numerous methods for HAR have been developed, they often exhibit limitations in accurately capturing fine-grained spatial and temporal dynamics.Consequently, this paper introduces a novel HAR system.This system incorporates Multiple Residual Shrinkage Building Units (MRSBU) for robust spatial feature extraction and Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) for modeling temporal dependencies.Specifically, it employs Inception v3 to extract comprehensive spatial features from video frames, while the MRSBU module learns intricate temporal features through an adaptive shrinkage mechanism that effectively filters out irrelevant information.Subsequently, these processed features are fed into a GRU augmented with a temporal attention mechanism.This attention mechanism dynamically weighs the significance of different video frames, thereby facilitating precise human activity recognition.The proposed model is characterized by its compact architecture and demonstrated high performance.Furthermore, its robustness across diverse and challenging HAR datasets is validated.The MRSBU with GRU method achieved impressive accuracy rates of 99.75% on UCF50, 99.55% on UCF101, and 98.95% on HMDB51.This performance significantly surpasses that of prior state-of-the-art methods, including CNN-LSTM, ViT, and 3D-CNN.These results collectively indicate the model's exceptional efficacy and robustness across varied video environments, highlighting its potential for real-world applications in surveillance systems and anomaly detection.
