Checking policy documents automatically is a really important thing to study. Before, people mostly had to do things by hand, which wasn't good for checking many policies at once. Plus, the ways they measured stuff only worked for specific policies and couldn't be used for others. So, to fix this, we came up with a new way to automatically check policy documents. We changed three basic ways to measure things so they work for any kind of policy. We also put a Policy Modeling Consistency Model, or PMC, into big language models, and then we made automatic PMC scoring models using LLaMA-3-Chinese-8B and Qwen-2.5-7B. We used Chinese science and technology policies as examples and made the first Chinese set of data for checking policies. It has 22,630 policy examples that are already labeled. Then we taught four models to automatically figure out the PMC indicators. Compared to the usual ways, the model using Qwen-2.5-7B worked best when checking what a policy was about, getting an F1-score of 80.41%. The LLaMA-3-Chinese-8B model worked best when checking how proper policies were and what they were supposed to do, getting F1-scores of 75.07% and 74.11%. This means we can automatically figure out the PMC numbers and make a table that shows lots of different information for a complete policy check. Using this system for biosafety and data governance policies showed that it works for different kinds of policies too. Since this is the first system for automatically checking PMC, our method is a new and smart way to check many different kinds of policies across various areas.
