You know how streamers talk on live shopping shows? What they say is super important for people deciding what to buy, especially when they're teaching you stuff while selling things. Other studies already showed that streamer talk can change how much they sell, but not many really looked deep into the specific meanings in their chat from all different angles and checked how much that helps guess future sales. So, our main thing in this research was to make a system that can predict sales for those live shows where they also share knowledge. This system takes ideas about people supporting each other and mixes them with really smart computer programs (we call them LLMs) that we tweaked. It digs out all kinds of hidden signals from what streamers say to guess sales. We grabbed info from 80 live streams on 35 different Douyin channels over two months for our tests. When we tested how well it could spot social support, our special Ernie-SFT program totally knocked out the best regular smart program. It was 11.12% more on the nose, and did way better on other technical scores too, like 11.87% and 7.83% better. Then, when we used it to guess sales, we tried our system with four common ways, and it always worked better. The top one of those methods was 12.53% more accurate, and also got much higher on other technical scores, like 10.83% and 4.24% better. So, this all pretty much shows that the friendly, supportive stuff streamers say is really good for predicting sales. This gives streamers useful tips and helps the platforms make smarter moves based on solid info.
