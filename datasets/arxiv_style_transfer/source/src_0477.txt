We're checking out how our system works on four simulation tests with 64 tasks and 5 real-world tasks, looking at three types of VLA policies. For the tests, we're using Simpler, Libero, Robotwin1.0, and Robotwin2.0. Simpler is a test that uses the SAPIEN simulator and ManiSkill2, and it's got tasks for WindowX and Google Robot platforms, but we're mainly using WindowX tasks. Libero is about lifelong learning in decision making and has four sets of tasks. We're focusing on the toughest one because others have already done really well on the rest. Robotwin1.0 and Robotwin2.0 are about using dual-arm grippers for different tasks and collecting data automatically. For the real-world setup, we're using a REALMAN75 dual-arm robot in an office-like setting. There are five tasks with common items like books, pens, a cabinet, a charger, a laptop, and a paper ball. The tasks are: Receive Book, where the robot takes a book from a person and puts it back on the shelf; Storage Charger, where it picks up the charger, puts it in the cabinet, and closes the door; Paper and Pen, where it puts a paper ball and a pen in the trash and pen holder; Laptop, where it closes the laptop, unplugs the charger, and puts it back in the cabinet; and Pick Books, where both arms pick up and lift a book at the same time.
