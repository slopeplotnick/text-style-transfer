The prompts were made in the first language of each language team, even though the interface was in English, so there was no translation involved. After a team member finished a certain path, they made the prompt in their first language. Before starting, everyone did a quick sample project to get what they needed to do. Then, each team made at least 2,000 prompts after understanding the project goals. The scripted prompts came from materials that were culturally relevant and specific to certain areas like farming and health. These included a government publication with info on public services, Wikipedia in South African languages, a database for accurate vocabulary, and translated manuals on animal health and farming. The data for prompts was cleaned and prepared using a process to make sure the text was good for training speech recognition models. Each step improved the data by removing stuff that wasn't needed, sorting text into themes, and finding or marking personal info. When the data was taken in, initial checks for quality were done, including reviews for any structural problems. The text was filtered to keep only the South African languages we wanted. If the top language didn't match what was expected, it was left out, but confidence scores were kept as extra info.
