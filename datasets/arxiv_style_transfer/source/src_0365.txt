This platform has been around for 5-6 years. Based on the FEF framework [vdLSR07], ITK's platform is a "Level 3: Software Platform" with a standard setup used for all products. Right now, the setup has shared parts and specific spots for new versions. The goal is to get to a point where what's common and what's different in the domain are clear, and there's a standard setup for the whole software product line. This means reusing parts in a planned way from a parts library, having clear differences in the parts, and a clear standard setup that controls what changes are allowed in application setups, while adding many quality solutions in the software product line setup. Managing differences is clearly dealt with in the software product line setup, setting allowed setups for applications and defining where changes can happen and the limits for most of these changes, including rules that specific versions must follow. The main goal is to provide a software setup for automatically creating different versions. According to the FEF framework [vdLSR07], at the company level, ITK is at the fourth level. This means working together and coordinating domain engineering and application engineering. The project is developed through strong teamwork between agronomists and software developers. The decision to migrate and work with our research team was made in a group discussion with project leaders and stakeholders. Before we started planning interviews, we hadn't set a clear research method for the rest of the project. We wanted to first understand how the company's people felt about moving to SPL. Depending on what we find out from the interviews, we'll work on creating a clear method for the migration project.
