LLaMA-Factory mainly works on making text, not turning text-making problems into sorting tasks. Our setup, PEFT-FACTORY, deals with the small number of PEFT methods and helps tweak big language models for sorting tasks. There are other setups for training big language models that aren't as easy to use as LLaMA-Factory but have their own perks. FastChat is good for training big models for chat stuff. LitGPT and LMFlow are flexible and easy-to-use setups that work with different generative models and training ways. Axolotl is a tool you use in the terminal for efficient post-training of big models without losing function or scale. Open-Instruct is about instruction tweaking for big models and offers different models and ways to do this. H2O LLM Studio is a business-focused tool with a graphical interface for making and using big models. GPT4All makes a user-friendly interface around LLaMA for efficient training. ColossalAI is about fine-tuning across multiple nodes for distributed computing. LLaMA-Adapter and LLaMA-Accessory are lighter setups, with LLaMA-Adapter adding trainable adapters to LLaMA models and LLaMA-Accessory offering a full toolkit for big model development. LLaMA-Adapter is often used in setups like LitGPT. From looking at related setups, we found three main features that are missing or limited and new in our work: training big models with methods other than reprametrization-based PEFT methods, adding new PEFT methods in a modular and dynamic way, and supporting training and checking big models for sorting tasks.
