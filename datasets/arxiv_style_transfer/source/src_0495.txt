We're suggesting a way to make safety rules for lots of languages using just a few big ones. We've made a small safety model that works for over 100 languages, but we only trained it with data from 13 big languages. We looked at how well stuff from big languages works for smaller ones. Early systems for checking content mostly focused on English, like Google's Perspective API, which started with just English. Later, tools like Aegis-Defensive, WalledGuard, and WildGuard showed they could handle safety and bad content in English. But new studies found that popular language models give more unsafe answers in non-English. Also, prompts in less common languages can get past safety checks more easily. New methods are trying to fix these English-focused safety issues in many languages. Other tools like MrGuard and X-Guard use low-quality translated or fake data, missing the real details of less common languages. Recent tools like LlamaGuard3-8B and NemoGuard-8B-content-safety are based on the Llama3.1-8B model, and DuoGuard uses a new way of training with two players to make a small safety tool. As far as we know, PolyGuard is the best model for safety checks right now, and it comes with the biggest set of safety data for many languages. But even PolyGuard only covers 17 languages, showing that most current tools still focus on a few big languages.
