The PF model is a way to look at how a system with different materials and phases changes over time to reach balance. It works for small sizes and short times, which is useful for making solar cells. The energy details of the system are in something called a Gibbs free energy functional. Our method looks at how the solar cell forms, focusing on things like solvent drying, liquid separating, and forming crystals. The forces that make the system reach balance come from the Gibbs free energy functional. The changes in phases, like drying and forming crystals, are explained by some equations that also cover how mass moves around. During the simulations, we made sure the model works with a fluid dynamics tool that considers how mass moves with flow. For more details on how we did the simulations, check Section S2. We use the material details we got to look at how light moves in solar cells. Depending on how complex the solar cells are, we use either computer simulations or simpler methods to do this. We see the solar cell as layers of materials and use a model to make it easier to understand how light affects it. A neural network helps predict how much charge is made based on the angle of light, thickness of the active layer, and light color. More info on this model is in Section S3.
