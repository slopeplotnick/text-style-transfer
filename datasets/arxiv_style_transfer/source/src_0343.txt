Guardrails are systems that help keep things safe when people and AI interact. At first, they used simple rules to filter stuff out, but that didn't work well as things changed and new problems came up. These systems weren't very good at covering everything or being accurate. Now, newer guardrails use advanced AI models, which makes them more flexible and better at generalizing. For example, Llama Guard is a model that helps keep conversations between users and AI safe, and it's based on special safety data from Meta AI. There are also models like Llama Guard 3 Vision and LlavaGuard that look out for visual safety issues. But even with these improvements, there are still problems. Most of the work done before only focused on text and images, while videos and audio are handled in a basic way, often just using simple methods that don't really understand the context. Also, most systems only work with one type of media or specific situations, and they can't handle different types of media together or reason across them. To fix these issues, OMNIGUARD was created. It's the first system that can handle different types of media and reason through them to keep things safe. OMNIGUARD aims to provide clear and complete safety checks across all types of media.
