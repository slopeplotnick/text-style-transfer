Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have led to more interest in omni-modal language models (OLLMs), which can handle different types of data at once. Unlike older models that used separate parts, OLLMs are trained on mixed data, allowing them to understand and create text, audio, images, and videos together. These models work by turning different inputs into a common format, which helps them reason across different types of data. Models like Qwen2.5-Omni and LLaMA-Omni can generate text and speech in real-time. NExT-OMNI goes even further by allowing any type of data to be understood and created. However, these models have safety problems because of parameter issues, which can lead to risky situations.
