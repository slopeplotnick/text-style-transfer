When reasoning models like DeepSeek-AI and OpenAI came out in 2024 and 2025, it was a big deal for Large Language Models, making them much better. But lately, there's been a split. Open-source models like MiniMax, MoonShot, Qwen, and ZhiPu-AI are improving, but closed-source models like Anthropic, DeepMind, and OpenAI are getting better even faster. So, the gap between them is getting bigger, with closed-source models doing better at hard tasks. We found three main problems with open-source models: First, they mostly use an old method called vanilla attention, which isn't good for long information and makes it hard to use them on a big scale. Second, they don't get enough computing power after training, so they don't do as well on tough tasks. Lastly, open-source models aren't as good at generalizing and following instructions as closed-source ones, which makes them less useful in real life.
