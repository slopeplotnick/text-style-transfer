People do science and also think about how it's done. This brings up questions about the right way to do science. What makes a good scientific method? Science has changed over time, starting with observing and moving to questions about how models and data relate. It also looks at how people work together in science, because different social interactions set the standards and rules, even if they might be wrong later. People inside the field might not see mistakes because the community accepts them as normal. Scientists might be too confident about what they know (Bond et al., 2007). Thomas Kuhn studied these groups and called them "paradigms". He thinks paradigms are made of certain beliefs, rules, methods, and uses that people in a scientific community agree on, like evolution or plate tectonics.
