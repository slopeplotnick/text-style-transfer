We're trying to reuse code as much as possible and speed up how fast we can deliver new apps. We want to move our code to a higher-level SPL, like Level 4 or 5. To make this switch easier, we're starting by talking to people in the company to see what they think about SPL engineering. These talks are set up to answer some research questions we'll go over later. We'll do another round of talks at the end of the project to fine-tune the migration plan. The next steps in the migration will look at how things can change, starting with figuring out where features are so we can create feature models. Then, we'll study how these features interact and depend on each other to get a full picture of the system. We did these interviews to find out what people think about how things are done now, the benefits, risks, and worries before moving to an SPL. RQ1 is about what people in the company think about the current good practices in development. We want to know their views on what's working well now. RQ2 is about what they think the benefits of the new SPL will be. We want to talk about how the new SPL will add value to developing apps. RQ3 is about what they think the risks of the new SPL are. After talking about the benefits, we want to look at the risks in their daily work. RQ4 is about what fears they have because of these risks. This question helps us understand what worries people in the company have about the migration. These fears are about what might go wrong, the challenges we might face, and the need to plan and deal with these risks properly.
