We used a method that mixes label-driven CoT generation with finding hard cases. This helped the model think better by using step-by-step reasoning and dealing with tough examples. We checked how well it works by focusing on hard cases to see if it can handle tricky situations and complex reasoning. We made it so the CoT-based knowledge system could be used online and transferred to smaller models, which was a big step in making deep reasoning work better in real-world situations. We faced two big challenges: first, understanding product details from images that don't have much text; second, making sure decisions follow strict rules and business standards. To solve these, we used a strategy that lets the model understand both images and text while sticking to business rules. We used a mix of CoT Generation, SFT, and Reinforcement Learning based on Qwen-2.5 LLM for text and Qwen-2.5 VL for images. This setup allowed the model to handle both visual and text reasoning, overcoming limits of using just one type of data. We used multimodal and rule-aware CoT generation, combining understanding of images and text with prompts that follow standards. We put business rules and example cases into CoT reasoning to help the model understand rules. Then, we used reinforcement learning to make the model better at using rules for making decisions in complex situations.
