Radiology reporting is a key part of checking medical images, but it takes a lot of time and mistakes can happen, especially with 3D images like CT and MRI. Making sure the reports are good quality is also really important, but it's a boring job that involves checking everything carefully. Current automated methods mostly focus on making the reports but skip the important quality checks, which means they can't fully help radiologists. We've come up with Radiologist Copilot, an AI helper with tools to automate report writing and quality checks. It uses big language models to think and pick the right tools, plan, and do tasks like a radiologist would. These tools help find regions in images, plan analysis, choose report templates, and improve quality through feedback. This way, Radiologist Copilot helps make accurate and complete reports faster, helping radiologists and making things more efficient. Tests show that Radiologist Copilot does better than other top methods in making reports. The code will be shared once it's approved. Radiology reporting needs radiologists to carefully look at medical images and write detailed reports with main findings. But it's often slow and mistakes happen, especially with 3D images. Quality checks are also crucial to make sure reports are right and complete, but they're tedious because they involve checking details like content and language. So, automating report writing with quality checks is really important to help radiologists and make things run smoother.
