In this study, we introduced SchED, a method that doesn’t need extra training and works with any model setup. It decides when to stop based on how stable the model’s predictions are, using a confidence measure and a smooth threshold that adjusts as it goes. This approach cuts down the number of steps needed for denoising in both Dream and LLaDA models, especially for instruction-tuned ones where uncertainty drops quickly, while keeping the performance solid. This was backed up by looking at entropy and the quality-speed trade-off. SchED works with existing schedules without changing the models themselves. The schedules it creates range from very careful ones for high accuracy to faster ones for situations where speed or handling small errors matters more.
