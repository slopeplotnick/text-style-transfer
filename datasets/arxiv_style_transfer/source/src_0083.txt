Editing videos of people is tricky because you need to make changes like how they look, their expressions, or adding stuff, while keeping everything in sync with the original video. The hard part is making sure the edited video matches the timing and behavior of the original. SyncLoRA is a way to edit videos of people that keeps the edits looking good and the timing accurate. It works by using a model that starts with changes to the first frame of the video and spreads those changes to the rest of the frames. To make sure everything stays in sync, the model is trained with pairs of videos that have the same movements but look different. These pairs are picked out using a process that checks how well the timing matches. The training helps the model combine the movements from the original video with the changes made to the first frame. After being trained on a small set of carefully chosen videos of people, SyncLoRA can handle new faces and different edits, like changing how someone looks, adding things, or swapping backgrounds, even when poses or expressions change. The results show that it can make edits that look good and stay in sync with the original video. This kind of editing is useful for ads, movies, games, and other media where you need to control how things look. Editing videos of people is especially tough because so much media involves people, and you need to make sure the edits look natural and match the original.
